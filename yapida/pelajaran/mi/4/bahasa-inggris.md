# Semester Ganjil

## Things in My Classroom | Benda di Kelasku

Let's learn the names of things in our classroom!

- a book — buku
- a pen — pulpen
- a pencil — pensil
- a ruler — penggaris
- an eraser — penghapus
- a bag — tas
- a desk — meja
- a chair — kursi
- a whiteboard — papan tulis
- a clock — jam dinding

Gunakan **a** sebelum kata yang berbunyi konsonan (a book, a pen) dan **an** sebelum kata yang berbunyi vokal (an eraser, an apple).

### Asking and answering

::en What is this? — It is a ruler.

::en What is that? — That is a whiteboard.

::en Where is my pencil? — It is on the desk.

Kata depan tempat: **in** (di dalam), **on** (di atas), **under** (di bawah).

::kuis "Penggaris" in English is …? | *ruler | eraser | pencil | desk >> A ruler is used to draw straight lines.
::kuis We say … eraser. | *an | a | the | is >> Eraser diawali bunyi vokal, jadi memakai "an".
::kuis "The book is under the table." Artinya buku itu ada … meja. | *di bawah | di atas | di dalam | di samping >> under = di bawah.
::kuis What is this? It is … bag. | *a | an | in | on >> Bag diawali konsonan, jadi memakai "a".
::cocok chair = kursi; desk = meja; clock = jam dinding; whiteboard = papan tulis

## What Time Is It? | Pukul Berapa?

To tell the time we use **o'clock** for full hours and **half past** for thirty minutes past.

- 07.00 — It is seven o'clock.
- 07.30 — It is half past seven.
- 12.00 — It is twelve o'clock.

### My daily activities

- I wake up at four thirty.
- I pray Subuh.
- I take a bath.
- I have breakfast.
- I go to school at six thirty.
- I study in the classroom.
- I go home at twelve o'clock.
- I go to bed at nine o'clock.

::en What time is it? — It is eight o'clock.

::en What time do you go to school? — I go to school at half past six.

::kuis "It is half past seven" berarti pukul …? | *07.30 | 07.15 | 06.30 | 08.30 >> half past = lewat setengah.
::kuis "I have breakfast" artinya …? | *aku sarapan | aku tidur | aku mandi | aku belajar >> breakfast = sarapan.
::kuis Bahasa Inggrisnya "aku mandi" adalah …? | *I take a bath | I go to bed | I wake up | I go home >> take a bath = mandi.
::kuis It is ten … (pukul 10.00) | *o'clock | half | past | time >> Untuk jam tepat gunakan o'clock.

# Semester Genap

## Animals Around Us | Hewan di Sekitar Kita

- a cat — kucing
- a cow — sapi
- a goat — kambing
- a chicken — ayam
- a duck — bebek
- a fish — ikan
- a bird — burung
- a rabbit — kelinci

### Describing animals

::en A cow has four legs. It eats grass.

::en A bird has two wings. It can fly.

::en A fish lives in the water. It can swim, but it cannot walk.

Kata **can** artinya "bisa" dan **cannot (can't)** artinya "tidak bisa".

::kuis "Kambing" in English is …? | *goat | cow | cat | duck >> goat = kambing.
::kuis A bird can … | *fly | swim | read | write >> Burung bisa terbang (fly).
::kuis "A cow has four legs." Artinya sapi mempunyai … | *empat kaki | dua kaki | empat sayap | dua tanduk >> four legs = empat kaki.
::kuis A fish can't … | *walk | swim | eat | breathe >> Ikan berenang, tetapi tidak bisa berjalan.
::cocok rabbit = kelinci; chicken = ayam; duck = bebek; fish = ikan

## My House | Rumahku

- living room — ruang tamu
- bedroom — kamar tidur
- kitchen — dapur
- bathroom — kamar mandi
- dining room — ruang makan
- garden — kebun / taman
- prayer room — musala

### There is / There are

Gunakan **there is** untuk satu benda dan **there are** untuk banyak benda.

::en There is a bed in my bedroom.

::en There are three chairs in the dining room.

::en My mother cooks in the kitchen.

::kuis "Dapur" in English is …? | *kitchen | bedroom | garden | bathroom >> kitchen = dapur.
::kuis There … two cats in the garden. | *are | is | am | be >> Untuk lebih dari satu benda gunakan "there are".
::kuis "There is a bed in my bedroom." Artinya ada … di kamarku. | *sebuah tempat tidur | dua kursi | sebuah meja | sebuah lemari >> bed = tempat tidur.
::cocok living room = ruang tamu; bedroom = kamar tidur; bathroom = kamar mandi; garden = kebun
