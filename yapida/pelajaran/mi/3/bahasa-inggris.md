## Numbers 11–100 | Angka 11–100

eleven (11), twelve (12), thirteen (13), fourteen (14), fifteen (15), sixteen (16), seventeen (17), eighteen (18), nineteen (19), twenty (20), thirty (30), forty (40), fifty (50), sixty (60), seventy (70), eighty (80), ninety (90), one hundred (100).

::en There are twenty students in my class.

::en Twelve plus eight is twenty.

::kuis "Fifteen" adalah angka …? | *15 | 50 | 5 | 105 >> fifteen = lima belas.
::kuis Angka 40 dalam bahasa Inggris adalah …? | *forty | fourteen | four | fourty >> Penulisannya forty, bukan fourty.
::kuis Twelve plus eight is … | *twenty | eighteen | thirty | twelve >> 12 + 8 = 20.
::cocok eleven = 11; thirty = 30; seventy = 70; one hundred = 100

## Places at School | Tempat di Sekolah

classroom (ruang kelas), library (perpustakaan), canteen (kantin), schoolyard (halaman sekolah), mosque (masjid), office (kantor), toilet (toilet).

::en Where is the library? — It is next to the mosque.

::en We read books in the library. We eat in the canteen.

::kuis "Perpustakaan" in English is …? | *library | canteen | classroom | office >> library = perpustakaan.
::kuis We eat in the … | *canteen | library | toilet | office >> Kita makan di kantin.
::kuis "Where is the mosque?" artinya …? | *Di mana masjid? | Apa itu masjid? | Kapan ke masjid? | Masjid siapa? >> where = di mana.
::cocok classroom = ruang kelas; schoolyard = halaman sekolah; office = kantor; mosque = masjid

## Hobbies | Hobi

reading (membaca), drawing (menggambar), swimming (berenang), cycling (bersepeda), playing football (bermain sepak bola), cooking (memasak), gardening (berkebun).

::en What is your hobby? — My hobby is reading.

::en I like drawing. My brother likes playing football.

::kuis "My hobby is swimming." Artinya hobiku …? | *berenang | membaca | menggambar | bersepeda >> swimming = berenang.
::kuis "Berkebun" in English is …? | *gardening | cooking | cycling | drawing >> garden = kebun.
::kuis "What is your hobby?" artinya …? | *Apa hobimu? | Siapa namamu? | Di mana rumahmu? | Berapa umurmu? >> hobby = hobi.
::cocok reading = membaca; drawing = menggambar; cycling = bersepeda; cooking = memasak

## The Weather | Cuaca

sunny (cerah), rainy (hujan), cloudy (berawan), windy (berangin), hot (panas), cold (dingin).

::en How is the weather today? — It is sunny and hot.

::en It is rainy. Don't forget your umbrella!

::kuis "Rainy" artinya …? | *hujan | cerah | berawan | berangin >> rain = hujan.
::kuis "It is cold." Artinya cuacanya …? | *dingin | panas | cerah | hujan >> cold = dingin.
::kuis "How is the weather today?" artinya …? | *Bagaimana cuaca hari ini? | Hari apa sekarang? | Jam berapa sekarang? | Apa kabarmu? >> weather = cuaca.
::cocok sunny = cerah; cloudy = berawan; windy = berangin; hot = panas
