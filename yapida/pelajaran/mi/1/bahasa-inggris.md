# Semester Ganjil

## Greetings | Salam dan Sapaan

::en Good morning! — Selamat pagi!

::en Good afternoon! — Selamat siang/sore!

::en Good night! — Selamat malam (sebelum tidur)!

::en Hello, my name is Ali. What is your name? — My name is Zahra.

::en How are you? — I am fine, thank you.

::en Goodbye! See you tomorrow!

Sebagai anak muslim, kita juga mengucapkan salam: Assalamu'alaikum.

::kuis "Good morning" artinya …? | *selamat pagi | selamat malam | selamat tinggal | terima kasih >> morning = pagi.
::kuis "What is your name?" artinya …? | *Siapa namamu? | Apa kabar? | Berapa umurmu? | Di mana rumahmu? >> name = nama.
::kuis Jawaban "How are you?" adalah …? | *I am fine, thank you. | Good night. | My name is Ali. | Goodbye. >> fine = baik.
::cocok Hello = halo; Goodbye = selamat tinggal; Thank you = terima kasih; Good night = selamat malam

## Numbers 1–10 | Angka 1–10

one (1), two (2), three (3), four (4), five (5), six (6), seven (7), eight (8), nine (9), ten (10).

::en How many books? — Three books.

::en I have two hands and ten fingers.

::kuis "Five" adalah angka …? | *5 | 4 | 6 | 9 >> five = lima.
::kuis Angka 8 dalam bahasa Inggris adalah …? | *eight | seven | nine | six >> eight = delapan.
::kuis "I have two hands." Artinya aku punya … tangan. | *dua | satu | tiga | sepuluh >> two = dua.
::cocok one = satu; three = tiga; seven = tujuh; ten = sepuluh

# Semester Genap

## Colors | Warna

red (merah), yellow (kuning), blue (biru), green (hijau), white (putih), black (hitam), orange (oranye), pink (merah muda).

::en What color is it? — It is green.

::en The sky is blue. The banana is yellow.

::kuis "Red" artinya …? | *merah | kuning | biru | hijau >> red = merah.
::kuis The sky is … | *blue | red | black | pink >> Langit berwarna biru.
::kuis "What color is it?" artinya …? | *Apa warnanya? | Apa ini? | Berapa ini? | Siapa itu? >> color = warna.
::cocok yellow = kuning; green = hijau; white = putih; black = hitam

## Fruits | Buah-buahan

apple (apel), banana (pisang), orange (jeruk), mango (mangga), grape (anggur), watermelon (semangka), papaya (pepaya), dates (kurma).

::en I like bananas. Do you like mangoes? — Yes, I do.

::en Dates are the favorite fruit of the Prophet's family. We break our fast with dates.

::kuis "Semangka" in English is …? | *watermelon | papaya | grape | mango >> watermelon = semangka.
::kuis "Kurma" in English is …? | *dates | grapes | apples | oranges >> Kita berbuka puasa dengan kurma.
::kuis "I like bananas." Artinya aku suka …? | *pisang | apel | jeruk | mangga >> banana = pisang.
::cocok apple = apel; grape = anggur; papaya = pepaya; mango = mangga
