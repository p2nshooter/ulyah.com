# Semester Ganjil

## Keep Our Environment Clean | Jagalah Lingkungan Kita

### Vocabulary

- clean — bersih
- dirty — kotor
- rubbish / trash — sampah
- trash bin — tempat sampah
- plant — menanam
- river — sungai
- tree — pohon

### Imperative sentences (kalimat perintah dan larangan)

::en Throw the rubbish into the trash bin.

::en Please water the plants every morning.

::en Don't throw rubbish into the river.

::en Don't waste water when you take ablution.

Kalimat perintah diawali kata kerja (Throw, Water, Close). Kalimat larangan diawali **Don't**. Tambahkan **please** agar lebih sopan.

::kuis "Don't throw rubbish into the river." Artinya …? | *Jangan membuang sampah ke sungai. | Buanglah sampah ke sungai. | Sungai itu kotor. | Bersihkan sungai. >> Don't = jangan.
::kuis "Tempat sampah" in English is …? | *trash bin | river | tree | garden >> trash bin = tempat sampah.
::kuis Kalimat larangan dalam bahasa Inggris diawali kata …? | *Don't | Please | Let's | Can >> Contoh: Don't run in the classroom.
::cocok clean = bersih; dirty = kotor; rubbish = sampah; tree = pohon

## Describing People | Menggambarkan Orang

### Vocabulary

- tall — tinggi
- short — pendek
- kind — baik hati
- smart — pintar
- diligent — rajin
- friendly — ramah
- long hair — rambut panjang

::en This is my friend, Aisyah. She is tall and friendly. She has long black hair. She is very diligent.

::en My teacher is kind and smart. He always helps us.

Gunakan **he** untuk laki-laki dan **she** untuk perempuan. Gunakan **has** untuk he/she (She has long hair) dan **have** untuk I/you/we/they.

::kuis "Rajin" in English is …? | *diligent | friendly | tall | short >> diligent = rajin.
::kuis She … long black hair. | *has | have | is | are >> Untuk she/he gunakan has.
::kuis "My teacher is kind." Artinya guruku …? | *baik hati | tinggi | pintar | ramah >> kind = baik hati.

# Semester Genap

## Celebrating Eid | Merayakan Hari Raya

### Vocabulary

- Eid al-Fitr — Idulfitri
- Eid al-Adha — Iduladha
- mosque — masjid
- visit — mengunjungi
- relatives — kerabat
- forgive — memaafkan

### Talking about the past

::en Last Eid, we prayed at the mosque. Then we visited our grandparents. We asked for forgiveness and ate ketupat together.

Untuk peristiwa yang sudah terjadi kita memakai kata kerja bentuk lampau: pray → prayed, visit → visited, eat → ate, go → went.

::en Happy Eid! — Eid Mubarak! Taqabbalallahu minna wa minkum.

::kuis "We visited our grandparents." Artinya kami … kakek dan nenek. | *mengunjungi | menelepon | menunggu | menolong >> visited = mengunjungi (lampau).
::kuis Bentuk lampau dari "go" adalah …? | *went | goed | gone | going >> go → went.
::kuis "Iduladha" in English is …? | *Eid al-Adha | Eid al-Fitr | New Year | Ramadan >> Eid al-Adha = hari raya kurban.
::cocok mosque = masjid; relatives = kerabat; forgive = memaafkan; ate = makan (lampau)

## My Dream | Cita-citaku

### Jobs

- teacher — guru
- doctor — dokter
- nurse — perawat
- pilot — pilot
- farmer — petani
- engineer — insinyur
- police officer — polisi

::en What do you want to be? — I want to be a doctor.

::en I will study hard to make my dream come true, insyaallah.

Kata **will** menunjukkan "akan". Kata **want to be** artinya "ingin menjadi".

::kuis "I want to be a teacher." Artinya aku ingin menjadi …? | *guru | dokter | petani | pilot >> teacher = guru.
::kuis "I will study hard." Kata "will" artinya …? | *akan | sudah | sedang | jangan >> will = akan.
::kuis A … helps sick people in the hospital. | *nurse | farmer | pilot | engineer >> nurse = perawat.
::cocok doctor = dokter; farmer = petani; engineer = insinyur; police officer = polisi
