# Semester Ganjil

## My Family | Keluargaku

father (ayah), mother (ibu), brother (saudara laki-laki), sister (saudara perempuan), grandfather (kakek), grandmother (nenek), baby (bayi).

::en This is my family. This is my father. This is my mother. I have one brother and one sister.

::en I love my family.

::kuis "Mother" artinya …? | *ibu | ayah | kakak | nenek >> mother = ibu.
::kuis "Kakek" in English is …? | *grandfather | grandmother | brother | father >> grandfather = kakek.
::kuis "I love my family." Artinya aku … keluargaku. | *sayang | punya | lihat | kunjungi >> love = sayang/cinta.
::cocok father = ayah; sister = saudara perempuan; brother = saudara laki-laki; grandmother = nenek

## Parts of the Body | Anggota Tubuh

head, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, hands, feet.

::en I see with my eyes. I hear with my ears. I smell with my nose.

::en Touch your head! Clap your hands!

::kuis "Nose" artinya …? | *hidung | mata | telinga | mulut >> nose = hidung.
::kuis I hear with my … | *ears | eyes | nose | hands >> Kita mendengar dengan telinga.
::kuis "Clap your hands!" artinya …? | *tepuk tanganmu! | angkat tanganmu! | cuci tanganmu! | sentuh kepalamu! >> clap = tepuk.
::cocok head = kepala; mouth = mulut; feet = kaki; eyes = mata

# Semester Genap

## Shapes | Bentuk

circle (lingkaran), square (persegi), triangle (segitiga), rectangle (persegi panjang), star (bintang), heart (hati).

::en The clock is a circle. The window is a square. The roof looks like a triangle.

::kuis "Triangle" artinya …? | *segitiga | lingkaran | persegi | bintang >> triangle = segitiga.
::kuis The ball is a … | *circle | square | triangle | star >> Bola berbentuk bulat seperti lingkaran.
::kuis "Persegi panjang" in English is …? | *rectangle | square | circle | heart >> rectangle = persegi panjang.
::cocok circle = lingkaran; square = persegi; star = bintang; heart = hati

## My Clothes | Pakaianku

shirt (kemeja), trousers (celana panjang), skirt (rok), cap (topi/peci), headscarf (kerudung), shoes (sepatu), socks (kaus kaki).

::en I wear a white shirt and a cap to the mosque.

::en My sister wears a headscarf.

::en Put on your shoes, please.

::kuis "Socks" artinya …? | *kaus kaki | sepatu | topi | kemeja >> socks = kaus kaki.
::kuis "Kerudung" in English is …? | *headscarf | shirt | skirt | cap >> headscarf = kerudung.
::kuis "I wear a white shirt." Artinya aku memakai … putih. | *kemeja | celana | sepatu | topi >> shirt = kemeja.
::cocok shoes = sepatu; trousers = celana panjang; skirt = rok; cap = peci/topi
