# Semester Ganjil

## My Body and My Health | Tubuh dan Kesehatanku

### Parts of the body

- head — kepala
- eye — mata
- ear — telinga
- nose — hidung
- mouth — mulut
- hand — tangan
- leg — kaki
- stomach — perut

### Feeling sick

::en What's wrong with you? — I have a headache.

::en I have a fever. I have a cough. I have a stomachache.

::en You should see a doctor and take some medicine. Get well soon!

Untuk memberi saran gunakan **should** (sebaiknya): You should drink a lot of water.

::kuis "I have a headache" artinya aku …? | *sakit kepala | sakit perut | demam | batuk >> headache = sakit kepala.
::kuis "Telinga" in English is …? | *ear | eye | nose | mouth >> ear = telinga, eye = mata.
::kuis "Get well soon!" diucapkan kepada orang yang …? | *sakit | berulang tahun | bepergian | menang lomba >> Artinya semoga cepat sembuh.
::kuis You … drink a lot of water. (sebaiknya) | *should | can | is | are >> should = sebaiknya.
::cocok fever = demam; cough = batuk; medicine = obat; doctor = dokter

## Days and Months | Hari dan Bulan

### Days of the week

Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.

### Months of the year

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

::en What day is it today? — It is Friday.

::en When is your birthday? — My birthday is in May.

::en We go to the mosque for Friday prayer.

Nama hari dan bulan dalam bahasa Inggris selalu diawali huruf kapital.

::kuis "Kamis" in English is …? | *Thursday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Friday >> Thursday = Kamis, Tuesday = Selasa.
::kuis The first month of the year is …? | *January | December | March | June >> January = Januari.
::kuis "What day is it today?" artinya …? | *Hari apa sekarang? | Tanggal berapa sekarang? | Jam berapa sekarang? | Bulan apa sekarang? >> day = hari.

# Semester Genap

## At the Market | Di Pasar

### Fruits and vegetables

- apple — apel
- banana — pisang
- orange — jeruk
- mango — mangga
- carrot — wortel
- tomato — tomat
- potato — kentang
- cabbage — kubis

### Buying and selling

::en Seller: Good morning. Can I help you?

::en Buyer: Yes, please. How much is this mango?

::en Seller: It is fifteen thousand rupiah.

::en Buyer: I will take two, please. Here is the money. Thank you!

::kuis "How much is this mango?" artinya …? | *Berapa harga mangga ini? | Di mana mangga ini? | Berapa banyak mangga? | Mangga apa ini? >> How much = berapa (harga).
::kuis "Wortel" in English is …? | *carrot | cabbage | potato | tomato >> carrot = wortel.
::kuis Fifteen thousand = …? | *15.000 | 50.000 | 5.000 | 1.500 >> fifteen = lima belas.
::cocok banana = pisang; orange = jeruk; potato = kentang; cabbage = kubis

## Transportation | Kendaraan

- car — mobil
- bus — bus
- bicycle — sepeda
- motorcycle — sepeda motor
- train — kereta api
- ship — kapal laut
- airplane — pesawat terbang
- on foot — berjalan kaki

::en How do you go to school? — I go to school by bicycle.

::en My father goes to work by motorcycle.

::en I go to school on foot because my house is near.

Gunakan **by** + nama kendaraan (by car, by bus), tetapi **on foot** untuk berjalan kaki.

::kuis "I go to school by bicycle" artinya aku ke sekolah dengan …? | *sepeda | mobil | bus | berjalan kaki >> bicycle = sepeda.
::kuis "Berjalan kaki" in English is …? | *on foot | by foot | in foot | at foot >> on foot = berjalan kaki.
::kuis A … flies in the sky. | *airplane | ship | train | bus >> airplane = pesawat terbang.
::cocok train = kereta api; ship = kapal laut; motorcycle = sepeda motor; car = mobil
