# Semester Ganjil

## Ability and Obligation | Kemampuan dan Kewajiban

### Can / can't (kemampuan)

::en I can recite the Qur'an fluently. My brother can swim, but he can't ride a horse yet.

### Must / have to (kewajiban)

::en We must wear the school uniform on Monday. Students have to come before seven o'clock.

### Mustn't (larangan)

::en You mustn't cheat in the exam. You mustn't litter in the schoolyard.

::kuis "I can recite the Qur'an fluently." Kata "can" menyatakan …? | *kemampuan | larangan | kewajiban | keinginan >> can = bisa.
::kuis "You mustn't cheat in the exam." Artinya …? | *Kamu tidak boleh menyontek saat ujian. | Kamu harus menyontek. | Kamu bisa menyontek. | Kamu sebaiknya menyontek. >> mustn't = tidak boleh.
::kuis "Students … come before seven o'clock." (wajib) | *have to | can | may | might >> have to = harus.
::cocok can = bisa; can't = tidak bisa; must = harus; mustn't = tidak boleh

## Describing Animals and Places | Teks Deskriptif

Teks deskriptif menggambarkan sesuatu secara rinci. Strukturnya: identification (pengenalan) dan description (gambaran ciri-ciri).

::en The camel is an amazing animal. It lives in the desert. It has a long neck, long legs, and one or two humps on its back. A camel can walk for days without drinking water. In the Qur'an, Allah asks us to look at how the camel is created (QS. Al-Ghasyiyah: 17).

::en Masjid Istiqlal is the largest mosque in Southeast Asia. It is located in Jakarta. It has a big dome and a tall minaret.

::kuis Struktur teks deskriptif adalah …? | *identification dan description | orientation dan events | goal dan steps | opinion dan reason >> Pengenalan, lalu gambaran ciri.
::kuis "It lives in the desert." Kata "desert" artinya …? | *gurun | hutan | laut | gunung >> desert = gurun pasir.
::kuis Masjid terbesar di Asia Tenggara menurut teks adalah …? | *Masjid Istiqlal | Masjid Raya Baiturrahman | Masjid Agung Demak | Masjid Al-Azhar >> Terletak di Jakarta.

# Semester Genap

## Past Experiences | Pengalaman Masa Lalu

Teks recount menceritakan pengalaman masa lalu. Strukturnya: orientation (siapa, kapan, di mana), events (urutan kejadian), dan reorientation (penutup/kesan).

### Simple Past Tense

Kata kerja beraturan ditambah -ed (visit → visited); kata kerja tidak beraturan berubah bentuk (go → went, eat → ate, see → saw, take → took).

::en Last holiday, my family went to Bandung. We visited a tea plantation and prayed at a beautiful mosque. In the evening we ate grilled corn. It was a wonderful trip.

::kuis Bentuk lampau dari "see" adalah …? | *saw | seed | seen | sees >> see – saw – seen.
::kuis Bagian teks recount yang berisi urutan kejadian disebut …? | *events | orientation | reorientation | identification >> Events = rangkaian peristiwa.
::kuis "We … a tea plantation last holiday." | *visited | visit | visits | visiting >> Peristiwa lampau memakai bentuk lampau.
::cocok go = went; eat = ate; take = took; pray = prayed

## Giving Instructions | Teks Prosedur

Teks prosedur menjelaskan cara membuat atau melakukan sesuatu. Strukturnya: goal (tujuan), materials (bahan), dan steps (langkah). Langkah memakai kalimat perintah dan kata urutan: first, next, then, after that, finally.

::en How to Make Fried Rice. Materials: a plate of rice, two eggs, onions, garlic, sweet soy sauce, salt, and cooking oil. Steps: First, heat the oil in a pan. Next, fry the onions and garlic. Then, add the eggs and stir them. After that, put in the rice, sweet soy sauce, and salt. Finally, serve the fried rice on a plate.

::kuis Struktur teks prosedur adalah …? | *goal, materials, steps | orientation, events, reorientation | identification, description | thesis, arguments >> Tujuan, bahan, dan langkah.
::kuis Kata yang menunjukkan langkah terakhir adalah …? | *finally | first | next | then >> finally = akhirnya.
::kuis "Heat the oil in a pan." Artinya …? | *Panaskan minyak di wajan. | Tuang minyak ke piring. | Goreng telur. | Masak nasi. >> heat = panaskan.
