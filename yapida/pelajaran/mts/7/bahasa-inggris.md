# Semester Ganjil

## Introducing Myself and Others | Perkenalan

::en Hi, my name is Fatimah. I am thirteen years old. I am a student of MTs Al Huda. I live in Sukakarya, Bekasi. Nice to meet you!

::en This is my friend, Umar. He is from Cikarang. He is in class seven, too.

### Grammar: to be (am, is, are)

- I **am** a student.
- He / She / It **is** …
- You / We / They **are** …

Kalimat negatif: I am not late. She is not (isn't) sick. Kalimat tanya: Are you a new student? — Yes, I am.

::kuis "She … my classmate." | *is | am | are | be >> She memakai "is".
::kuis "Nice to meet you" diucapkan ketika …? | *pertama kali berkenalan | berpisah | meminta maaf | berterima kasih >> Jawabannya: Nice to meet you, too.
::kuis "They … from Bekasi." | *are | is | am | be >> They memakai "are".
::cocok I = am; He = is; We = are; It = is

## Describing People | Menggambarkan Orang

### Appearance (penampilan)

tall, short, slim, chubby, long hair, short hair, straight hair, curly hair, round face, oval face.

### Personality (sifat)

kind, friendly, honest, diligent, polite, patient, generous, funny.

::en My teacher, Mr. Hasan, is tall and slim. He has short black hair. He is patient and kind. He always helps us when we have difficulties.

Urutan kata sifat sebelum benda: opinion – size – shape – color – noun, misalnya "a beautiful long black scarf".

::kuis "Honest" artinya …? | *jujur | sabar | rajin | lucu >> honest = jujur.
::kuis "He is patient." Artinya ia …? | *sabar | sombong | malas | pemalu >> patient = sabar.
::kuis Susunan yang benar adalah …? | *a beautiful long black scarf | a black long beautiful scarf | a long black beautiful scarf | a scarf beautiful long black >> Opinion – size – color – noun.

# Semester Genap

## My Daily Activities | Kegiatan Sehari-hari

### Simple Present Tense

Dipakai untuk kebiasaan dan kegiatan rutin. Untuk he/she/it, kata kerja ditambah -s/-es: she reads, he goes, she studies.

::en I wake up at four o'clock. I pray Subuh with my father at the mosque. Then I read the Qur'an for twenty minutes.

::en My sister usually helps my mother in the kitchen. She always goes to school by bicycle.

### Adverbs of frequency

always (selalu) – usually (biasanya) – often (sering) – sometimes (kadang-kadang) – never (tidak pernah).

::kuis "She … to school by bicycle every day." | *goes | go | going | gone >> He/she/it + kata kerja -s/-es.
::kuis "Always" artinya …? | *selalu | kadang-kadang | jarang | tidak pernah >> always = selalu.
::kuis Kalimat negatif yang benar adalah …? | *He does not eat pork. | He not eat pork. | He do not eats pork. | He does not eats pork. >> does not + kata kerja dasar.
::cocok usually = biasanya; often = sering; sometimes = kadang-kadang; never = tidak pernah

## Things around Us | Benda di Sekitar Kita

### There is / There are and prepositions

::en There is a whiteboard in front of the class. There are thirty desks in my classroom.

::en The library is next to the mosque. The canteen is behind the laboratory. My bag is under the chair.

Kata depan tempat: in (di dalam), on (di atas), under (di bawah), next to (di samping), behind (di belakang), in front of (di depan), between (di antara).

::kuis "The canteen is behind the laboratory." Artinya kantin berada … laboratorium. | *di belakang | di depan | di samping | di dalam >> behind = di belakang.
::kuis "There … thirty desks in my classroom." | *are | is | am | be >> Benda jamak memakai there are.
::kuis "Di antara" dalam bahasa Inggris adalah …? | *between | behind | beside | below >> between = di antara.
