## Greetings | Salam

::en Assalamu’alaikum. Good morning, teacher!
::tr Assalamu’alaikum. Selamat pagi, Bu Guru / Pak Guru!

::en Good morning, everyone.
::tr Selamat pagi, semuanya.

::en How are you today?
::tr Apa kabarmu hari ini?

::en I am fine, alhamdulillah. Thank you.
::tr Aku baik, alhamdulillah. Terima kasih.

::en What is your name?
::tr Siapa namamu?

::en My name is Aisyah. Nice to meet you.
::tr Namaku Aisyah. Senang berkenalan denganmu.

::en See you tomorrow!
::tr Sampai jumpa besok!

## My Classroom | Kelasku

::en This is my classroom.
::tr Ini kelasku.

::en This is a book. That is a pencil.
::tr Ini buku. Itu pensil.

::en Please open your book.
::tr Silakan buka bukumu.

::en Please close the door.
::tr Tolong tutup pintunya.

::en May I go to the toilet, please?
::tr Bolehkah saya ke kamar kecil?

::en Let us read together.
::tr Mari kita membaca bersama.

::en I have a question.
::tr Saya punya pertanyaan.

## My Family | Keluargaku

::en This is my father.
::tr Ini ayahku.

::en This is my mother.
::tr Ini ibuku.

::en I have one brother and two sisters.
::tr Aku punya satu saudara laki-laki dan dua saudara perempuan.

::en My grandfather lives in Bekasi.
::tr Kakekku tinggal di Bekasi.

::en I love my family.
::tr Aku sayang keluargaku.

## My Day | Kegiatanku

::en I wake up at half past four.
::tr Aku bangun jam setengah lima.

::en I pray Subuh with my father at the mosque.
::tr Aku shalat Subuh bersama ayah di masjid.

::en I take a bath and have breakfast.
::tr Aku mandi dan sarapan.

::en I go to school at seven o’clock.
::tr Aku berangkat ke sekolah jam tujuh.

::en In the afternoon, I study the Qur’an at TPQ.
::tr Sore hari, aku belajar Al-Qur’an di TPQ.

::en At night, I do my homework and go to bed early.
::tr Malam hari, aku mengerjakan PR dan tidur lebih awal.

## Good Manners | Sopan Santun

::en Please.
::tr Tolong.

::en Thank you very much.
::tr Terima kasih banyak.

::en You are welcome.
::tr Sama-sama.

::en I am sorry.
::tr Maaf.

::en Excuse me.
::tr Permisi.

::en Let me help you.
::tr Biar aku bantu.

::en Always say bismillah before you start.
::tr Selalu ucapkan bismillah sebelum memulai.

Great job! Practice these sentences every day with your friends.
