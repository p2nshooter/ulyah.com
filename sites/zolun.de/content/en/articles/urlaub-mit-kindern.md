Vacationing with children is different than vacationing for two. Demands change with each year of life: a baby needs peace and quiet and short distances, a kindergarten child needs a sandbox and animals, a school child needs adventure and a teenager needs WiFi and freedom. Germany is an ideal travel destination for families, with short journeys, safe beaches, farms, lakes and countless leisure activities. This guide will help you plan your destination, accommodation and daily routine so that everyone, including adults, can relax.

> **The most important things in brief**
>
> - Choose the destination based on the age of the children, not the postcard. A short journey and plenty of space to play are more important than famous sights.
> - Holiday apartments, farms and family hotels are often more relaxed than normal hotel rooms.
> - Plan less program than you think. One trip per day is enough.
> - When on the water, never let your children out of your sight, even for a short time.
> - You should have a plan B for rainy days and sick days.

## Goals by age

**Babies and toddlers up to three years**

At this age, short distances, a quiet place to sleep and a fixed rhythm count more than excursions. The following are well suited:

- A holiday apartment with a separate bedroom so that parents don't have to sit in the dark in the evening.
- Flat sandy beaches on the Baltic Sea or quiet swimming areas on lakes.
- Holiday destinations with a short journey so that the journey does not take half the day.

Ask the accommodation for a cot, high chair, baby monitor, stair gate and bathtub.

**Kindergarten children aged three to six**

Now the time of great discoveries begins. Animals, sand, water and playgrounds are the highlights.

- **Farm vacation** where children feed animals, drive tractors and play in the hay.
- **North and Baltic Sea** with mudflat hikes, building castles and searching for shells. More in the guide [North Sea or Baltic Sea] (/reisen/nordsee-oder-ostsee/).
- **Family-friendly campsites** with a playground and children's program.

**School children aged seven to twelve**

School children want to experience something and have a say. Include them in the planning.

- **Active vacation** with bike tours, canoe trips, climbing gardens and hikes with a destination. More ideas in the guide [Mecklenburg Lake District](/reisen/mecklenburgische-seenplatte/).
- **Islands** with ferry ride, seals and lighthouses. More in the guide [East Frisian Islands](/reisen/ostfriesische-inseln/).
- **Cities with participatory museums** such as technology and natural history museums, zoos and castles.

**Young people**

Teenagers need space. Choose destinations where they can move around independently, such as a place with a beach, bus, and bike rentals. Let them plan a day of vacation themselves. WiFi in the accommodation is not an afterthought for many young people.

## The right accommodation

- **Holiday apartment or holiday home:** Space, kitchen, your own rhythms. Often the best choice for families, especially for a week or more.
- **Farm:** Animals, nature and lots of space to play. Ideal for children up to around ten years old.
- **Family Hotel:** Child care, games room, pool and no cooking meals. More expensive, but relaxing for parents.
- **Campsite:** Adventure, community and quickly making new friends for the children.
- **Youth hostel:** Family rooms, breakfast and program at a low price.

The guide [Hotel, guesthouse, holiday apartment or farm?] (/reisen/hotel-pension-ferienwohnung/) offers a detailed comparison.

**What you should pay attention to:** Location in relation to street and water, stairs and balconies, playground nearby, washing machine, shopping facilities and whether the accommodation is really quiet. Read reviews from other families.

## The arrival

**By Car:**

- Go early in the morning or late at night when the kids are asleep. Avoid the first days of vacation and Saturdays during peak travel times.
- Plan to take an exercise break every two hours, not just at the gas station, but at a rest area with a playground.
- Radio plays, quizzes and snacks save long journeys.
- Check whether child seats fit everyone and are installed correctly.

**By train:**

- Children travel free on long-distance transport when accompanied by parents or grandparents if they are listed on the ticket.
- Reserve seats in the family area.
- Children can move around on the train and no one has to concentrate on the traffic.

More about this in the guide [Going on vacation by train](/reisen/mit-der-bahn-in-den-urlaub/).

## The daily rhythm

The most common mistake on a family vacation is too much program. Children need time to play, rest and get bored. A proven rhythm:

- **Morning:** Excursion or activity when everyone is rested.
- **Lunch:** Meal and rest break, afternoon nap for small children.
- **Afternoon:** Beach, playground, garden or pool near the property.
- **Evening:** Dinner together, a walk, early to bed.

One big trip per day is enough. Also plan entire days without a program where everyone does whatever they feel like.

## Packing with children

With children, your luggage quickly doubles in size. Less is still more, because almost everything can be bought or washed on the go.

- **One week's worth of clothes** will last for two weeks if there is a washing machine.
- **Weatherproof clothing** for everyone: rain jacket, mud pants for the little ones and sturdy shoes.
- **The favorite cuddly toy** and the usual sleep book help on the first night in a strange place.
- **A travel bag** with a change of clothes, snacks, water, wipes and bandages.
- **Toys that take up little space:** Card games, art supplies, a ball and sand toys that you can buy locally if necessary.

Have older children pack their own backpack, with a short list to help them. This way they feel responsible and don't miss anything important at their destination.

## City trips with children

Cities can also be fun with children if the program is right. Choose one big destination per day, such as an interactive museum, a zoo or a castle, and plan playgrounds, parks and ice cream in between. Ferry, tram or elevator rides up a tower are often highlights for children. Many cities offer children's tours and city rallies. More tips in the guides [Discover Hamburg](/reisen/hamburg-entdecken/) and [Munich](/reisen/muenchen-staedtereise/).

## Safety on the water

Water is the greatest fun on a family vacation and at the same time the greatest danger. Drowning happens quietly and quickly.

- **Never take your eyes off children in the water,** not even to look at your cell phone.
- **Choose supervised swimming areas** and pay attention to the lifeguard flags.
- **Water wings and floating animals** are not life-saving devices. When there is an offshore wind, swimming animals quickly drift out into the open water.
- **Agree on clear rules:** Only go into the water with an adult, arrange a meeting point on the beach.
- **Swimming course before vacation:** Children who can swim safely are better protected.

Detailed rules can be found in the guides [Swimming safely by the sea](/reisen/sicher-baden-am-meer/) and [Swimming in lakes and rivers](/reisen/baden-in-seen-und-fluessen/).

## Sun, ticks and small emergencies

- **Sun protection:** Sunscreen with a high sun protection factor, sun hat, UV shirt on the beach and shade at lunchtime.
- **Ticks:** Check the children thoroughly after trips to the forest and meadow.
- **Travel first aid kit:** Plasters, thermometer, medicine for fever and pain in children's doses, mosquito repellent, disinfectants and the medication your child needs regularly.
- **Pack your health insurance card and vaccination certificate**. Make a note of the number of the medical on-call service, which applies throughout Germany: 116 117. In an emergency, dial 112.

## Plan B for rainy days

Rain is part of a vacation in Germany. With a few ideas it can still be a nice day:

- **Swimming pools and fun pools** nearby.
- **Participatory museums**, aquariums, planetariums and children's theaters.
- **Indoor playgrounds** and climbing halls.
- **Mud pants on and off:** With weatherproof clothing, puddles and mudflat walks in drizzle are an adventure.
- **Game day in the holiday apartment** with board games, crafts and pancakes.

More ideas in the guide [Rainy days on vacation](/reisen/regentage-im-urlaub/).

## Keep an eye on the budget

Family vacations can be expensive: accommodation for several people, entrance fees, dining out and souvenirs all add up. A few savings tips:

- Accommodation is cheaper in low seasons and in small towns. If you are tied to school holidays, you can at least avoid the most expensive week.
- Guest cards offer free local transport and reduced entrance fees in many holiday resorts.
- Breakfast and dinner in the apartment, a picnic at lunchtime.
- Plan free experiences such as beaches, forests, playgrounds and hikes.
- A fixed holiday pocket money for school children ends the daily discussion about ice cream, souvenirs and carousels. The children decide for themselves how they spend it and learn how to handle money at the same time.

More about this in the guide [Plan your vacation budget honestly](/reisen/urlaubsbudget-planen/).

## Typical mistakes and how to avoid them

1. **Travel too long for small children.** A closer destination means more vacation.
2. **A big excursion every day.** Children need free time.
3. **Choose accommodation based on pictures only.** Ask about location, stairs and street.
4. **Look away briefly at the water.** Drowning happens quickly and silently.
5. **No plan for rainy days.** A few ideas in hand will save the mood.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which region in Germany is the most family-friendly?

That depends on the age of the children. Flat Baltic Sea beaches and farms are ideal for small children, lake regions and low mountain ranges with active activities for school children. Many regions have their own family offers and guest cards.

### When is the best time to travel with children?

If you are bound to school holidays, it is best to choose the beginning or end of the holidays when other federal states are still in school. More in the guide [Best travel time for a vacation in Germany] (/reisen/beste-reisezeit/).

### What to do if a child gets sick while on vacation?

If you have mild symptoms, just a quiet day in your accommodation often helps. Outside office hours, you can reach the medical on-call service on 116 117, who will tell you a practice or on-call practice nearby. Pharmacies have an emergency service, the address of which is posted at every pharmacy. If an infant has a high fever, shortness of breath or impaired consciousness, dial 112 immediately. With the health insurance card you are insured everywhere in Germany.

### How long should a family vacation last?

Children take a few days to arrive. One week is the minimum for real recovery, two weeks is ideal. For more planning topics, see [Travel Planning](/themen/reiseplanung/).
