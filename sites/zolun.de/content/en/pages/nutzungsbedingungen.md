It is general information based on consistent knowledge. They do not replace binding information on timetables, tariffs, prices, ferry times, opening times, tourist taxes or local rules. This information is determined by transport companies, shipping companies, municipalities and hosts, and it changes often. Therefore, check them before each booking and before departure at the responsible office.

Likewise, our texts do not replace legal advice, for example on cancellations or passenger rights, or medical or veterinary advice, for example on first aid kits or traveling with a dog.

## Personal responsibility on the go

Many guides lead you to the water: to beaches, lakes, rivers and the Wadden Sea. Wind, weather, currents and tides can change quickly. You decide for yourself on site what you feel comfortable doing for yourself and your fellow travelers. Adhere to signs, flags, closures and the instructions of lifeguards, national park administrations and authorities. These always take precedence over our general descriptions. We have summarized the basic rules for safe travel on the water in [Travel Check](/reisecheck/).
