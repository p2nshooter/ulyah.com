ZOLUN stands for **Between **O**stsee, **L**and **u**nd **N**ordsee. In between lies almost everything that makes a holiday in Germany: the Wadden Sea and the Bodden, islands with and without cars, lakes in the foothills of the Alps and in Mecklenburg, river valleys, small old towns and large cities. We write for everyone who wants to vacation here: for the family who is going to the North Sea for the first time with two children, sand toys and too much luggage, for the couple who is planning a long weekend in a foreign city, for the dog owner who is looking for accommodation where their dog is truly welcome, and for everyone who prefers to travel by train rather than by car.

## Why ZOLUN exists

Vacationing in your own country seems easy, but you're still constantly stumbling over questions. When does the ferry leave if it depends on the tide? Is the Germany ticket also valid on the island? What is a tourist tax and who collects it? Is a holiday apartment really cheaper than a hotel if final cleaning and bed linen are included? The answers are spread across advertising brochures, booking portals, forums with posts from ten years ago and websites where you can't tell who wrote them.

We want to be a calm, reliable contact point. A guide that explains how things work instead of just giving out superlatives. Who honestly says when a beach disappears at high tide, when an island is very quiet in November and when a trip is not worth it. And in the end, you leave the decision up to you because only you know what's good for you on vacation.

## What you will find with us

ZOLUN is divided into six topics that we call “courses”:

1. **North Sea & Baltic Sea:** Tides and mudflats, seaside resorts, beach chairs, piers and cliffs
2. **Islands:** from Sylt to Usedom, from the Halligen to Fehmarn, with ferries and everyday life on the island
3. **Lakes & Rivers:** Lake Constance, Chiemsee, Lake District, Spreewald and quiet days on the shore
4. **City trips:** Weekends in big and small towns, museums and markets
5. **Arrival & accommodation:** Train and Germany ticket, long-distance bus, car, ferry, holiday apartment, hotel and camping
6. **Travel Planning:** Travel time, packing lists, vacation with kids or dog, rainy days and budget

## How our texts are created

Each guide is developed, checked and maintained individually by the ZOLUN editorial team. We rely on information from transport companies, municipalities, tourism organizations and authorities, such as tides, weather warnings or bathing waters, and on what has proven itself time and again when traveling in your own country. Prices, timetables and opening times are constantly changing. That's why we don't mention them from memory, but tell you where you can officially read them. How we work exactly is stated in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## What we don't do

We do not sell trips, do not arrange accommodations and do not receive any commissions from hotels, booking portals, shipping companies or transport companies. No place pays to be mentioned with us. ZOLUN is financed through advertising from Google AdSense. They are recognizable as advertising and have no influence on what we write about.

## The name and the picture

In the first book of Kings it is told that King Solomon had a fleet of Tarshish ships on the sea. Every three years these ships came home bringing gold, silver and other valuables (1 Kings 10:22). Our design is reminiscent of this fleet: a brass compass rose, a porthole with a view of a ship with a striped square sail, dotted routes like on an old nautical map. When you point to a topic in the menu, the compass needle swings there and a route is drawn to your destination. Our cargo is more modest than gold, but suitable for everyday use: reliable knowledge for your next vacation.
