This policy explains in detail which cookies and similar technologies are used when you visit zolun.de and how you decide what is stored on your device. It supplements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device via your browser. The next time you access it, the website or an integrated service can read it again. Other technologies work similarly, for example the browser's local storage and session memory, device identifiers or small graphics that report a call. Some of these technologies are technically necessary for a website, others are used for advertising or measurement.

## What ZOLUN stores itself

ZOLUN does not set its own cookies to recognize you. Our page view count works completely without cookies and without identifiers. We don't remember which destinations you've visited or when you'll be back.

The website stores a single piece of information in your browser's session memory: whether the short celebration animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown in the head of the page. This means it only appears once per visit and not every time you change pages. The information does not leave your device and disappears when you close the browser. It is technically necessary for the desired display (Section 25 Para. 2 TDDDG).

| What | From whom | For what | How long |

## Google AdSense and third party cookies

We use Google AdSense for the advertisements on this website. Google and Google's advertising partners listed as third parties in the consent message may use cookies and similar technologies for these purposes:

- **Deliver ads:** so that an ad appears at all
- **Limit frequency:** so you don't see the same ad over and over again
- **Detect fraud and abuse:** such as automated clicks
- **Measure impact:** how often an ad was shown or clicked on
- **Personalize ads:** only with your consent, based on your interests

Which cookies are set in detail depends on your selection, your browser and the advertisements delivered and can change. The storage period also differs depending on the cookie. Google describes its advertising cookies in its privacy policy at policies.google.com. We cannot read these cookies ourselves.

## Google Fonts

The fonts on this website are loaded from Google servers. To the best of our knowledge, no cookies are set. However, your IP address will be transmitted to Google, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/).

## The consent message

A consent message appears on the first visit. There you can agree to all purposes, reject all or select individual purposes and third parties using the options. No cookies will be stored for personalized advertising without your consent. Ads that are not based on your interests may continue to appear. Which information Google uses for this depends on the purposes you have agreed to.

## Change or revoke consent

You can change your decision at any time. To do this, use the link to the privacy settings provided in the consent message or delete the cookies from this website in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. A revocation applies to the future. What has happened up to that point based on your consent remains lawful.

## Manage cookies in the browser

In your browser settings you can view and delete stored cookies, generally block third-party cookies or set cookies to be deleted when you close them. How to do this is described in your browser's help. This is also practical when you're on the go: If you use someone else's or shared device while traveling, such as in a holiday apartment, delete your history and cookies afterwards. ZOLUN's guides remain fully readable even without cookies.
