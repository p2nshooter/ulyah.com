Anyone planning a vacation often reveals a lot about themselves online: travel dates, place of residence, who is going with them. With ZOLUN you don't have to specify any of this. You can read each guide without registering, without filling out a form and without telling us where you're going. In accordance with Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), this declaration informs you about what data is still technically generated when you visit zolun.de, who is responsible for it and what rights you have.

## Responsible person

The provider named in [Imprint](/impressum/), p2nshooter as the publisher of ZOLUN, is responsible for data processing on this website. You can reach us at [kontakt@zolun.de](mailto:kontakt@zolun.de).

## What ZOLUN does not raise

ZOLUN is an advisor and not a travel agency. There are no bookings, no registration, no customer account, no comment function, no newsletter and no contact form. We do not ask for your location or travel details, and we do not sell data to third parties.

## Hosting and server protocols

The website is delivered via the Cloudflare, Inc. network. When you access a page, Cloudflare processes technically necessary information: your IP address, date and time of access, the address accessed, browser type and operating system as well as the amount of data transferred. This information is needed to send the page to your browser and to protect it from attacks and misuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operations (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). Cloudflare can also process data in the USA. The transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on EU standard contractual clauses.

## Range measurement without cookies

To find out which guides are being read, we count page views using the publisher's own slim count, which is also operated via Cloudflare. Your browser only transmits the name of the website and the path accessed, for example the address of a guide about island ferries. A total of the views per day and page is saved. So that we can see how many people are currently reading, a timestamp is stored for each view without any further information and is automatically deleted after a short time. No cookies are set, no IP addresses are saved and no profiles are created. The browser identifier is only evaluated at the moment of access in order to count visits by search engine robots separately and is not saved. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the offer (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR).

## Fonts from Google Fonts

To display the fonts, your browser loads font files from servers at Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, when you access the page. Your IP address is transmitted to Google and Google learns that zolun.de was accessed. We use the fonts for a uniform, easy-to-read appearance (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). If your browser blocks external fonts, a standard font on your device will be used and the page will remain completely readable. Google describes details in its data protection declaration at policies.google.com.

## Advertising with Google AdSense

ZOLUN is free and is financed by advertisements from Google AdSense, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited. Google and its partners, so-called third parties, may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to serve ads, limit their frequency, prevent fraud and measure the effectiveness of ads. If you consent, advertisements may also be tailored to your interests derived from your visits to this and other websites.

Such information will only be stored and read on your device with your consent (Section 25 Paragraph 1 TDDDG, Art. 6 Paragraph 1 Letter a GDPR). We obtain consent via a consent message that appears on the first visit. There you can see the third parties involved and can agree or reject individual purposes. Non-personalized ads may continue to appear without consent. Data can also be transferred to the USA. Google is certified according to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Google also explains how Google uses data from partner websites and how you can change your ad settings at policies.google.com.

## Revoke consent

You can revoke or change your consent at any time with future effect: via the link to the data protection settings provided in the consent message or by deleting the cookies from this website in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. Details can be found in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-richtlinie/).

## Storage in the browser

Your browser will remember in the session memory whether the short celebration animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown in the top of the page. This information does not leave your device and is deleted when you close the browser.

## Encrypted connection

The website is delivered via an encrypted connection (HTTPS). You can recognize this by the lock symbol and the address that begins with "https://“. This means that no one can read which guides you are accessing during the transmission.

## Contact via email

If you write us an email, for example with a reference to an outdated ferry connection or with a desired topic, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to respond to you (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b or lit. f GDPR). We will not pass on this data and will delete it as soon as your request has been completed and there are no legal retention obligations to the contrary. Please do not send us any booking documents, copies of ID or health information. We don't need them.

## Places that we mention in guides

In our guides we refer to transport companies, shipping companies, municipalities, tourist information centers and authorities where you can find up-to-date information. If you access their websites or apps, you will leave ZOLUN. The data protection information of the respective provider applies there, over which we have no influence.

## Your rights

According to the GDPR, you have the right to information about the data stored about you (Article 15), to rectification (Article 16), to deletion (Article 17), to restriction of processing (Article 18), to data portability (Article 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21). You can revoke your consent at any time (Art. 7 Para. 3). You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77), for example the authority of the federal state in which you live. For all inquiries, send an email to [kontakt@zolun.de](mailto:kontakt@zolun.de).

## No automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing and we do not create profiles ourselves. You are not legally or contractually obliged to provide us with personal data. You can read all guides even if you refuse consent for advertising.

## Changes

We will adapt this statement if the website, the services used or the legal situation change. The version published here applies.
