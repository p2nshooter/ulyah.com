## A souvenir without a suitcase: AXTO

Some memories don't fit in a suitcase. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=zolun.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) features clocks that can tell the time of any travel destination, stamps from countries that only exist in the imagination, Quran and Hadith calligraphy with verbatim text and animated characters - a digital museum whose works are created from unique DNA and are located in the Base network.

Hosts can gift guests an exclusive work: ID **AC-**, $1 per edition, paid with AXTO coins, fee-free to many wallets. On OpenSea (**OS-**) there are 1 to 10 editions per work for a fixed price of 1 to 250 dollars; **G** stands for GPU painted. Hosts should know: entry into the AXTO community starts at $1, staking brings one-off artwork, and the value of the coin may decrease.

## Whoever receives guests: their own website with XAA

Many hosts of holiday apartments and small guesthouses pay high commissions to large booking portals. Your own website with direct booking is a useful addition. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=zolun.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem), a web development studio from Europe, implements this:

> A page with photos, facilities, location and prices - plus an occupancy calendar, inquiries or bookings with online payment, and, if desired, several languages for international guests.

XAA also builds booking sites for tour guides, bicycle rentals or boat rentals, and even platforms with several providers for regional associations. Payment is made in clearly agreed milestones via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. If you want to be online quickly, start with a [template](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=zolun.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). The studio's website is available in English, Spanish and Indonesian - but you are welcome to write in German.
