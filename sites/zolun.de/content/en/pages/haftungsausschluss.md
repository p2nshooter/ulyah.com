ZOLUN would like to help you start your vacation in Germany calmly and well prepared. So that it is clear where the limits of our guides lie, we have summarized them here. Please read this note before planning or booking a trip based on our texts.

## General information without guarantee

We develop each guide carefully and revise it if something significant changes. Nevertheless, we cannot guarantee that all information is complete, correct and up-to-date at all times. Travel information ages particularly quickly: a train station is being rebuilt, a bus route is being given a new route, a municipality is changing the rules for the beach.

## Prices, timetables and rules on site

We deliberately do not mention any specific prices, timetables, ferry times, tariffs, opening times or tourist tax amounts in our guides. Where we describe processes, such as how a tide-dependent ferry works or what a guest card is, these are general explanations. Only the information provided by transport companies, shipping companies, municipalities, tourist information centers, authorities and hosts is binding. Check before each booking and again before departure. Where you can find something is written in the [traveler's check](/reisecheck/).

## Nature, water and weather

The sea, mud flats, lakes, rivers and cliffs are beautiful and at the same time not without risk. Tides, currents, wind, fog, thunderstorms and breaking cliff edges can be dangerous and the situation on site can change within a short period of time. Our descriptions do not replace the assessment of the situation on site or the advice of lifeguards, national park administrations and authorities. Stick to flags, signs and closures, heed the warnings of the German Weather Service and only enter the mudflats with expert guidance.

## Health

When we write about sun protection, travel first aid kits, traveling while pregnant or with small children, seasickness on the ferry or heat in the city, we give general advice. They are not medical advice. If you have previous illnesses, are taking medication regularly or are unsure, talk to your doctor or the pharmacy before traveling. If you have any questions about your dog's health, vaccinations or its resilience in heat and long journeys, please contact your veterinary practice.

## Law, money and insurance

When we mention passenger rights, cancellation conditions, package holidays or travel insurance, we give a general overview. This is not legal advice or a recommendation for a specific product. What applies in your case depends on your contract, your ticket and the current regulations. For binding information, contact the provider, a consumer advice center or a lawyer. When we write about travel budgets, it's about planning and priorities, not specific amounts.

## Locations, accommodations and providers

We describe regions, islands, lakes and cities with what is consistent and commonly known there. We do not evaluate individual hotels, holiday apartments, campsites, restaurants or excursion providers and do not recommend specific businesses. When we compare types of accommodation or means of transport, it's about advantages and disadvantages, not about purchasing recommendations. It is best to ask the host directly for information about accessibility, pets or accommodation facilities.

## External websites

Our guides name places where you can find up-to-date information. If you access their websites or apps, the respective operators are solely responsible for their content. If we link to an external website, no legal violations were apparent at the time of the link. If we become aware of any such links, we will remove the link immediately.
