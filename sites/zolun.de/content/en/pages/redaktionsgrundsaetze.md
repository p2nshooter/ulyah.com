A travel guide is only as good as the trust you can place in it. If you rely on our explanation of how an island ferry works or what a holiday apartment really costs, it should be correct. These principles guide the work of the ZOLUN editorial team in every text.

## Written specifically

Every guide on ZOLUN is written specifically for this website. We do not copy texts from brochures, travel guides or other websites. Where we rely on generally known knowledge, we put it in our own words and organize it for your travel planning. When structuring and formulating, we also use digital writing tools, including AI-supported aids. This doesn't change the rules on this page: They apply to every sentence, regardless of how it was created.

## No made-up numbers, times and companies

We do not mention prices, timetables, ferry times, opening times or tourist tax amounts from memory because they are constantly changing. We do not invent statistics, visitor numbers, surveys, quotes from experts or testimonials. We do not introduce individual hotels, restaurants or providers that we do not know. Instead, we explain how something works and tell you where you can find the most current and authoritative information.

## Where our knowledge comes from

- Information from transport companies, transport associations and shipping companies on how tickets, connections and ferries work
- Information from municipalities, spa administrations and tourism organizations, such as guest cards, beach rules and dog beaches
- Information from authorities and public bodies, including the German Weather Service on weather warnings, the Federal Maritime and Hydrographic Agency on tides and water levels, and the national park administrations on protecting the Wadden Sea and coast
- consistent geographical knowledge of coasts, islands, lakes, river landscapes and cities
- Proven travel knowledge, always with the question in mind: Does this also work with small children, with a dog, without a car, with a small budget or in the off-season?

## What every guide should do

Before a text appears, it must be able to answer these questions:

1. **Who is it for?** Each guide begins with a real travel situation, such as your first trip to the North Sea with children or a city weekend in November.
2. **What can you do specifically?** A good advisor will help you make a decision or take the next planning step instead of just raving about it.
3. **Where are the limits?** He says openly when a travel destination is not suitable, when it gets crowded, when the weather is unpleasant and when a trip is hardly worth it.
4. **What changes?** Where rules, times or prices fluctuate, he points this out and tells you where to check.

## Peace instead of superlatives

We don’t write about the “most beautiful beach in Germany” or the “insider tip that no one knows about”. Each region has strengths and weaknesses, and what is ideal for a family may be completely wrong for a couple seeking silence. We honestly describe the downsides: crowds in the high season, closed restaurants in winter, paths that are difficult with luggage. So you can decide for yourself.

## Safety first

Where water, mud flats, cliffs, tides or weather play a role, we state the risks clearly and without drama. We do not describe any risky shortcuts and refer to guarded swimming areas, official warnings and knowledgeable guides. For health questions we refer you to medical advice, for questions about animals to veterinary advice and for legal questions we refer you to expert advice.

## For everyone who travels

We think of people who travel by train and bus and those who travel by car. For families, solo travelers, older people, travelers with dogs and people for whom steps or long distances are an obstacle. We do not judge anyone for their choice of transport or accommodation. Where there are more environmentally friendly options, we point them out without pointing fingers.

## Independence

ZOLUN is financed through advertisements that Google automatically delivers. We do not choose the ads and they have no influence on our texts. We do not take money for mentions, do not publish paid posts, do not work with commission links and do not allow any location, hotel or transport company to have a say in our content.

## Updates and corrections

Each guide has a date. We revise texts when rules, offers or connections change fundamentally or when readers point out errors to us. We correct any factual errors quickly. Information from people who live or work locally are particularly valuable. Write to us via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.

## language

We address our readers on a first-name basis and write clearly, in a friendly manner and without advertising language. We explain technical terms such as Priel, Bodden, Hallig or spa fee the first time they appear. A guide should be so easy to read that you'll be happy to finish reading it on the sofa in the evening and know what you're planning next the next morning.
