We look forward to hearing from everyone who is on, planning or dreaming of a holiday in Germany. The easiest way to reach us is by email:

**Email:** [kontakt@zolun.de](mailto:kontakt@zolun.de)

We read every message and aim to respond within about a week. During peak travel times it can sometimes take a little longer. There is deliberately no contact form: This way it is clear who wrote what and we don't save anything that we don't need.

## What you can write to us about

- **Outdated information:** Travel information ages quickly. A ferry runs on a new timetable, a train station is being rebuilt, a municipality is changing the rules for dogs on the beach. If you notice something like this in one of our texts, please tell us. Tips from people who live or work locally are particularly valuable. We check every reference and correct the text if it is justified.
- **Topic requests:** Are you missing a guide, for example on a specific island, on barrier-free accommodation or on city trips on a small budget? Write to us what you would like to read more about.
- **Experiences:** Have you traveled to Usedom with a small child and train or have you spent a week on a Hallig with your dog? Tell us what went well and what didn't. We do not copy texts verbatim, but your experience can make a guide better.
- **Legal and data protection:** We also answer inquiries about your data, the content of this website or the imprint via this address.

## Which we can't help with

ZOLUN is an advisor, not a travel agency. We do not accept bookings or reservations, do not sell tickets and cannot provide any binding information about timetables, prices or available rooms. The transport companies, shipping companies, tourist information centers and your hosts are responsible for such questions. We have put together where you can find something in the [Travel Check](/reisecheck/).

We also cannot mediate if you have trouble with accommodation or a transport company. First contact the provider itself. If you have a dispute with a public transport company, there are also arbitration boards that passengers can turn to.

## Collaborations and advertising

We do not publish any paid articles, no guest posts for money and no texts in which a location, a hotel or a company has a say in the content. We therefore generally do not answer inquiries of this type. The advertisements on ZOLUN come from Google AdSense and are separate from the editorial content.

## Your data

If you write to us, we will only use your email address and the content of your message to respond to you. You can find out more about this in our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).
