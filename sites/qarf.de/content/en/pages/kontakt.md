The easiest way to reach the QARF editorial team is by email:

**Email:** [kontakt@qarf.de](mailto:kontakt@qarf.de)

We read every message and usually respond within a few business days. Please understand if it takes a little longer during busy weeks.

## What we can help you with

- **Questions about a guide:** Something is unclear, a guide doesn't work for you as described or you want to know how a value can be transferred to your device. It's best to tell us the title of the guide, your device and what exactly you tried, i.e. quantity, grinding degree, temperature and time.
- **Notes on errors:** If you find a number, caffeine or temperature information or a term wrong, please let us know, ideally with a source. We check every reference and correct the text if it is actually inaccurate. The date under “Status” shows you when a guide was last revised.
- **Topic requests:** Are you missing a preparation method, a type of tea or a background topic? We collect all suggestions and take them into account when planning new guides.
- **Data protection:** We will also answer questions about your data and how to exercise your rights under the GDPR at this address. More about this in the [Data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What we can't do

We are an editorial office and not a doctor's office. If you have any questions about caffeine during pregnancy, interactions with medications or symptoms after drinking coffee, please ask your doctor. Likewise, we cannot provide repair instructions for individual machine models; The manufacturer's customer service is the right address for this, also because of guarantee and security.

We also do not test submitted products and do not accept paid posts, guest articles with advertising links, or link exchange requests. Such messages remain unanswered.
