QARF stands for **Source of aromatic roasts & fine teas**. QARF is a project by **p2nshooter** and a guide for anyone who wants to brew better coffee and tea at home without needing barista training or tea ceremony school.

## Why QARF exists

Coffee and tea are among the most common drinks of all, and that is precisely why people rarely think about why one cup tastes wonderful and the next is bitter or weak. Most of the time it is not due to expensive devices, but rather a few adjustment screws: the ratio of coffee or tea to water, the grind, the temperature, the time and the water itself. We explain exactly these adjustment screws, step by step and with concrete numbers that you can try out directly.

## The name and picture of the caravan

In the first book of Kings (1 Kings 10:10) it is told that the Queen of Sheba came to King Solomon with a large caravan and gave him gold, precious stones and spices in abundance that was never seen again. The country of Saba is located in the south of the Arabian Peninsula, in today's Yemen, exactly where coffee was first cultivated and drunk centuries later. That's why our picture is a spice caravan in camp for the night: copper pots over the embers, open sacks of cardamom and saffron, tea glasses and porcelain bowls. Our six subjects are the six sacks of this caravan.

## What you will find with us

- **Beans & Roasting:** Arabica and Robusta, preparation, roasting levels, roasting date and origin.
- **Prepare coffee:** Hand filter, French press, espresso maker, AeroPress, portafilter, cold brew and mocha.
- **Grinds & Water:** Grinders, grinding degree, care, water hardness, descaling and extraction.
- **Types of tea:** Green tea, black tea, oolong, white tea, matcha, pu-erh and herbal teas.
- **Preparing tea:** Brewing times, accessories, Gongfu Cha, iced tea, chai and East Frisian tea culture.
- **Enjoyment & Knowledge:** Caffeine, storage, decaffeination, sealing, tasting and history.

There is also our [Brewing table](/bruehtabelle/), which collects all the important values ​​on one page.

## How we work

The QARF editorial team writes the texts. We are guided by recommendations from specialist organizations such as the Specialty Coffee Association, information from authorities such as the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) and the Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR), as well as the instructions from the device manufacturers. Where values ​​fluctuate, we call ranges instead of seemingly exact numbers. How we do this is described in detail in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

QARF does not sell coffee, tea or appliances. We mention device types and methods, but we are not paid by manufacturers to recommend specific products. The website is financed by Google AdSense advertisements, which are clearly separated from the editorial content.
