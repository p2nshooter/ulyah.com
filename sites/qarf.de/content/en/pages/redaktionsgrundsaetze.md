Good advice about coffee and tea needs two things: accuracy in the numbers and honesty about where taste begins. These principles guide the work of the QARF editorial team.

## Numbers with origins

When we mention temperatures, brewing ratios, amounts of caffeine or water values, we rely on verifiable sources. These include the recommendations of the Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) on brewing ratio, extraction and water, the assessments of the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) on caffeine, information from the Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) on herbal teas, German regulations such as the Coffee Ordinance and the Detergents and Cleaning Agents Act with its hardness ranges as well as the instructions from the device manufacturers.

## Tension instead of apparent precision

Coffee and tea are natural products. The caffeine content of a cup depends on the type, quantity and preparation, the ideal brewing time depends on the leaf and your own taste. We therefore give ranges, explain what the value depends on and give a sensible starting point. Where the data is sparse, we write that down instead of making up a number.

## Taste is not a mistake

We differentiate between what can be measured and what is a matter of taste. A dark roasted espresso is not “wrong”, nor is a Darjeeling with milk. Our job is to explain how you get the result you like.

## Independence

QARF does not sell products, accept payment for mentions, or publish paid guest posts. We name device types and methods, not bestseller lists. The website is funded by ads from Google AdSense. Advertisements are separate from editorial copy and advertisers have no influence on content.

## Safety first

Where boiling water, pressure, steam or open heat is involved, we point out risks. When it comes to health issues such as caffeine during pregnancy, we refer you to medical advice instead of making a diagnosis.

## language

We write to you directly, explain technical terms the first time they appear and avoid using marketing language. A guide should be so specific that you can have it next to the pot and follow it step by step.

## Corrections and updates

We revise guides when recommendations change or errors are reported to us. The date under “As of” shows the last revision. We would be grateful to receive information about errors at [kontakt@qarf.de](mailto:kontakt@qarf.de).
