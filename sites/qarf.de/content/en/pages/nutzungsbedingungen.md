## General information instead of individual advice

Our guides describe proven methods and guidelines for preparing coffee and tea. Every mill, every machine, every tap water and every taste is different. The quantities, temperatures and times mentioned are therefore starting points that you adapt to your situation. The manufacturer's instructions apply to the correct operation of your devices. If you have health questions, such as the amount of caffeine during pregnancy or if you have previous illnesses, you can clarify them with a medical professional.

## Safety when imitating

Many instructions use boiling water, hot steam, pressure or open heat. You act on your own responsibility when imitating. Keep children away from hot appliances, never leave espresso makers and roasting pans unattended and only use intact appliances with a working safety valve.
