QARF wants to help you make good coffee and tea at home. So that the limitations of our guides are clear, we summarize them here.

## General information

The contents of QARF are carefully researched and regularly revised. Nevertheless, we cannot guarantee that all information is complete, correct and up-to-date at all times. Brewing values ​​such as quantities, grinding degrees, temperatures and times are guidelines. They depend on the bean, roast, mill, device and water and must be adjusted by you.

## Caffeine and health

Our information on caffeine, tannins or ingredients in herbal teas is based on published assessments by authorities such as the EFSA and the BfR. It is general information and not medical advice. How much caffeine you can tolerate depends, among other things, on your body weight, metabolism, medications, pregnancy and previous illnesses. If you have palpitations, sleep disorders, stomach problems or uncertainty, contact your doctor.

## Hot devices, pressure and fire

Boiling water, steam wands, espresso makers and portafilter machines can cause scalding. Roasting coffee creates smoke, heat and flammable silver membranes. Always follow the manufacturer's operating instructions and safety instructions; they take precedence over our general descriptions. Never open pressure equipment while it is hot and under pressure, and do not leave heat sources unattended.

## Descaling and cleaning

When we mention descaling or cleaning products, we describe common procedures. Some devices do not tolerate certain agents, and incorrect agents can damage seals or jeopardize the warranty. If in doubt, use the product recommended by the manufacturer.

## Products and prices

When we mention device types, varieties or seals, this is not a recommendation to buy a specific product. Prices, certification rules and ranges change; The current information from providers and organizations is decisive.
