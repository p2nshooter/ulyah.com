## For the cup afterwards: AXTO

If you like to look at beautiful things over a cup of coffee, at [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=qarf.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) you will find a digital museum full of moving works: clocks that show the real time, stamps from imaginary countries, calligraphy from the Koran and the Hadith with text copied verbatim, and animated figures. Each work is created uniquely from its own DNA and is never reprinted.

Exclusive editions with **AC-** cost $1 and are paid for with AXTO coins - a small gift for regular customers that can be distributed to many wallets without a gas fee. At OpenSea there are the collections with **OS-**, 1 to 10 editions per work, fixed price 1 to 250 dollars; a **G** stands for GPU painting. The community coin AXTO starts at $1; Fixed coins are rewarded with unique originals, but their price is not guaranteed.

## Your online roastery

Small roasters and teahouses thrive on regular customers - and more and more of them are ordering online. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=qarf.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European studio that develops custom websites and shops. Typical projects for our industry would be:

| Projects | What XAA is building |
|---|---|
| Become visible | a website with history, range and café opening times |
| Sell online | a shop for beans, teas and accessories, also with subscriptions |
| Grow | a wholesale portal for restaurants and offices |

The work is planned and paid for in clear milestones. For a quick start, it's worth taking a look at [XAA's templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=qarf.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). The studio's website is in English, Spanish and Indonesian; Inquiries in German will also be answered.
