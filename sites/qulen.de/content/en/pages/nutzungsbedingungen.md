By using qulen.de you accept the following conditions. We deliberately kept them short and understandable so that students can read them without a dictionary.

## Free offer

All stories and practical parts on QULEN are free and accessible without registration. The website is financed through advertisements that Google automatically delivers and that are recognizable as advertisements. Advertising has no influence on what we write about or what learning methods we present. We do not sell courses, learning apps or tutoring and do not receive any commission for things that appear in our texts.

## Made up stories, real methods

Each article consists of a fictional encouraging story and a practical part. The characters, their schools, companies and universities are made up. Not every detail of what a character does is intended to be imitated: sometimes someone stays up half the night or learns the wrong way at first, and that's exactly where the story comes from. What we actually recommend is in the practical section “What you can take from it”.

The practical parts are general information for learning. They may fit your situation, but they don't have to. You decide for yourself what you try. They do not replace advice from teachers, school psychologists, doctors or advice centers who know you personally.

## No guarantee of grades

Good learning methods make many things easier, but no one can promise you a certain grade, passing an exam or graduating, not even us. Examination rules, deadlines and requirements are determined by the federal states, the chambers and the universities, and they can change. What your school, your training company, your chamber or your examination regulations say is always binding.

## Use in school, training and studies

You may print individual posts for your own use, such as a weekly schedule for your desk, and share the link with others. Teachers, trainers and consultants are allowed to read individual stories in their learning group, pass on the link or print out a post for their group as long as QULEN is cited as the source and no money is charged. You need our written consent for school books, workbooks, paid courses or other publications.

## Citation in presentations and specialist papers

If you use an idea from QULEN in a paper, presentation or term paper, please cite us as the source, as you would with any other source. Do not copy any texts as your own: this would be plagiarism and can have serious consequences at school and at university. QULEN is not a suitable specialist source for a specialist paper or a scientific paper. Ask your teacher or supervisor what literature is expected there.

You are welcome to link to our pages, for example from your school's learning portal, a class chat or a link list. Please do not embed our pages in third-party offerings in such a way that it gives the impression that the content comes from someone else.

We will endeavor to provide the website without interruption, but we cannot guarantee this. Maintenance work, technical malfunctions or events beyond our control can lead to short outages. It is therefore better to save important plans or checklists as a printout or note before an exam.
