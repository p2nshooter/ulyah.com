At QULEN, you read stories and learning tips without logging in, and we want to know as little about you as possible. In accordance with Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), this declaration informs you about which personal data is processed when you visit qulen.de, for what purpose and on what legal basis, and what rights you have. We have written them in such a way that you can understand them even without any previous legal knowledge.

## Responsible person

The provider named in [Imprint](/impressum/), p2nshooter, is responsible for data processing on this website. You can reach us at [kontakt@qulen.de](mailto:kontakt@qulen.de).

## What we don't collect

QULEN has no registration, no user account, no comment function, no newsletter and no contact form. You cannot upload homework, report cards or files. We don't ask for your name, age or school. All stories and practical parts can be read without you telling us anything about yourself.

## Hosting and server protocols

The website is delivered via the Cloudflare, Inc. network. When you access a page, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data: your IP address, date and time of access, the address accessed, the browser type, the operating system and the amount of data transferred. This data is needed to be able to display the page to you and to protect the website from attacks and misuse. They will only be kept for as long as necessary for these purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operations (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). Cloudflare can also process data in the USA. The transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on EU standard contractual clauses.

## Counting page views without cookies

So that we know which stories are being read and which topics are in demand, we count page views using our own, slim count. Only an identifier for this website and the path accessed are transmitted. Only the total number of views per day and page is saved. No cookies are set, no IP addresses are saved and no profiles are created. The browser identifier is only evaluated briefly in order to count visits from search engine robots separately. The legal basis is Article 6 Paragraph 1 Letter f GDPR.

## Fonts from Google Fonts

For the fonts on this website, your browser loads font files from servers at Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, when you access the page. Your IP address is transmitted to Google and Google learns that qulen.de was accessed. We use the fonts for a uniform and easy-to-read typeface (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). If your browser blocks external fonts, a default font on your device will be used and all text will remain readable.

## Advertising with Google AdSense

QULEN is free and is financed by advertisements from Google AdSense, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited. Google and its partners, identified as third parties in the consent notice, may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to serve ads, limit their frequency, prevent fraud and measure the effectiveness of ads. If you consent, ads may also be tailored to interests derived from your visits to this and other websites.

Such information will only be stored and read on your device with your consent (Section 25 Paragraph 1 TDDDG, Art. 6 Paragraph 1 Letter a GDPR). We obtain consent via a consent message that appears on your first visit. There you can see the third parties involved and can agree or reject individual purposes. Non-personalized ads may continue to appear without consent. Data can also be transferred to the USA. Google is certified according to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. How Google handles data from websites that use its services is described in its data protection information at policies.google.com.

## If you are younger than 16 years old

QULEN is also aimed at school students. In Germany, according to Art. 8 GDPR, young people can only effectively consent to the processing of their data through online services from the age of 16. For younger children, parents must agree. If you are under 16, please select “Decline” in the consent message or ask your parents first. You can read all stories and practical parts in full even without consent.

## Revoke consent

You can revoke or change your consent at any time with future effect via the link to the data protection settings in the consent message or by deleting the cookies from this website in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. Details can be found in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-richtlinie/).

## Contact via email

If you write us an email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to respond to you (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b or lit. f GDPR). We will not pass on this data and will delete it as soon as your request has been completed and there are no legal retention obligations to the contrary. Please do not send us any certificates, class assignments or health information. We don't need them for your request.

## Storage in the browser

Your browser will remember in the session memory whether the short celebration animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown. This information does not leave your device and is deleted when you close the browser. Furthermore, QULEN itself does not store anything on your device.

## Your rights

According to the GDPR, you have the right to information about the data we process about you (Article 15), to rectification (Article 16), to deletion (Article 17), to restriction of processing (Article 18), to data portability (Article 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21). You can revoke your consent at any time (Art. 7 Para. 3). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77), for example the authority of the federal state in which you live. For inquiries, send an email to [kontakt@qulen.de](mailto:kontakt@qulen.de). You have these rights regardless of your age.

## Changes

We will adapt this statement if the website, the services used or the legal situation change. The version published here applies.
