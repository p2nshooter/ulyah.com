QULEN is about courage in learning: stories of people who didn't give up and practical paths through school, training and study. Artificial intelligence is now part of this path for many people. That's why we're introducing you to two projects that come from the same operator as QULEN. This is self-promotion – and we think you should know that before reading any further.

## First the rules, then the tools

Before we talk about software, the most important thing: an AI is allowed to help you understand, but it is not allowed to think for you. Exams, homework and applications must be your own work. If in doubt, ask your teacher or university which aids are permitted. Anyone who lets AI secretly write texts learns nothing and risks a lot.

## AXTO: a museum to marvel at

Learning also needs breaks where you can simply be amazed. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=qulen.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) shows works of art that are alive: a clock that shows the time in your city, stamps from empires that don't exist, verses from the Koran and Hadith in calligraphy - reproduced unadulterated - and moving figures. Mathematics plays a role: every work follows a unique DNA and only exists once.

Exclusive works with **AC-** cost $1 per edition in AXTO coins; Learning groups can share a motif ten times and give it away without a gas fee. Public works with **OS-** are available on OpenSea, with 1 to 10 editions at fixed prices between 1 and 250 dollars, GPU paintings with **G**. Learning groups can join AXTO, the coin of the axto.io community, with just one dollar; Anyone who locks them up for a while collects individual items, but the value remains a risk.

## XAA: a platform for everyone to make learning easier for others

Do you give tutoring, lead a learning group, organize language courses or are you planning a small online academy? [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=qulen.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European development studio and builds websites and platforms for:

- a page where parents and learners can find your offer and prices,
- Booking appointments and payment,
- a learning platform with courses, materials and a member area as a SaaS solution.

XAA works in milestones: each stage is approved before the next one begins, and payment is made via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. To get started quickly, there are [Templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=qulen.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). The studio website is in English, Spanish and Indonesian, inquiries in German are welcome.

## What remains

The encouraging stories and learning tips on QULEN are created independently of these two projects. You don't need AXTO or XAA to learn well - curiosity, a plan and a little patience are enough. If a good tool helps you, even better.
