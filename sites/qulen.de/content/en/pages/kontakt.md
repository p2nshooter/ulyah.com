Would you like to tell us something? We welcome mail from pupils, trainees, students, parents, teachers and everyone else who reads our stories. The easiest way to reach the editorial team is by email:

**Email:** [kontakt@qulen.de](mailto:kontakt@qulen.de)

We read every message. However, a response may take a few days, or a little longer during holiday periods. We ask you for patience.

## What you can write to us about

- **Topic requests:** Which learning situation should we write a story about next? Maybe about the oral exam in your Abitur, the intermediate exam in your training or the first exam phase in your studies? Write to us about what is currently bothering you.
- **Errors and ambiguities:** If you find something wrong, outdated or unclear in a practical section, please let us know. We check every reference and correct the text if it is justified. This particularly applies to information about exams, advice centers and telephone numbers.
- **Experiences with a method:** Have you tried spaced repetition, really stuck to a learning plan or completed your first presentation without shaking? Tell us what helped and what didn't. Your feedback can make a practical part more understandable. However, we never take letters verbatim and do not turn your experience into a story about you.
- **Feedback from schools, companies and universities:** Teachers, trainers and advisory specialists are welcome to tell us what questions they encounter in everyday life and what learners should read more about.
- **Data protection and legal matters:** We respond to requests for information according to the GDPR and information about the content of this website via the same address.

## Please do not send us any personal information

Especially if you're still going to school: You don't have to tell us what your name is, what school you go to or where you live. Please do not send us any certificates, class assignments, photos or information about your health. A few sentences are enough to request a topic or report an error. The less we know about you, the better it is for your privacy.

## What we can't do

- We do not do any homework, write any presentations, technical papers or term papers and do not correct any texts. That wouldn't be in your interest either: what you work out yourself sticks much better.
- We do not provide any tutoring or personal learning advice via email.
- We cannot answer any questions about your examination regulations, your training contract or your grades. Your school, your chamber or the examination office at your university are responsible for this.
- We are not a psychological counseling center and cannot provide any assessment of anxiety, concentration problems or learning disorders.

## If you're not feeling well right now

Emailing us is not the way to go if you need urgent help. If you are overwhelmed by fear, pressure or worries, please talk to someone you trust, for example a teacher or school social work. Children and young people can reach the **number against grief** on **116 111**, free of charge and anonymously. In acute danger, dial **112**. You can find further contact points on the [Help & Advice](/hilfe-und-beratung/) page.

## Advertising and collaborations

We do not publish paid contributions or advertising texts for learning apps, tutoring institutes, learning materials or courses. We do not answer inquiries about this. The ads on this website are automatically served by Google; we do not choose them.

## Data protection

We only use your message to reply to it and delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose. More information can be found in the [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/). You can find out who is behind QULEN in the [Imprint](/impressum/) and on the page [About us](/ueber-uns/).
