QULEN stands for **Source of **Le**r**n**ens. We write for everyone who needs, wants or should learn: for the eighth-grader who lies awake at night before doing her math work, for the trainee in his first year of training who is suddenly surrounded by technical terms and formulas at vocational school, for the student who has been putting off his first homework for three weeks. And for parents, teachers, trainers who accompany such moments and are looking for the right words.

## Why stories?

Tips for learning are everywhere. Write a study plan, put your cell phone away, start early: you already know most of this. Nevertheless, it often doesn't work, and then every additional tip feels like a small reproach. Stories work differently. If you read about how a fictional student wants to run away from her presentation and manages to do it in the end, you might recognize yourself in it. You see your own situation from a little distance, and from this distance the first small step becomes easier.

That's why every post on QULEN consists of two parts:

1. **An invented encouraging story** from school, training or study in Germany, told with warmth, with setbacks and without a miracle solution.
2. **The practical part “What you can take from it”** with learning methods, plans, checklists and small exercises that you can try out on the same day.

## Our stories are fictional

Because this is important to us, we say it very clearly: **All stories on QULEN are fictional.** The characters, their names, their schools, training companies and universities do not exist. Any similarities to real people or institutions would be coincidental. We create these stories to illustrate typical situations without writing about real teenagers or young adults and violating their privacy.

The people are invented, not the hurdles. Many learners are familiar with the fear of the exam, the gap from seventh grade that extends into the Abitur, or the palpitations before the first lecture. Each story is marked as a “fictional story” at the top of the post. The practical part, on the other hand, is not based on imagination, but on generally recognized learning strategies. Our [editorial principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/) describe how we do this.

## Six chapters like in one booklet

Our stories are sorted into six chapters, like the tab pages in a ring binder:

- [Learning plan & homework](/themen/lernplan-und-hausaufgaben/): find a weekly routine, overcome procrastination and complete homework without constant struggle
- [Exams & exam anxiety](/themen/pruefungen-und-pruefungsangst/): Prepare well for class tests, exams, high school diplomas and final exams and deal with anxiety and blackouts
- [Concentration & Motivation](/themen/konzentration-und-motivation/): Distraction through the cell phone, the right place to learn, perseverance after setbacks
- [Vocabulary & Learning Methods](/themen/vokabeln-und-lernmethoden/): Flashcards, spaced repetition, queries instead of re-reading, summaries and mind maps
- [Math & Learning Gaps](/themen/mathe-und-lernluecken/): Find gaps, catch up on the basics and understand word problems
- [Speaking & Presenting](/themen/referate-und-praesentieren/): from a blank page to a confident presentation and oral participation

## The name and the picture

In an old story from the first book of Kings (1 Kings 3:9), the young King Solomon desires neither wealth nor a long life, but rather a listening heart in order to understand. This picture is labeled QULEN. Good learning begins with listening: What is blocking you right now? What are you still missing? What is good for you?

That's why our website looks like a reading room at night. There is a rose window made of eight hearts, pages as if they were made of parchment with a red binding edge and small gold stars, like those that used to be stuck under a successful piece of work. The chapters are the tabs of an issue. You won't find photos of people here, just hearts, stars, books, lines and paper.

## What we are not

QULEN is not a tutoring service, a school or an advice center. We do not correct homework, write reports or make diagnoses. If learning is very difficult for a long time, if fear or pressure determines your everyday life, or if something is wrong in class or at home, you need people who know you personally. We have put together who is there for this on the [Help & Advice](/hilfe-und-beratung/) page.

## Who is behind QULEN?

QULEN is a project by **p2nshooter**, which publishes other independent advice and knowledge sites. The QULEN editorial team writes the stories and practical parts. All articles are free and can be read without registration. The website is financed by Google advertisements, which are recognizable as advertisements and have no influence on our topics. We do not sell courses, learning apps or tutoring sessions. The legal information can be found in [Imprint](/impressum/).
