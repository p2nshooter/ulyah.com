This policy explains step by step which cookies and comparable technologies are used when you visit qulen.de. It complements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/), in which you can also read about your rights.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device via your browser. The next time you access it, the website or an integrated service can read it again. Other technologies work similarly: the browser's local and session memory, device identifiers, or tiny graphics that report a call, often called pixels. Some of these technologies are necessary for the operation of a website, others are used for advertising or measurement.

## What QULEN stores itself

QULEN does not set its own cookies to recognize you. Our page view count works without cookies and without profiles.

We only store a single entry in your browser's session memory: a marker indicating whether the short celebration animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown. This means it doesn't appear anew every time you change pages. This entry contains no information about you, does not leave your device and disappears as soon as you close the browser. It is technically necessary for the desired display of the page (§ 25 Para. 2 TDDDG).

## Google AdSense and third party cookies

We use Google AdSense for the advertisements on this website. Google and Google's advertising partners listed as third parties in the consent message may use cookies and similar technologies for these purposes:

- **Deliver ads:** so that an ad appears in the appropriate place
- **Limit frequency:** so you don't see the same ad over and over again
- **Detect fraud:** such as automated clicks or fake views
- **Measure impact:** how often an ad was shown or clicked on
- **Personalize ads:** Ads matching interests derived from your surfing behavior, only with your consent

Before information is stored or read on your device that is not absolutely necessary for displaying the website, we ask for your consent. Which cookies are set in detail depends on your selection, your browser and the respective advertisements and can change. Depending on the cookie, the storage period ranges from the duration of a visit to several months. Google describes its advertising cookies in its privacy policy at policies.google.com.

## Google Fonts

The fonts on this website are loaded from Google servers. To the best of our knowledge, no cookies are set. However, your IP address will be transmitted to Google, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/).

## The consent message

A consent message will appear on your first visit. There you can agree to all purposes, reject all or select individual purposes and third parties from the options. No cookies will be stored for personalized advertising without your consent. Non-personalized ads may then continue to appear.

If you are under 16, please select “Decline” or speak to your parents first. This makes no difference when it comes to reading: all stories and practical parts remain completely readable.

## Change or revoke consent

You can change your decision at any time. To do this, use the link to the privacy settings provided in the consent message or delete the cookies from this website in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. A revocation applies to the future.

## Manage cookies in the browser

In your browser settings you can view and delete stored cookies, generally block third-party cookies or set cookies to be deleted when you close the browser. How exactly to do this can be found in the help of your browser. If you use a shared computer, such as at school, library or work, a private window is a good choice: then cookies and history are automatically removed when you close it.
