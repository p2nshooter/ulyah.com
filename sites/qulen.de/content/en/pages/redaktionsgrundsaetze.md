Anyone facing an exam or a mountain of homework needs courage and useful help, not additional pressure. So that you can trust our contributions, we adhere to these principles.

## Two parts, two standards

Every post on QULEN has two parts, and different rules apply to both. The **story** is invented and can be told, pointed out and touched. The **practical part** “What you can take from it” is factual text and must be correct. We don't mix the two: what is given as a tip in the practical section is never just based on the fact that it worked in history.

## How our stories are created

- **Everything is fictional.** Our characters, their names, families, teachers, schools, training companies and universities do not exist. We don't write about real people or real institutions.
- **Each story is labeled.** At the top of the post it says “Fictional Story,” and a note explains that people and places are fictional.
- **Realistic instead of brilliant.** Our characters sometimes fail, procrastinate, despair of a task and need several attempts. Nobody becomes a top candidate overnight. It shows how a small, feasible step makes the difference.
- **Many paths through learning.** Our figures are intended to show the diversity of educational paths in Germany: students at different types of schools, trainees between the company and vocational school, students in the first semester and shortly before graduation. For us, no educational path is better than another.
- **Comments remain private.** When readers tell us about their experiences, we never use names, places or recognizable details.

## Where the knowledge in the practical part comes from

Our practical parts are based on learning strategies that have long been recognized in learning research and pedagogical practice. This includes repeated repetition over several days instead of cramming on the last evening, active retrieval of knowledge, i.e. questioning yourself instead of just re-reading, explaining in your own words, practicing with examples and learning times with real breaks. There is also general information from schools, chambers, universities and advice centers as well as practical experience from everyday school and training life.

## No made-up numbers, studies or experts

There are many catchy claims about learning, for example exact percentages of how much you supposedly retain from reading, listening or doing things yourself. Many such numbers cannot be substantiated. We therefore do not provide any statistics, studies, percentages or quotes from experts that we cannot verify, and we do not invent experts. Where there is no reliable statement, we say so openly.

## Clear boundaries

We do not make any diagnoses and do not give any medical, psychotherapeutic or legal advice in individual cases. Contributions about test anxiety, exhaustion or concentration describe usual excitement and usual slack and clearly state when teachers, school psychologists, doctors or advice centers are needed. For this we refer you to our page [Help & Advice](/hilfe-und-beratung/). When it comes to examination rules, deadlines and compensation for disadvantages, we would like to point out that federal states, chambers and universities have different rules and the responsible body provides binding information.

## Written for young readers

Many of our readers still go to school. We therefore write in clear, friendly language and use first names. We explain technical terms. We don't shame anyone for bad grades, mistakes, or setbacks, we don't portray any character as stupid or lazy, and we don't glorify nights without sleep or studying to the point of exhaustion.

## Independence

QULEN is financed through advertisements that Google automatically delivers. We do not choose the ads and they have no influence on our texts. We do not take any money for contributions or mentions and do not name any learning apps, tutoring institutes, publishers or brands as purchase recommendations.

## Updates and corrections

Each post has a date. We revise texts if recommendations, rules or contact points change or if someone points out an error. We correct any factual errors quickly and adjust the date. You can send us information via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.
