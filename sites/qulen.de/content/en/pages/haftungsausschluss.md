QULEN tells fictional encouraging stories about learning and explains learning methods as thoroughly and understandably as possible. Please read this note before implementing suggestions from our practical sections.

## The stories are fiction

All stories on qulen.de are fictional. People, names, schools, training companies, universities and places are made up; any similarities to real people or institutions would be coincidental. The stories show what typical hurdles can feel like and how someone finds a way through them. They are not reports of experience and are not proof that a method works equally well for all people.

## Learning tips, no guarantee

Our practical parts are based on generally recognized learning strategies, for example distributed repetition, active retrieval of knowledge, fixed learning times with breaks and thorough preparation for lectures. These methods help many learners. How well they work for you depends on a lot: your subject, your previous knowledge, your life situation and how much time and energy you have at the moment. We cannot therefore promise any improvement in your grades or passing an exam.

## No diagnosis, no therapy

When we write about test anxiety, concentration or motivation, we describe everyday strategies for common excitement and common slacks. We do not diagnose or provide medical or psychotherapeutic advice. Only a specialist can clarify whether persistent difficulties are caused by, for example, an anxiety disorder, reading and spelling difficulties, a math disorder or ADHD.

## When you should ask professionals

Please get support if any of the following apply to you:

- The fear of exams is so strong that you can no longer sleep, have repeated physical complaints or avoid school, vocational school or university.
- You feel depressed, exhausted or hopeless for weeks, or you have thoughts of not wanting to live anymore.
- You practice a lot and regularly, but you still don't get better in an area like reading, writing or arithmetic over a long period of time.
- You are bullied, threatened or experience violence, at school, at work, online or at home.

Anyone who can help in these cases can be found on our [Help & Advice](/hilfe-und-beratung/) page. If you are in acute danger, please dial 112. Children and young people can call the number against grief free of charge and anonymously on 116 111.

## Examination rules and deadlines

When we write about class tests, high school diplomas, final exams during training or exams during studies, we give a general overview. The federal states, the chambers and the universities determine the binding rules, for example regarding registration, withdrawal, sick leave, repetition or compensation for disadvantages. They differ and are subject to change. Please always check anything important with your school, your chamber, your examination office or in your examination regulations.

## Tools, apps and materials

When we describe tools such as flashcards, weekly plans, timers or learning apps, we do so in general terms and without brand endorsement. Apps and their conditions change frequently. Before using it, check what data an app collects and ask your parents if you are a minor. Many methods work with paper and pen anyway.
