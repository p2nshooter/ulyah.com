From the rough stone to the value that lasts: this is how ROUGH summarizes his way of explaining gems, metals and the craft of jewelry. On this page we leave the workshop for a moment to present two digital projects that belong to the same editor as ROUGH. Out of respect for our readers, we indicate it clearly: this is our own advertising.

## Unique pieces, also in digital: AXTO

Anyone who works with gems knows what a unique piece is worth. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=rough.es&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) applies that idea to digital art: clocks that show real time, seals from fictional kingdoms, calligraphy from the Quran and hadith faithful to the original text, and animated figures, each generated from a unique mathematical DNA and registered on the Base network.

Exclusive editions carry the code **AC-**, cost $1 per edition in AXTO coins and can be given as a gift without gas; There are ten per work. The public ones, with **OS-**, are sold on OpenSea with 1 to 10 editions and fixed prices from 1 to 250 dollars depending on their rarity; a **G** identifies those painted by GPU. For jewelers: AXTO is a participation in the axto.io community from one dollar; Whoever blocks it receives original parts, although their quote is not guaranteed.

## Your digital showcase: XAA

A jewelry workshop needs to show pieces that are appreciated in the details, and selling high-value objects requires trust. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=rough.es&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European web development studio that custom builds:

1. **corporate sites** with high-quality galleries and the history of the workshop;
2. **e-commerce** for jewelry and gems, with detailed records and secure payments;
3. **platforms and marketplaces** that bring together several workshops or suppliers, and private portals for professional clients.

Each project progresses through milestones that are approved and paid separately, through PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. For an agile start there are [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=rough.es&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) that adapt to the identity of each brand. The XAA site can be consulted in Spanish, English and Indonesian.
