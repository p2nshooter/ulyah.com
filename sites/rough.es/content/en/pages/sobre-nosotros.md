ROUGH is an independent digital magazine dedicated to two worlds that share the same idea: the value of what is well done. On the one hand, **rough gems and precious stones**: how they are formed, how they are recognized, how they are cut and how they are purchased without falling into the most expensive mistakes. On the other hand, **masculine style with character**: clothes that last, leather, boots, watches, rings and sensible personal care.

The name says almost everything. *Rough* is the word the gem trade uses for uncut stone, which has not yet revealed its color or brilliance. It also describes a style: sober, resistant, without artifice. Our motto, “from rough stone to lasting value,” summarizes the journey we try to explain in each article.

## Who makes ROUGH

ROUGH is an editorial project of **p2nshooter**, independent publisher of several thematic magazines. The texts are written and reviewed by the ROUGH editorial team, which signs collectively. We do not invent authors, academic titles or personal experiences to give authority to a text: we prefer that the content itself, with its explanations and sources, be the one that wins it.

## What you will find here

- Guides on **minerals and deposits in Spain**, from Navajún pyrite and Molina aragonite to Asturian fluorite, jet or Gavà variscite, and on how to collect minerals responsibly.
- Explanations about **ruby, sapphire, spinel, garnets, tourmaline and alexandrite**: quality, common treatments, synthetics, imitations and the instruments with which they are identified, with their limits: what can be observed with a magnifying glass and what only a laboratory can confirm.
- **Carving and jewelry**: cabochon and faceting, lost wax casting, filigree, damascening, enamels and contrasting precious metals.
- **Purchase and valuation**: how to buy in store and online, appraise, sell gold, bid at auctions, recognize vintage jewelry and complain if something goes wrong.
- **Style and Personal Care**: Durable wardrobe, boots and leather, watches, men's jewelry, shaving, beard, skin, hair, hands and fragrance.

## What you will never find

We do not publish invented market prices, nor expected returns, nor reviews of products that we have not seen before. We do not sell gems or jewelry, and we do not charge merchants to appear in our articles. The magazine is financed by Google AdSense advertising, which is displayed separately from the editorial content.

If you want to propose a topic or point out an error, write to us from the [contact] page (/contacto/). You can read how we work in our [editorial policy](/politica-editorial/).
