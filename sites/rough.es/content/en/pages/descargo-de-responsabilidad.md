ROUGH publishes general information about gems, precious stones, jewelry, crafts, style and personal care. We strive to keep it rigorous and up-to-date, but no article can take into account the circumstances of each reader or each stone.

## Gems and jewelry

- **Identification.** Many natural gems have imitations and synthetic equivalents that are almost identical to the naked eye. Reliable identification requires instruments and often a laboratory. The clues we describe are for asking better questions, not for reaching a definitive conclusion.
- **Treatments.** Heating, filling cracks with oils or resins and other treatments are common and affect the value. Only a laboratory analysis can detect them for sure.
- **Value and prices.** ROUGH does not publish market prices or value estimates for specific stones. The price of a gem depends on many factors that only a professional can assess with the stone in front of them.

## Investment

Gems are not a regulated financial product: they do not have a public reference price, their resale can be slow and expensive, and offers abound with unrealistic promises of profitability. Nothing published on ROUGH is an investment recommendation.

## Personal care

Articles on skin, beard, shaving or fragrance are indicative. If you have a skin condition, allergies, or a reaction to a product, consult a dermatologist or your doctor.
