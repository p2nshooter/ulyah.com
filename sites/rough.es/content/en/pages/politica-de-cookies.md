A cookie is a small file that a website saves on your browser to remember information about your visit. This page explains what cookies may intervene when you read ROUGH.

## Own cookies

ROUGH does not install its own cookies to analyze your behavior or create profiles. Visit statistics are calculated without cookies. The site saves a single session timestamp in your browser (in session storage, not in a cookie) so as not to repeat the welcome animation on each page; It disappears when you close the tab.

## Third party cookies: advertising

The cookies that may appear come from **Google AdSense** and its advertising partners. They are used for:

- display ads and limit the times you see the same ad;
- measure whether an ad has been viewed and combat fraud;
- If you authorize it, show ads related to your interests.

If you visit us from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, these cookies are only installed after you give your consent in the notice that appears on your first visit. You can change your decision at any time from that same notice or by deleting your browser cookies.

More information on Google's page about [how it uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## How to manage cookies in your browser

All browsers allow you to view, block and delete cookies:

- **Chrome:** Settings › Privacy and security › Cookies.
- **Firefox:** Settings › Privacy and security.
- **Safari:** Settings › Privacy.
- **Edge:** Settings › Cookies and site permissions.

If you block all cookies, ROUGH will continue to function normally; only the advertising you see will change.

## Changes

If the cookies involved in the site change, we will update this page and the revision date.
