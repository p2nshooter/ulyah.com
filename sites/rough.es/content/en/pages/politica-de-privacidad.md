At ROUGH we respect your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed when you visit rough.es, why and how you can exercise your rights in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (RGPD) and Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and guarantee of digital rights.

## Responsible for the treatment

The person responsible is p2nshooter, editor of ROUGH. You can write to [contacto@rough.es](mailto:contacto@rough.es) for any questions about your data.

## What data we process

- **Messages you send us.** If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of the message only to respond to you. We delete them when the conversation has ended, unless it is necessary to retain them for a legal obligation.
- **Visit statistics.** We record in aggregate which pages are read (the page address and the site name), without our own cookies, without profiles and without saving your full IP address. It is used to know which articles are useful.
- **Technical server data.** Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes technical data, such as the IP address, to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks.

## Google AdSense Advertising

ROUGH displays Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or identifiers to display ads based on your visits to this and other sites and to measure their performance. If you visit the site from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before advertising cookies are used.

You can turn off personalized advertising in [Google ads settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and see [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Legal basis and conservation

We process messages based on your consent when writing to us, and advertising cookies based on the consent you grant. Technical data is processed out of legitimate interest in the security of the site.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification, deletion, opposition, limitation of processing and portability by writing to our email. If you consider that we have not responded well to your request, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).

To know the specific cookies, consult the [cookie policy](/politica-de-cookies/).
