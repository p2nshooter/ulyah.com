The ROUGH editorial team reads all the messages it receives. The most direct way to write to us is by email:

**[contacto@rough.es](mailto:contacto@rough.es)**

## Why can you write to us?

- **Corrections.** If you find an incorrect information, a confusing explanation or a broken link, please tell us the address of the article and the paragraph. We review each notice and, if you are correct, we correct the text and record the review date.
- **Topic proposals.** Is there a gem, a carving technique or a style question that you would like to see explained? Tell us about it. Reader questions are our best source of ideas.
- **Questions about the magazine.** Advertising, permissions to quote texts or any other question about the operation of ROUGH.

## What we can't do

We do not appraise stones or jewelry by mail, nor do we identify gems from photographs. A photograph does not allow measuring the refractive index, density or inclusions that distinguish a natural stone from a synthetic or treated one. For that you need a qualified appraiser or a gemological laboratory; In our articles we explain how to choose them.

We also do not offer investment advice nor do we recommend buying or selling gems as a financial asset.

## Response time

We try to respond within five business days. Messages with corrections have priority. We do not respond to unsolicited advertising messages or sponsored link offers.

Your data is processed only to answer you, as explained in our [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/).
