This page explains how ROUGH is made, so you know what to expect from each item.

## How we choose topics

We start from the real questions of those who are beginning to be interested in gems or want to dress better with less: how to distinguish a natural stone from an imitation, what a laboratory report means, why a size changes color, which boots last ten years. We avoid publishing two articles that answer the same question.

## How we write

- Each article is written from scratch, in Spanish, by the ROUGH editorial team.
- We explain the reason for each recommendation and point out its limits.
- We clearly distinguish between established facts, stylistic opinions and estimates.
- We do not invent personal experiences, interviews, product tests or credentials.

## How we verify

Gemological data (hardness, refractive index, composition, treatments) are contrasted with reference sources: recognized gemological laboratories and schools, scientific publications and official organizations. When data is doubtful or varies depending on the source, we say so.

## What we don't do

- We do not publish market prices or promises of profitability.
- We do not accept payments from merchants for being featured or recommended.
- We do not use affiliate links.
- We do not present advertising as if it were editorial content.

## Corrections

If an article contains an error, we correct it and update its revision date. You can notify us at [contacto@rough.es](mailto:contacto@rough.es).
