Many men have a full closet and still feel like they have nothing to wear. It usually happens for the same reason: clothes bought on impulse, on sale or following fashions that last a season, that do not combine with each other or feel quite right. The alternative is not to spend more, but to buy with a plan: a small number of good quality garments, in colors that combine with each other and with a flattering cut. With that you dress well for work, for the weekend and for almost any occasion.

In this guide you will see the principles of a durable men's wardrobe, the basic garments and what each one is for, how each piece should fit, what fabrics to choose according to the climate of each season in Spain, how to recognize quality, in what order to buy and how to care for the clothes so that they last for years.

## The principles

1. **Less and better:** a few pieces of clothing that are worn a lot, instead of many that are worn little.
2. **The fit above the mark:** A simple garment that fits well looks better than a face that feels bad.
3. **Colors that combine:** a base of neutral tones allows you to multiply the combinations.
4. **Quality Fibers and Construction:** Last longer and age better.
5. **Consistency with your life:** The wardrobe of someone who works in a formal office is not that of someone who works outdoors.

## The color palette

| Color type | Examples | What is it for? |
|---|---|---|

With navy blue, gray, white, beige and brown you can build practically the entire wardrobe, and add color with knitwear, ties or scarves.

## How each garment should fit

| Garment | What to look at |
|---|---|
| American | shoulders end where your shoulder ends; the sleeve shows a little of the shirt cuff; buttoned, does not pull or form an |
| Shirt | Buttoned collar holds one finger without squeezing; the sleeves reach the base of the thumb; there is no excess fabric at the waist |
| Trousers | fastens at the waist without a belt; the hem lightly brushes the shoe or sits just above it, depending on the style |
| Jersey | tight unmarked; the hem covers the waist of the pants |
| coat | a jacket fits underneath; the length, at knee height or slightly above, is the most versatile |

Almost no garment leaves the store perfect. A good repair of hems, sleeves or waist costs little compared to the garment and completely changes the result.

## Fabrics according to the season

Spain has very hot summers in much of the territory, cold winters in the interior and mild on the coast, and a long half-season. It is advisable to choose fabrics accordingly:

| Station | Recommended fabrics |
|---|---|
| summer | linen, light cotton, cold open-weave wools, seersucker |
| Halftime | thick cotton, light wool, fine merino knit |
| winter | flannel, tweed, thick wool, lamb or cashmere knit, broadcloth coats |

Natural fibers, such as wool, cotton or linen, breathe better and age more gracefully than synthetic ones. The composition of each garment must appear on the label: read it before buying.

## Signs of quality

- **Fabric:** dense, with good drape, which does not show through or pill when rubbed.
- **Seams:** straight, regular, with small stitches and no loose threads.
- **Buttons:** well sewn, with a foot of thread that gives them clearance on thick garments.
- **Married drawings:** on striped or checked shirts, the drawing continues on the seams and pockets.
- **Linings:** well fitted, without tightness, in jackets and coats.
- **Interior finishes:** Finished seams that do not fray.

## Three sets to start

| Occasion | Set |
|---|---|

| weekend | beige chinos, white Oxford shirt, navy blue crew-neck sweater and white leather sneakers or loafers |

## Confection, industrial measurement and tailoring

| Option | How it works | When it pays |
|---|---|---|
| Clothing | garments in standard sizes, ready to wear | for most garments, with minor alterations |
| industrial measurement | It is based on existing patterns that fit your measurements and are manufactured in series | if your body does not fit into the standard sizes |
| Bespoke tailoring | the tailor creates his own pattern, with several tests | for suits and jackets that you want to wear for many years |

Spain preserves tailor shops with a long tradition in many cities. A tailored suit requires time and several tests, but the result adapts to the body like no ready-made garment. If now is not the time, a good alteration in a quality garment is an excellent alternative.

## In what order to buy

1. **What you wear most:** pants, shirts and everyday shoes.
2. **The navy blue blazer:** multiplies the possibilities of everything else.
3. **A quality coat:** worn for many winters.
4. **The suit:** when your work or commitments demand it.
5. **Character pieces:** a leather jacket, a good watch or a ring, when the base is resolved.

Before buying something new, ask yourself which three items of clothing you already own would you pair it with. If you can't think of them, it's probably not a good buy.

## Take care of clothes so that they last

- **Wash less:** Many wool garments only need to be aired and brushed.
- **Use appropriate hangers:** wide ones for jackets and coats.
- **Doubles the point:** hanging is deformed.
- **Let the garments rest** between uses.
- **Protect wool from moths** with cedar or lavender, and store it clean.
- **Fix in Time:** A loose button or open seam is repaired in minutes.

## Frequent errors

- **Impulse buying** clothes that don't match anything.
- **Choose the large size** for comfort.
- **Do not fix** hems and sleeves.
- **Abusy logos and flashy prints.**
- **Neglecting footwear,** which gives away the whole outfit.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many clothes do I need?

It depends on your life, but with a small number of well-chosen clothes, in matching colors, you can create dozens of outfits.

### Is it worth paying more for a garment?

If the quality is real and you will use it a lot, yes: it lasts longer and feels better. Paying more for the brand without better quality is not worth it.

### What do I do with the clothes I don't wear?

If it is in good condition, donate it, sell it or take it for recycling. If you like it but it doesn't fit, ask a tailor if it can be fixed.

### How often should you renew your closet?

There is no fixed schedule. The sensible thing to do is to go through the closet when changing seasons: remove what is no longer used, repair what is in repair and note what is really missing. This way you buy with a specific objective, not on impulse, and good clothes can last for many years.

### Jeans with a blazer?

Yes, as long as they are dark, unscuffed and well cut. It is one of the most versatile combinations.

## In summary

An essential men's wardrobe is built with a few quality pieces, in a palette of neutrals such as navy blue, gray, white, beige and brown, and with a careful fit. The base includes a navy blue blazer, a suit, wool and chinos pants, dark jeans, shirts, knitwear, a wool coat, a trench coat and quality footwear. Choose fabrics according to the season, check seams, buttons and composition, buy in order of use and take care of the clothes so that they last: wash less, brush, air and repair on time.
