Buying jewelry online has become common: it allows you to compare hundreds of pieces in an afternoon, access workshops and designers from anywhere and, often, find better prices than in a physical store. But jewelry has something that makes it especially delicate for remote purchasing: its value depends on details that a photograph does not always show, such as the actual grade of the metal, the exact size of a stone or whether it is natural, synthetic or treated. And jewelry e-commerce is also one of the favorite areas of fraudulent stores.

In this guide you will see how to verify that an online store is real and reliable, what signs reveal a fake store, what information the product sheet should give you, how to get the size right, what payment methods protect you the most, what rights you have as a consumer in Spain, what happens if you buy outside the European Union and what to do when receiving the package.

## Check who sells

Spanish regulations require information society service providers to display their identification data on their website. Before purchasing, look for the legal notice and check:

| Data | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Name or company name | know who you contract with |
| Tax identification number | check that it exists as a company or professional |
| Physical address | be able to claim and, if you want, visit the establishment |
| Email and phone | direct and verifiable contact |
| Registration data | in companies, their registration in the commercial register |

Then look for opinions on independent sites, not just on the website itself, and be wary of stores that have no trace outside their page or their ads on social networks.

## Signs of a fake store

- **Prices well below the market,** especially on brand name or gold jewelry.
- **Recent domain** or with names that imitate well-known brands.
- **Absence of legal notice** or incomplete data.
- **Only supports transfers** to individual accounts, app payments between friends or cryptocurrencies.
- **Poorly translated texts** and photographs copied from other websites.
- **Countdown offers** and pressure to buy now.
- **Ads on social networks** that lead to unknown stores.

If you have doubts about a website or believe you have been a victim of fraud, the National Cybersecurity Institute offers a free and confidential help service by calling 017. If you have already paid, also contact your bank as soon as possible and report it to the police.

## What the product sheet should say

| Information | Example |
|---|---|
| Metal and law | 18K white gold (750) |
| Metal weight | approximate or exact |
| Measurements | hoop width, pendant size, chain length |
| stones | species, weight or measurements, quality, natural or synthetic |
| Treatments | expressly declared |
| Laboratory report | laboratory and report number, verifiable |
| Photographs and video | real, of the specific piece, from various angles |
| Delivery time and shipping costs | before paying |
| Return Policy | clear and accessible |

If something important is missing, ask in writing and save the answer: it is part of what they have offered you.

## Get the size right

In Spain, the size of the rings is usually expressed with a number that corresponds, approximately, to the inner circumference of the ring in millimeters minus forty. But charts vary between countries and brands, and home methods with thread or paper are unreliable. The safest thing to do is to measure yourself at a jewelry store with a set of measuring rings, preferably at the end of the day, when your fingers are a little more swollen. Remember that wide rings usually need to be sized up half a size.

Also ask if the store adjusts the size after purchase, how much it costs, and if that affects returns. Custom-made or engraved rings are usually excluded from the right of withdrawal.

## Pay safely

| Medium | Protection |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | allows you to request the bank to reverse charges in case of fraud or non-compliance |
| Payment platforms with buyer protection | offer their own complaint procedures |
| Bank transfer | difficult to recover; only with verified sellers |
| Payments between individuals by mobile | almost no protection when purchasing from strangers |
| Cryptocurrencies | practically irrecoverable |

Activate notifications from your bank, do not save card details on websites you do not know and check that the payment page is secure.

## Your rights when buying online in Spain

- **Right of withdrawal:** you have fourteen calendar days from receipt to return the product without giving explanations. If the seller did not inform you of this right, the period is extended.
- **Refund:** the seller must return what you paid, including ordinary shipping costs, within a maximum period of fourteen days from when you communicate the withdrawal, although you can wait to receive the product.
- **Return costs:** These are normally your responsibility if you were informed of this before purchasing.
- **Exceptions:** personalized or custom-made products, and other cases established by law.
- **Legal warranty:** three years from delivery for new products purchased from 2022.

Details, with exceptions and how to claim, are in the guide on [guarantees, returns and claims](/es/garantias-devoluciones-y-reclamaciones/).

## Purchases outside the European Union

Buying from stores outside the European Union has implications that many people discover when they receive the customs notice:

- **Import VAT:** Since 2021, all commercial shipments from outside the Union pay VAT, regardless of their value.
- **Tariffs:** may apply from a certain value.
- **Management fees:** Transport companies usually charge for processing customs.
- **Complicated Returns:** Resending the package can be expensive and time-consuming.
- **Counterfeit:** Brand imitations may be detained at customs.
- **Guarantees:** claiming from a foreign company is more difficult.

## Upon receiving the package

1. **Check the packaging** before signing for delivery and note any visible damage.
2. **Record the opening** of the package on video, especially valuable pieces.
3. **Check the piece:** contrasts, measurements, stones and condition, with a magnifying glass.
4. **Check the laboratory report** on the laboratory website, if there is one.
5. **If something doesn't match, report it in writing** immediately and save all evidence.
6. **If you plan to give up,** do not use the piece beyond what is necessary to check it.

How to read the contrasts is explained in the guide on [contrasts and guarantee marks](/es/contrastes-y-marcas-de-garantia/).

## Brand jewelry and watches

Brand-name jewelry and watches are the favorite target of fakes on the internet. Some specific precautions:

- **Buy from official distributors:** Most brands publish a list of authorized stores on their website.
- **Be wary of expressions such as "style", "inspired by" or "replica":** they indicate that it is not an original piece, and selling imitations of registered trademarks is illegal.
- **Check documentation:** warranty cards, serial numbers and certificates must match the part.
- **For second-hand,** ask for photographs of the serial number and documents, and consult the brand or a watchmaker if in doubt.
- **A price much lower than the official price** on a new and current piece is almost always a red flag.

Watches have their own keys, which we explain in the guide on [mechanical watches](/es/relojes-mecanicos/).

## Second-hand markets

Trading platforms between individuals offer interesting pieces, but without the guarantees of a purchase from a professional. Use the payment and shipping systems of the platform itself, be wary of those who want to take the conversation outside of it, ask for photographs of the contrasts and, for valuable pieces, meet in person and, if possible, at a jewelry store where they can inspect it.

## Frequent errors

- **Buy in stores without legal notice** or verifiable data.
- **Pay by transfer** to strangers.
- **Choose the size with homemade methods.**
- **Do not read the return policy** before paying.
- **Forget VAT and customs** on purchases from outside the European Union.
- **Do not inspect the piece upon receipt.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I return a piece of jewelry purchased online?

Yes, in general you have fourteen calendar days from receipt, with exceptions such as personalized or made-to-measure pieces.

### Who pays for return shipping?

Normally you, if the seller indicated it to you before the purchase. Original shipping costs must be reimbursed in the ordinary way.

### How do I know if an online store is trustworthy?

Check your legal notice, your tax identification number and your address, look for independent opinions and be wary of prices that are too low and payments through unprotected means.

### Do I have to pay customs for a jewelery purchased outside the EU?

You will pay import VAT and, depending on the value, tariffs and carrier management costs.

## In summary

Before buying jewelry online, verify the seller in their legal notice and independent opinions, and be wary of unrealistic prices, recent domains and unprotected payments. Demand a complete file with metal, grade, measurements, stones, treatments and reports, measure your size at a jewelry store and pay by card or with platforms that protect the buyer. In Spain you have fourteen days of withdrawal, with exceptions, and three years of legal guarantee. For purchases outside the European Union, you are subject to import VAT and customs. Upon receiving the package, record the opening, inspect the piece, and communicate any problems in writing.
