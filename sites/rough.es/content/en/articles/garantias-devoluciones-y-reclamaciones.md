A clasp that breaks after a few weeks, a stone that falls out of the setting, a bath that disappears in a month, a ring that turns out not to be of the correct quality or a jewelery purchased online that does not look like the one in the photos. These are common problems, and many people do not know if they have the right to something, who to complain to or how to do it. Spanish consumer legislation offers fairly solid protection, but it is worth knowing its rules, because not everything that is usually taken for granted is true: for example, there is no general right to return a piece of jewelry purchased in a physical store just because you no longer like it.

In this guide you will see what the legal guarantee is and how long it lasts, what is considered a lack of conformity in a piece of jewelry, what you can demand, how the right of withdrawal differs from the stores' exchange policies, what exceptions exist and how to complain step by step, from the first writing to the seller to arbitration. It is an informative guide, not legal advice: for specific cases, consult a consumer office or a professional.

## The legal guarantee

When you buy from a professional, they are responsible for ensuring that the product complies with the contract: that it has the quality, features and performance that were described and that can reasonably be expected.

| Appearance | General rule |
|---|---|
| Duration for new products | three years from delivery, for purchases made from January 1, 2022 |
| Second hand products | A shorter term can be agreed upon, with a minimum of one year |
| Presumption in favor of the consumer | In new products, if the defect appears in the first two years, it is presumed that it already existed upon delivery, unless proven otherwise. |
| Who is claimed? | to the seller, as a general rule |
| Cost to the consumer | solutions should be free |

The legal guarantee is independent of any commercial guarantee offered by the store or brand, which can only add rights, never cut them.

## What is a lack of conformity in a jewel?

| Situation | Lack of conformity? |
|---|---|
| The metal is not of the indicated law | yes: does not correspond to what was contracted |
| The stone is synthetic and was sold as natural | yes |
| An important treatment was not declared | It may be, according to what was described |
| A fastener or weld fails under normal use | yes, if not due to misuse |
| A stone comes loose due to a faulty setting | yes, if there are no knocks or misuse |
| A bath disappears in a very short time | It may be, depending on what you would expect from the piece. |
| The jewel was damaged by impact or chemicals | no, in general |
| You just don't like it anymore | no |

In many cases, the key is in the description: that is why it is so important that the invoice details the metal, the grade, the stones and their treatments, as we explain in the guide on [buying jewelry in Spain](/es/comprar-joyas-en-espana/).

## What can you demand?

1. **Repair or replacement:** you can choose one of the two, unless it is impossible or disproportionate for the seller. It must be done free of charge, within a reasonable period of time and without major inconvenience for you.
2. **Price reduction or contract termination:** if the repair or replacement is not possible, is not done on time or does not solve the problem. The resolution, which involves returning the jewel and recovering the money, does not apply when the lack of conformity is of minor importance.

While the jewelry is being repaired, keep the receipt with the description of the piece and the problem.

## Withdrawal: only in distance purchases and outside the establishment

The right of withdrawal allows you to return a product without giving explanations, but it only exists in distance purchases, such as those made online or by telephone, and in those made outside the establishment.

| Appearance | General rule |
|---|---|
| Term | fourteen calendar days from receipt |
| If you were not informed of the right | the term is extended to twelve months |
| Refund | within a maximum of fourteen days from the moment you communicate your withdrawal, including ordinary shipping costs |
| Return costs | at your expense if they informed you of it |
| Manipulation | You are responsible for the depreciation if you use the part beyond what is necessary to test it |

### Exceptions relevant to jewelry

- **Pieces made to your specifications or clearly personalized,** such as custom or engraved rings.
- **Sealed products** that cannot be returned for hygiene reasons once unsealed.
- **Products whose price depends on fluctuations in the financial markets** that the seller does not control, an assumption that some businesses apply to certain investment gold products.
- **Public auctions,** as we explain in the guide on [jewelry auctions](/es/subastas-de-joyas/).

## Physical stores: the change policy

In a physical store there is no legal right to return a piece of jewelry due to regret. Many stores, however, offer exchanges or returns as a business courtesy. If they do, that policy binds them in the terms in which they announce it: posters, tickets or websites are part of the offer. Before paying, ask the conditions and ask that they appear on the receipt: deadline, if money is returned or a voucher is given, and if there are exceptions, such as adjusted or engraved pieces.

## How to claim step by step

1. **Gather documentation:** invoice, photographs of the part and the problem, reports and communications.
2. **Written complaint to the seller:** by email, certified letter or burofax in important cases, describing the problem and what you request. Save a copy.
3. **Ask for the complaint form** if the response is not satisfactory. Establishments are obliged to have it available to customers. Deliver the corresponding copy to the consumer administration.
4. **Go to a consumer office:** the municipal consumer information offices and the regional consumer services provide guidance and intermediation.
5. **Propose consumer arbitration:** It is voluntary for companies, but many are members and show it with a badge. It is free and its resolution is binding.
6. **Value the judicial route:** For small claims there are simplified procedures, and in many cases the law requires first trying negotiation or mediation.

If you paid by card and the seller does not respond or has disappeared, check with your bank about the possibility of requesting a refund of the charge. When purchasing from sellers in other countries of the European Union, the European Consumer Center in Spain can help you.

## How to write the claim

Clear and complete writing facilitates a quick response. Includes:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Your data | name, identification document and contact information |
| Seller details | name or company name and address |
| Purchase | date, invoice or order number and description of the part |
| Problem | what happened, when you detected it and how you used the jewel |
| Application | repair, replacement, discount or money back |
| Term | a reasonable time to respond |
| Annexes | copy of the invoice, photographs and reports |

Write in a correct and concrete tone, without unnecessary adjectives. Such a writing later serves as evidence before the consumer office, arbitration or court.

## Repairs and orders

- **Always ask for a prior quote** in writing, with the work to be done.
- **Require a receipt** with a description of the piece you are leaving, including the metal and stones.
- **Keep the repair invoice,** which is also the basis for claiming if the job fails.
- **In custom orders,** leave in writing the approved design, materials, price, deposit and deadline.

## Frequent errors

- **Believe that any jewelry purchased in a store can be returned**.
- **Claim only verbally,** without leaving a written record.
- **Lose the invoice** or accept invoices without description.
- **Let time pass** before claiming a defect.
- **Use the jewel** after detecting the problem, aggravating it.
- **Accept vouchers** when another solution is appropriate.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does the warranty last on a new piece of jewelry?

Three years from delivery for purchases made after January 1, 2022.

### Can I be forced to accept a voucher instead of money?

If you return due to regret in a physical store, it depends on the policy that the store has announced. If there is a lack of conformity or you exercise withdrawal from a remote purchase, the voucher does not replace your legal rights.

### What do I do if the store refuses to give me the complaint form?

Write down the details of the establishment, the date and time, and go to the consumer office of your town hall or autonomous community, or to the local police if necessary.

### Does the warranty cover a falling stone?

If it is due to a manufacturing defect in the setting and not to a blow or misuse, it may be a lack of conformity.

## In summary

When buying jewelry from a professional in Spain you have a three-year legal guarantee for new products purchased from 2022, and during the first two years it is presumed that the defect already existed. In the event of a lack of conformity, such as a lower grade, an undeclared stone or a defective closure, you can demand repair or replacement and, if they do not work, a reduction or resolution. The fourteen-day withdrawal only exists for remote and off-premises purchases, with exceptions such as personalized pieces. In the physical store the announced policy governs. Always complain in writing, use the complaints form and resort to consumer and arbitration.
