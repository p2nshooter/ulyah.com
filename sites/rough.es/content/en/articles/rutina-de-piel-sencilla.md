For a long time, many men's skin routine was limited to washing their face with shower gel and, at most, applying lotion after shaving. At the other extreme, social networks today propose ten-step routines with products that promise miracles. Between one thing and another there is a sensible middle ground: three basic gestures, well chosen and constant, that truly take care of the skin, prevent much of premature aging and are done in a couple of minutes a day.

In this guide you will see how to identify your skin type, what the basic morning and evening routine is, why sunscreen is the most important step in a country with as much sun as Spain, what ingredients it is worth knowing without getting carried away by fashion, what habits influence the skin and, above all, when you should stop using the products and go to the doctor. It is an informative guide: when faced with any skin problem, the judgment of a dermatologist is what counts.

## Know your skin type

A simple test: wash your face with a mild cleanser, pat dry, and don't apply anything for an hour. Then, observe.

| Skin type | How to notice | What do you need |
|---|---|---|
| Fat | shine all over the face, visible pores | gentle cleansing, light moisturizers that do not clog pores |
| dry | tightness, peeling, dull appearance | very gentle cleansers and more nourishing moisturizers |
| Mixed | shine on forehead, nose and chin; normal or dry on the cheeks | balanced products, without drying |
| Sensitive | redness, itching or stinging easily | few products, unscented, tested before |
| normal | neither excessive shine nor tightness | keep the basics |

The skin changes with age, the seasons and the climate: that of a humid summer on the coast is not that of a dry winter in the interior. Adjust your routine when your skin asks you to.

## The basic routine

| Moment | Steps |
|---|---|
| Tomorrow | cleanse with water or a mild cleanser, moisturize and apply sunscreen |
| night | cleanse to remove sweat, oil, contamination and sunscreen, and moisturize |
| Once or twice a week | exfoliate gently, if your skin tolerates it |

### Clean

Use a mild facial cleanser and warm water, never too hot. Shower gel and hand soap are usually too aggressive for the face. Pat dry with a clean towel, without rubbing.

### Moisturize

A moisturizing cream suitable for your skin type reinforces the skin barrier, the layer that retains water and protects against external aggressions. If you have oily skin, you also need to hydrate it, with light textures.

### Protect from the sun

It is the most important step and the one that is most forgotten. Ultraviolet radiation is the main cause of premature skin aging and a risk factor for skin cancer.

- **Use sunscreen daily** on your face, neck and ears, also in winter and on cloudy days.
- **Choose broad spectrum protection** against ultraviolet A and B rays, with a high protection factor.
- **Apply enough amount:** Most people apply less than necessary.
- **Reapply every two hours** if you are outdoors, and after bathing or sweating a lot.
- **Complement with shade, a cap or hat and sunglasses,** and avoid the middle of the day in summer.

Spain has high ultraviolet radiation rates for much of the year. The State Meteorological Agency publishes the expected ultraviolet index daily, a useful reference for planning exposure.

## Eyes, lips, neck and scalp

- **Eye contour:** the skin is thinner; Apply any product gently, without rubbing, and protect the area with sunglasses.
- **Lips:** they crack in the cold, wind and sun. A lip balm, better with sun protection if you are going to be outdoors, avoids discomfort; Moistening them with your tongue dries them out more.
- **Neck and ears:** they are part of the face for care purposes; include them when cleansing, moisturizing and applying protector.
- **Scalp:** If you have little hair or wear it very short, it burns easily. A cap or hat is the best protection. Hair and scalp care has its own guide: [hair: care and hair loss](/es/cabello-cuidado-y-caida/).

## Skin according to the season

In summer, light textures are more comfortable, sunscreen becomes essential several times a day if you spend time outside, and it is a good idea to cleanse your skin after sweating or bathing in the sea or pool. In winter, cold, wind and heating dry out your skin: you may need a more nourishing moisturizer and even gentler cleansers. After playing sports, a simple cleanse removes sweat and reduces the appearance of pimples. Listening to your skin and adapting your routine is more useful than following any fixed guideline.

## Exfoliate in moderation

Exfoliation removes dead cells and can help with ingrown beard hairs. But over-exfoliating irritates the skin and weakens its barrier. Once or twice a week, with mild products, is enough for most. If your skin is sensitive or irritated, skip this step.

## Ingredients that should be known

| Ingredient | What is it used for? |
|---|---|
| Glycerin and hyaluronic acid | they attract and retain water; hydration |
| Ceramides | reinforce the skin barrier |
| Niacinamide | May help balance oil and improve the appearance of skin |
| salicylic acid | exfoliant that penetrates the pores; useful in oily skin |
| Alpha hydroxy acids, such as glycolic | surface exfoliants; can irritate |
| Retinoids | skin renewal; They are used at night, starting little by little, because they irritate and increase sensitivity to the sun |
| Vitamin C | antioxidant; some formulas are unstable |

No ingredient is essential, and more products do not mean better results. Add one at a time, test a small area first, and consult your pharmacy or dermatologist if you have concerns, especially with retinoids and chemical exfoliants.

## Shaving and skin

Shaving is a small daily aggression for the skin. Prepare your beard well, use sharp blades, do not press too much and avoid lotions with a lot of alcohol if they irritate you. We explain it in the guide on [classic shaving](/es/afeitado-clasico/), and if you have a beard, in the [beard care] guide(/es/cuidado-de-la-barba/).

## Habits that are noticeable on the skin

- **Tobacco:** accelerates skin aging, in addition to its effects on general health.
- **Sleep:** little sleep is reflected on the face.
- **Excessive alcohol:** dehydrates and can worsen some skin conditions.
- **Varied diet:** There are no miracle foods, but a balanced diet promotes overall health.
- **Hygiene of what touches the face:** pillowcases, towels, cell phone screens and helmets.
- **Clean hands:** touching your face with dirty hands promotes pimples; Hand care is explained in the guide on [hands and nails](/es/manos-y-unas/).

## When to go to the dermatologist

- **Moles that change:** look at the ABCDE rule, which summarizes the warning signs: asymmetry, irregular edges, various colors, large diameter and evolution or changes. Before any of them, consult.
- **Wounds that do not heal** in weeks.
- **Persistent acne** or that leaves marks.
- **Redness, peeling or itching** that do not improve with basic care.
- **Pimples and irritations** from shaving that recur or become infected.

Do not try to solve with cosmetics what needs a diagnosis. And check your skin from time to time, including your back, with the help of a mirror or another person.

## Buy products wisely

- **Few products, well chosen.**
- **Unscented** if your skin is sensitive.
- **Distrust miraculous promises** and "immediate results."
- **Ask the pharmacy for advice** if you don't know which texture to choose.
- **Respect the expiration date** and the symbol that indicates how many months the product lasts once opened.

## Frequent errors

- **Wash your face with shower gel** or very hot water.
- **Forget sunscreen** outside of summer.
- **Exfoliate daily.**
- **Accumulate products** for fashion.
- **Use retinoids or acids** without introducing them little by little.
- **Ignore changing moles.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Do men need men-specific products?

Not necessarily. Men's skin tends to be somewhat oilier and thicker, but the important thing is to choose according to your skin type, not according to the packaging.

### Do I need sunscreen if I'm not going to the beach?

Yes. Daily exposure to the sun, on the street, in the car or on terraces, accumulates over the years.

### If I have oily skin, should I moisturize it?

Yes, with light textures. Oily skin can also be dehydrated.

### How many products do I need?

To start, three: a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer and a sunscreen.

## In summary

A good skin routine does not need ten steps: just cleanse with a mild product, moisturize according to your skin type and protect from the sun daily, also in winter, reapplying if you are outdoors. Exfoliate in moderation, know the ingredients without getting carried away by trends and introduce the active ingredients one at a time. Take care of your habits, such as sleep and smoking, and monitor your skin: moles that change, wounds that do not heal or persistent problems deserve a visit to the dermatologist.
