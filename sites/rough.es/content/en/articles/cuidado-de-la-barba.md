A well-groomed beard can define a face, balance its proportions and give an air of maturity and personality. An unkempt beard, on the other hand, conveys just the opposite. The difference is rarely in expensive products or complicated routines: it comes down to a few consistent habits, choosing a style that flatters your face, and keeping your lines clean. And, above all, to understand that a beard is hair that grows on skin, and that both need attention.

In this guide you will see how to overcome the first weeks of growth, what styles exist and which one may suit you best depending on the shape of your face, how to mark the lines of the neck and cheeks, what tools you need, how to wash, moisturize and style the beard, how often to trim it and what to do with common problems such as itching, flaking or bald spots. If you notice sudden changes in your skin or hair, consult a dermatologist: this guide is informative.

## The first weeks

Growing a beard requires patience. During the first few weeks, the hair grows unevenly, may be itchy, and the beard appears more unkempt than anything else. It's normal.

- **Don't trim it too soon:** Wait several weeks before deciding on the shape, except for the neck line, which you can keep clean from the beginning.
- **Moisturizes the skin:** The itching is partly due to dryness and the ends of the hair rubbing against the skin; A simple oil or cream relieves it.
- **Wash the area daily with water** and a few times a week with a gentle cleanser.
- **Take genetics into account:** The density, color and way your beard grows depend mainly on hereditary factors. Some areas with little hair fill in over time; others don't.

## Styles

| Style | Features | Whom does it favor? |
|---|---|---|
| Beard of a few days | very short and uniform hair | almost everyone; requires frequent trimming |
| short beard | between short and medium, with defined shape | Versatile style, suitable for the office |
| full beard | covers cheeks, jaw and chin with some volume | faces that need structure |
| long beard | great volume and length | requires more care and patience |
| Knob or padlock | only on the chin and mustache | beards with little hair on the cheeks |

## Choose according to face shape

- **Round face:** More length in the chin and shorter sides, to visually lengthen the face.
- **Long face:** sides with a little more volume and a shorter chin, to balance.
- **Square Face:** Rounded edges at the bottom, to soften the jaw.
- **Oval face:** supports almost any style.

A barber can help you find the right shape on your first visit, and then maintain it at home.

## The lines that make the difference

| Line | How to mark it |
|---|---|
| Neck | a curved line that passes approximately two finger widths above the apple and goes up behind the ears; underneath, all shaved |
| Cheeks | natural or slightly defined, following the line that goes from the sideburns to the corner of the mustache |
| mustache | trimmed so that it does not cover the upper lip |
| Sideburns | attached to the beard without abrupt steps |

The most common mistake is raising the neck line too much, which makes the beard appear attached to the jaw and highlights the double chin. Better a little lower than too high.

## Tools

| Tool | What is it for? |
|---|---|
| Trimmer with guide combs | maintain a uniform length |
| barber scissors | retouch ends and mustache with precision |
| Wooden or horn comb | detangling and shaping without static electricity |
| Natural bristle brush | distribute the oils and discipline the hair |
| Razor or razor | clear neck and cheek lines |

## Wash and moisturize

1. **Rinse beard daily** with water during the shower.
2. **Wash with a mild cleanser** a few times a week; Shampooing your head hair can dry out your facial skin.
3. **Dry it gently** with a towel, without rubbing.
4. **Apply beard oil** with your beard still somewhat damp: spread a few drops with your fingers, reaching the skin.
5. **Use balm** if you need some hold and volume control.
6. **Comb or brush** in desired direction.

Choose simple products without strong perfumes if your skin is sensitive, and test any new product on a small area.

## Trim at home

- **Trim with dry beard:** Wet hair appears longer and you end up cutting too much.
- **Start with a long guide comb** and work your way down little by little.
- **Work symmetrically:** one side and then the other, comparing in the mirror.
- **Touch up the mustache with scissors,** combing it downwards.
- **Clean the lines at the end** with the trimmer without a comb or with a clipper.
- **Frequency:** every week or two, depending on the style.

## The mustache

The mustache deserves its own attention, because it is the part of the beard that is seen the most and the one that is most neglected.

- **Styles:** the classic, cropped just above the lip; the most populated, which slightly covers the commissure; and those with long tips, which are molded with wax.
- **Mustache wax:** a minimal amount, warmed between the fingers, helps set the shape and keep the tips in place.
- **Trimming:** Comb the mustache downwards and trim with scissors following the lip line, little by little.
- **Hygiene:** after eating or drinking, check that there are no remains; a small comb in your pocket helps.

## Gray hair in the beard

Gray hair usually appears earlier and more irregularly in the beard than in the hair on the head, often on the chin or on the sides. There are several options:

- **Accept it:** Many gray beards look great if they are well trimmed.
- **Keep your beard shorter:** Gray hair is less noticeable in short, uniform beards.
- **Dye it:** there are specific dyes for beards. Follow the manufacturer's instructions, always do the previous allergy test they indicate and do not use dyes intended for hair on your face. If you notice itching, redness or swelling, remove the product and consult a doctor. ## Common problems

| Problem | Possible causes | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Itching | dryness, initial growth | moisturize the skin and be patient |
| Flaking | dryness or irritation of the skin | gentle cleanser, hydration; If it persists or becomes inflamed, consult a dermatologist |
| ingrown hairs | curly hair that goes back into the skin | gentle exfoliation; do not pull out hair |
| Hairless areas | genetics or, if they appear suddenly in the form of plaques, some condition of the follicle | let it grow or choose a style that hides it; In case of sudden plaques, go to the doctor |
| rough beard | dry hair | oil and balm regularly |

Be wary of products that promise to grow your beard in a short time. Before using any treatment that affects hair growth, consult a doctor.

## Beard and daily life

- **After eating,** check and clean your beard and mustache.
- **In summer,** a shorter beard is cooler and more comfortable.
- **With masks or helmets,** a short beard looks better and allows for a good fit.
- **At work,** a well-shaped beard is as professional as a shave.

If you prefer to alternate between beard and shave, consult the guide on [classic shaving](/es/afeitado-clasico/).

## Frequent errors

- **Trim too soon,** without letting the beard grow.
- **Raise the neck line too much.**
- **Wash with aggressive shampoo** daily.
- **Trim with wet beard.**
- **Ignore the skin** underneath.
- **Let the lines and mustache grow without maintenance**.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long do you have to wait to see how your beard looks?

Several weeks. Before that time, uneven growth makes it impossible to judge the final shape.

### Is beard oil necessary?

It is not essential, but it helps hydrate the skin and hair, reduces itching and improves appearance.

### Why does my beard have gaps?

Normally due to genetics, and sometimes they fill in over time. If hairless patches suddenly appear, consult a doctor.

### What do I do if my beard is still itchy after months?

Check the basics first: that the skin is well hydrated, that you do not use an aggressive shampoo and that you rinse the products well. If the itching continues, flakes, redness or pimples appear, consult a dermatologist, because some facial skin conditions require specific treatment.

### How often should I go to the barber?

It depends on the style: to maintain a defined shape, every few weeks; At home you can touch up between visits.

## In summary

A good beard is built with patience: let it grow for several weeks before shaping it, moisturize the skin to soothe the itch, and choose a style that suits your face. Mark the line of the neck about two fingers above the apple, define the cheeks without excesses and keep the mustache above the lip. Wash with a gentle cleanser a few times a week, apply oil and balm, trim dry and symmetrically, and check the skin. If you experience persistent peeling or sudden hairless patches, consult a dermatologist.
