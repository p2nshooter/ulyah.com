Hair is one of the parts of the appearance that worries many men the most, and also one of the ones that accumulate the most myths. Shampoos that promise to stop baldness, miracle lotions, supplements that would make hair grow in weeks... The reality is simpler and, at the same time, more demanding: daily hair care is easy and does not require expensive products, while hair loss, when it goes beyond normal, is a medical issue that deserves a diagnosis and not home remedies.

In this guide you will see how the hair cycle works, how to wash and care for it according to its type, what styling products exist and what each one is for, how to care for the scalp and treat dandruff, how much hair it is normal to lose, what are the most common types of hair loss, what medically backed options exist and what promises should be ignored. It is an informative guide: if you have a fall that worries you, the right person is a dermatologist.

## The hair cycle

Each hair goes through several phases:

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| Growth | hair actively grows for years |
| Transition | growth stops for a short period |
| Rest | The hair remains in the follicle until it falls out and is replaced by a new one. |

Since each follicle has its own rhythm, losing hair every day is completely normal. Dermatology societies consider it normal to lose between 50 and 100 hairs a day. What should be worrying is not seeing hair on the pillow or in the shower, but rather a clearly greater loss than usual, areas that lighten or patches without hair.

## wash hair

- **Frequency:** depends on your hair type and your activity. Oily hair or those who exercise daily may need to wash it every day; dry hair, every two or three days. Washing it daily with a mild shampoo does not cause hair loss.
- **Shampoo:** Choose one suitable for your scalp, not just your hair. Apply it to the scalp with your fingertips, without scratching with your nails.
- **Conditioner:** useful for medium or long hair, applied to the middle and ends, not to the roots.
- **Water:** warm; Very hot water dries out the scalp.
- **Drying:** with the towel, without rubbing hard, and with the dryer at a moderate temperature if you use it.

## Nuances depending on hair type

| hair type | Care keys |
|---|---|
| Fine | light shampoos, little conditioner and products that add volume without weighing them down, such as foams or matte clays |
| Thick or abundant | more hydration, conditioner on the mid-lengths and ends and products with a little more hold |
| Curly | less frequent washing, generous conditioner, wet detangling with a wide-toothed comb and avoiding dry brushing, which causes frizz |
| Oily scalp with dry ends | mild shampoo for the roots and conditioner only for the ends |

In curly hair, natural sebum takes longer to travel through the fiber, so it tends to dry easily; Respecting its washing times and combing it gently greatly improves its appearance. For fine hair, however, the most common mistake is to load it with dense products that leave it stuck to the scalp. In all cases, observing how your hair responds over a few weeks is the best way to adjust your routine.

## hair styling products

| Product | Fixing and finishing | For what hair |
|---|---|---|
| Gel or gel | strong hold, glossy or wet finish | defined hairstyles and short hair |
| Ointment | medium hold, glossy finish | classic hairstyles back or parted |
| Wax | medium hold, natural or satin finish | short and medium textured hair |
| Clay or matte paste | medium or strong hold, matte finish | fine hair, to give volume |
| Foam | light hold, volume | fine or curly hair |
| Hairspray or fixative | final fixation | to maintain the hairstyle |

Use a small amount, spread it between your hands before applying it and remove it at the end of the day. Excess product weighs down the hair and can irritate the scalp.

## The cut

A good cut adapted to the shape of your face and the texture of your hair does more than any product. Depending on the style, it is advisable to review it every few weeks. Explain to the barber or hairdresser how you style your hair and how much time you spend on it each morning: a cut that requires twenty minutes of blow-drying is not suitable for someone who has five. If your hair begins to thin, a shorter cut usually hides it better than leaving it long to cover the areas with less density.

## The scalp and dandruff

Dandruff, those white or yellowish scales that fall on the shoulders, is very common and is usually related to a mild form of seborrheic dermatitis, which is influenced by a fungus that lives naturally on the skin. It is not contagious nor is it due to lack of hygiene.

- **Anti-dandruff shampoos:** in pharmacies there are shampoos with different active ingredients that help control it; follow the instructions for use.
- **Alternate** anti-dandruff shampoo with a mild one if it dries you out.
- **Consult your doctor** if dandruff does not improve, if there is intense redness, strong itching, scabs or if it also affects the face, eyebrows or beard.

## Most frequent types of falls

| Guy | Traits | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Androgenetic alopecia | entrances and crown that lighten little by little; has a strong hereditary component | consult a dermatologist if you want to treat it; The sooner, the more options |
| Telogen effluvium | diffuse, heavy hair loss weeks or months after an illness, high fever, surgery, intense stress, drastic diets, or some medications | It is usually temporary, but it is advisable to confirm the diagnosis |
| Alopecia areata | rounded patches without hair, on the head or beard | requires medical evaluation |
| Other causes | nutritional deficiencies, thyroid problems, infections or scalp diseases | medical diagnosis and treatment |

Only a doctor can safely distinguish one from the other. Often, a consultation and, if necessary, an analysis are enough to guide the problem.

## Medically Backed Options

For androgenetic alopecia there are treatments with proven effectiveness, both topical and oral, and procedures such as hair transplant. They all have indications, limitations and possible side effects, and must be indicated and supervised by a doctor. Hair transplant is a surgical intervention that must be performed by a medical team in an authorized health center.

Before deciding, ask your dermatologist what you can really expect, how long to continue the treatment, and what happens if you stop. Be wary of clinics that promise guaranteed results, of low-price offers in centers without health guarantees and of those who do not clearly explain the risks.

## Promises that should be ignored

- **Shampoos that "cure" baldness:** a cleansing shampoo; does not reverse androgenetic alopecia.
- **Miracle supplements:** They only help if there is a diagnosed deficiency.
- **Homemade lotions:** with garlic, onion or oils, without evidence and with risk of irritation.
- **Results in weeks:** Hair grows slowly and any effective treatment takes months to show effect.

## If you decide not to treat the fall

Many men accept hair loss quite naturally, and having very short hair or a shaved head can look very good. If you do, protect your scalp from the sun with a cap, hat, or sunscreen, and moisturize it if it becomes dry. We explain it in the guide on [simple skin routine](/es/rutina-de-piel-sencilla/).

## White hair

Gray hair appears due to the progressive loss of pigment in the follicle, and the moment in which it appears depends above all on genetics. If you decide to dye them, follow the manufacturer's instructions, do the prior allergy test indicated by the product and, if you notice a reaction, remove it and consult a doctor.

## Frequent errors

- **Be alarmed by the normal decline** of each day.
- **Ignore noticeable hair loss** or hairless patches.
- **Spend on miracle products** instead of consulting.
- **Scratch the scalp** with your nails when washing.
- **Abusing styling products.**
- **Start treatments** without medical supervision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it bad to wash your hair every day?

Not if you use a mild shampoo suitable for your scalp. Washing it daily does not cause hair loss.

### How much hair is normal to lose per day?

Dermatology societies consider it normal to lose between 50 and 100 hairs a day.

### Does the cap cause baldness?

No. Wearing a cap does not cause alopecia, as long as it is clean and does not tighten excessively.

### When should I go to the dermatologist?

If you notice more hair loss than usual for weeks, thinning areas, hairless patches, or itchy dandruff, redness or scabs that do not improve.

## In summary

Losing between 50 and 100 hairs a day is normal, because each hair has its own cycle. Wash your hair with a mild shampoo suitable for your scalp, with warm water, and use styling products sparingly. Dandruff is common and is controlled with pharmacy shampoos; If it persists, consult. Noticeable hair loss, receding hairlines or hairless patches deserve a diagnosis: there are treatments with medical support, which must be indicated and supervised by a dermatologist. Ignore the miracle promises and, if you decide not to treat hair loss, protect your scalp from the sun.
