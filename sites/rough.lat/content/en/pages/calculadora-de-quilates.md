The carat of gems is a unit of weight: one carat is equivalent to 0.2 grams, that is, 200 milligrams, and is divided into one hundred points. It has nothing to do with the carat of gold, which measures its grade. The calculator above does the conversions and also estimates the weight of a stone that cannot be weighed because it is set.

## Conversions

| Carat | Grams | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 0.25ct | 0.05g | 25 |
| 0.50ct | 0.10g | 50 |
| 1.00ct | 0.20g | 100 |
| 2.00ct | 0.40g | 200 |
| 5.00ct | 1.00g | 500 |

## Estimated weight from measurements

For a set gem, the craft uses formulas that multiply the measurements in millimeters by a factor depending on the shape and by the specific weight (density) of the gem:

| Shape | Approximate formula (measurements in mm) |
|---|---|
| Round | diameter × diameter × depth × 0.0018 × specific weight |
| Oval | length × width × depth × 0.0020 × specific weight |
| Emerald or rectangular cut | length × width × depth × 0.0025 × specific weight |
| Pear | length × width × depth × 0.00175 × specific weight |
| Marchioness | length × width × depth × 0.0016 × specific weight |

| Gem | Typical Specific Weight |
|---|---|
| Diamond | 3,52 |
| ruby and sapphire | 4,00 |
| Emerald and aquamarine | 2,72 |
| Topaz | 3,53 |
| Tourmaline | 3,06 |
| Quartzs (amethyst, citrine) | 2,65 |
| Spinel | 3,60 |
| Peridot | 3,34 |
| Tanzanite | 3,35 |
| Opal | 2,15 |

An example: a round diamond 6.5 mm in diameter and 4.0 mm deep weighs about 6.5 × 6.5 × 4.0 × 0.0018 × 3.52 ≈ 1.07 carats. These formulas are usually right within 10 to 15%; They deviate more if the stone has a bulging pavilion, a thick girdle or an irregular cut. The exact weight is only given by a carat scale, and what appears in a laboratory report is rounded to two decimal places.

Remember that weight is not visible size: two diamonds of the same weight can look different depending on their cut. More in our guides [Diamonds: essential guide](/es/diamantes-guia-esencial/) and [Gemological laboratory reports](/es/informes-de-laboratorio-gemologico/).
