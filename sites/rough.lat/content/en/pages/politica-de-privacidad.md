This policy describes what information is processed when you read ROUGH.LAT and how you can exercise your rights. We apply it to all readers, wherever they live.

## Responsible

The data controller is p2nshooter, editor of ROUGH.LAT. Contact: [contacto@rough.lat](mailto:contacto@rough.lat).

## Information we process

**Emails.** If you write to us, we use your address and message only to respond to you. We do not add them to mailing lists or share them with third parties.

**Reading statistics.** We count which pages are visited in aggregate (the page address and the site name). We do not use our own cookies for this, nor do we save your full IP address or create profiles.

**Technical data.** Cloudflare, the provider that delivers the pages, processes technical data such as the IP address so that the site functions and is protected from attacks.

## Google AdSense Advertising

The magazine displays Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and identifiers to display ads, measure their performance and, if you allow, personalize them based on your activity on this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads in [Google ads settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and read [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before advertising cookies are used.

## Your rights

According to the law of your country – for example, the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties in Mexico, Law 1581 of 2012 in Colombia, Law 25,326 in Argentina, Law 29733 in Peru, Chilean data protection regulations or the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Union – you can request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition to the processing of your data. Write to us and we will attend to your request. You can also go to the data protection authority in your country.

## Conservation and security

We save messages only as long as necessary to address your query. The site is always served via encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Please also consult the [cookie policy](/politica-de-cookies/).
