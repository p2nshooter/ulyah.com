Not all gems support the same. Hardness measures resistance to scratches, toughness measures resistance to impacts, and stability measures resistance to heat, light and chemicals. The tool above brings together the essentials of each gem; The tables below say the same to consult or print.

## Hardness, tenacity and use

| Gem | Hardness (Mohs) | Tenacity | Everyday ring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diamond | 10 | good (may chip) | yes |
| ruby and sapphire | 9 | excellent | yes |
| Chrysoberyl and alexandrite | 8,5 | excellent | yes |
| Spinel | 8 | good | yes |
| Topaz | 8 | regular | with protective bezel |
| Aquamarine | 7.5 to 8 | good | yes |
| Emerald | 7.5 to 8 | fair to poor | with protective bezel |
| Tourmaline | 7 to 7.5 | regular | carefully |
| Quartz | 7 | good | yes |
| Garnets | 6.5 to 7.5 | fair to good | carefully |
| Peridot | 6.5 to 7 | regular | with protective bezel |
| Tanzanite | 6 to 7 | regular | with protective bezel |
| Opal | 5.5 to 6.5 | poor to fair | best in pendants and earrings |
| Turquoise | 5 to 6 | regular | best in pendants and earrings |
| Lapis lazuli | 5 to 5.5 | regular | best in pendants and earrings |
| Pearls | 2.5 to 4.5 | regular | carefully |

## How to clean each one

| Gem | Warm soapy water | Ultrasounds | Steam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diamond, ruby and sapphire without fillings | yes | In general, yes | In general, yes |
| Emerald | just a short rinse | no | no |
| Glass Filled Ruby and Fissured Gems | damp cloth | no | no |
| Topaz, tourmaline, peridot | yes, without sudden temperature changes | no | no |
| Tanzanite | yes, carefully | with great caution | no |
| Opal, pearls, amber, coral | damp cloth | no | no |
| Turquoise, lapis lazuli, malachite | dry or slightly damp cloth | no | no |

The safe method for almost all gems is warm water, a few drops of mild soap and a soft bristle brush, with a good rinse and drying with a lint-free cloth. Ultrasounds and steam are only suitable for hard and tenacious stones without cracks, fillings or treatments, and never for a jewel with loose stones. A lab report indicates whether the gem is treated; When in doubt, ask the jeweler.
