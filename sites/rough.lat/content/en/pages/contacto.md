We want ROUGH.LAT to be useful for readers throughout Latin America, and for that we need to know what questions you have. Write to us at:

**[contact@rough.lat](mailto:contacto@rough.lat)**

## Topics you can write to us about

1. **Errors and corrections.** Indicate the article and the information that seems incorrect to you. If you're right, we corrected it and updated the review date.
2. **Article ideas.** A gem from your country that you want to know better? A traditional workshop technique? We love receiving proposals from different countries in the region.
3. **Local terms.** Jewelry vocabulary changes from country to country. If a term we use is said differently where you live, let us know.
4. **General matters** about the magazine, advertising or the use of our texts.

## What we don't do by mail

We do not identify or appraise gems from photos or videos, we do not certify pieces, and we do not broker purchases or sales. To identify a stone you need a gemological laboratory; to value it, an independent appraiser who examines it in person.

## Deadlines

We generally respond within five business days. Corrections take priority. The data you send us is only used to respond to you, as explained in the [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/).
