ROUGH.LAT publishes general information about gems, precious stones, jewelry and crafts. We work carefully to make it correct, but no article can replace the examination of a specific piece by a professional.

## About gem identification

Many gems have imitations and synthetic versions that, at first glance, are identical to natural ones. Treatments—heating, filling fissures with oil or resin, diffusion, irradiation—that change the appearance and value of a stone are also common. The signs we describe help to ask the right questions, but confirmation can only be given by a gemological laboratory with the appropriate instruments.

## About prices and value

We do not publish prices for specific stones or value estimates. The price of a gem depends on its identity, origin, treatments, size, color, purity, cut and the market in which it is sold. Only an independent appraiser who examines the piece can value it.

## About investment

Gems are not regulated financial products: they lack a public reference price and their resale can be slow and expensive. Be wary of any offer that promises guaranteed profitability. Nothing we publish is an investment recommendation.
