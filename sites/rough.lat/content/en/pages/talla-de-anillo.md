The size of a ring depends on a single physical measurement: the inner size of the ring. What changes from one country to another is the name it receives. The converter above goes from one form to another; The table below shows the most common equivalences, also without JavaScript.

## How sizes are defined

- **European size (ISO 8653):** is the inner circumference of the ring in millimeters. A size 54 measures 54 mm inside.
- **United States, Canada and Mexico sizing:** Each size adds about 0.81 mm to the inner diameter. Size 0 corresponds to 11.63 mm in diameter; The formula is diameter = 11.63 mm + 0.8128 mm × size.
- **Diameter and circumference:** The circumference is the diameter multiplied by π (about 3.1416).

## How to measure without making mistakes

1. Measure a ring that already fits on the same finger, inside and edge to edge, with a ruler or gauge.
2. Measure at the end of the day and with warm hands: in the cold the fingers shrink, in the heat and after exercise they swell.
3. If the knuckle is wider than the base of the finger, the size must pass the knuckle.
4. For wide bands, 6 mm or more, a half size larger is usually necessary.

The strip of paper or string method gives an idea, but it stretches and bends easily. For an engagement ring or wedding ring, confirm the size with a jewelry sizer at a shop.
