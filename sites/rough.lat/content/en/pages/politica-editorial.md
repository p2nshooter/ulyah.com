This policy explains how we work so that you know what to expect from each ROUGH.LAT item.

## Principles

- **Usefulness.** Each article answers a specific question from a real reader: how to recognize, compare, care for or buy wisely.
- **Originality.** We write each text from scratch in Spanish. We do not translate or rewrite articles from other media. To research and write we also use artificial intelligence tools; The rules on this page apply to every sentence, regardless of how it was written.
- **Precision.** We contrast gemological data with reference sources: recognized laboratories and gemology schools, scientific publications and official organizations. We cite them at the end of each guide, with the date of the last revision.
- **Regional context.** When a piece of information depends on the country—a site, a norm, a custom—we indicate it.
- **Honesty.** We separate facts, opinions and estimates. If something is unclear or sources disagree, we say so.

## What we never do

- Invent prices, returns, market statistics or search volumes.
- Attributing texts to non-existent experts or faking personal experiences and product tests.
- Charge merchants to appear in an article or use affiliate links.
- Present advertising as editorial content.

## Review and corrections

Before publishing, each article is reviewed for accuracy, clarity and structure. If we later detect an error, we correct it and update the article's revision date. You can report errors to us at [contacto@rough.lat](mailto:contacto@rough.lat).
