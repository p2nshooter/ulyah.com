Our guides explain how to recognize, compare and care for a gem or piece of jewelry. These four tools do the math with your own data, the moment you need it: in front of an inherited ring, on a jeweler's counter, or before cleaning a piece. They work entirely in your browser: we don't send anything to any servers, you don't need to register, and we don't give prices or investment advice.

## Ring size converter

Go from the diameter or inner circumference in millimeters to the size of the United States, Canada and Mexico and to the European size, which according to the ISO 8653 standard is the inner circumference in millimeters. Includes tips for wide bands and large knuckles. [Open size converter](/talla-de-anillo/)

## carat calculator

Convert carats, grams and points (a carat is 0.2 grams and one hundred points) and estimate the weight of a gem set from its measurements, its shape and its specific weight, with the margin of error of these formulas in view. [Open carat calculator](/calculadora-de-quilates/)

## Metal law calculator

Indicates how much pure gold, silver or platinum a piece contains according to its grade in carats or thousandths and its weight, and converts the carats of gold into thousandths. No prices: just the composition. [Open law calculator](/ley-de-los-metales/)

## Gem Care Guide

Choose one of eighteen gems and organic materials and you will see its hardness on the Mohs scale, its tenacity, whether it supports warm soapy water, ultrasound or steam, and whether it is suitable for a ring for daily use. [Open care guide](/cuidado-de-gemas/)

## Where does the data come from?

Each tool uses the same data as our guides and cites its sources on its own page: the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) for carat, durability and gem care, ISO 8653 for ring sizes, and the US Federal Trade Commission Jewelry Guides for metal marking. A guiding tool; It is not a substitute for a carat scale, a jewelry sizer, or a gemological laboratory report. If you find an error, write to us from the [contact](/contacto/) page.
