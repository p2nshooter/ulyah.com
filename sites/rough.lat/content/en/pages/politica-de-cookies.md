Cookies are small files that a website saves on your browser. This page explains which ones may appear when you visit ROUGH.LAT.

## Without own tracking cookies

ROUGH.LAT does not install its own cookies to measure your behavior or to create profiles. Visits are counted without cookies. The site only saves a flag in the browser's session storage to not repeat the welcome animation on each page; It clears itself when you close the tab.

## Google AdSense Cookies

The magazine's ads are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies to:

- show ads and prevent you from always seeing the same one;
- check that an ad was displayed correctly and prevent fraud;
- personalize ads based on your interests, when you allow it.

If you visit us from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will see a prompt to accept or reject these cookies before they are installed. More information in [how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## How to control them

You can block or delete cookies from your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge or other). On mobile, the option is usually in the browser's privacy settings. If you block them, the magazine will continue to work; only the advertising you see will change.

## Updates

If the cookies used on the site change, we will update this page and its revision date.
