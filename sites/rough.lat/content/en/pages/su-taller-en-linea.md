From Colombian emeralds to Mexican opals and Brazilian tourmalines, Latin America has an extraordinary gemological heritage, and thousands of workshops that transform it into jewelry. ROUGH.LAT tells it calmly and rigorously. On this page we present two digital projects that belong to the same publisher as ROUGH.LAT; That's why we announce it from the beginning: it's our own advertising.

## AXTO: a museum of unrepeatable works

[AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=rough.lat&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) brings together digital art that breathes: clocks with true time, stamps from imaginary kingdoms, verses from the Koran and hadiths in calligraphy without changes in the text and figures that move. Behind each work there is a mathematical DNA that is never repeated, and each one is saved as an NFT in Base.

Whoever wants to give something to clients from other countries can choose an exclusive **AC-** edition for 1 dollar, paid with AXTO coins, ten per work and without gas cost. On OpenSea there are **OS-** public collections: from 1 to 10 editions, fixed price from 1 to 250 dollars; **G** points to GPU paint. In the digital workshop, AXTO works as a community investment starting from one dollar; Lock coins are rewarded with unique works and the value may drop.

## A store or platform tailored to you: XAA

From a small family workshop to a cooperative of artisanal miners, each business needs something different. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=rough.lat&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European web development studio working for clients around the world and building:

- **online stores** for finished jewelry, with detailed galleries and international shipping;
- **B2B portals** where jewelers and wholesalers consult lots, prices and availability;
- **regional marketplaces** that bring together several workshops under the same brand.

The work is divided into milestones: each stage is agreed, delivered and paid for separately, by PayPal, transfer or USDT, an advantage when dealing with clients in different countries. There are also [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=rough.lat&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) to start with a contained budget. The XAA site is in Spanish, English and Indonesian.
