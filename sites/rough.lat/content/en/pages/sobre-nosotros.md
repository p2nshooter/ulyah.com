Latin America is one of the great gemological regions of the world. Some of the most appreciated emeralds come from Colombia; Brazil is famous for its tourmalines, topazes and aquamarines; Mexico for its fire opals; Chile for lapis lazuli; Peru for its silver tradition. However, much of the information about gems that is read in Spanish comes translated from distant markets, with other references and other prices.

**ROUGH.LAT** was born to fill that gap: an editorial magazine in Spanish, designed for readers from Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Uruguay and the rest of the region, which calmly explains how gems are formed, how they are recognized, how they are cut and how they become jewelry that lasts generations.

## Our motto

*From Raw Beauty to Timeless Value* describes the path we follow in each article: from the stone that comes out of the rock to the finished piece, without shortcuts or easy promises.

## Who edits the magazine

ROUGH.LAT is a project by **p2nshooter**, independent publisher of thematic magazines. The articles are prepared by the ROUGH.LAT editorial team, which signs collectively, with the help of artificial intelligence tools, and reviews them before publishing them against reference sources that we cite at the end of each guide. The editorial tools (ring size, carat, metal grade, and gem care) are in [Tools](/herramientas/), and each publication and review appears with its date in [News](/novedades/). We do not attribute the texts to invented experts nor do we present experiences that we have not lived.

## Five themed rooms

- **Rough gems:** geological formation, crystals, identification and safe purchase of uncut material.
- **Precious stones:** quality, treatments, synthetics, certificates and conservation.
- **Jewelry and design:** metals, settings, engagement rings and custom design.
- **Carving and crafts:** the lapidary wheel, carving styles and traditional workshops.
- **Luxury and lifestyle:** how to wear, give and collect jewelry with discretion and responsibility.

## Independence

ROUGH.LAT does not sell gems or jewelry and does not charge merchants to appear on its pages. The magazine is supported by Google AdSense advertising, always separated from editorial content. You can write to us from the [contact](/contacto/) page.
