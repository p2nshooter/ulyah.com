Few things are as exciting as opening a box and finding a jewel designed for you. A piece of jewelry is never worn out or forgotten: it is worn, remembered who gave it, and often ends up being passed on to the next generation. That is why giving jewelry is such a deep-rooted custom in Latin America, linked to the great moments of life, from baptism to the fifteenth birthday, from the engagement to the golden wedding anniversary.

But getting it right is not that easy. You have to guess the other person's style, the metal they prefer, whether they have allergies, whether they wear earrings or not, and, in the case of rings, the size. In this guide you will see how to observe before choosing, what jewelry is traditionally given as a gift at each stage, what precautions to take with babies, what pieces are less risky, how to personalize a gift and what should accompany the jewelry so that the gift is complete.

## Before choosing: observe

The best clues are in what the person already uses:

- **The metal:** Does it have yellow, white, rose or silver gold?
- **The size:** Do you prefer small and discreet pieces or large and striking pieces?
- **The style:** classic, modern, artisanal, minimalist.
- **The earrings:** Do you have pierced ears? Do you wear push-back, dangle, or hoop earrings?
- **Allergies:** some people do not tolerate metals with nickel or low quality bathrooms.
- **The routine:** someone who works with their hands will appreciate a pendant more than a ring.

If you have doubts, a casual conversation or the complicity of someone close can save the gift.

## Traditional gifts at every stage

| Moment | Common gifts |
|---|---|
| Birth and baptism | religious medal, bracelet with name, small earrings |
| first communion | medal, crucifix, rosary, fine chain |
| fifteen years | ring, earrings, chain with pendant, sometimes a tiara for the celebration |
| Graduation | ring, watch, pendant with initials |
| Commitment | engagement ring |
| Wedding | rings or alliances, jewelry for the bride or groom |
| Anniversaries | pieces according to the tradition of each anniversary or with their own meaning |
| Appointed dates | small and meaningful jewelry |

Customs vary from country to country and family to family, so it's a good idea to ask what is expected in each case.

## Baptism and early childhood

Giving a medal, a bracelet or some earrings to a baby is a widespread tradition in the region. Some important precautions:

- **Necklaces and chains for babies:** Health authorities in several countries advise against babies wearing necklaces, including amber teething necklaces, due to the risk of suffocation and strangulation. A medal can be saved for when the child is older or pinned to clothing with a secure pin under supervision.
- **Earrings:** Choose hypoallergenic metals, such as sterling gold, with secure closures that won't come loose.
- **Bracelets:** that do not tighten and do not have small pieces that can come off.

If you have any questions, consult your pediatrician.

## first communion

Religious medals, crucifixes and rosaries are the most common gifts. A fine quality gold or silver chain will accompany the person for years. A discreet engraving with the date or initials turns the piece into a keepsake.

## fifteen years

The quinceanera party is one of the most important celebrations in many countries in the region, and jewelry is part of it: rings, earrings, pendants and, in some families, a tiara for the day of the party. It is a good occasion to give a first quality piece of jewelry that the person can continue wearing as an adult, instead of a piece that is only useful for that night.

## Graduation

A class ring, a watch or a pendant with initials marks the passage to a new stage. If the ring bears the emblem of an institution, check the size carefully, because rings with reliefs are difficult to fit.

## Engagement, wedding and anniversaries

The engagement ring and rings have their own guide: [engagement rings](/es/anillos-de-compromiso/). For wedding anniversaries, there is a tradition that associates some anniversaries with specific materials:

| Anniversary | Material associated in tradition |
|---|---|
| 25 years | silver |
| 50 years | gold |
| 60 years | diamond |

Other intermediate anniversaries are associated, depending on the source, with materials such as pearls, rubies, sapphires or emeralds. There is no need to follow the list to the letter: a piece with meaning for the couple is usually the best gift.

## Calendar dates

- **Mother's Day:** is celebrated on different dates depending on the country; A small piece of jewelry with your child's initials or birthstones is a classic gift.
- **Day of Love and Friendship:** in many countries it coincides with February 14; In Colombia, for example, it is celebrated in September.
- **Christmas and New Year's Eve:** shopping time, ideal for versatile pieces.

If you want to include stones with meaning, the guide on [birthstones](/es/piedras-de-nacimiento/) has the traditional list.

## Which piece risks the least?

| Piece | Risk of not getting it right | why |
|---|---|---|
| Earrings | low, if the person has pierced ears | They do not depend on a size |
| Pendant or necklace | low | The length can be adjusted or the chain can be changed |
| Bracelet | medium | some have size; adjustable ones are safer |
| Ring | high | Depends on size and personal taste |
| Clock | medium | the style is very personal |

If you want to give a ring, find out the size discreetly, as we explain in the guide on [how to measure ring size](/es/medir-la-talla-de-anillo/), and choose a design that can be adjusted.

## Gift jewelry to men

Many men wear little jewelry, but the ones they do wear are worn daily, so it is a good idea to choose practical, good quality pieces:

- **A watch** according to your style, sporty, classic or dressy.
- **Cufflinks,** if you usually wear French cuff shirts.
- **A signet ring** with your initials or a family symbol, if you like rings.
- **A leather bracelet** with silver details or a thin chain.
- **A chain with a medal,** with religious or family meaning.

Look at what metal you use in your watch or wedding ring, if you have one, and choose sober, sturdy designs. A discreet engraving on the inside of a ring or on the back of a watch is often more exciting than a statement piece.

## Personalize with engravings

An engraving with initials, a date or a word makes a piece of jewelry unique. Please note that:

- **The interior engravings** are discreet and well preserved.
- **An engraved item** often cannot be exchanged or returned.
- **Engravings take time:** order them in advance.

## What should accompany the gift

- **Invoice or gift receipt** with exchange conditions.
- **Description of the metal and stones,** and the laboratory report if it is a valuable piece.
- **Guarantee** and size adjustment service, if available.
- **Care instructions,** especially if the stone is delicate. You can rely on the guide on [jewelry maintenance and cleaning](/es/mantenimiento-y-limpieza-de-joyas/).
- **A suitable case** to store it.

## Gifting on a budget

- **Sterling silver** allows for beautiful and long-lasting designer pieces.
- **Small gold pieces** of good quality last a lifetime.
- **A small stone of good color** looks better than a large, dull one.
- **Design and meaning** count more than price.

Avoid low-quality dipped pieces for gifts that are expected to last: the dip wears out and so does the illusion.

## Frequent errors

- **Choose according to your taste** and not that of the person.
- **Giving a ring without knowing the size** or being able to adjust it.
- **Purchase stud earrings** for someone who does not have pierced ears.
- **Ignore allergies** to certain metals.
- **Give necklaces to babies** so they can wear them without supervision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which jewel is the easiest to hit?

Simple earrings or a pendant with a good quality chain are usually the safest gifts.

### Is it a good idea to give a ring as a surprise?

It can be if you know the person's size and style, and if the ring can be adjusted. If not, a piece that does not depend on size is better.

### What is given for fifteen years?

It is common to give rings, earrings or pendants, often made of gold or silver, and in some families a tiara for the party. A quality piece that can continue to be used is a great souvenir.

### Can babies wear jewelry?

Small earrings with secure closures are a widespread tradition, but health authorities advise against necklaces for babies due to the risk of suffocation and strangulation.

## In summary

To choose a piece of jewelry, look at the metal, the size and style the person wears, whether they have pierced ears, and whether they suffer from allergies. Each stage of life has its traditional jewels, from baptism to fifteen years, graduation, engagement and anniversaries, but the most important thing is the meaning. With babies, avoid necklaces and choose safe earrings. Earrings and pendants are less risky than rings. Accompany the gift with an invoice, description, warranty and care instructions, and prefer small, good quality pieces over large pieces that will not last.
