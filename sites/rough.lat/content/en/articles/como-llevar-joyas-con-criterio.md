Wearing jewelry with elegance has nothing to do with how much you have or how much it cost. One person with three well-chosen pieces can look better than another with a full jewelry box, and a simple heirloom ring can say more than a piece of fashion jewelry. The criterion is knowing which pieces go with each moment, how to combine them with each other and with the clothes, and what story you want to tell with them.

There are no absolute rules: personal style always rules. But there are principles of proportion, balance and occasion that help to get it right. In this guide you will see why it is advisable to opt for a few quality pieces, how to think about proportion, how to mix metals and layer layers, what is true in the rules about skin tone, what jewelry is appropriate depending on the occasion, how to combine them with necklines and why pieces with meaning have a special place.

## Fewer pieces, better chosen

A well-constructed jewelry box usually has a base of versatile pieces that go with almost everything, plus a few special pieces:

- **Small gold, silver or pearl earrings** for everyday wear.
- **A thin chain** with or without a pendant.
- **A ring** that is always worn, often with sentimental value.
- **A bracelet or watch** for everyday use.
- **A protagonist piece,** such as large earrings, a statement necklace or a cocktail ring, for special occasions.

Investing in a few good quality pieces, in metals that last, is more satisfying and sustainable in the long term than accumulating jewelry that deteriorates.

## Proportion and scale

| Situation | Useful idea |
|---|---|
| small hands | delicately proportioned rings or a single protagonist piece |
| big hands | rings with more presence, that are not lost |
| short collar | Longer or V-shaped necklaces visually lengthen |
| small face | moderately sized earrings that don't overpower you |
| Printed or highly detailed clothing | simpler jewelry |
| plain clothes | accepts more striking jewelry |

They are guidelines, not rules: the important thing is that the jewelry looks integrated and does not compete with everything else.

## Mix metals

For years it was said that gold and silver should not be combined. Today it is a common and elegant practice, if done with intention:

- **Repeat each metal** at least twice to make the mix appear deliberate.
- **Use a bridge piece** like a ring or a watch that combines both metals.
- **Keep a consistent style:** Similar shapes and thicknesses help everything fit together.

We explain this in more detail in the guide on [gold, silver and platinum](/es/oro-plata-y-platino/).

## Overlay necklaces, rings and bracelets

- **Necklaces:** combine different lengths, with several centimeters of distance between each one, so that they do not get tangled or covered. Mix a thin chain with another with a pendant.
- **Rings:** distribute the pieces between several fingers or combine a main ring with thin hoops. Leave some fingers free so that the hand can breathe.
- **Bracelets:** Mix textures, such as a chain, a rigid hoop, and a watch, instead of identical pieces.

When in doubt, remove a piece: excess detracts from elegance.

## The watch is also a jewel

A good watch is as much a part of the outfit as a bracelet. So that it coexists well with other pieces:

- **Coordinate the metal** of the case and strap with that of your jewelry, or use it as a bridge piece if you mix metals.
- **Separate the watch from rigid bracelets,** which can scratch the glass or case; put them on the other wrist or between soft bracelets.
- **Adjust the size:** a very large case on a slim wrist can unbalance the set.

## Build your jewelry box little by little

Instead of buying on impulse, think about what your jewelry box is missing: perhaps a pair of earrings for the evening, a longer chain, or a ring that matches your favorite pieces. Buying with intention, prioritizing quality and personal style over fads, results in a set of jewelry that is truly worn and that, over time, tells your story.

## Skin tone: fewer rules than what is said

It is common to hear that warm undertone skin looks better with yellow gold and cold undertone skin looks better with silver or platinum. It can serve as a starting point, but it is not a law. Rose gold flatters many skin tones, and the most important thing is how you feel in the piece and how it matches your clothing and other jewelry. Try both options in front of a mirror with natural light before deciding.

## Jewelry according to the occasion

| Occasion | General recommendation |
|---|---|
| day by day | small, comfortable and resistant pieces |
| Work | discreet pieces that do not make noise or snag |
| Dinner or celebration | a statement piece, such as statement earrings or a necklace |
| Wedding as a guest | elegance without overshadowing the couple or competing with their outfit |
| religious ceremonies | sober pieces, respecting the customs of the place |
| Trip | the minimum, pieces that you don't worry about losing |
| Sport | none, or only a suitable watch |

## at work

In professional environments, it is advisable that the jewelry accompany without distracting: small or medium-sized earrings, a discreet chain, a watch and one or two rings. Avoid bracelets that rattle when you type on the keyboard or rings that get caught on clothing or documents. In manual, laboratory or machinery work, it is safest not to wear jewelry on your hands.

## Weddings and celebrations

At weddings, the couple is the protagonist. As a guest, choose elegant but not ostentatious jewelry, coordinated with your clothing. If you are part of a couple, one or two special pieces, perhaps with family significance, tend to shine more than many. In religious ceremonies, sobriety is always a success.

## Jewelry and necklines

| Neckline | Necklace that usually flatters |
|---|---|
| Round or closed | short necklaces above the neckline or long ones outside the garment |
| V | pendants that follow the shape of the V |
| Boat | short necklaces or protagonist earrings without necklace |
| word of honor | chokers or necklaces at the height of the collarbones |
| High collar | long necklaces over the garment or just earrings |

## Jewelry with meaning

In Latin America, many jewels accompany important moments in life: the baptism medal or bracelet, early childhood earrings, the religious medal, the graduation ring, the fifteenth birthday gifts, the engagement ring and wedding rings, or the pieces that are passed from one generation to the next. These jewels are not chosen just for aesthetics: they carry stories. Combining them with current pieces is a beautiful way to bring the past into the present. We see it in the guide on [inherited and second-hand jewelry](/es/joyas-heredadas-y-de-segunda-mano/).

## Discretion on the street

In some cities and situations, it is advisable to be discreet with valuable jewelry: on public transportation, in busy areas, or when traveling. A chain inside your clothes, a ring turned towards the palm or leaving the most valuable pieces at home avoid unnecessary risks. Also check that your insurance covers jewelry outside the home, as we explain in the guide on [jewelry insurance and appraisal](/es/seguro-y-tasacion-de-joyas/).

## Take care of what you wear

Jewelry that is worn daily suffers more: bumps, friction, creams and sweat. Put your jewelry on after applying perfume and makeup, take it off for sports or household chores, and store it separately. More tips in the guide on [jewelry maintenance and cleaning](/es/mantenimiento-y-limpieza-de-joyas/).

## Frequent errors

- **Put on all the jewelry at once** without a balance point.
- **Choose pieces that compete** with very busy clothing.
- **Follow rigid rules** of untested metals or skin tones.
- **Wear valuable jewelry** in risky situations.
- **Wear rings or bracelets** in manual work or sports.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can gold and silver be combined?

Yes. To make it look intentional, repeat each metal at least twice or use a piece that combines both.

### How many rings is too many?

There is no exact number, but leaving some fingers free and combining fine pieces with a protagonist usually gives a balanced result.

### What jewelry is essential?

Simple earrings, a thin chain, an everyday ring and a statement piece cover almost every occasion.

### Are big jewelry only for the night?

Not necessarily. A large piece can look great during the day with simple clothes, as long as it is the only protagonist.

## In summary

Wearing jewelry wisely means choosing a few good, versatile pieces, thinking about proportion to your body and your clothes, mixing metals and layering with intention, and not getting hung up on rigid rules about skin tone. Adapt the jewelry to each occasion, from the office to a wedding, combine the necklaces with the neckline and give a special place to pieces with history. Be discreet with valuable jewelry on the street and take care of it so that it will accompany you for years.
