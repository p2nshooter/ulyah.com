The wedding ring is, for many people, the jewel they wear the most years. It is used every day, at work, at home, on vacation, for decades. Therefore, more than a matter of fashion, choosing it is a practical decision: it has to be comfortable, withstand wear, combine with the engagement ring if there is one and be liked in twenty years as much as on the wedding day.

In this guide you will see what profiles and widths exist, what metal is best for your lifestyle, what finishes age better, how to choose an engraving, what to take into account in wedding rings with stones and how to combine them with the engagement ring without them wearing down each other.

## Alliance, ring, hoop: the names

Depending on the country, the alliance is called **ring**, **wedding ring**, **wedding ring** or simply **wedding ring**. In some families it is given only in the ceremony; In others it is carried from the engagement in one hand and changed to the other on the wedding day. The custom of which hand and which finger it is carried on varies between countries and between families, so it is a good idea to discuss it before ordering them. If you want to review the customs of engagement, the guide on [engagement rings](/es/anillos-de-compromiso/) summarizes them.

## The profile: what the ring is like inside and out

The profile is the shape of the ring in section. Change both the look and the comfort.

| Profile | How is it | What is good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Plane | flat exterior, flat interior | modern and sober; the edges are more noticeable |
| Half round | rounded exterior, flat interior | the classic; comfortable and timeless |
| Comfort | rounded interior | glides better over the knuckle and feels lighter; may require a different size |
| Flat with comfort interior | flat exterior, rounded interior | modern look with comfort |
| Beveled | angled cut edges | gives shine and a finer line |
| Bombé or domed | very rounded exterior | presence and volume |

If in doubt, try both profiles in the store: many people find that the comfort is much more comfortable, especially in wide wedding rings.

## The width

The width is measured in millimeters. As a general reference:

- **2 to 3 mm:** thin and discreet; They go well with an engagement ring.
- **4 to 5 mm:** the most common width; good balance between presence and comfort.
- **6 to 8 mm or more:** wide and with character; They are more noticeable on the finger and may need half a size larger.

Some guidelines for choosing:

- on **short fingers**, thin wedding rings visually lengthen the hand;
- in **large hands**, a very fine alliance can be lost;
- If it is to be worn with an engagement ring, it is advisable that the width of the alliance be in proportion to the ring ring.

## The metal

The metal decides the color, weight, wear, and repairability of the wedding band in the future. The guide to [gold, silver and platinum](/es/oro-plata-y-platino/) explains the laws and brands in detail; Here are the keys for alliances:

- **Yellow gold:** the classic. The 18 carat has a more intense color; The 14 is somewhat harder and more resistant to friction.
- **White gold:** It usually has a rhodium plating that gives it a brilliant white and wears over the years; It can be bathed again in the workshop.
- **Rose gold:** warm and flattering; Its color comes from the copper of the alloy.
- **Platinum:** dense, heavy and very durable. It does not lose metal with impact: it moves, and over time it acquires a matte patina that some appreciate and that can be polished again.
- **Palladium:** from the platinum family, lighter; less common in jewelry stores in the region.
- **Silver:** beautiful and economical, but soft for decades of use.
- **Titanium, steel, tungsten and others:** Strong and inexpensive, but many **cannot be made larger or smaller**, and some cannot be easily cut in an emergency. It is good to know before choosing them.

For couples who want matching wedding rings in different colors, a common solution is to choose the same design in two different golds.

## The finishes

- **Bright polished:** the classic; The scratches from use are noticeable, but it can be polished again.
- **Satin or matte:** elegant and modern; Over time, the rubbing areas become shiny and the finish must be repeated.
- **Hammered:** small marks that hide the scratches very well.
- **Engraved or diamond textures:** sparkles produced by cuts in the metal.
- **Combinations:** polished and matte stripes, or two golds in the same alliance.

If the couple works with their hands, a hammered or textured finish ages better than a mirror polished one.

## The engraving

The interior engraving is a classic: the wedding date, names, initials or a short phrase. Some tips:

- **Less is more:** Interior space is limited, especially in fine wedding rings.
- **Check spelling** and dates before confirming the order.
- Ask if the engraving is **hand, mechanical or laser**: it changes the look and depth.
- Please note that if the size is changed one day, the engraving may be affected.

There are also exterior engravings, fingerprints or small personalized pieces. The guide on [bespoke jewellery](/es/joyas-a-medida/) explains how to order your own design.

## Alliances with stones

Wedding rings with diamonds or other gems are increasingly common:

- **Half ring:** stones in the upper half of the ring. Comfortable and easy to adjust size.
- **Eternity ring:** stones around the entire hoop. Very shiny, but almost impossible to change size and more exposed to friction at the bottom.
- **A single stone or a few stones** set flush with the metal: discreet and resistant.

The setting matters a lot in a piece of jewelry for daily use. **flush** or **rail** settings protect stones better than pavé or high prongs. We explain it in the guide on [types of crimping](/es/tipos-de-engaste/).

## How to combine it with the engagement ring

If the alliance is going to be worn together with the engagement ring, they must be thought of as a set.

1. **The height of the setting:** If the ring stone is set too low, the straight band may not stick together. There are rings designed so that the wedding ring rests under the stone.
2. **Contoured rings:** have a curve that adapts to the shape of the engagement ring. They look perfect together, but they look strange alone.
3. **The same metal:** Bringing two metals of different hardness into contact causes the harder one to wear down the softer one over the years. If they are combined, they should be of similar hardness.
4. **The same width or one provided**, so that the whole looks harmonious.
5. **The order:** in many families, the wedding ring is closer to the hand and the engagement ring is on the outside. It's a custom, not a rule.

The ideal is to try the two rings together before deciding.

## The size

Wedding rings are worn for decades, and fingers change with age, weather, pregnancy or weight. Some tips:

- measure the size at the end of the day, with a normal temperature;
- Remember that a wide wedding band or a comfort profile may require a different size than usual;
- Choose a metal that allows the size to be adjusted in the future.

The guide on [how to measure ring size](/es/medir-la-talla-de-anillo/) explains how to do it accurately.

## Budget and documents

- Decide beforehand if the two alliances will be the same or different: the budget changes a lot.
- Ask for an invoice with the **metal, grade, weight, width and stones** if any.
- Ask about the **warranty**, the **size adjustments** included and the subsequent **maintenance**.
- Secure them, especially if they have stones. The guide to [jewelry insurance and appraisal](/es/seguro-y-tasacion-de-joyas/) explains how to document your jewelry.

## Care

- Take it off for heavy manual work, gym, gardening or strong chemicals;
- clean it with warm water and mild soap;
- check stones and settings once a year;
- polish it again or bathe it in rhodium when needed.

More details in the [jewelry maintenance and cleaning] guide (/es/mantenimiento-y-limpieza-de-joyas/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do the two alliances have to be the same?

No. Many couples choose the same design in different widths or colors, and others choose completely different wedding rings.

### Which profile is more comfortable?

For most people, the comfort profile, with the rounded interior. But it's worth trying it, because it changes the feeling of the size.

### Can I change the size of a titanium or tungsten wedding ring?

In general no, or with great difficulty. If you anticipate changes in size, choose gold or platinum.

More guides on metals, settings and design in the [Jewelry and design] section (/es/categoria/joyeria-y-diseno/).

## In summary

A good alliance combines comfort, resistance and a style that lasts. It is advisable to choose the profile, flat, half-round or comfort, trying it on your finger; a width proportionate to the hand and the engagement ring; a metal that can be repaired and adjusted over the years; a finish that ages well; and a short, error-free engraving. If the wedding ring has stones, a flush or rail setting is better, and if it is worn together with the engagement ring, both should be thought of as a set, made of the same metal or of similar hardness, so that they do not wear down each other.
