You can reach the QURM editorial team by email at **[kontakt@qurm.de](mailto:kontakt@qurm.de)**. We read every message and usually respond within a few days. In the main hiking season between May and October it may take a little longer.

## What we can help you with

- **Error in the text:** A stage length is no longer correct, a path has been changed, a hut has changed operator? Write to us on which page you found the error and where your information comes from. We check the note and correct the guide.
- **Closures and danger spots:** If you notice a closure, a fallen tree across the path or a damaged bridge along the way, please report it first to the responsible hiking club, municipality or national park. You are welcome to inform us additionally so that we can add a note.
- **Topic requests:** Are you missing a long-distance hiking trail, a region or a question about equipment? We collect all suggestions and incorporate them into our planning.
- **General questions:** We are happy to answer questions about our content as long as they go beyond a guide, but not beyond what we can reliably say from a distance.

## What we can't do

We cannot give personal tour advice for a specific hike and cannot make any statements as to whether a particular path will be accessible tomorrow. For current conditions, contact the region's tourist information office, the hut landlords, the national park administration or, in the Alps, the Alpine associations' advice centers.

**In an emergency, do not write an email, but dial 112.** The number works throughout Germany and the EU, even without credit. The mountain rescue service is also alerted in the mountains. Find out more on our page [Safe on the Road](/sicher-unterwegs/).

## Advertising and collaborations

We reject inquiries about paid contributions, product tests for payment or link exchanges. QURM is financed exclusively through automatically displayed advertisements, which do not influence our content.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your email address and details to respond to your message. We do not pass them on and delete them as soon as they are no longer needed. Please see our [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/) for details. The provider information can be found in [Imprint](/impressum/).
