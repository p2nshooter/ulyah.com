QURM is a project by **p2nshooter**. The name stands for four things that make a good hike: **springs, jungle, backpack, dawn**. The source where you refill your bottle. The old forest through which the path leads. The backpack that contains everything you need for the day. And the dawn when you set off because the early hours are the quietest and usually the safest.

## What you will find with us

We write about hiking in Germany, and only about it. For us it's not about city trips or beach holidays, but about paths: the large long-distance hiking trails such as Rennsteig, Malerweg, Westweg, Rheinsteig, Eifelsteig and Harzer Hexenstieg, about day tours in the Alps and in the low mountain ranges, about equipment, weather and safety, about planning and navigation and about hiking with children and dogs.

Our topics are divided into six “paths”, which you can find as a guide in the menu at the top. Each guide is intended to answer a specific question: How long is a stage really? Which shoes do I need for which surface? What do I do if a thunderstorm approaches? How do I get a toddler to enjoy running?

## How we work

We describe paths the way they feel, not the way a brochure sells them. If a path is steep, crowded or slippery when wet, we write it down. When it comes to lengths, stages and heights, we adhere to the information provided by the path operators, hiking clubs and national parks. Where this information fluctuates, for example because routes have been rerouted, we give orders of magnitude instead of seemingly precise numbers.

For us, safety takes priority over summit happiness. That's why you'll find advice in many guides to turn back if the weather, strength or time isn't enough. We have collected the most important rules on the [Safe on the Road](/sicher-unterwegs/) page.

## What QURM is not

We are not a mountain school and are not a substitute for training, a first aid course or a guided tour. We cannot check every route on a daily basis. You can find out about closures after storms, forest fires or rock falls from the areas themselves, for example from national park administrations, tourist information centers and hiking clubs.

## Advertising

QURM is financed through ads from Google AdSense. The ads have no influence on our content. We are not paid to recommend specific products, trails or accommodations. How we deal with sources, errors and updates is described in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).
