Out on the trail, all that matters is what you have in your backpack and what you know about the weather, terrain and your own strength. QURM talks about this. Things look different at home before and after the tour: Then you sit at the screen, collect stages, sort photos, write reports for the club. For these hours we will introduce you to two projects that belong to the same operator as QURM. This makes it clear: This site is advertising on its own behalf.

## After the tour: Art by AXTO

Back from the hut? [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=qurm.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a museum in which images wander, tick and glow: clocks in the local time of your summit, postage stamps from fantasy landscapes, Koran and Hadith calligraphy in the unchanged original text and animated characters. Each work is calculated from unique DNA and secured in the Base network.

The exclusive editions are available for the hiking club: ID **AC-**, 1 US dollar per edition, payment with AXTO coins, ten copies per motif and shipping to all members without a gas fee. The public collections (**OS-**) are on OpenSea with 1 to 10 editions and fixed prices from $1 to $250; **G** means GPU painted. The following applies to club funds: AXTO is a joint crypto investment starting at one dollar, blocking periods are compensated with unique items, exchange rate losses remain possible.

## For clubs, hiking guides and huts: XAA

Hiking clubs need a tour calendar, hiking guides need a booking option, huts and inns need a page with rooms, directions and opening times. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=qurm.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a web development studio from Europe and implements such projects:

- **Club website** with tour program, registration and member area,
- **Booking page** for guided tours with online payment,
- **Platform** for multiple providers in one region.

It is planned and paid in milestones so that you can see and accept each step. If you want to get online quickly, start with a [template](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=qurm.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). The XAA website is available in English, Spanish and Indonesian, and German messages are also responded to.
