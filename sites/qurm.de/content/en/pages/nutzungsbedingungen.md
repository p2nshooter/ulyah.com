## Personal responsibility when hiking

Hiking and mountaineering are activities with risks that can never be completely ruled out. You decide for yourself whether you want to go on a tour, depending on your experience, your fitness, your equipment, the weather and the current condition of the path. Our descriptions reflect the status at publication or last check. They may be obsolete due to closures, diversions, weather, forestry work or natural events.

Follow local signs, rules in national parks and nature reserves and instructions from mountain rescue services, foresters, rangers and authorities. They always take precedence over what is written in a guide.
