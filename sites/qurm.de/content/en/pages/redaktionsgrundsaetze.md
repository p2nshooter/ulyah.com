QURM is intended to be a hiking guide you can trust. These principles describe how our texts are created and what their limits are.

## We write for people who want to get going

Every guide begins with a question that really arises when hiking: How many days do I need to hike the Rennsteig? Which backpack is suitable for a hut tour? What do I do if my child doesn't want to go anymore after an hour? We write in such a way that you can understand the answer without any specialist knowledge and apply it directly. Summaries at the beginning, tables and lists should help you find the most important things quickly.

## Sources and numbers

For lengths, stages, heights and rules, we rely on the information provided by trail operators, hiking clubs, national park administrations, Alpine clubs, the German Weather Service and specialist authorities such as the Robert Koch Institute. Where this information fluctuates, for example because a route has been rerouted or because different sources measure differently, we call rounded values ​​or ranges. We don't invent numbers just to make a text appear more precise. If we don't know something for sure, we write it down.

## Safety from summit happiness

We are consciously cautious when it comes to security issues. We would rather recommend an easier tour, an earlier start or turning back than downplaying a risk. We describe difficult paths as difficult, even if they are popular.

## Independence

QURM is financed through automatically displayed advertisements from Google AdSense. We do not accept money for mentions, recommendations or reviews, and advertisers have no influence on our writing. When we describe equipment, we mention features and criteria instead of brands.

## Nature and consideration

We only recommend paths that are officially accessible. We point out protected areas, trail regulations, breeding and nesting times and the ban on wild camping where applicable. Anyone who hikes is a guest in nature and in the meadows and pastures of the people who work there.

## Updates and corrections

We regularly review our guides and adapt them if paths, rules or recommendations change. The date in the summit book next to each guide shows the status. If you find an error, write to us at [kontakt@qurm.de](mailto:kontakt@qurm.de). We check every tip and correct errors quickly and without further ado.
