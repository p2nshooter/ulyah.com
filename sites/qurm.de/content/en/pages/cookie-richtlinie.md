This policy explains how qurm.de uses cookies and similar technologies such as your browser's memory. It supplements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in the browser. Similar technologies include the browser's local storage or session memory and so-called pixels. Some are technically necessary, others are used for advertising or statistics. According to § 25 TDDDG, non-essential information may only be stored or read on your device with your consent.

## Technically necessary storage

QURM itself does not set cookies to recognize you. We only store in your browser's session memory whether the short pennant animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown, so that it does not appear again every time the page is accessed. This information does not leave your browser and is deleted as soon as you close the tab or window. Our visitor counting works without cookies and without identification.

## Advertising cookies from Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to deliver advertisements. Google may set cookies to serve ads, limit their frequency, detect fraud and, if you agree, to tailor ads to your interests. Personalized advertising only takes place with your consent via the consent banner. If you decline, non-personalized ads may continue to appear for which only technically necessary data is used.

## Manage consent

During your first visit, you can select which purposes you agree to in the consent banner. You can change or revoke your selection at any time using the privacy settings link in the banner. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers also offer a private mode in which saved data is deleted when closed.
