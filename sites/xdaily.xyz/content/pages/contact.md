Curiosity is the reason this site exists, so your questions matter to us. Write to:

**[hello@xdaily.xyz](mailto:hello@xdaily.xyz)**

## What helps us most

- **Corrections:** Name the explainer and the sentence you think is wrong. A link to a reliable source helps us check quickly.
- **Questions:** If an explanation left you puzzled, tell us where you got lost. Often a second example is all that is missing.
- **Ideas:** Which everyday mystery or big scientific idea would you like explained next?

## What we cannot do

We cannot give medical advice, interpret test results or recommend treatments; please talk to a doctor. We also cannot help with homework answers, though we are happy if an explainer helps you understand the topic.

We do not publish sponsored articles, paid product reviews or guest posts with advertising links, and we will not reply to such requests.

## Your privacy

We use your email address only to answer you. Please do not send personal health information. More in our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).
