A guide about cycling is only worth something if you can trust it. These principles apply to every text on VELNIXO.

## Independent and without purchased recommendations

We take no money for test reviews, no free products in exchange for mentions and no commissions for purchase links. When we compare bike types, components or equipment, we mention criteria instead of brand names so that you can decide for yourself what suits you. Advertising is delivered by Google AdSense and has no influence on our texts.

## Explain instead of impress

We write for people who ride bikes, not engineers. We explain technical terms the first time they appear. Each instruction lists the necessary tools, the steps in the correct order and typical errors.

## Safety is a priority

When it comes to brakes, steering, frame, wheels, battery and motor, we openly say when a specialist workshop is the better choice. We do not give any tips on how to manipulate the motor support or speed limits of Pedelecs. Such interventions are dangerous and legally problematic.

## Comprehensible sources

Legal information is based on the road traffic regulations, the StVZO and the publications of the responsible authorities. Technical values ​​are based on usual manufacturer information and standards. Where experts disagree, we say so.

## Honest boundaries

We do not invent test results, quotes or statistics. If a question doesn't have a general answer, such as what is the perfect frame size without a test drive, we'll explain how you can answer it for yourself.

## Review and corrections

We regularly revise our articles, especially when there are changes to the law and new technologies. Did you find an error? Write to us at [kontakt@velnixo.de](mailto:kontakt@velnixo.de). We check every reference and correct the text if it is incorrect or misleading.

## Own projects

We only present projects from the same publisher on the designated page [Digital Companions](/digitale-begleiter/), never hidden in a guide.
