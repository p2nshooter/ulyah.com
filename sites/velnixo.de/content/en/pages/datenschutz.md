This declaration informs you in accordance with Art. 13 GDPR about which data is processed when you visit velnixo.de. VELNIXO has no user account, no comment system, no newsletter and no contact form. Therefore, there is only a small amount of data and we want you to know exactly which one.

## Responsible person

The provider named in [Imprint](/impressum/) is responsible for data processing and can be reached at [kontakt@velnixo.de](mailto:kontakt@velnixo.de).

## Hosting and server protocols

The website is delivered via Cloudflare's network. When you access a page, technically necessary data is processed: IP address, date and time, address accessed, browser type and amount of data transferred. This serves to ensure the secure and stable provision of the pages and to defend against attacks (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). Cloudflare can also process data outside the EU; the transfer is based on appropriate guarantees such as EU standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Fonts

The fonts Brygada 1918 and Schibsted Grotesk are loaded from Google Fonts. Your IP address is transferred to Google so that the font files can be delivered (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR; our interest is a uniform, easily readable display on all devices).

## Range measurement

We count page views anonymously and without cookies. Only the name of the website and the path accessed are transmitted, no identifier that recognizes you and no profile across multiple visits.

## Advertising with Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense from Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure the effectiveness of ads. Personalized ads will only be displayed with your consent (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a GDPR, Section 25 Para. 1 TDDDG). We obtain consent via a consent banner certified by Google; you can revoke it at any time. For more information, see [How Google uses website data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Contact via email

If you write to us, we will process your information exclusively to answer your request (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b or f GDPR). We delete them as soon as they are no longer needed and there are no legal retention requirements. Please do not send us photos of people, license plates or frame numbers that you do not want to pass on.

## Your rights

You have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection as well as the right to revoke your consent at any time with future effect. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the federal state in which you live.

You can find out more about cookies in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-richtlinie/).
