VELNIXO is a guide for everyone who cycles in Germany: to work and school, to go shopping, out into the countryside at the weekend or with luggage over several days. The name stands for what it's all about: **Velo**, i.e. the bicycle, the **nature** through which it carries us, the **infrastructure** of cycle paths, parking facilities and train connections, the **X-routes** off the usual route and a bit of **order** in the basement so that the bike can still run reliably tomorrow.

## Who we write for

For people who want to understand their bike better without becoming a mechanic. For beginners who are about to make their first purchase and for everyday cyclists who have been riding for years but have never changed a chain themselves. For families with child seats and trailers, for commuters in winter and for everyone who is considering whether an e-bike can replace the car.

## What you find here

- **Buying advice:** Bicycle types, frame sizes, new and used purchases, honestly and without brand advertising.
- **Maintenance & Repair:** Step-by-step instructions with tool list and clear boundaries.
- **E-Bike & Pedelec:** Battery, motor, range and law, clearly explained.
- **Safe on the road:** Traffic rules, visibility, theft protection and smart driving.
- **Tours & cycling trips:** Planning, long-distance cycle paths and the combination of train and bike.
- **Equipment & Everyday Life:** Bags, clothing, children and loads.

## How we work

Each contribution is developed specifically for VELNIXO, checked and updated if necessary; We also use AI tools when researching and writing. We don't take any money for recommendations and we name criteria instead of brands. When it comes to safety-related work, we openly say when a specialist workshop is the better option. The details can be found in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## How VELNIXO finances itself

The site is free and is funded by ads from Google AdSense. The ads are labeled and have no influence on the content.
