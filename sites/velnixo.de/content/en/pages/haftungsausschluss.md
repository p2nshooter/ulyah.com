VELNIXO is designed to help you understand your bike better, do small jobs yourself and be safer on the road. Precisely because it is about your safety, we would like to clearly state the limits of our guides.

## General information, not individual advice

Our articles are general. They don't know your bike, your components, your body and your routes. Size recommendations, tire pressures, torques and maintenance intervals are guidelines. The manufacturer's information, which can be found on the components, in the operating instructions or on the manufacturer's website, is always decisive.

## Safety-related work

Brakes, headset, stem, handlebars, fork, seat post, wheels, frame as well as battery and motor of e-bikes are safety-relevant. A mistake can result in falls and serious injuries. Only work with suitable tools, with a torque wrench where a torque is specified and with a test drive in a quiet place. Never open an e-bike battery. If you are unsure about a step, take the bike to a specialist workshop.

## Law and traffic

Our information on road traffic regulations, the StVZO, Pedelecs and S-Pedelecs summarizes the legal situation in an understandable manner, but does not replace legal advice. Laws and fines change; The current legal texts and information from the responsible authorities are binding.

## Health

If you are cycling again after a long break, have health problems or are taking medication, talk to your doctor before going on longer tours. VELNIXO does not provide medical advice.
