## No guarantee for every bike

Bicycles, components and manufacturers vary greatly. Instructions that apply to derailleur gears do not necessarily fit a hub gear, and a torque for aluminum is not the right one for carbon. Read the manufacturer's instructions before doing any work. Work on the brakes, steering, fork, frame, battery and motor is safety-relevant; If you are unsure, have it done by a specialist workshop.
