VELNIXO explains bicycles. But those who ride bikes also plan tours, film rides, and perhaps run a small workshop or bike rental company. On this page we present three projects that belong to the same publisher as VELNIXO. So it's advertising for our own work, and we'd rather tell you that in the first sentence.

## AXTO: moving art on the internet

[AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=velnixo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) sees itself as a living museum. The exhibits include clocks that show the real time, stamps from fantasy worlds, calligraphy from the Koran and Hadith without any changes to the text, and moving figures. A mathematical process gives each work a unique DNA and is stored as an NFT in the Base network.

There are two collection types: Exclusive with the identifier **AC-** for $1 per edition, paid for with AXTO coins and sendable to multiple wallets as a gift without a gas fee, and Public with **OS-** on OpenSea, 1 to 10 editions per work at fixed prices from $1 to $250. A **G** indicates GPU painting. Please note: AXTO sees itself as part of a crypto community, starts at one dollar and rewards staking with originals - fluctuations are part of it.

## XAA: a website for your bike shop

Do you run a small workshop, a rental company or guided bike tours? [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=velnixo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European web development studio. It builds websites, online shops with appointment booking and customized customer portals. Projects are planned, delivered and paid for in stages, so you never pay for work you haven't seen yet. If you want to get started quickly, you will find ready-made [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=velnixo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem).

## ULYAH: a portal for Islamic knowledge

[ULYAH](https://ulyah.com/?utm_source=velnixo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a free portal with the Koran including translations and recitations, hadith collections, classic books and stories. For readers in German there is the sister portal [Tilawa](https://tilawa.de/?utm_source=velnixo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). We mention it here because it belongs to the same family of projects, not to convince anyone.
