We look forward to every message. The quickest way to reach us is by email:

**[kontakt@velnixo.de](mailto:kontakt@velnixo.de)**

## What you can write to us about

- **Error in a post:** Please tell us the title of the article and the location. We check every reference and correct the text if necessary.
- **Topic requests:** Which repair, purchase decision or tour should we explain next?
- **Questions about a guide:** We answer comprehension questions about our texts as best we can.

## What we can't do

We are not a workshop and cannot assess your bike from a distance. If there are noises in the brakes, steering or frame, if there are cracks, after falls or if you have problems with the battery and motor, please contact a specialist workshop or the dealer where you bought the bike. We do not offer legal advice, for example on accidents or insurance cases.

## No advertising requests

We do not publish paid articles or product tests for payment. We do not answer inquiries of this type.

## Data protection

We only use your email to reply to you. Find out more in our [Data Protection Policy](/datenschutz/).
