A bicycle that is regularly maintained rides easier, brakes more safely and costs less in the long run because worn parts do not wear out others. When something happens depends primarily on the kilometers. The maintenance planner converts the rough service life from our [annual maintenance plan](/ratgeber/fahrrad-wartung-jahresplan/) to your mileage.

## The rough lifespans

| part | rough lifespan | How do you recognize wear and tear? |
|---|---|---|
| chain | 2,000-5,000km | Measurement with chain gauge, jumping gears |
| Brake pads rim brake | 1,000-3,000km | Grooves in the surface disappeared |
| Disc brake pads | 1,000-4,000km | Cover thickness less than about 0.5 mm |
| tires | 3,000-8,000km | Puncture protection visible, cracks, no profile |
| Brake and shift cables | 1-3 years | Stiff levers, frayed ends |

The values apply to everyday bikes without a motor. In Pedelecs, the drive and brakes wear out more quickly due to the higher forces and weight; Rain, slush and road salt also shorten the distances. The planner shortens the margins in these cases.

## An example

If you drive 60 kilometers a week, you'll cover a good 3,000 kilometers a year. The chain should then be cleaned and oiled approximately every four weeks and measured with the chain gauge every three months; It is changed after the measurement, typically after around a year. The planner opens with these values.

## What the plan does not replace

- **Look before every ride:** Air pressure, brakes, lights. This takes a minute.
- **The measurement:** The kilometer data are ranges. Only the chain gauge, as described in the guide [Measuring chain wear] (/ratgeber/kettenverschleiss-messen-kette-wechseln/), shows whether the chain is really worn.
- **The workshop for safety parts:** Brakes that rub or whose levers go all the way to the handlebars, cracks on the frame, problems with the e-bike drive should be taken to a specialist workshop immediately.

Record the mileage with each change. After a year, you'll know how long the parts will last given your driving style and you can adjust the plan. The guides for [cleaning and oiling the chain](/ratgeber/fahrradkette-reinigen-und-oelen/) and [adjusting the brakes](/ratgeber/fahrradbremsen-einstellen/) show how the individual tasks are carried out.
