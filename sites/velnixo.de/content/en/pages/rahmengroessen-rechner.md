The frame size determines whether you sit relaxed and securely or whether you feel your back, neck and knees after an hour. Body size alone is not enough: people of the same height have legs of very different lengths. The stride length is more meaningful and, with a factor for each type of bike, this gives a good starting point.

## This is how you measure your stride length

1. Stand barefoot with your back against a wall and your feet about shoulder-width apart.
2. Clamp a book tightly between your legs with the spine facing up, just as you would a saddle.
3. Mark the top edge of the book on the wall.
4. Measure the distance from the mark to the ground. This is your stride length.

It is best to have a second person measure and repeat the measurement twice.

## The factors

| Type of bike | Formula | Unity |
|---|---|---|
| City and trekking bike | Stride length × 0.66 | centimeters |
| Road bike | Stride length × 0.665 | centimeters |
| Gravel bike | Stride length × 0.665 | centimeters |
| Mountain bike | Stride length × 0.226 | inches |
| Mountain bike (in cm) | Stride length × 0.574 | centimeters |

**Example:** With a stride length of 84 centimeters, this results in 84 × 0.66 = 55.4 centimeters for a trekking bike. A frame between 54 and 56 centimeters is a good starting point. For a mountain bike it is 84 × 0.226 = 19 inches, for many manufacturers size M or L. The calculator opens with these values.

## Why the formula is just a starting point

The factors are empirical values ​​for typical geometries. A sporty trekking bike is different than a comfortable model with a low entry, and manufacturers do not all measure the frame height the same. The manufacturer's size table is tailored to your own geometry and is therefore more accurate; If she calls the stride length, this takes precedence over height. If you are between two sizes, the upper body, arm length and desired posture decide.

## The test drive decides

- There should be a few centimeters of air left above the top tube.
- When driving, the arms are slightly bent, not straight.
- In the lowest pedal position, the knee is slightly bent if the saddle is adjusted correctly.
- Neck and wrists feel relaxed even after ten minutes.
