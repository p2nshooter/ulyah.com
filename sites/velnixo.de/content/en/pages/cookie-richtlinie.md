This policy explains how velnixo.de uses cookies and similar technologies such as your browser's memory. It supplements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies are the browser's local storage and session storage, or so-called pixels. Some of them are technically necessary, others are used for advertising or statistics.

## Technically necessary storage

VELNIXO itself does not set any cookies that recognize you. In your browser's session memory, we only remember whether the short celebration animation for Spain's 2026 World Cup title has already been shown and whether you have opened the menu on your smartphone. This information does not leave your browser and disappears as soon as you close the window.

## Anonymous counting of page views

For our statistics, when a page is accessed, we only transmit the name of the website and the path of the page. No cookie is set and no identifier is stored.

## Advertising cookies from Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to deliver advertisements. Google may set cookies to serve ads, limit their frequency, detect fraud and, if you agree, to tailor ads to your interests. Without consent, non-personalized advertisements may appear that are based on the topic of the page.

## Manage consent

On your first visit, you select which purposes you agree to in the consent banner. You can change or revoke your selection at any time using the link to the data protection settings. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings; All guides on VELNIXO then remain completely readable.
