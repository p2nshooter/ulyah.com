Our guides explain what is important when it comes to cycling. These four calculators apply what has been explained to your own bike: with your stride length, your battery, your kilometers and your gears. They run in your browser, without registration, and nothing you enter is sent to us. Only your browser remembers the last input so that you don't have to start from the beginning next time.

## Frame size calculator

Enter stride length, select bike type, and you will get the calculated frame height in centimeters, or in inches for a mountain bike, with a sensible range for the test ride. [To the frame size calculator](/rahmengroessen-rechner/)

## E-bike range calculator

Enter battery capacity, support level, terrain, weight and temperature: The calculator estimates the consumption per kilometer, the range with reserve and how many trips to work one charge can make. [To the range calculator](/e-bike-reichweitenrechner/)

## Maintenance planner

Your kilometers per week become a plan: when the chain is oiled and measured, when the chain, brake pads and tires are due, with shorter intervals for Pedelecs and rides in any weather. [To maintenance planner](/wartungsplaner/)

## Translation calculator

Enter the teeth of the chainring and sprocket, wheel size and cadence: You will see the development per crank revolution and the speed at 60 to 90 revolutions per minute. This allows you to compare gears, circuits and bandwidths. [To the translation calculator](/uebersetzungsrechner/)

## Where the numbers come from

| Calculator | Foundation | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| Frame size | Factors from our frame size guide | Stride length, bike type |
| Range | typical consumption values from the e-bike range guide | Watt-hours of battery, your conditions |
| Maintenance | rough service life from our annual maintenance plan | kilometers per week |
| Translation | Wheel circumference × chainring ÷ sprocket | Number of teeth, tire size |

A calculator provides an estimate: the manufacturer's size chart, your e-bike's display and the chain gauge have the final say. The following applies to brakes, steering and batteries: If in doubt, go to a specialist workshop. Error discovered? Write to us via the [contact page](/kontakt/).
