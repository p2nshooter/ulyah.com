SAVED publishes general information about running a household for less: cooking, energy use, repairs, shopping, budgeting and family life. Please read this disclaimer before acting on anything you read here.

## Not personal financial advice

Guides in the **Budget Basics** section and elsewhere explain how common money tools and ideas work — budgets, sinking funds, emergency savings, interest on debt. They do not take your personal circumstances into account and are not recommendations to buy, sell, borrow or invest. We do not offer investment, tax, pension, insurance or credit advice. If you are struggling with debt, contact a free, regulated debt advice service in your country or a qualified adviser before making decisions.

## Repairs and safety

Our repair guides describe simple jobs that many people do themselves, with the precautions we consider important. You are responsible for your own safety. Always switch off and isolate power or water before working, follow the manufacturer's instructions, use suitable protective equipment and stop if you are unsure. **Work on gas appliances, mains wiring, structural elements and anything covered by local regulations must be done by a qualified, registered professional.**

## Food safety

Kitchen guides follow widely accepted food-safety practice, but storage times and rules can differ. If you or someone you cook for is pregnant, very young, elderly, immunocompromised or has allergies, follow official guidance and medical advice.

## Prices and examples

Prices, tariffs and savings mentioned in guides are illustrative examples to show how a calculation works. Your actual costs and results will differ depending on where you live, your supplier, your home and your habits.
