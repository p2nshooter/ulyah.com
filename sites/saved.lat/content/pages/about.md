**SAVED** stands for *Smart, Affordable Value for Everyday Decisions*. It is a practical guide to running a household for less money and with less waste: what to cook this week, how to bring the heating bill down, how to fix a dripping tap, when to buy second-hand, how a simple budget works and how to give children a full life without spending a fortune.

## Why this site exists

Most money advice online falls into two camps. One promises that a single trick will make you rich. The other is so abstract that it never touches the actual bills on your kitchen table. SAVED sits between them. We write about the ordinary decisions that add up over a year: the weekly shop, the thermostat, the subscription you forgot, the jacket you could repair instead of replace. None of it is glamorous, and that is the point. Small, repeatable habits are what most households can actually control.

## What we cover

- **Kitchen & Groceries** — meal planning, pantry staples, batch cooking, food storage and cutting waste.
- **Home & Energy** — heating, cooling, lighting, water and laundry habits that lower bills.
- **Repair & Reuse** — basic tools, mending, simple plumbing and making appliances last.
- **Smart Shopping** — second-hand buying, sales seasons, warranties, subscriptions and scams.
- **Budget Basics** — budgets, sinking funds, emergency savings, paperwork and how interest works.
- **Family & Leisure** — activities, parties, pets, gifts, school costs and affordable holidays.

## How we write

Every guide on SAVED answers one practical household question, and lists the sources it relies on at the end. Four free calculators live on the [Tools](/tools/) page, and every new guide and update is listed with its date on [What's new](/whats-new/). Guides are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools and checked against reliable public sources such as government consumer and energy agencies, manufacturers' instructions and recognised safety guidance before they appear. We describe methods and trade-offs rather than promising results, because every household, country and tariff is different. When a task carries real risk — gas, mains electricity, structural work, debt problems — we say so plainly and point you to a qualified professional.

We do not accept payment to recommend products, and we do not write sponsored reviews. Advertising on the site is shown by Google AdSense and is clearly separate from our editorial content. Read more in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Who we are

SAVED is an independent publication. We are not financial advisers, lawyers or engineers, and the site is not a substitute for personal advice from one. What we offer is clear, honest, general information that helps you ask better questions and make your own decisions.
