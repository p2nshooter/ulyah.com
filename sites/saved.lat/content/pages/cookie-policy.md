Cookies are small text files that a website or a third party stores in your browser. This page explains which cookies may be used when you read SAVED and how you can control them.

## Cookies set by SAVED

SAVED itself does not set cookies. Our page statistics are counted without cookies and without identifying you, and the site does not have logins, shopping carts or comment forms that would need them.

## Third-party cookies

**Google AdSense.** Advertising on SAVED is served by Google. Google and its advertising partners may set cookies or use similar technologies (such as device identifiers and local storage) to:

- show ads and limit how often you see the same ad;
- measure whether ads were seen and clicked;
- personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites, where you have allowed it;
- detect fraud and protect advertisers and publishers.

In regions where consent is required, Google's consent message asks for your choice before these cookies are used for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy or cookie link shown by that message, from [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or through the industry opt-out at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/).

**Google Fonts and Cloudflare.** Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the site is delivered through Cloudflare. These services receive technical data such as your IP address to deliver files, but they are not used by us for tracking.

## Controlling cookies in your browser

Every modern browser lets you see, block and delete cookies. Look for "Privacy" or "Cookies" in your browser's settings. Blocking third-party cookies will not stop SAVED from working; you will simply see less personalised advertising.

## Questions

If you have questions about cookies on this site, write to **hello@saved.lat**. See also our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).
