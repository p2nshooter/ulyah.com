The calendar above shows twelve vegetables when they should be grown on the windowsill, sown directly into the bed or planted out. The current month is highlighted. The table below gives the periods in more detail.

## Sowing and planting times

| vegetables | Prefer | Direct seeding | Plants |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomatoes | Mid-March to early April | – | after the Ice Saints |
| Paprika | February to March | – | End of May |
| Zucchini, pumpkin | Mid to late April | Mid to late May | after the Ice Saints |
| French beans | – | Mid-May to early July | – |
| Peas | – | March to May | – |
| carrots | – | March to June | – |
| Beetroot | – | April to June | – |
| Salad | February to July | March to August | April to August |
| Kohlrabi | February to July | April to July | April to August |
| Kale | May to June | – | June to July |
| Lamb's lettuce | – | Mid-August to September | – |
| Spinach | – | March to April, August to September | – |

## Nature as a calendar

| Natural event | Phenological season | Appropriate gardening |
|---|---|---|
| Hazel is blooming | Early spring | Prefer peppers and celery |
| Forsythia blooms | First spring | Sow peas, spinach, radishes, carrots, plant onions |
| Apple blooms | Full spring | Lay potatoes, plant cabbage and lettuce |
| Elderberry is blooming | Early summer | Sow beans, heat-loving crops safely outside |
| Linden blossoms | Midsummer | Plant fall and winter vegetables |

Nature shows better than any date how far into the year it is in your area. If you sow lettuce, radishes or beans at intervals of two to four weeks, you will harvest the whole summer instead of everything at once.
