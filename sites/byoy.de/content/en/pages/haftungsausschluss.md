We write every instruction on BYOY with care and check information on planting dates, spacing, varieties and quantities from recognized sources. Nevertheless, a garden is a living system, and what works in one garden can turn out differently in the next.

## Guidelines, no guarantees

All dates, distances, fertilizer quantities and harvest quantities in our texts are guidelines for average conditions in Germany. They depend on your region, altitude, microclimate, soil type, weather of the year and the varieties chosen. There are easily three to four weeks between the wine-growing climate on the Upper Rhine and a garden in the Erzgebirge during the season. We assume no liability for crop failure, frost damage, disease infestation or other consequences resulting from the application of our advice.

## Plant protection

BYOY does not recommend chemical-synthetic pesticides. Where we mention home remedies, plant manure or beneficial insects, we describe common practices from organic gardening. Biological agents and home-made broths can also damage plants if they are used in too high doses or in full sun. Always test new products on a few leaves first. Please note that for approved plant protection products, including those that are permitted in organic farming, the information on the package and the Plant Protection Act apply.

## Diseases and pests

Our descriptions of diseases and pests do not replace professional diagnosis. If you have reportable or dangerous pathogens, such as suspected fire blight on pome fruit, contact your state's plant protection service.

## Health and safety

Gardening can lead to injuries, such as trimming trees on a ladder, handling sharp tools or lifting heavy loads. Some plants are poisonous or can cause allergies. Work carefully, wear appropriate protective clothing and seek medical advice if you have any health problems. Consuming plants is at your own risk.
