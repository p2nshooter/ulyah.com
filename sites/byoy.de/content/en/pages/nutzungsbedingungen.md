## Purpose of the website

BYOY is a free guide for organic vegetable and orchards in Germany. The instructions are intended to help you create beds, care for the soil, plan crops, grow fruit and berries, promote beneficial insects and collect seeds yourself. They are general information and not individual advice for your garden.

## Personal responsibility in the garden

Gardening is physical work with tools, ladders, water and occasionally plants that can irritate skin or mucous membranes. Please work carefully, use ladders that are stable, wear gloves and safety glasses when pruning fruit trees and keep children away from unfamiliar plants and fruits. Before eating wild-grown plants, always check with certainty what species they are.

If you use manure, compost or organic fertilizers, follow the usual hygiene rules: wash your hands, clean vegetables thoroughly before eating and do not put fresh manure directly on lettuce or root vegetables that will soon be harvested.
