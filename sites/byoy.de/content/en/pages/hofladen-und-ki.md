A lot of things in an organic garden take time: the soil only comes to life after years, the crop rotation needs to be carefully considered, and the knowledge about varieties, sowing dates and beneficial organisms grows season after season. We write about it on BYOY. Here, on a separate page, we show you two projects from the same company - with the honest note that they are self-promotion.

### For long winter evenings: AXTO

When there is nothing to do outside, it is worth a look at [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=byoy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem), a digital museum whose works move. On show are clocks in real local time, stamps from lands of fantasy, calligraphy from the Qur'an and hadith – reproduced faithfully – and living figures. No work resembles another, because each rests on a unique digital genome; they are secured on the Base network.

The exclusive editions (**AC-**) cost 1 US dollar each in AXTO coins and make good gifts for family or the garden club, even to many wallets at once. On OpenSea (**OS-**) AXTO sells 1 to 10 editions per work at fixed prices from 1 to 250 dollars; a **G** marks pictures by a GPU. Anyone can join the AXTO community coin from 1 dollar and receive unique 1/1 works by staking; like every crypto asset, its price moves both ways.

### For your farm shop or garden club: XAA

A farm shop with vegetable boxes, a solidarity farm with members, a community garden with bed sponsorships - they all need a place on the internet where you can find them. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=byoy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) develops such places as a European studio for websites and software:

- a clear website with harvest calendar and contact,
- a shop for subscription boxes, seeds or young plants,
- a member portal in which shares, appointments and work assignments are managed.

The work is divided into milestones and paid step by step, via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. If you want to start small, start with one of the [ready-made templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=byoy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). XAA is available in English, Spanish and Indonesian, but also accepts inquiries in German.

### Our promise

BYOY does not recommend paid products in its guides, and this page does not change that. If you prefer to work with a pencil and a garden calendar, that's completely fine - the soil won't notice any difference. But if digital helpers give you time for what's important, namely working outside, then these two projects are worth taking a look at.
