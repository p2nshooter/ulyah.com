Do you have a question about a guide, discovered an error or would you like a topic that is still missing from BYOY? Write to us. We read every message.

**Email:** [kontakt@byoy.de](mailto:kontakt@byoy.de)

## What we can help with

- **Notes on errors:** If a variety information, a planting date or a quantity information is incorrect, we would be grateful for any information. Please tell us the title of the instructions and, if possible, a source. We check the notice and correct the text if it is justified.
- **Topic requests:** Many of our instructions arose from questions that gardeners asked us. If you are missing a topic about vegetable gardening, compost, mixed cultivation, fruit, beneficial insects or seeds, let us know.
- **Experiences from your garden:** Do you have a variety that thrives particularly well in your region, or a method that works reliably for you? Such reports help us make instructions more practical.

## What we can't do

We cannot replace individual gardening advice on site and cannot guarantee remote diagnosis of plant diseases based on photos. If symptoms of fruit trees are unclear or if there are major problems, we recommend gardening advice from your Chamber of Agriculture, a gardening academy, the plant protection service in your federal state or specialist advice from the local allotment garden association. Many of these places offer telephone consultation hours.

Only a laboratory can reliably carry out soil tests for nutrients or pollutants. We explain how to take a sample in our instructions, but we do not analyze samples.

## Response time

We aim to respond within a few working days. In the spring, when everyone is sowing and planting at the same time, it can take a little longer. Please be patient; You learn that in the garden anyway.

## Advertising and collaborations

BYOY is financed through advertisements that Google AdSense automatically delivers. We do not sell any test reports or paid recommendations in our texts. We are still happy to answer inquiries about collaborations as long as they fit our [editorial principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message. Find out more in our [Data Protection Policy](/datenschutz/).
