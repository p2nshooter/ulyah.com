Mixed cultivation means planting different vegetables next to each other so that they share space well and make life more difficult for pests. The helper above shows who goes well together for 13 vegetables; the table below is the same for printing.

## Good and bad neighbors

| vegetables | Good neighbors | Unfavorable neighbors |
|---|---|---|
| Tomato | Lettuce, spinach, carrots, parsley, cabbage, marigold | Potato, fennel, pea |
| carrot | Onion, leek, garlic, lettuce, radish, pea | Dill in large quantities |
| Onion, garlic | Carrot, strawberry, beetroot, lettuce, tomato | Bean, pea, cabbage |
| French bean | Savory, cucumber, cabbage, lettuce, beetroot, potato | Onion, leek, garlic, peas, fennel |
| Pea | Carrot, radish, lettuce, kohlrabi, cucumber | Bean, onion family, tomato |
| Cabbage | Celery, lettuce, spinach, tomato, bean, beetroot | Strawberry, onion, mustard and other cruciferous vegetables |
| Cucumber | Dill, bean, lettuce, pea, onion | Potato, tomato |
| Salad | almost all of them, especially carrots, radishes, strawberries, tomatoes | Parsley, celery |
| Potato | French bean, kohlrabi, spinach, nasturtium | Tomato, pumpkin, sunflower |
| Leek | Carrot, celery, strawberry, tomato | bean, pea |
| Beetroot | Onion, cabbage, lettuce, French bean | Spinach, chard |
| strawberry | Garlic, onion, leek, lettuce, spinach | Cabbage |
| spinach | Cabbage, potato, tomato, strawberry, radish | Beetroot, chard |

## How reliable are such lists?

To be honest: less than many gardening books suggest. The Bavarian Garden Academy points out that mutual support is difficult to prove and that recommendations for good and bad couples sometimes contradict each other. However, three things are well documented: Plants need space for their final size, tall crops are not allowed to shade low ones, and vegetables from the same family don't belong in the same place every year. The mixed culture table is therefore a good starting point, not a law.
