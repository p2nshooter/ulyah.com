BYOY stands for “Organic, without poison: garden with heart”. There is a simple belief behind the name: A garden that produces vegetables, fruit and berries does not need chemical-synthetic sprays or artificial fertilizers from the bag. It needs living soil, clever planning, suitable varieties and people who look.

BYOY is a project by **p2nshooter**. The BYOY editorial team writes and maintains the texts.

## What you will find with us

We write for everyone who manages a kitchen garden in Germany or would like to create one: in the home garden, in the allotment, on the Grabeland or in the community garden. Our guides are organized into six topics:

- **Vegetable garden:** Create beds and raised beds, potatoes, tomatoes, zucchini, beans, onions and garlic outdoors.
- **Compost & Soil:** Add compost, identify soil type, mulch, green manure and organic fertilizer.
- **Mixed culture & crop rotation:** good neighbors, heavy and weak feeders, cultivation plans for small areas.
- **Fruit & Berries:** Planting and cutting fruit trees, berry bushes and strawberries.
- **Natural garden & beneficial insects:** Promote beneficial insects, deal with snails and fungal diseases without poison, create habitats.
- **Seeds & young plants:** seed-resistant seeds, growing your own seeds, direct sowing, cold frames and hardening off.

What you won't find with us: Guides to house plants, balcony flowers or herbs on the windowsill. We stay outside in the beds, by the compost and under the fruit trees.

## How we work

Each instruction is written individually, not assembled from building blocks. We also use AI tools when researching and writing. In terms of content, we rely on recognized gardening knowledge and recommendations from specialist bodies such as the Bavarian Garden Academy, the Federal Environment Agency, the Julius Kühn Institute and NABU; We list the most important sources under each guide. You can find four planners for mixed culture, crop rotation, sowing and bed size under [Planner](/planer/). We base times on the Central European climate and say when there are big differences between the Upper Rhine, the coast and the low mountain ranges.

We do not recommend chemical-synthetic pesticides. If a problem cannot be completely solved without poison, we say so honestly instead of promising miracle cures. How we select, check and correct topics is described in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## Our picture: the vineyard of Baal-Hamon

BYOY's design draws on an ancient image: the vineyard of Baal-Hamon from the Song of Songs, which its owner leased to guardians to tend it and harvest its fruits. We like this attitude. A garden is never entirely ours; we look after it for a while, improving the soil and passing it on richer than we received it.
