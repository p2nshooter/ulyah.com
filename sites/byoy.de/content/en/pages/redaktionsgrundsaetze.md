BYOY is intended to be a guide you can trust in the garden. These principles explain how our instructions are created and what we adhere to.

## Our topic area

We write about the organic outdoor kitchen garden: vegetable beds and raised beds, compost and soil, mixed culture and crop rotation, fruit trees and berry bushes, beneficial insects and natural garden elements as well as seeds and young plants. Houseplants, balcony flowers and herbs on the windowsill are not part of our topic.

## Gardening without poison

We do not recommend any chemical-synthetic pesticides or mineral fertilizers. Our approach follows the principles of organic farming: first prevent through healthy soil, site-appropriate and resilient varieties, crop rotation and mixed culture; then promote beneficial insects; then mechanical measures such as netting, collecting or pruning. If this does not completely solve a problem, we will say so openly.

## How instructions are created

1. **Select topic:** We address questions that gardeners really have, in the rhythm of the gardening year.
2. **Research:** We rely on specialist literature, publications from chambers of agriculture, gardening academies, universities and organic farming associations as well as practical experience.
3. **Writing:** Each instruction is written individually. We name specific months, intervals, quantities and varieties, but also say where these depend on the region and weather.
4. **Check:** Figures and dates are checked against at least one other source before publication. Where experts disagree, we present the positions rather than passing off an opinion as fact.

## Variety information

When we mention varieties, these are examples that are widely available in Germany and have proven themselves. Varieties disappear from the market and new ones appear. A mention is not advertising; we don't receive any money for this.

## Independence

BYOY is financed through automatically delivered ads from Google AdSense. Advertisers have no influence on our texts. We don't publish paid posts disguised as instructions.

## Bugs and updates

We revise instructions if recommendations change or if readers point out errors to us. The "As of" date in the sidebar of each guide shows the last revision. You can send information to [kontakt@byoy.de](mailto:kontakt@byoy.de).

## language

We write in a comprehensible manner and use the first name of our readers because people also use the first name at the garden fence. We explain technical terms the first time they appear.
