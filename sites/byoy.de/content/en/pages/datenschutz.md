Protecting your data is important to us. This declaration informs you in accordance with Art. 13 GDPR about which data is processed when you visit byoy.de. BYOY has no user account, no comment system and no contact form; therefore only a small amount of data is generated.

## Responsible person

The provider named in [Imprint](/impressum/) is responsible for data processing and can be reached at [kontakt@byoy.de](mailto:kontakt@byoy.de).

## Hosting and server protocols

The website is delivered via Cloudflare's network. When you access a page, technically necessary data is processed, such as IP address, date and time, address accessed, browser type and amount of data transferred. This serves to ensure the secure and stable provision of the website and to defend against attacks (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). Cloudflare can also process data in third countries; The transfer takes place on the basis of suitable guarantees such as EU standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Fonts

The Alegreya and Alegreya Sans fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your IP address is transferred to Google so that the font files can be delivered (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR; our interest is a uniform, easy-to-read presentation).

## Range measurement

We count page views anonymously and without cookies: only the name of the website and the path accessed are transmitted, no identifier that recognizes you and no profile over multiple visits.

## Advertising with Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense from Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure the effectiveness of ads. Personalized ads will only be displayed with your consent (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a GDPR, Section 25 Para. 1 TDDDG). We obtain consent via a consent banner certified by Google; you can revoke it at any time. Further information: [How Google uses data from websites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Contact via email

If you write to us, we will process your information to answer the request (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b or f GDPR) and delete it as soon as it is no longer needed and there are no legal retention obligations.

## Your rights

You have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection as well as the right to revoke your consent at any time with future effect. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the federal state in which you live.

You can find out more about cookies in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-richtlinie/).
