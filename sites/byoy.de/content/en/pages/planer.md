Our gardening guides explain why a bed works. These four planners help you plan at the kitchen table: What fits next to each other, how much compost does which bed need, what is sown when, and how much space does a household need? All four run directly in your browser, without an account, and nothing is sent to us.

## Mixed culture neighbors

Good and unfavorable neighbors for 13 vegetables, from tomatoes to spinach, with a pair check for two vegetables and an honest indication of how well documented such lists are. [To the Neighbors](/mischkultur-nachbarn/)

## Nutrient and crop rotation helper

Whether a vegetable is a heavy, medium or weak feeder, how much compost your bed gets per year, which plant family it belongs to and how long it should not be in the same place. [To the crop rotation helper](/fruchtfolge-helfer/)

## Vegetable sowing calendar

Prefer, direct sow and plant twelve vegetables at a glance, with the current month highlighted and a list of what's on. [To the sowing calendar](/gemuese-aussaatkalender/)

## Bed planner

How many tomatoes, zucchini, beans or carrots a household needs for fresh consumption and how much area that gives. [To the bed planner](/beetplaner/)

## Where the information comes from

All values come from our guides on mixed culture, feeders, crop rotation, sowing and bed planning, which are based on the Bavarian Garden Academy's guide to organic gardening, the Federal Environment Agency and NABU. Garden means region, soil and weather: the planners give guidelines, not guarantees. You can find the monthly overview for all gardening work in the [Garden Calendar](/gartenkalender/).
