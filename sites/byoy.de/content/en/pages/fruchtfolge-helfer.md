Not all vegetables are equally hungry, and not all of them are allowed to go to the same place every year. The helper above combines both: He tells how much compost a bed gets for a vegetable and when the same plant family can return.

## Nutrient requirements and compost

| group | Typical vegetables | Compost per m² and year | Additionally |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy feeder | Cabbage, broccoli, pumpkin, zucchini, cucumbers, tomatoes, peppers, potatoes, celeriac, leeks, corn | 3 to 5 liters | 50 to 80 g horn shavings, manure in summer |
| middle eater | Carrots, parsnips, beetroot, chard, spinach, fennel, kohlrabi, lettuce, onions, garlic, radish | 2 to 3 liters | if necessary 30 g horn shavings |
| weak eater | Peas, beans, lamb's lettuce, radishes, cress, rocket, many herbs | 1 to 2 liters | none |

A bed of 4 m² with tomatoes receives 12 to 20 liters of compost per year.

## Plant families and cultivation break

| family | vegetables | Cultivation break at the same place |
|---|---|---|
| Cruciferous vegetables | Cabbage, kohlrabi, broccoli, radishes, radish, arugula | 4 years, significantly longer with clubroot |
| Nightshade family | Tomatoes, potatoes, peppers, eggplant | 3 to 4 years |
| Cucurbitaceae | Pumpkin, zucchini, cucumbers | 3 years |
| Umbelliferous plants | Carrots, parsnips, celery, fennel | 3 to 4 years |
| legumes | Peas, beans | Peas 4 to 5 years, beans 3 years |
| Foxtail family | Spinach, chard, beetroot | 3 years |
| Allium family | Onions, garlic, leeks | 3 to 4 years |
| Asteraceae | Lettuce, endive | 2 to 3 years |
| Valerian family | Lamb's lettuce | 2 years |

## Four-field alternation

| bed | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Heavy feeder | middle eater | weak eater | recovery |
| b | recovery | Heavy feeder | middle eater | weak eater |
| C | weak eater | recovery | Heavy feeder | middle eater |
| D | middle eater | weak eater | recovery | Heavy feeder |

According to the Bavarian Garden Academy's organic gardening guidelines, the most important purpose of crop rotation is to prevent diseases and pests.
