This policy explains how byoy.de uses cookies and similar technologies such as your browser's memory. It supplements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include the browser's local memory or session memory and so-called pixels. Some of these techniques are technically necessary for a site to function, others are used for advertising or statistics.

## Technically necessary storage

BYOY itself does not set any cookies that recognize you. We only save in your browser's session memory whether the short celebration animation with tomatoes for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown so that it doesn't bother you again every time you view the page. This information does not leave your browser and is deleted as soon as you close the browser window.

## Anonymous counting of page views

For our statistics, when a page is accessed, we only transmit the name of the website and the path of the page. No cookie is set and no identifier is stored for this purpose.

## Advertising cookies from Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to deliver advertisements. Google may set cookies to serve ads, limit their frequency, detect fraud and, if you agree, to tailor ads to your interests. Personalized advertising only takes place with your consent via the consent banner. Without consent, non-personalized advertisements may appear, for example based on the content of the page.

## Manage consent

During your first visit, you can select which purposes you agree to in the consent banner. You can change or revoke your selection at any time using the privacy settings link in the banner. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. The instructions on BYOY still work completely.
