Cucumbers are the vegetable for hot days. They consist of around 95 percent water, taste cool and fresh and can be made into a salad in just a few minutes without cooking. In Germany, cucumbers grow primarily in greenhouses and are therefore available almost all year round. However, the best time for domestic cultivation, including outdoors, is the summer from June to September. Then the other types of cucumbers that are hard to find outside of these months also come onto the market: thick stewed cucumbers and small, warty pickling cucumbers.

Many people only know cucumbers raw. Braised cucumber is a classic German summer dish that has been forgotten. With minced meat or simply with potatoes and a sauce made from mustard, dill and cream, it is a light, quick lunch.

> **The most important things in brief**
>
> - You eat cucumbers raw, braised cucumbers are cooked, pickled cucumbers are pickled.
> - Cucumbers are sensitive to cold. They keep best at 10 to 13 degrees, in the fridge for just a few days.
> - If a cucumber tastes noticeably bitter, throw it away. The bitter substances can cause poisoning.
> - To prevent watery cucumber salad, it helps to salt the slices, let them drain and only dress them shortly before serving.
> - Braised cucumbers are peeled, deseeded and cooked until soft in about 10 minutes.

## The three types of cucumbers

| Type | Features | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Cucumber | long, dark green, smooth, thin peel, hardly any seeds | raw as a salad, raw food, cold dish |
| Braised cucumber | short, thick, yellowish-green, firm shell, many seeds | braise, stuff, in soup |
| Pickling cucumber | small, 6–12 cm, warty shell, firm flesh | Pickled cucumbers, salted cucumbers, mustard cucumbers |

There are also mini cucumbers and snack cucumbers that taste like small cucumbers, and old varieties with light or yellowish skin. Outdoor cucumbers, which are more robust than the greenhouse varieties, are worth having in your own garden.

## Shopping and storage

Good cucumbers are firm, plump, and evenly colored, with no soft spots or wrinkled ends. A cucumber that bends at the end is too old. Braised cucumbers can be yellowish, this is their natural color.

Cucumbers come from warm regions and react to cold with glassy, ​​mushy spots. It's best to store them in a cool pantry or basement at 10 to 13 degrees. They will keep for about three to five days in the vegetable drawer of the refrigerator, or a little longer if wrapped in a kitchen towel. Don't store cucumbers next to tomatoes, apples, or bananas. Their ripening gas causes them to turn yellow and soft more quickly. Place cut cucumbers cut side down in a jar or cover them and use them within one to two days.

## Be careful with bitter cucumbers

Like zucchini and pumpkins, cucumbers belong to the cucurbit family and can produce cucurbitacins, bitter substances that, in large quantities, cause nausea, vomiting and diarrhea. In modern varieties, these substances have largely been bred away. A slight bitterness directly at the end of the stem still occurs, especially with outdoor cucumbers after heat or drought.

So cut a piece off the end of the stem and try it. If only the end is slightly bitter, you can cut it off generously and try the rest again. If the cucumber tastes noticeably bitter overall, spit out the piece and throw the whole cucumber away. We explain more about this in the article about [zucchini and bitter substances](/kueche/zucchini-rezepte-und-bitterstoffe/).

## Cucumber salad with dill

A good cucumber salad is fresh, crunchy and not watery. Whether with a vinegar and oil dressing or creamy with yoghurt and cream is a matter of taste and varies from region to region. Here are both variants.

For 4 people as a side dish:

- 2 cucumbers (about 400 g each)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 small onion or 2 spring onions
- 1 bunch of dill

**For the vinegar-oil variant**

- 3 tablespoons white wine vinegar or mild apple cider vinegar
- 3 tablespoons rapeseed oil or sunflower oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- pepper

**For the creamy version**

- 150 g yogurt or sour cream
- 2 tbsp cream
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp sugar
- pepper

### Preparation

1. Wash the cucumbers. Peel them whole, in strips or not at all. They are crunchier when unpeeled, but the peel can be slightly bitter.
2. Slice the cucumbers into very thin slices, preferably with a vegetable slicer.
3. Mix the slices with the salt in a sieve and let them drain over a bowl for 15 to 20 minutes. Then squeeze it out lightly. This step prevents the lettuce from floating in a puddle. If you like it crunchier, leave it out and eat the salad immediately after dressing.
4. Cut the onion into very fine cubes or rings and chop the dill.
5. Mix the ingredients for your desired dressing. Season carefully with salt; the cucumbers are already salted.
6. Mix cucumbers, onion, dill and dressing together just before serving.

Cucumber salad goes well with potato pancakes, meatballs, fish, schnitzels and grilled dishes. In Silesia and East Prussia it was traditionally eaten with sour cream, in southern Germany more often with vinegar and oil.

## Braised cucumbers in mustard-dill sauce

Braised cucumbers have firmer flesh than cucumbers and do not fall apart as quickly during cooking. They taste mild, slightly sweet and absorb sauces well. If you can't get braised cucumbers, you can also use cucumbers; the cooking time will then be shortened to five to six minutes.

For 4 people:

- 1.5 kg braised cucumbers (3 to 4 pieces)
- 1 onion
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp flour
- 250 ml vegetable broth
- 150 ml cream
- 2 tbsp medium hot mustard
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 to 2 tablespoons lemon juice or mild vinegar
- Salt, pepper
- 1 large bunch of dill
- 300 g mixed minced meat or 150 g diced bacon, as desired

### Preparation

1. Peel the cucumbers, cut them in half lengthways and scrape out the seeds with a teaspoon. Cut the halves into pieces about two centimeters wide.
2. Dice the onion finely. Heat the butter in a wide pot and sauté the onion until translucent. If you use minced meat or bacon, fry it in the pot beforehand until crumbly or crispy and use some of the rendered fat instead of the butter.
3. Sprinkle the flour over the onions and let it sauté for a minute while stirring, without letting it brown.
4. Gradually pour in the broth, stirring vigorously so that no lumps form. Add the cream and let the sauce boil once.
5. Add the cucumber pieces to the sauce, put the lid on and let them simmer over a low heat for eight to twelve minutes. They should be soft and slightly glassy, ​​but still have a bit of bite. Cucumbers release water when stewing, which makes the sauce a little thinner.
6. Stir in mustard, sugar and lemon juice and season with salt and pepper. Chop the dill and fold it in at the end. Don't let it boil anymore, otherwise the dill will lose its color.

Serve with boiled potatoes, mashed potatoes or rice. With minced meat, the dish is a complete main course, and a vegetarian option goes well with a fried egg.

## Stuffed braised cucumbers

For a variant, halve the braised cucumbers lengthways, hollow them out and fill them with a mixture of 400 g minced meat, a soaked, squeezed roll, an egg, a chopped onion, mustard, salt and pepper. Place them in a greased baking dish, pour in 200 ml of stock and bake them at 190 degrees for 30 to 35 minutes. You can mix the liquid in the mold with a little sour cream to make a sauce.

## Cucumbers as a cold dish

On very hot days, cold cucumber soup is refreshing. Puree a peeled cucumber with 300 g of yogurt, a small clove of garlic, half a bunch of dill or mint, a tablespoon of lemon juice, salt and pepper and a little cold water until the soup has the desired consistency. Cool for at least an hour and serve with diced cucumber and a drizzle of olive oil.

## Tzatziki: the Greek classic

To make a tzatziki, roughly grate half a cucumber, lightly salt it and after ten minutes squeeze it vigorously in a cloth. Mix them with 400 g of Greek yogurt, one or two finely grated garlic cloves, a tablespoon of olive oil, a squeeze of lemon juice and some chopped dill or mint. Season with salt and pepper and chill for an hour. Squeezing the cucumber is the crucial step, otherwise the tzatziki will be thin and watery. It goes well with grilled food, baked potatoes and flatbread.

## Pickling cucumbers

The small warty cucumbers are firmer and have a thicker skin than cucumbers. This means they stay crunchy in the sour brew. They can be found at markets in July and August, and sometimes by the box from the producer. We describe in detail how to pickle them in vinegar stock with dill, mustard seeds and onions in the article about [spiced cucumbers](/kueche/gewuerzgurken-einlegen/).

## Spreewald cucumbers

The most famous cucumbers in Germany come from the Spreewald in Brandenburg. The moist, humus-rich soil and the water of the many streams offer ideal conditions for pickling cucumbers. “Spreewald cucumbers” have been registered as a protected geographical indication in the European Union since 1999. Traditionally they are pickled as salted cucumbers, mustard cucumbers or pickled gherkins. Anyone visiting the region can cycle from farm to farm on a cucumber cycle path and try the varieties directly from the producer.

## Cucumbers in your own garden

Cucumbers require warmth and are sensitive to frost. They only come outdoors after the ice saints in mid-May, ideally in a sunny, wind-protected place. They need evenly moist soil. If possible, water in the morning with room temperature water directly to the roots, not over the leaves, as this prevents fungal diseases. Harvest regularly and not too late. Overripe, yellow cucumbers weaken the plant and it produces fewer new fruits. A trellis saves space and keeps the cucumbers clean.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to peel cucumbers?

No. The peel of cucumbers is edible and contains some vitamins. For outdoor cucumbers and braised cucumbers it is thicker and sometimes bitter, so it's better to peel them.

### Why does cucumber salad offend some people?

Cucumbers are difficult for some people to digest, especially raw and with the peel on. Peeled, seeded cucumbers, a little onion and dill as a seasoning are often better tolerated.

### Can I freeze cucumbers?

Raw cucumbers become mushy after thawing and are not suitable for salad. Pureed, for example for a cold bowl or a smoothie, they can be frozen.
