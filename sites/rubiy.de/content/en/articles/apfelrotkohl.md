Hardly any other side dish is as closely linked to the German Sunday roast as red cabbage. It is on the table with goose at St. Martin's Day, roast beef, roulades, game and at Christmas, often with dumplings and dark sauce. In northern Germany it is called red cabbage, in Bavaria and parts of Swabia it is called red cabbage, and elsewhere it is called red cabbage. It is harvested fresh from late summer to winter and lasts as storage cabbage until spring.

Ready-made red cabbage from a jar or bag is convenient, but often tastes very sweet and monotonous. When cooked home it is spicier, fresher and can be adjusted to suit your taste. The effort is manageable, and because red cabbage tastes even better warmed up, you can easily prepare it a day or two in advance.

> **The most important things in brief**
>
> - A head of red cabbage weighing around one kilo is enough as a side dish for six people.
> - Acid in the form of vinegar, wine or tart apples makes cabbage bright red. Without acid it turns blue-violet.
> - Bay leaf, cloves, juniper and allspice provide the classic spice, ideally in a tea bag or spice sachet.
> - Red cabbage braises for 60 to 90 minutes, depending on how soft you like it.
> - It tastes better warmed up the next day and freezes very well.

## Red or blue?

Red cabbage contains anthocyanins, pigments that function as a natural acidity indicator. In an acidic environment they turn red, in a neutral environment they turn purple and in a more alkaline environment they turn blue to greenish. Whether the cabbage ends up being red cabbage or red cabbage depends on how it is prepared. With vinegar, wine and tart apples it turns bright red. If it is cooked without acid or in hard water, it appears more blue.

The regional names are actually said to be partly due to these differences. Whether this is true or just a nice story cannot be said for sure. For the kitchen this means: If you want a beautifully red cabbage, add the acid early.

### A kitchen experiment for children

The color changes of red cabbage can be demonstrated wonderfully. Boil a few leaves in a little water for ten minutes and pour off the purple broth. Divide it into three glasses. Put a dash of vinegar in the first, nothing in the second, and a pinch of baking soda in the third. The first glass turns red, the second remains violet, the third turns blue to greenish. It is precisely this principle that decides whether red cabbage or red cabbage ends up on the plate.

## Shop and prepare

A good head of red cabbage is firm, heavy and has shiny, tight-fitting leaves. Wilted outer leaves are not a problem, they are removed. A head lasts for two to three weeks in the vegetable drawer of the refrigerator, and even longer in a cool cellar.

This is how you prepare it:

1. Remove the outer, wilted leaves.
2. Quarter the head from top to bottom and cut out the hard, wedge-shaped stem from each quarter.
3. Plane the quarters into fine strips with a vegetable slicer or cut them into strips about three millimeters wide with a large knife.
4. Wear disposable gloves or work quickly. The dye stains fingers and wooden boards.

## Apple red cabbage

For 6 people as a side dish:

- 1 red cabbage (about 1 kg)
- 2 tart apples, for example Boskoop or Elstar
- 1 large onion
- 2 tablespoons goose fat, clarified butter or butter
- 2 tablespoons sugar or 1 tablespoon sugar and 2 tablespoons currant jelly
- 100 ml red wine or apple juice
- 4 tablespoons red wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 200 ml water or vegetable broth
- 1 bay leaf
- 3 cloves
- 4 juniper berries, lightly crushed
- 3 allspice seeds
- 1 small cinnamon stick, if desired
- 1 tsp salt, pepper
- 1 tbsp cornstarch if you want to thicken the cabbage

### Preparation

1. Slice the red cabbage as described above. Peel the apples, remove the core and cut them into small cubes or grate them coarsely. Dice the onion.
2. If you have time, mix the shredded cabbage with vinegar, salt and sugar and let it sit covered in the fridge overnight. This makes it softer, absorbs the spice better and gives it a more intense color. But that is not necessary.
3. Heat the lard in a large pot and sauté the onion until translucent. Sprinkle the sugar on top and let it caramelize slightly, stirring until it is golden yellow.
4. Add the red cabbage and apples and sauté everything, turning, for about five minutes until the cabbage wilts.
5. Deglaze with red wine or apple juice and add the vinegar if you haven't already pickled the cabbage. Pour on the water.
6. Place the spices in a tea filter or spice bag, tie it shut and place it in the pot. This way you don't have to look for cloves from the cabbage later.
7. Cover and let the red cabbage stew over a low heat for 60 to 90 minutes. Stir occasionally and add a little water if necessary to prevent it from sticking.
8. Take out the spice bag. Season the cabbage with salt, pepper, sugar and vinegar. It should have a balanced sweet and sour note.
9. If there is still a lot of liquid in the pot, stir the cornstarch with a little cold water until smooth, add it and bring everything to the boil. This gives the cabbage a slight shine.

### Variants

- **With cranberries:** Stir in two tablespoons of cranberries from the jar at the end; they go particularly well with game.
- **With orange:** Grated zest and juice of an orange instead of some of the vinegar, plus a pinch of cinnamon. A Christmas variant.
- **With chestnuts:** Glaze peeled, cooked chestnuts in butter and fold them into the finished cabbage. You can read how to roast chestnuts in the article about [chestnuts](/kueche/maronen-roesten/).
- **Without alcohol:** Replace red wine with apple juice, cherry juice or blackcurrant juice.

## Red cabbage steaks from the oven

A modern way to serve red cabbage is with oven-baked steaks. Cut a small head into slices about two centimeters from top to bottom, so that each slice is held together by the stalk. Place the slices on a baking tray lined with baking paper, brush both sides with oil and season with salt, pepper and a little caraway. Roast them at 220 degrees top and bottom heat for 25 to 30 minutes and turn them halfway through. The edges become crispy, the inside remains juicy. Drizzle the steaks with a mixture of honey and apple cider vinegar and sprinkle toasted walnuts and crumbled feta cheese over them. This makes cabbage a vegetarian main dish.

## Pre-cook, store and freeze

Red cabbage is an ideal dish to make ahead. It will keep in a sealed container in the refrigerator for three to four days, and each day the flavors will combine better. Heat it in a saucepan over medium heat with a sip of water or juice and stir.

To freeze, let the cabbage cool completely and pour it into freezer containers or bags in portions. It lasts for several months at minus 18 degrees. You can put it frozen in a pot and heat it slowly with a little liquid. This saves a lot of work, especially before Christmas.

## Red cabbage as raw food

Raw red cabbage is crunchy and spicy. So that it is not too hard, slice it very finely and knead it with your hands with a little salt for two to three minutes until it becomes softer.

To make a red cabbage salad for 4 people, mix 400 g of finely shredded red cabbage, a grated apple, a handful of roasted walnuts and a dressing made from 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar, 3 tablespoons oil, 1 teaspoon honey, 1 teaspoon mustard, salt and pepper. Let the salad sit for at least 30 minutes. It will last two days in the fridge and will tend to get better. With orange fillets and pomegranate seeds, it becomes a festive appetizer.

## Red cabbage and health

Red cabbage provides plenty of vitamin C, fiber and the coloring anthocyanins. Like all cabbage, it can cause bloating in some people. Spices such as caraway, juniper and bay leaf are said to make it more digestible, although the effect varies from person to person. For many people, stewing for a long time makes cabbage easier to digest than raw food.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why has my red cabbage turned blue?

There was a lack of acidity, or the water in your region is very calcareous. Add a dash of vinegar or lemon juice and it will turn red again.

### Can I cook red cabbage in the pressure cooker?

Yes. The cabbage will be cooked in the pressure cooker after about 15 to 20 minutes. It will taste a little less round than long-braised cabbage, but this will be evened out if you let it sit for a day.

### How do I get red cabbage stains out of the wooden board?

Rub the board with lemon juice and coarse salt and then rinse it with hot water. Rinse fresh stains on textiles immediately with cold water.

### Which pot is suitable for red cabbage?

A pot made of stainless steel, enameled cast iron or a coated roasting pan is best. You should not braise or store acidic foods such as red cabbage in uncoated aluminum pots because the acid can cause aluminum to dissolve and pass into the food. The color then also suffers.

### How do I make red cabbage less sweet?

Halve the sugar and leave out the currant jelly. The apples bring enough natural sweetness. Finally, season with salt and a splash of vinegar until the sweetness and acidity are balanced.
