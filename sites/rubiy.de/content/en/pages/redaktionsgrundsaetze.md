A recipe is a promise: Anyone who cooks it trusts that the quantities, times and temperatures are correct. Preserving, fermenting and collecting wild plants is also about health. That's why we adhere to these principles.

## Written specifically, not composed

Every post on RUBIY is written specifically for this website. We do not copy recipes from other sources. We also use AI tools when researching and formulating; The rules on this page apply to every sentence, regardless of how it was created. Classics such as red cabbage, onion cake or Frankfurt green sauce are available in a thousand family variations; We describe a variant and say where others do it differently.

## Recipes that work

Our recipes specify quantities for a specific number of portions, times for preparation and cooking and the temperature for top and bottom heat or circulating air. We give quantities in grams and milliliters and spoon measurements only where a few grams are not important. Cooking times are guidelines because every stove and every vegetable is different. That's why we also describe how you can tell when something is finished.

## Food safety first

When it comes to topics that pose health risks, we follow the recommendations of authorities and specialist bodies for risk assessment, consumer protection and nutrition. This particularly applies to:

- **Cooking low-acid foods** such as beans, peas, asparagus or mushrooms that pose a risk of botulism. We recommend freezing or souring and explain the risks openly.
- **Wild plants and mushrooms:** We describe the risks of confusion in detail and always advise you to only eat certain things you find and, if in doubt, to seek mushroom advice. Photos and apps do not replace purpose.
- **Raw eggs, leftovers and storage:** We provide cooling temperatures and deadlines and point out risk groups such as pregnant women, small children, the elderly and immunocompromised people.

Where we are uncertain, we say less instead of more.

## No health promises

We do not write that a vegetable heals, detoxifies or prevents disease. Seasonal fruits and vegetables are a good part of a varied diet, and that's enough.

## Independence

RUBIY is financed through advertisements that are automatically delivered by Google. We do not choose the ads and they have no influence on our texts. We do not take any money for articles, product tests or recommendations and we do not name brands in recipes, but rather describe what is important in an ingredient or device.

## Updates and corrections

Each post has a date. We revise texts if recommendations change or if readers point out errors to us. We correct any factual errors quickly. You can send us information via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.

## language

We write in simple, friendly language and use the first-name terms of our readers. We explain regional terms such as red cabbage, datschi or lamb's lettuce so that everyone can find their way around in Kiel and Konstanz.
