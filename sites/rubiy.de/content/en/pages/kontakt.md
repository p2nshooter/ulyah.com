We welcome mail from other kitchens. The best way to reach us is by email:

**Email:** [kontakt@rubiy.de](mailto:kontakt@rubiy.de)

We read every message and usually respond within a week. In high season, when we have the preserving jars on the stove, it can take a little longer.

## What you can write to us about

- **Recipe requests and topics:** Are you missing a vegetable, a regional specialty or a technique, such as pickling sweet and sour pumpkin or baking with spelled? Write to us what you would like to read more about.
- **Errors and additions:** If you think a quantity, temperature or cooking time is wrong in a recipe, please let us know. We check every tip, rework it if in doubt and correct the text if the tip is justified. We particularly welcome suggestions from cooks, nutrition experts, gardeners and mushroom experts.
- **Experiences from your kitchen:** Do you have an old family recipe for quince bread or a trick against the zucchini glut? Tell us about it. We don't copy texts verbatim, but your experience can make a post better.
- **Legal and data protection:** We also answer inquiries about your data or the content of this website via this address.

## Please do not identify mushrooms or plants using photos

We cannot say with certainty based on photos whether a mushroom or wild plant is edible. Many poisonous species look very similar to their edible counterparts in a picture, and crucial characteristics such as smell, tuber or spore powder cannot be recognized in a photo. Please bring your finds to a mushroom advice center or mushroom expert in your area. If you suspect poisoning, contact your state's poison control center immediately or call 112.

## Health issues

We cannot answer individual questions about allergies, intolerances or special diets for illnesses. Doctors and nutritionists are the right contacts for this.

## Advertising and collaborations

We do not publish paid posts, paid product tests or sponsored recipes. We do not answer inquiries about this.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message. More about this in the [Data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).
