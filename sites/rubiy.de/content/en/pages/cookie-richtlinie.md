This policy explains how rubiy.de uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in the browser. Similar technologies include the browser's local storage or pixels. Some are technically necessary, others are used for advertising or statistics.

## Technically necessary storage

RUBIY itself does not set cookies for recognition. We only save in your browser's session memory whether the short celebration animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown so that it doesn't bother you again every time you view the page. This information does not leave your browser.

## Range measurement without cookies

In order to find out which recipes and guides are being read, each page sends a short message with the name of the website and the path accessed, for example “/kitchen/rhubarb cake/”. No cookie is set and no identifier is stored in your browser. We only see how many times a page was viewed in total, not who read it.

## Fonts

The fonts on this website are loaded via Google Fonts. According to Google, it does not use cookies, but for technical reasons your browser transmits your IP address to Google's servers. Details can be found in the data protection declaration.

## Advertising cookies from Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to deliver advertisements. Google may set cookies to serve ads, limit their frequency, detect fraud and, if you agree, to tailor ads to your interests. Personalized advertising only takes place with your consent via the consent banner.

## Manage consent

During your first visit, you can select which purposes you agree to in the consent banner. You can change or revoke your selection at any time using the privacy settings link in the banner. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings; Then you will continue to see ads, but not personalized.
