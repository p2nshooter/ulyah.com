RUBIY shows how to cook with seasonal ingredients, store them and preserve them. Please read these instructions before cooking a recipe or trying out a method.

## Guidelines instead of guarantees

Amounts, cooking times and temperatures in our recipes are carefully tested guidelines. However, results may vary because ingredients, equipment and preferences vary. Always check for yourself whether meat, fish, eggs and legumes are fully cooked.

## Allergies and intolerances

Many recipes contain common allergens such as gluten, milk, egg, nuts, celery, mustard or sesame. We do not always mention all allergens separately in recipes. If you have allergies or intolerances, check each ingredient and the ingredient lists of the products used.

## Wild plants and mushrooms

Some guides describe collecting wild plants such as wild garlic or mushrooms. Many edible species have poisonous, sometimes fatal counterparts, such as lily of the valley and autumn crocus in wild garlic or the green death cap in several edible mushrooms. Only collect what you can determine with certainty and, if in doubt, have mushrooms checked by a mushroom expert. If you suspect poisoning, call the poison control center or 112 immediately.

## Preservation

When boiling, fermenting, pickling in oil and drying, if done incorrectly, germs can multiply and form dangerous poisons. Follow the hygiene rules, temperatures and amounts of salt or acid described exactly. Jars with domed lids, gas formation, unusual odors or mold should be discarded without tasting.

## Nutrition and health

Our content is not nutritional advice. Special rules apply in cases of pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses, special diets or for infants and small children. In such cases, talk to your doctor or a nutritionist.
