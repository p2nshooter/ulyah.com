Cooking seasonally means working with what's growing - and that requires planning: What's available in March? How long do preserved tomatoes last? How does one pumpkin turn into three different meals? RUBIY answers such questions. On this page we talk about two digital projects from the same company as RUBIY. We say it openly: This is advertising on our own behalf.

## Something for the eye: AXTO

Good food is also design - and that's what [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=rubiy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem), a digital museum of moving art, thrives on. Clocks there run in real time, stamps tell of fictitious empires, Koranic verses and hadiths appear as calligraphy whose text remains untouched, and figures move. There is a unique DNA behind every work; it will not be made a second time.

If you want to give as a gift, you can choose exclusive editions (**AC-**): 1 US dollar in AXTO coins, ten editions per work, without gas fee to many wallets at the same time. Public Works (**OS-**) are available on OpenSea with 1 to 10 editions for a fixed price of $1 to $250; GPU images have a **G**. If you prefer to do math in the kitchen: With AXTO you can buy into the community behind axto.io for as little as one dollar, locked coins generate rewards from individual works, nothing is certain.

## A separate address for your kitchen

Whether it's a country inn, caterer, jam factory or cooking school: guests and customers first look online. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=rubiy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem), a development studio from Europe, is building:

- Websites with menus, table reservations and seasonal offers,
- Online shops for canned, dried or baked specialties,
- Order and course portals, for example for a cooking school with fixed dates.

The work is divided into stages, which are paid individually - via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. If you start small, you will find suitable [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=rubiy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). XAA presents itself in English, Spanish and Indonesian; A request in German is still no problem.

## Everything on the stove remains the same

The recipes and pantry knowledge on RUBIY are created independently. No ingredient, device or recipe is recommended here because it has to do with AXTO or XAA. If you enjoy one of the two projects, great – the best part is the cooking itself.
