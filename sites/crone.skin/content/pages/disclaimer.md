CRONE is a lifestyle magazine. We write about beauty, style, everyday movement, home, relationships and travel for women in the second half of life. Please read this disclaimer before acting on anything you read here.

## Not medical advice

Some of our stories touch on the body — skin and hair changes, exercise, balance, sleep, energy. They describe general, widely accepted information and practical habits; they are **not** a diagnosis, treatment plan or substitute for advice from your doctor, nurse, pharmacist or physiotherapist. Before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, osteoporosis, joint problems, recent surgery or take regular medication, talk to a health professional. Stop any activity that causes pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or breathlessness and seek medical help.

If you notice a change in a mole, a sore that does not heal, sudden hair loss, or any symptom that worries you, see a doctor promptly. Beauty products cannot treat medical conditions.

## Products and ingredients

When we mention cosmetic products or ingredients, we describe them in general terms. Skin and hair react differently from person to person, and sensitivities and allergies can develop at any age. Patch-test new products as the manufacturer advises and stop using anything that causes irritation.

## Money, legal and property matters

Stories about downsizing, travel or volunteering may mention costs, contracts or insurance. These are general considerations only, not financial or legal advice. For decisions about property, pensions, wills or insurance, consult a qualified professional.

## Travel

Travel requirements, health advice and safety conditions change. Always check official government travel advice and entry requirements for your destination before you go.

## No guarantees

We work carefully and correct errors, but we cannot guarantee that every detail is accurate, complete or right for you. Any action you take based on our content is at your own risk.
