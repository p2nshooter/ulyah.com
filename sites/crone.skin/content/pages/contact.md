Letters from readers are the heart of any good magazine, and CRONE is no exception. Whether you want to share a story, ask a question, suggest a topic or point out something we got wrong, we would be delighted to hear from you.

## How to reach us

Email **hello@crone.skin**. A short subject line helps us pass your message to the right editor — for example "Story idea: learning the piano at 70" or "Correction: grey hair guide".

## What we welcome

- **Story ideas.** Tell us what you would like to read about. Many of our best stories began with a reader's question.
- **Corrections.** If something in a story is inaccurate or out of date, please tell us which page and what you believe is wrong. We check every report and update the story where needed.
- **Questions.** We cannot give medical, legal or financial advice about your personal situation, but we are glad to explain what a story means or suggest a reliable source.
- **Privacy requests.** To ask about any personal data we hold, write with the subject "Privacy request". Our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) explains your rights.

## When you will hear back

We aim to reply within five working days. We read every message, though we may not be able to answer every story suggestion individually.

## Please note

We do not publish guest posts, sponsored articles or paid links, and we do not sell advertising directly; advertising on CRONE is provided by Google AdSense. Please never send us passwords, bank details or identity documents — we will never ask for them.
