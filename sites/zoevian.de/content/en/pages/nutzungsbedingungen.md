## Our offer

ZOEVIAN publishes guides about garden birds, feeding, nesting boxes and natural gardens, bird identification, observation and photography as well as the species-appropriate keeping of ornamental birds. All content is free. The site is financed by advertisements that are marked as advertising.

## Nature is not standardized

Birds don't follow textbooks. Breeding times vary with the weather and region, plumage varies, and what works well in a garden in northern Germany does not necessarily fit in a city garden in the south. When it comes to pet birds, every animal reacts differently. Our advice is therefore general; Veterinary practices and wild bird stations are responsible for sick or injured birds. You use our content at your own risk, see [Disclaimer](/haftungsausschluss/).

## Protection of wild birds

Please never use our guides in a way that disturbs wild birds. Nests must not be opened, young birds must not be touched without necessity and birds must not be lured with sound dummies where this will cause harm. All European bird species are specially protected under the Federal Nature Conservation Act.
