## AXTO: Works that live

Bird watchers know the amazement of movement. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=zoevian.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) brings it into a digital museum: clocks chime in real time, stamps come from imaginary empires, Koran and Hadith verses appear as calligraphy in the original wording, figures move. Each work follows its own DNA and remains unique; it is stored in the base network.

Exclusive works are marked **AC-**, cost $1 per edition in AXTO Coins, and can be gifted tenfold to friends with no gas fee. Public works with **OS-** can be found at OpenSea: 1 to 10 editions, fixed prices from 1 to 250 dollars, GPU works with **G**. AXTO, the coin of this art community, is available from one dollar; Locking is worth it with unique items, but no return is promised.

## XAA: Websites for clubs, garden centers and pet shops

A bird protection group is looking for new members, a nursery wants to show its native wild shrubs online, a specialist shop for ornamental bird supplies needs a shop with a collection appointment? [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=zoevian.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a web development studio in Europe that builds custom websites, shops and membership areas. Work and billing is done in stages: you pay for what you have already seen and checked. There are also ready-made [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=zoevian.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) for a quick start.

## ULYAH: a free portal for Islamic knowledge

[ULYAH](https://ulyah.com/?utm_source=zoevian.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) offers the Quran with translations and recitations, hadith collections, classic works and stories for free. If you prefer to read in German, you can find the sister portal [Tilawa](https://tilawa.de/?utm_source=zoevian.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). We mention it here because it belongs to the same project family; We don't want to proselytize anyone.
