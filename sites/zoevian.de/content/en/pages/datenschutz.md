With this declaration we inform you in accordance with Art. 13 GDPR what data is generated when you visit zoevian.de. On ZOEVIAN there is no user account, no comments, no newsletter and no form. Accordingly, little is processed.

## Responsible person

The provider named in [Imprint](/impressum/) is responsible. You can reach us at [kontakt@zoevian.de](mailto:kontakt@zoevian.de).

## Provision of the website

ZOEVIAN is delivered via Cloudflare's network. When you access a page, Cloudflare processes technically necessary information: your IP address, date and time, the address accessed, browser information and the amount of data transferred. This is necessary to deliver the pages securely and reliably and to ward off attacks (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). Processing outside the EU is possible; it is based on appropriate safeguards such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Fonts

The Newsreader and Plus Jakarta Sans fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address in order to deliver the font files (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR; our interest is an easily readable, uniform representation).

## Range measurement

We count page views anonymously and without cookies. Only the name of the website and the path accessed are transferred. There is no identifier that recognizes you and no profile across multiple visits.

## Advertising with Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense from Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver ads, prevent abuse and measure the effectiveness of ads. Personalized ads only appear if you have consented (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a GDPR, Section 25 Para. 1 TDDDG). We request consent via a Google-certified consent banner; you can revoke it at any time. For more information, see [How Google uses data from websites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Emails to us

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b or f GDPR) and delete it as soon as it is no longer needed and there is no obligation to retain it. If you send us a bird photo, please make sure that no people, house numbers or license plates are visible on it.

## Your rights

You have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can revoke your consent at any time in the future. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example in your federal state.

Further information can be found in the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-richtlinie/).
