ZOEVIAN was created from a bird feeder. Anyone who stands at the window in the winter morning with their coffee and consciously sees for the first time how a great tit clamps a sunflower seed between its feet and chops it open begins to ask questions: Which bird is that actually? Can I feed in summer? Why doesn't anyone move into my nesting box? And is the budgie in the cage next door actually happy?

There are many answers to these questions on the internet, unfortunately often contradictory ones. Some texts primarily sell fodder, others are so technical that you give up after three paragraphs. ZOEVIAN wants to avoid both.

## What the name means

**Zoë** is the Greek word for life, **avian** is the English word for everything that has to do with birds. ZOEVIAN roughly means “living with birds”, and that’s exactly what it’s about: the birds that live with us in gardens, parks and cities, the ones we watch on holiday on the Wadden Sea or in the mountains, and the pet birds that live in our homes.

## What you find here

Our guides are divided into six topics: garden birds, feeding and watering, nesting boxes and habitat, recognizing bird species, observing and photographing and species-appropriate ornamental birds. Each text should answer a specific question in such a way that you can then do something: hang a nesting box correctly, distinguish a blackbird from a thrush, keep a feeding place clean or plan an aviary.

## How we work

We rely on the recommendations of nature conservation associations, bird observatories and specialist literature, on identification books and on the experience from many hours with binoculars and bird feeders. Where experts disagree, for example on the question of year-round feeding, we put the positions side by side instead of pretending that there is only one truth. Our [editorial principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/) describe how we research, check and correct.

## What we are not

We are not a wild bird station or a veterinary practice. If you find an injured bird or your pet bird seems sick, a text will only be of limited help. In our guides, we always say when you need experts and where you can find them.

## How ZOEVIAN is financed

ZOEVIAN is free and funded by Google AdSense ads. We do not sell bird food or nesting boxes and do not take money for recommendations. Questions, tips on errors and topic requests can be reached via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.
