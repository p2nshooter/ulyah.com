ZOEVIAN wants to help you understand birds better, help them in the garden and keep pet birds well. To make this successful, we say openly what an advisor can and cannot do.

## General information

Our texts describe what applies to most gardens, birds and households. They don't know your garden or your bird. Information on food quantities, breeding times, nest box hole sizes or temperatures are guidelines that you must adapt to your situation.

## Injured and sick birds

An injured or weakened wild bird belongs in the hands of a wild bird station or a veterinary practice. Do not attempt to self-medicate with medication or home remedies, and do not feed him milk or bread. The same applies to pet birds: If a bird sits bloated, breathes heavily, doesn't eat or has conspicuous droppings, you should go to an avian veterinary practice, not a text on the Internet.

## Building and working

Construction instructions for nesting boxes, feeders or aviaries require the safe use of tools. Use safety glasses, secure footing and suitable ladders when installing boxes in trees or on facades. Only use wood that is untreated or treated to be safe for animals.

## Right

All European bird species are specially protected. Our information on the Federal Nature Conservation Act, on pruning trees during the breeding season or on keeping certain species reflects the legal situation in simplified form and does not replace legal advice. The current legal texts and the information from the responsible authorities, such as the lower nature conservation authority or the veterinary office, are binding.

## Health

Anyone who keeps birds or cleans feeders should pay attention to hygiene: wear gloves, wash hands and seek medical advice if you have known allergies or a weakened immune system. ZOEVIAN does not provide medical advice to people.
