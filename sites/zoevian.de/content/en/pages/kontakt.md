Do you have a question about one of our guides, discovered an error or made an observation that you would like to share? Feel free to write to us:

**[kontakt@zoevian.de](mailto:kontakt@zoevian.de)**

## What we are happy about

- **Corrections:** Please tell us the title of the post and the part that is incorrect. There are regional differences, especially when it comes to bird identification and breeding times, and we like to learn more.
- **Topic requests:** Which species should we introduce, which question remains unanswered about feeding places, nesting boxes or aviaries?
- **Comprehension questions:** If something is unclear in a text, we will be happy to explain it to you in more detail.

## When a bird needs help

In an emergency, please do not wait for our response. If you have an injured or weakened wild bird, take it to a wild bird station, a veterinary practice or ask the local nature conservation authority. A young bird that sits alone on the ground is usually still cared for by its parents; We explain how you can recognize this in our guides. An avian veterinary practice is the right address for sick pet birds. We cannot diagnose remotely.

## What we don't answer

We do not publish paid articles, purchased product tests or guest articles with advertising links. Such requests remain unanswered.

## Data protection

We only use your email address to respond to you. Please do not send us photos that reveal people or exact addresses. Details can be found in our [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/).
