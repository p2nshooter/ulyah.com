Here we explain how zoevian.de deals with cookies and similar technologies. The policy supplements our [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/).

## Briefly explained: Cookies and browser storage

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in the browser. The browser's local storage and session storage work similarly. Some of these techniques need a site to function at all, others are used for statistics or advertising.

## What ZOEVIAN stores itself

ZOEVIAN does not create any cookies that could be used to recognize you. We only note in your browser's session memory whether the little feather animation for Spain's 2026 World Cup title has already run before, so that it doesn't greet you again on every page. This notice remains in your browser and is deleted as soon as you close the window.

## Counting page views

So that we know which guides are being read, we count views anonymously. Only the name of the website and the path to the page are transmitted, without a cookie and without a stored identifier.

## Cookies from Google AdSense

Google AdSense delivers the ads on ZOEVIAN. Google may use cookies for this purpose, for example to display ads, prevent you from constantly seeing the same ad, detect click fraud and, if you agree, to tailor ads to your interests. Without consent, non-personalized advertisements may appear that are based on the content of the page.

## Your choice

During your first visit, you specify in the consent banner which purposes you agree to. You can change or withdraw your decision later using the privacy settings link. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser; All guides on ZOEVIAN can then be read in full.
