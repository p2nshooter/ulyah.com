If you want to help birds, you need reliable information. These principles apply to every guide on ZOEVIAN.

## Independent of trade

We do not sell food, nesting boxes, binoculars, or cages, and we do not take money for referrals. When we compare food mixes, box models or appearance, we mention criteria instead of brands so that you can decide for yourself. Google AdSense delivers the ads on the site; they have no influence on our texts.

## The welfare of the birds first

No photo or observation is worth disturbing a bird. We do not recommend methods that involve opening nests, scaring up breeding birds, or luring animals with sound dummies during the breeding season. When it comes to pet birds, we focus on the animals' needs: companionship from other birds, daily free flight, varied food and enough space.

## Comprehensible sources

Our information is based on identification literature, on the recommendations of nature conservation associations and bird watchdogs, on veterinary literature and on information sheets from the responsible authorities. Where experts disagree, such as year-round feeding, we present the arguments from both sides.

## Write clearly

ZOEVIAN is aimed at people who like birds, not specialist ornithologists. We explain technical terms the first time they appear, and we describe identifying characteristics in such a way that you can actually see them with the naked eye or binoculars.

## Honest boundaries

We do not invent studies, quotes or numbers. We only present stock figures and trends in rounded form and with reference to their uncertainty. When a question doesn't have a clear answer, we say so.

## Corrections

We revise our guides regularly, for example when recommendations change. Have you discovered an error? Write to us at [kontakt@zoevian.de](mailto:kontakt@zoevian.de). We check every information and correct anything that is incorrect or misleading.

## Own projects

Projects from the same publisher only appear on the designated [Related Projects](/verbundene-projekte/) page, never hidden in a guide.
