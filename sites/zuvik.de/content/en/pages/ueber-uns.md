ZUVIK stands for **Home, lots of children's laughter**. The name is a wish, not a promise: In real families, people laugh, argue, dawdle, cry and make up again, often all on a single Tuesday. That's exactly what we're talking about.

## Why stories?

There are many guides for parents. They explain what you should do and sometimes leave you feeling like you're doing it all wrong. Stories work differently. When we read about how the Brandt family sinks into chaos the morning before school, we recognize ourselves, smile and suddenly look at our own everyday lives with a little distance. From this distance it is easier to change a small thing.

That's why every post on ZUVIK consists of two parts:

1. **a fictional story** from family life, told with warmth and without pointing fingers,
2. **the “For your everyday life” section** with concrete, small ideas that you can try out on the same day.

## Six rooms, one home

Our stories live in six rooms:

- **Morning & Evening**: the edges of the day where crunches occur most often.
- **Siblings**: Quarrels, jealousy, solidarity.
- **Grandparents & Generations**: what connects families across generations.
- **Festivals & Seasons**: from the lantern parade to the summer holidays.
- **School & daycare**: settling in, homework, friendships.
- **Living together at home**: pocket money, household services, screen time, family council.

## What we are not

ZUVIK is not an advice center or a doctor’s office. We write for parents, grandparents and everyone who supports children, and we take seriously that every family is different. If something is permanently bothering you, you will find information about where you can get support in our [Disclaimer](/haftungsausschluss/).

## Who is behind ZUVIK

ZUVIK is a project by **p2nshooter**, who also publishes other independent advice and knowledge sites. The ZUVIK editorial team writes the stories. Feedback and ideas can be reached via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.

## How we work

Every story is written, proofread and checked for comprehensibility by our editorial team. The suggestions are based on what has proven successful in many families and what experts have long recommended for children and families: reliability, care, clear and friendly agreements, time to play. How we proceed exactly is stated in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## Free thanks to advertising

All stories are free. ZUVIK is financed through advertisements that are recognizable as such. Ads have no influence on our topics. We do not recommend products for payment.
