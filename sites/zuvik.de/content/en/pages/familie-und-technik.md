## AXTO: a museum to marvel at together

Children love things that move. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=zuvik.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a digital museum full of such works: clocks with real time, stamps from fairytale lands, calligraphy from the Koran and Hadith with original text, and animated characters. Every work arises from a unique DNA; no one exists twice.

Exclusive editions (**AC-**) are suitable for families and daycare centers: 1 US dollar per edition in AXTO coins, ten per work, can be given away to many wallets without a gas fee - for example for birthdays or Ramadan. Public works (**OS-**) are on OpenSea, 1 to 10 editions, fixed price 1 to 250 dollars, GPU works with **G**. For adults, AXTO is a community coin starting at $1 with staking rewards in the form of unique works; their price can rise or fall.

## XAA: a digital home for daycare centers, parents' initiatives and clubs

Daycare centers, parent initiatives, sports clubs and school support associations organize a lot on a voluntary basis - often with lists, messenger groups and a website that was built at some point by someone who has long since moved away. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=zuvik.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem), a European website and software studio, offers:

- a friendly website with appointments, contact persons and news,
- Registration for courses, parties or holiday care with online payment,
- a protected member area for documents and agreements.

Payment is made according to milestones that are agreed upon together in advance - via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. If you want to get started quickly, choose a [Template](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=zuvik.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) as a starting point. XAA's website is in English, Spanish and Indonesian; but you can also write in German.
