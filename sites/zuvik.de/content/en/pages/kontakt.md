We read every message. The quickest way to reach us is by email:

**[kontakt@zuvik.de](mailto:kontakt@zuvik.de)**

## What we are happy about

- **Topic requests:** Is there a situation from your everyday family life that you would like us to write a story about? Tell us about it. We never take real names or recognizable details; Your suggestion becomes a new, invented story.
- **Feedback on the suggestions:** Did an idea work well for you or not at all? The second thing in particular helps us to write better.
- **Notes on errors:** We quickly correct typos, inaccuracies or outdated information.
- **Reading aloud in daycare and school:** Would you like to read a story in a group? This is expressly permitted provided the source is cited. If you have any questions about this, please write to us.

## What we can't do

We cannot offer personal parenting or family advice and cannot assess individual cases. If you need support, please contact a parenting and family counseling center in your area, your pediatrician or the parents' hotline against grief. In emergencies, dial 112.

## Response time

We usually respond within a few working days. Please understand that we cannot implement every topic request immediately.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message. Find out more in our [Data Protection Policy](/datenschutz/).
