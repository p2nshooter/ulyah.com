ZUVIK tells fictional family stories and supplements them with suggestions for everyday life. Please read this note before trying an idea with your family.

## Made up stories

The families, children, grandparents, teachers and places in our stories are imagined. They are intended to illustrate typical situations, not to describe real people. Any similarities to actual persons are coincidental.

## No advice

The suggestions at the end of each story are based on general experiences from everyday family life and on well-documented principles, such as that children need reliability, attention and clear agreements. They are not educational, psychological, medical or legal advice and do not replace professionals who know your situation.

## When you should get help

Contact professionals if

- a child sleeps poorly for weeks, hardly eats, withdraws a lot or appears noticeably sad,
- An argument in the family escalates or someone is afraid
- a child complains of pain that doesn't go away,
- you as a parent constantly feel exhausted or overwhelmed.

The first point of contact is your pediatrician, parenting and family counseling centers in your city or district, and the parents' hotline against grief. In acute emergencies, dial the emergency number 112.

## Health and safety

If a story touches on topics like nutrition, sleep, screen time, or household safety, we provide general guidance. Medical advice is essential for health questions.

## Liability

We create the content with care, but we do not guarantee that every suggestion will work for every family. You are responsible for decisions you make based on our content, to the extent permitted by law.
