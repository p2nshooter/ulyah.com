These principles describe how our stories are created and what you can rely on at ZUVIK.

## Fictional, but honest

Our stories are fiction. The Brandt, Yilmaz, Hoffmann and all the others families do not exist. We invent them to illustrate typical situations without violating the privacy of real families. When readers tell us about their own experiences, we never use names, places or recognizable details.

For us, honest means: The stories don't always end smoothly. Sometimes an idea doesn't work, sometimes it takes three tries. That's how family life is.

## Where the suggestions come from

The “For your everyday life” section at the end of each story does not contain panaceas, but rather ideas that have proven successful in many families. We are guided by principles that are widely recognized in family education:

- Children need reliability: fixed routines, announced transitions, kept promises.
- Children need attention: time in which they receive the full attention of an adult.
- Children need a say: age-appropriate decisions, family council, mutually agreed rules.
- Feelings are allowed, behavior has limits: anger is allowed, hitting is not.
- Parents are allowed to make mistakes and apologize.

Where a story touches on health, sleep, nutrition or safety, we stick to general information and refer to medical advice.

## No assessment of family forms

In our stories, children live with a mother and father, with one parent, in patchwork families, with grandparents, with two mothers or two fathers, with and without siblings, in the city and in the country, with different origin stories. For us, no type of family is the norm by which others are measured.

## language

We write in such a way that parents can read the stories to their children: without technical jargon, without derogation, without depictions of violence. We talk about conflicts from the perspective of those involved, not with a finger raised.

## Advertising and independence

ZUVIK is financed through advertising. Ads are recognizable as such. Companies cannot buy stories or mentions. We do not name products for payment.

## Corrections

If we make a mistake, we will correct it and update the date of the story. We accept information at any time at [kontakt@zuvik.de](mailto:kontakt@zuvik.de).
