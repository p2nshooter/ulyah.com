**PAB** —Paisajes, Aventuras y Logs— is a travel magazine dedicated to Latin America. We wrote it for those who want to get to know the region calmly: the person planning their first trip through the Andes, the family looking for a quiet beach in the Caribbean, the couple who dreams of traveling the Pacific coast by car or the traveler who moves by bus from town to town.

Our idea is simple: a good travel guide is not a list of "the ten essential places", but the information that allows you to make the right decision. When to go and when not to, how much time it really takes, how to get around, what documents you need, how to take care of your health at altitude or in the tropics and how to spend wisely.

## six directions

- **Beaches and coasts:** seasons, safety at sea, currents, sargassum and respect for ecosystems.
- **Cities and culture:** historic centers, museums, neighborhoods and festivals.
- **Mountains and nature:** Andes, jungles, deserts and national parks.
- **Road routes:** car, bus and train itineraries with realistic distances.
- **Planning and budget:** documents, insurance, money, traveler's health and luggage.
- **Flavors of the trip:** markets, regional cuisines and traditional drinks.

## Who makes PAB

PAB is an editorial project of **p2nshooter**. The editorial team writes and reviews the guides and signs them collectively. We don't invent trips, testimonials or reviews of hotels we haven't been to: what we publish are planning guides based on verifiable sources, and we say it that way.

## Independence

We do not sell tour packages, we do not charge hotels or agencies to appear and we do not use affiliate links. The magazine is financed by Google AdSense advertising, always separated from the content. Write to us from the [contact] page (/contacto/).
