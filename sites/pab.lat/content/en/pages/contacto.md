The PAB editorial team reads all messages. Write to us at:

**[contacto@pab.lat](mailto:contacto@pab.lat)**

## Why write to us?

- **One fact has changed.** Hours, entry prices, immigration requirements, and transportation routes change frequently. If you notice that something in a guide is no longer true, let us know with the link to the article; we will check and update it.
- **You want to propose a destination.** A little-known town, a hiking route, a local festival or a market that deserves a guide.
- **Questions about the magazine**, advertising or the use of our texts.

## What we don't do

We do not sell trips, we do not make reservations, we do not create personalized itineraries by mail and we cannot guarantee the security situation of a destination on a specific date. For entry requirements and travel alerts, the reference is always the official information from the immigration authorities and the chancellery of your country.

## Response time

We typically respond within five business days; Out of date data notices take priority. Your data is used only to answer you, according to the [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/).
