PAB processes as little data as possible. This policy explains which ones and how you can control them.

## Responsible

p2nshooter, PAB editor. Contact: [contacto@pab.lat](mailto:contacto@pab.lat).

## Information we process

- **Emails you send us:** just to respond to you. We do not use them for commercial purposes or share them.
- **Readings:** we count in aggregate which pages are visited, without our own cookies, without profiles and without saving your full IP.
- **Technical data:** Cloudflare, the service that delivers the site, processes data such as the IP address so that pages reach your browser and to protect the site from attacks.

PAB does not ask for your passport, itinerary or payment information: we do not make reservations.

## Advertising

The magazine displays Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and identifiers to display ads, measure results, and, if you allow, personalize them based on your activity on other sites. Manage your preferences in [Google ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and see [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent is requested before advertising cookies are used.

## Your rights

In accordance with the law of your country - for example, the Mexican regulations on the protection of data held by individuals, Law 1581 of 2012 of Colombia, Law 25,326 of Argentina, Law 29733 of Peru or the European GDPR - you can request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition. Write to us and we will attend to your request; You can also go to the data protection authority in your country.

## Security and conservation

The site always works with an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We save messages only as long as necessary to respond to them.

More information in the [cookies policy](/politica-de-cookies/).
