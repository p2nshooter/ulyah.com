Cookies are small files that websites save on your browser. This page explains which ones may appear in PAB.

## What we keep

PAB does not install its own analysis or tracking cookies: we count visits without them. We only save a flag in the browser's session storage so that the welcome animation does not repeat on each page; It is deleted when you close the tab.

## Google advertising cookies

Ads are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies to display ads, limit how many times you see them, measure whether they were viewed, prevent fraud, and—if you authorize—personalize them to your interests. If you visit us from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will see a prompt to accept or reject them before they are installed. More details in [how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## How to manage them

In the privacy settings of your browser, on your computer or mobile phone, you can view, block and delete cookies. If you block them, PAB will continue to work; only the ads you see will change.

## Changes

If the cookies used by the site change, we will update this page and its revision date.
