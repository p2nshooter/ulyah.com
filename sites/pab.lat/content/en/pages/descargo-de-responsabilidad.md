PAB publishes general guides for planning trips through Latin America. We take care to verify them, but we cannot guarantee that each information will remain valid when you travel.

## Entry requirements and documents

Passport, visa, minor permit and entry form requirements depend on your nationality and change without notice. The reference is always the information from the immigration authorities of the destination country and the chancellery of your country.

## Health

Recommendations on altitude, vaccines, water, and mosquito-borne diseases are general. Consult a doctor or travel medicine center before leaving, especially if you are pregnant, traveling with children, or have a health condition.

## Safety and risk activities

The security conditions of an area can change in days. Follow official and local instructions. For mountains, diving, navigation or expeditions, hire authorized operators and travel insured.

## Prices

When we mention costs, they are indicative and may vary by season, exchange rate and provider. We do not publish rates as if they are guaranteed.
