That's what we do at PAB. This page is different: here we present two projects from the same publisher as PAB, and out of honesty we make it clear from the beginning: it is our own advertising.

## A memory without luggage: AXTO

There are memories that don't take up room in the backpack. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=pab.lat&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a digital museum of works that move: clocks capable of showing the time of any destination, seals of imaginary countries, Koranic and hadith calligraphies with their original text and animated figures. Each work comes from a unique DNA and lives as an NFT on the Base network.

Agencies can give their travelers an exclusive **AC-** edition: 1 dollar in AXTO coins, ten editions per work and gas-free shipping to several wallets. Public works **OS-** are purchased on OpenSea, with 1 to 10 editions and a fixed price of 1 to 250 dollars; **G** marks those painted by GPU. From 1 dollar you can invest in AXTO, the community coin behind axto.io; staking pays out one-of-one pieces, though profits are never promised.

## If you live off tourism: XAA

Small agencies, independent guides, inns, ecolodges and adventure operators compete with huge platforms that charge high commissions. Your own website with direct reservations changes that equation. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=pab.lat&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem), a European web development studio, builds:

| Need | XAA Solution |
|---|---|
| May they find you | a website with your routes, photos, prices and opinions |
| Get paid without intermediaries | online reservations and payments, in several languages |
| Grow with others | a platform or marketplace for several operators in a region |

Each project is divided into milestones that you approve and pay for separately, by PayPal, transfer or USDT. To get started on a low budget there are [adaptable templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=pab.lat&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). The XAA site is available in Spanish, English and Indonesian.
