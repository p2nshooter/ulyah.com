This page explains how we work at PAB.

## What we publish

Planning guides: when to go, how to get there, how to get around, how much time to spend, what to consider for health and safety, how to respect the place and its people. Each guide answers a practical travel question and avoids repeating what another has already covered.

## How we verify

Practical data is compared with official sources: tourist offices, immigration authorities, chancelleries, national park services, health authorities and transport operators. When information changes frequently, we point it out and recommend confirming it before traveling. Each guide shows its revision date.

## Honesty

- We do not invent trips, anecdotes, testimonials or reviews of hotels or restaurants.
- We do not publish paid rankings or sponsored content presented as editorial.
- We do not use affiliate links or charge hotels, agencies or airlines.
- We distinguish between verified information and judgmental recommendations.

## Corrections

If a guide contains an error or outdated information, we correct it and update its date. Write to us at [contacto@pab.lat](mailto:contacto@pab.lat).
