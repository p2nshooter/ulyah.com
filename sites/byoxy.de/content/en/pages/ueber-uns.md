BYOXY stands for **Bring Your OXYgen home**. Plants make rooms livelier, calmer and more beautiful, and a balcony full of herbs and flowers is a little piece of nature in the middle of the city. We want people who previously thought they didn’t have a “green thumb” to be able to do this.

## What you find here

BYOXY is an independent, German-language guide for green living. Our guides are divided into six gardens:

- **Houseplants:** Portraits of popular plants with location, care and typical problems.
- **Care Basics:** Watering, light, soil, fertilizer and repotting.
- **Pests & Problems:** Fungus gnats, spider mites, yellow leaves, root rot.
- **Propagate:** Cuttings, offshoots, division and sowing.
- **Balcony & Terrace:** Boxes, buckets, privacy screens and winter storage.
- **Herbs & Vegetables:** fresh harvest on windowsill and balcony.

## How we work

We explain not only what to do, but why: If you understand how light, water and roots are connected, you can react to every plant and every location. Each instruction lists the level of difficulty, typical errors and information about toxicity to children and pets. More about this in our [Editorial principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/) and [Information on plant safety](/pflanzensicherheit/).

We also use AI tools when researching and writing. Before publication, we compare each guide with sources that we name below the text: with the Federal Environment Agency, NABU, the Bavarian Garden Academy, the Royal Horticultural Society and, for toxicity in pets, with the ASPCA lists. Four helpers - plant profile, sowing calendar, soil and pot calculator and fertilizer dosage calculator - can be found under [Helper](/helfer/), and what has been published or revised can be found under [Updates](/aktualisierungen/).

## What we don't do

We do not sell plants, fertilizers or pesticides and do not write articles on behalf of manufacturers. BYOXY is financed through advertising, which is displayed via Google AdSense and is clearly separated from the editorial content.
