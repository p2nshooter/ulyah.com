Many popular plants are harmless to humans and animals, but some contain substances that can cause discomfort if chewed or swallowed. Small children, cats, dogs, rabbits and birds are particularly at risk. This page provides an overview and applies to all guides on BYOXY.

## Common plants with poisonous components

| plant | Note |
|---|---|
| Dieffenbachia | Plant sap severely irritates skin and mucous membranes |
| Monstera and Philodendron | Calcium oxalate crystals, irritation in the mouth |
| Ivy plant (Epipremnum) | Irritation of mouth and stomach |
| Single leaf (Spathiphyllum) | Irritation of mouth and stomach |
| Poinsettia | Milky juice irritates skin and mucous membranes |
| Aloe vera | Certain components have a laxative effect |
| Oleander | Extremely toxic, especially to animals |
| Lilies | Dangerous for cats even in small quantities |
| Christ thorn and many spurge plants | Milky juice irritating |

The list is not complete. With every new plant, it is worth taking a look at its compatibility if there are children or animals in the household.

## Prevention

- **Place poisonous plants out of reach**, for example on high shelves or in rooms that animals do not enter.
- **Remove fallen leaves**.
- **Wear gloves when repotting and cutting** plants with latex and wash your hands afterwards.
- Offer **cat grass**: Cats will then nibble less on other plants.
- **Store fertilizers and pesticides** safely and child-proof, only use according to the instructions for use.

## In case of emergency

- **For children:** Keep calm, remove residues from the mouth, give them some water or tea to drink, do not induce vomiting. Call the **poison emergency line** of the relevant poison control center. If you have difficulty breathing, cramps or loss of consciousness, immediately call the emergency number **112**.
- **For animals:** Contact a veterinary practice or emergency veterinary service immediately.
- **Take the plant with you** or take a photo so it can be identified.
