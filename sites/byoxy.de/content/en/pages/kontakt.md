We look forward to feedback. Whether you found an error in a guide, missed a topic or have a question about your plant: write to us.

## Email

**[kontakt@byoxy.de](mailto:kontakt@byoxy.de)**

We read every message and usually respond within a few business days.

## What particularly helps us

- **Corrections:** Please state the address of the guide and the part that you think is incorrect or misleading. We correct errors quickly and note major changes.
- **Questions about your plant:** Describe the location, light, watering rhythm and what you noticed. Photos of leaves, stems and soil help a lot.
- **Topic suggestions:** Which plant or balcony project are you interested in? Many of our guides arose from reader questions.

## What we don't offer

We do not sell plants or products and do not accept orders for sponsored posts. Requests for advertising space are processed via Google AdSense.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message. Please see our [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/) for details.
