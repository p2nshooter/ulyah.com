These principles describe how our guides are created and what you can rely on.

## Understandable and practical

Each guide explains the needs of a plant or the steps of a care measure in such a way that even beginners can implement them. We mention location, light, watering rhythm, soil, difficulty level and the most common mistakes, and we explain the why behind each recommendation.

## Sources

We rely on basic botanical knowledge, information from horticultural advice centers, botanical gardens and plant protection services, on manufacturer information on substrates and fertilizers as well as on practical experience in the home and on the balcony.

## Gentle methods first

When it comes to pests and diseases, we recommend mechanical and biological measures first. We only mention plant protection products as active ingredient groups and with the advice to follow the instructions for use and the approval for home and allotment gardens.

## Security

We would like to point out that it is toxic to children and pets. Our [Plant Safety Information](/pflanzensicherheit/) applies to all guides.

## Audit, independence, corrections

Each guide has a review date and is revised if necessary. We do not write paid posts and do not receive commissions for products mentioned. Advertising is displayed via Google AdSense and is recognizable as an ad. We correct errors quickly; The editorial team accepts information via the [contact page](/kontakt/).
