Our guides explain why a plant reacts the way it does. These four helpers answer the quick questions in between: Where should it be? What should I sow now? How much soil do I need? How much fertilizer goes into the can? All four run directly in your browser, without an account, and nothing is sent to us.

## Plant profile

Light, temperature, watering, humidity and toxicity for nine of the most popular houseplants, from monsteras to orchids, with a filter for plants considered non-toxic to pets. [To the profile](/pflanzen-steckbrief/)

## Sowing calendar

When chili, tomatoes, basil, lettuce etc. should be grown on the windowsill, at what germination temperature, whether they are light or dark germinators and when they are allowed outside after the ice saints - with the current month highlighted. [To the sowing calendar](/aussaatkalender/)

## Soil and pot calculator

The volume of round and conical pots, balcony boxes and tubs in liters, the number of earth bags and the appropriate next pot size when repotting. [To the soil and pot calculator](/erd-und-topfrechner/)

## Fertilizer dosage calculator

Convert the information on the fertilizer bottle to your watering can, if desired at half strength, and translate milliliters into tea spoons and tablespoons. [To the fertilizer dosage calculator](/duenger-dosierrechner/)

## Where the information comes from

The care values come from our guides, the toxicity for cats and dogs from the ASPCA plant lists, sowing times and germination temperatures from our sowing guide and the information from the Bavarian Garden Academy. The information on seed bags, fertilizer bottles and plant labels always comes first. If poisoning is suspected, the poison control center in your state or the veterinary practice can help; more about this on the page [Plant Safety](/pflanzensicherheit/).
