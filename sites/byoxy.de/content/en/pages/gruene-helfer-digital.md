## If you like collecting beautiful things: AXTO

Not everything that grows needs soil. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=byoxy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) grows digital artworks – each from a unique blueprint that never repeats. There are ticking clocks that know your local time, stamps from distant fantasy lands, calligraphed Qur'anic verses and hadith whose text is not changed, and animated figures. They are stored as NFTs on the Base network.

As a gift for plant lovers, the exclusive works with the code **AC-** fit well: 1 US dollar per edition, paid in AXTO coins, ten editions per motif, sendable to several wallets without gas fees. The public collections with **OS-** are on OpenSea (1–10 editions, fixed price 1 to 10,000 dollars); GPU paintings carry a **G**. This is not an investment and nobody promises a return.

## If you sell plants

Maybe you run a small nursery, a flower shop or sell cuttings at the weekly market. Then you know the problem: customers search online, but can't find you or can only find you on a site that no one has touched in years.

[XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=byoxy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a development studio from Europe that builds custom websites and platforms:

1. a nice business card with opening times, directions and seasonal offers,
2. an online shop for plants, pots and soil with shipping or collection,
3. larger solutions such as a marketplace for multiple providers.

Payment is based on milestones - so you can see what is finished at each step before the next one begins. There are also customizable [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=byoxy.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) to get you started quickly. XAA's pages are in English, Spanish and Indonesian; Just write in German if you prefer.
