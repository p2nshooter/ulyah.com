If you sow too early, you will grow long, pale plants; Those who sow too late hardly reap. The calendar above shows the most popular balcony plants when they are preferred on the windowsill and when they are allowed outside. The current month is highlighted.

## Prefer and plant out

| plant | Sowing on the windowsill | Outside |
|---|---|---|
| Chili and peppers | End of February to mid-March | after the Ice Saints, mid-May |
| Tomatoes | Mid-March to early April | Mid-May |
| eggplant | End of February to mid-March | End of May |
| Basil | April | At the end of May, or left on the windowsill |
| Cucumbers and zucchini | End of April to beginning of May | End of May |
| Nasturtium | April | Mid-May |
| Summer flowers like zinnias and marigolds | April | Mid-May |
| Salad | from February, ongoing | from April, frost hardy |

## Germination temperature and light

| plant | Germination temperature | Germ type |
|---|---|---|
| Chili, peppers, eggplant | 25 to 28°C | neutral |
| Tomato | 20 to 25°C | Dark germinators: cover with soil |
| Basil | 20 to 25°C | Light germinator: just press |
| Salad | It germinates poorly at temperatures of 15 to 18 °C and above 20 °C | Light germinator |
| Summer flowers | 18 to 22°C | depending on the type |

As soon as the seedlings appear, they need more light and a little less heat, otherwise they will rot. The Ice Saints in mid-May are a rule of thumb for the end of night frosts; In rough locations it is better to wait a week longer and slowly accustom the young plants to the sun on cloudy days. Our guide [Sowing on the windowsill](/ratgeber/aussaat-auf-der-fensterbank/) explains it step by step, and how tomatoes in the pot are successful can be found in [Tomatoes on the balcony](/ratgeber/tomaten-auf-dem-balkon/).
