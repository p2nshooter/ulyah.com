An appointment at the Citizens' Office, waiting marks, opening times that don't fit with everyday work, and in the end a form is missing: going to the authorities costs many people time and nerves. In Germany, many matters can now be taken care of online, from applying for a certificate of good conduct to filing tax returns to checking your score in Flensburg. Most people already have the key in their wallet: their ID card with its online ID function. Many have never set it up because they don't know how to do it or if it's safe. These instructions explain what the online ID can do, how to set it up step by step and what you should pay attention to.

## What can the online ID card do?

The online ID card is the digital function of the identity card. There is a chip in the ID card on which your personal data is securely stored. With this chip you can clearly identify yourself to authorities and some companies on the Internet, just as you show your ID at the counter. To do this, hold your ID card up to a smartphone or card reader and enter your six-digit PIN.

The function is not just in the ID card. They also have the electronic residence permit for people from non-EU countries and the eID card for citizens of other EU countries. Since July 2017, the online ID function has been activated automatically for newly issued ID cards. For older ID cards, it can be activated later at the Citizens' Registration Office.

## What you need

- **ID card with activated online ID function:** The ID card app will show you whether it is switched on when you read it for the first time.
- **An NFC-enabled smartphone:** Most current Android smartphones and iPhones can read the chip in the ID card via NFC. Alternatively, a special card reader works on the computer.
- **The ID card app:** The federal government's free app is available for Android, iOS, Windows and macOS. Download only from the official app stores or official website.
- **The PIN letter:** With your ID you received a letter in the mail with a five-digit transport PIN and a ten-digit PUK.

## Setup step by step

### Replace transport PIN with your own PIN

Before you can use the online ID card for the first time, you must replace the five-digit transport PIN from the PIN letter with your own six-digit PIN. This is how it works:

1. Install the ID card app on your smartphone and open it.
2. In the app, select the item to change the PIN and indicate that you have a five-digit transport PIN.
3. Hold the ID card flat against the back of the smartphone. The app shows exactly where the NFC area is. On many iPhones it is at the top, on Android devices it is often in the middle.
4. Enter the five-digit transport PIN.
5. Set your own six-digit PIN and confirm it.

Choose a PIN that is not easy to guess, i.e. not your date of birth and not a simple sequence of numbers like 123456. Keep the PIN letter with the PUK in a safe place, but not with your ID.

### If the ID is not recognized

Reading via NFC does not always work on the first try. Remove a thick case from the smartphone, place the ID card on a table and the smartphone on top, and slowly move the device until the app reports a connection. It is important to leave your ID card still during the entire process. On Android, NFC must be turned on in the settings. The ID card app also offers a function to test your own device in advance.

## This is how the online ID process works

A typical process looks like this: You are on the website of an authority, for example when applying for a certificate of good conduct, and select “Register with online ID”. The ID card app opens and shows you who wants to read your data and what data that is, such as name, address and date of birth. You check the information, hold your ID card up to your smartphone and enter your PIN. The data will then be transmitted encrypted and you can continue filling out the application.

It's also practical that you can work on the computer and only use the smartphone as a card reader. To do this, you pair the ID card app on your computer with the app on your smartphone. The computer then asks the smartphone for each process and you simply hold the ID card there.

## These administrative procedures are already online

How many services are available online varies depending on the federal state and municipality. Some examples that are possible nationwide or in many regions:

- **Certificate of good conduct:** You can apply for and pay for a certificate of good conduct online at the Federal Office of Justice.
- **Points in Flensburg:** You can check your points in the driving fitness register online at the Federal Motor Transport Authority.
- **Pension insurance:** With the German pension insurance, pension information and many applications can be accessed and submitted online.
- **Vehicle registration:** Many registration offices allow vehicles to be registered, re-registered and de-registered online.
- **Moving:** In many municipalities, your place of residence can be changed online after moving.
- **Family benefits:** In many federal states, parental allowance and other benefits can be applied for digitally.
- **BAföG:** Applications for training funding can be submitted online.

A central federal user account, the BundID, helps with this. You can register with your online ID, submit applications to various authorities and receive notifications in a digital mailbox. It's worth checking the website of your city or municipality to see what services are already offered online there.

### Taxes with ELSTER

The tax return can be submitted online via the tax administration’s “Mein ELSTER” portal. To register, you can use your own ELSTER certificate or register with your online ID. Using the online ID card is quicker because you don't have to wait for an activation letter in the mail. In ELSTER you can also access a lot of data that employers, health insurance companies and other bodies have already transmitted to the tax office.

### Health: electronic patient record

Digital identity also plays a role in healthcare. Since 2025, those with statutory health insurance have generally had an electronic patient file, unless they have objected. If you want to view it yourself in your health insurance company's app, you can register there with a digital identity, the so-called HealthID. In addition to the health card with PIN, the online ID card can also be used to set it up.

## How secure is the online ID?

The online ID is considered a very secure procedure. Several protective mechanisms work together:

- **Two factors:** You need the ID itself and the PIN. If you only have one of them, you can't do anything with it.
- **Verified providers:** Only authorities and companies with government authorization are allowed to request data from the ID card, and only the data that they really need for their purpose.
- **Full transparency:** Before each reading, the ID card app shows you who is requesting which data. You decide whether you agree.
- **Encrypted transmission:** The data is encrypted and transmitted directly from the chip to the authorized provider.

As with any procedure, you should still remain vigilant. Never give your PIN to anyone else, not even to supposed government officials on the phone. Only download the ID card app from official sources and check with each process whether the provider displayed matches what you want to do. If your ID is lost or stolen, you can have the online ID function blocked via the blocking hotline 116 116. You must also report the loss of your ID card to your citizen's office.

## Forgot your PIN or blocked it?

If you enter the PIN incorrectly twice, you must also enter the access number, the so-called CAN, before the third attempt. It appears as a six-digit number on the front of the ID card. If the third attempt is also incorrect, the PIN will be blocked. Then the ten-digit PUK from the PIN letter helps to remove the block. Then you have three attempts again.

If you have completely forgotten your PIN or no longer have the PIN letter, you can have a new PIN set at the Citizens' Office. Bring your ID with you. Some municipalities also offer this service at special self-service terminals.

## Outlook: the digital wallet

The European Union obliges its member states to offer their citizens a digital wallet, the so-called EUDI wallet. In the future, not only ID data, but also, for example, driver's licenses, certificates or certificates will be stored securely on the smartphone. Germany is working on its own implementation. The current online ID function forms an important basis. Anyone who already uses it today is well prepared for the next steps in digital administration.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the online ID function cost anything?**
The use and the ID card app are free of charge. The usual fees apply for individual government services such as a certificate of good conduct, which you then pay online.

**Does the online ID work with any smartphone?**
The smartphone needs NFC, which is present in most current devices. The ID card app shows you whether your device is suitable. If you don't have a suitable smartphone, you can use a certified card reader on your computer.

**Can someone use my ID to apply online if I lose it?**
Not without your PIN. Nevertheless, if you lose it, you should have the online ID function blocked via the blocking hotline and report the loss to the citizen's office.

**Can I also use the online ID card for private companies?**
Yes, some banks, insurance companies and mobile phone providers accept it, for example when opening an account or concluding a contract. There you can also see in advance which data will be requested.

**What is the difference between PIN, CAN and PUK?**
You enter the PIN every time you use it. You will need the CAN on the front of the ID card after two incorrect PIN entries. The PUK from the PIN letter cancels a blocked PIN.

## Conclusion

With the online ID card, many administrative procedures can now be completed from the comfort of your own home, often faster than with an appointment and a waiting ticket. Setting it up only takes a few minutes: install the ID card app, replace the transport PIN with your own six-digit PIN, and you're done. The process is secure because ID and PIN are required together and you can see who gets which data before each reading. Keep the PIN letter safe, do not give your PIN to anyone and check your municipality's website to see what services are already available online. So your next visit to the authorities might just be a click.
