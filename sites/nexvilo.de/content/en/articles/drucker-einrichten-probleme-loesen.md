Printers have a special reputation: They work without a problem for months and then refuse to work when the train ticket, tax return or homework urgently needs to be printed on paper. However, most printing problems have simple causes and can be solved in just a few minutes. This guide will show you how to properly set up a new printer, how to connect it to your computer and smartphone, and what to do about the most common errors.

## Before you buy: ink or laser?

If you're thinking about buying a printer, a quick overview:

- **Inkjet printers** are inexpensive to purchase, print good quality photos, and are suitable for households that occasionally print in color. Disadvantage: Ink is expensive, and if the printer is not used for weeks, the nozzles can dry out.
- **Ink tank printers** have large, refillable tanks instead of small cartridges. They cost more to purchase, but print very cheaply per page.
- **Laser printers** are ideal if you primarily print text. The toner doesn't dry out, the cost per page is low, and they're fast. Simple black and white laser printers are often more robust than inkjet printers.

Many devices are **multifunctional devices** with scanners and copiers. Look for Wi-Fi, automatic two-sided printing (duplex) and, if you often scan multi-page documents, an automatic document feeder.

## Step 1: Set up the printer and turn it on

1. Remove all adhesive strips and transport locks. They are often stuck inside, for example on the cartridge holder or on the paper cassette.
2. Insert ink or toner as described in the quick start guide.
3. Load paper and turn on the printer. Many printers perform a cleaning and alignment process when they first start up, which takes a few minutes.
4. Set the language, date and time on the control panel if the printer asks for them.

## Step 2: Connect the printer to the WiFi

Today most printers are connected via WiFi. This means that all devices in the household can print without cables.

### Via the manufacturer app

The easiest way is to use the manufacturer's app on your smartphone: **HP Smart**, **Epson Smart Panel**, **Canon PRINT** or **Brother Mobile Connect**. The app finds the nearby printer, asks for your WiFi password and sets everything up. HP and some other manufacturers require you to create an account.

### Via the control panel

If the printer has a display, you can set up the WLAN there: Select “WLAN”, “Network” or “Wireless Setup Assistant” in the menu, select your network and enter the password.

### Via WPS button

Many routers have a **WPS button**. First press the WPS button on the printer or start WPS in the printer menu, then within two minutes press the WPS button on the router. The devices connect automatically.

**Tip:** If your router offers separate networks for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, connect older printers to the 2.4 GHz network. Many printers do not support 5GHz.

## Step 3: Add the printer to the computer

### Windows 11

1. Open *Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners.*
2. Click on **Add Device**. Windows searches for printers on the network.
3. Select your printer. In most cases, Windows automatically installs a suitable driver.

Modern printers support the **Mopria** or **IPP Everywhere** standard, which allows Windows to print without additional drivers. The manufacturer's software can still be useful for special functions such as scanning, ink levels or special paper formats. Only download it from the manufacturer's official website or the Microsoft Store, not from any download portals.

### Mac

Under *System Settings > Printers & Scanners* click on “Add printer, scanner or fax” and select the printer. Most printers work via **AirPrint** without additional software.

### Smartphone and tablet

- **iPhone and iPad** print via AirPrint: Tap “Share” in an app, then select “Print”.
- **Android** uses the manufacturer's built-in printing service or plugin. In an app, select “Print” from the menu and select the printer.

## Step 4: Test print and basic settings

Print a test page to check if everything works. In Windows you can do this under *Printers and Scanners > [Printer] > Print Test Page*.

Useful basic settings:

- **Double-sided printing** as standard saves paper.
- **Draft Quality** or “Economic Mode” for everyday printing saves ink.
- **Grayscale** as default if you rarely need color.

You can find these settings under *Print Settings* in the printer properties.

## The most common printing problems and their solutions

### “Printer is offline”

This is the classic. The computer can no longer find the printer on the network.

1. **Restart:** Turn off the printer and router, wait a minute and turn them back on. Also restart the computer.
2. **Check WiFi:** Is the printer still connected to the correct network? Many printers show this on the display or with a WLAN light. After changing your router or getting a new WiFi password, you have to reconnect the printer.
3. **Fixed IP Address:** If the printer goes offline regularly, it may have been given a new address by the router. In the router interface you can set that the printer always receives the same IP address.
4. **Add a new printer:** Remove the printer in Windows under *Printers and Scanners* and add it again.
5. **Disable offline mode:** In the print queue under *Printer*, check whether “Use printer offline” is checked and uncheck it.

### The print queue hangs

If one order is stuck, all others behind it are stuck.

1. Open *Printers and Scanners > [Printers] > Open Print Queue.*
2. Right-click the hanging job and select **Cancel**.
3. If that doesn't help, restart the **Print Spooler service**: Type "Services" in the Start menu, open the app, find "Print Spooler", right-click it and select **Restart**.

### Blank pages, stripes or pale colors

For inkjet printers, this is usually a sign of dried or clogged nozzles.

1. Check the **ink level** in the manufacturer app or on the display.
2. Start a **nozzle cleaning** or **print head cleaning** via the printer menu or the software. It uses some ink, but often solves the problem.
3. Print a **Nozzle Test Pattern**. If gaps are still visible, repeat the cleaning after a few minutes, but no more than two or three times in a row.
4. The **print head alignment** helps with crooked lines.

**Prevention:** Print a page in color at least once a week to prevent the nozzles from drying out.

On **laser printers**, streaks are more likely to indicate that the toner is almost empty or that the image drum is dirty. Remove the toner cartridge, gently shake it horizontally and reinsert it. If that doesn't help, an exchange is due.

### Paper jam

1. Turn off the printer and unplug it.
2. Open all the flaps and pull out the paper **carefully** in the direction of travel**, not jerkily, so that no shreds are left behind.
3. Check for small pieces of paper stuck inside.
4. Reload the paper: not too much at once, loosen the stack beforehand, push the paper guides towards the stack.

Common causes include damp, curled or thick paper, an overfilled cassette and dirty feed rollers. You can clean the rollers with a slightly damp, lint-free cloth.

### The printer does not recognize the cartridge

- Remove the cartridge and reinsert it until you hear it click into place.
- Check whether the protective film is completely removed.
- Clean the contacts carefully with a dry, lint-free cloth.

Some manufacturers block cartridges from third-party manufacturers via firmware updates. If you use compatible cartridges and the printer rejects them after an update, this may be the cause. Before buying a printer, find out about the manufacturer's policy if cheap alternatives are important to you.

## Save ink and reduce costs

Ink is one of the most expensive liquids in the home. This is how you save:

- **Use preview:** Before printing, check whether all pages are really necessary. For websites, the “Simplified View” option or printing as PDF helps.
- **Set economy mode and grayscale** as default.
- **Compatible cartridges** from reputable suppliers are often significantly cheaper. Pay attention to reviews and return policy.
- **Subscription models** such as HP Instant Ink or Epson ReadyPrint are worthwhile for those who print a lot, but can be more expensive for those who print a lot. Do the math carefully and note that if you cancel your subscription, the printer will often no longer accept the subscription cartridges.
- **Ink tank printers** are the cheapest in the long run for families with a lot of printing.

## Scanning with the multifunction device

On Windows, you can scan using the **Windows Scan** app from the Microsoft Store, or you can use the manufacturer's software. On the Mac, go to *Printers & Scanners > Open Scanner*. The manufacturer apps on the smartphone can also scan and save the file directly as a PDF.

Save important scans with a meaningful name and in the right folder so that they don't disappear in the download folder.

## Security: Printers also need updates

Network printers are small computers and can have security vulnerabilities. Install **firmware updates** that the manufacturer offers, usually via the app or the printer menu. Change the printer's default web interface password, if available, and disable features you don't use, such as printing from the Internet.

## When nothing helps anymore

- Check the **manufacturer's support page** for known issues and solutions for your model.
- Reset the printer to **factory settings** and set it up again.
- If you have mechanical problems or error codes that indicate a defect, customer service will help. The dealer must repair the defect within the two-year warranty period.
- For very old devices, repairs are often more expensive than a new printer. Dispose of old devices and empty cartridges at a recycling center or dealer, not with household waste.

## Conclusion

Most printing problems can be solved with just a few simple steps: restart the printer and router, empty the print queue, add a new printer or clean the nozzles. If you set up the printer via WLAN with the manufacturer's app, give it a fixed address in the router and regularly print a color page, you rarely have any problems. And if you print a lot, you can save a lot of money in the long run with economy mode, double-sided printing and an ink tank or laser printer.
