This policy explains how nexvilo.de handles cookies and similar technologies. It supplements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What cookies and browser storage are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser and reads again when you visit it later. The browser's local storage and session storage work in a similar way. Some of these techniques are necessary for a site to function at all, others are used for statistics or advertising. We also explain how to manage cookies in your own browser in our instructions.

## What NEXVILO stores itself

NEXVILO does not set any cookies that could be used to recognize you. In your browser's session memory, we only remember whether the little animation with red and gold grains of Spain's 2026 World Cup title has already run, so that it doesn't appear again on every page. This notice remains in your browser and disappears as soon as you close the window.

## Counting page views

So that we know which instructions are needed, we count views anonymously. Only the name of the website and the path to the page are transmitted, without a cookie and without a stored identifier.

## Cookies from Google AdSense

The ads on NEXVILO are delivered by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads, prevent you from seeing the same ad repeatedly, detect click fraud and, if you agree, to tailor ads to your interests. Without consent, non-personalized advertisements may appear that are based on the content of the page.

## Your choice

During your first visit, you specify in the consent banner which purposes you agree to. You can change or revoke your decision later using the link to the data protection settings. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser; All instructions on NEXVILO then remain completely readable.
