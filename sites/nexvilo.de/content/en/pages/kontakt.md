Do you have a question about one of our instructions, a menu that now looks different for you or you would like a topic? Write to us:

**[kontakt@nexvilo.de](mailto:kontakt@nexvilo.de)**

## What we are happy about

- **Deprecated steps:** Manufacturers change menus with almost every update. Please tell us the title of the instructions, your device and the operating system version, then we will adapt the text.
- **Errors and ambiguities:** If a step doesn't work or an explanation is misleading, we want to know about it.
- **Topic requests:** What questions about cell phones, computers, smart homes or AI remain unanswered for you?

## When it burns

In an emergency, please do not wait for our response. If you have entered bank details on a fake site or transferred money to fraudsters, call your bank immediately or have your cards blocked using the blocking emergency number **116 116** and report it to the police. If an account has been taken over, use the respective provider's recovery option. We explain how to proceed step by step in such cases in our instructions.

## What we can't do

We cannot remotely repair devices, unlock accounts, or reset passwords. We do not publish paid contributions, purchased product tests or guest articles with advertising links; such requests remain unanswered.

## Data protection

Please never send us passwords, PINs, TANs, recovery codes or photos of IDs and bank cards, even to show a problem. A screenshot often helps us, but first black out names, phone numbers and account details. We only use your email address to reply to you; Details can be found in the [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/).
