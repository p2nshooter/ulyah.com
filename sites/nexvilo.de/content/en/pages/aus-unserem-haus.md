## AXTO: works of art with their own DNA

[AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=nexvilo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is not a tool, but an exhibition house on the Internet. It shows moving clocks with real time, brands from fictional states, Koran and Hadith calligraphy without text changes and animated series of figures. An algorithm creates each work from a unique DNA; as an NFT it is on the Base network.

Those interested in technology can find two options: exclusive works with the identifier **AC-** for 1 US dollar per edition, paid for with the AXTO coin and can be given away to many wallets free of charge, or public works with **OS-** at OpenSea, 1 to 10 editions each for a fixed price of 1 to 250 dollars. If there is a **G** in the identifier, the image comes from a GPU. AXTO is the community coin of axto.io, available from 1 dollar; staked coins are rewarded with 1/1 artworks, and no return is guaranteed.

## XAA: the website that the club or store still needs

The sports club still manages members using a table, the bicycle repair shop around the corner would like online appointments, the tutor would like to make lessons bookable? [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=nexvilo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European studio that develops custom websites, shops and booking systems. You pay step by step: for the part that you have already viewed and tested. If you want to get started quickly, choose one of the ready-made [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=nexvilo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) and customize it.

## ULYAH: an open portal for Islamic knowledge

[ULYAH](https://ulyah.com/?utm_source=nexvilo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) provides the Quran with translations and recitations, hadith collections and classical works for free. For readers in German there is the sister offer [Tilawa](https://tilawa.de/?utm_source=nexvilo.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). We mention it because it belongs to the same family of projects, not to convince anyone.
