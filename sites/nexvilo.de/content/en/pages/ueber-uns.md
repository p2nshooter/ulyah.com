NEXVILO emerged from a kitchen table where the same questions were asked over and over again. Why is the phone's memory full again? Is this SMS from the parcel service real? Do I really have to install the Windows update now? And what does this AI that everyone is talking about actually do?

The answers to these questions are available online, but rarely in one place and almost never in a way that you can understand them without prior knowledge. Many texts are written for technology enthusiasts, others primarily want to sell a new device. NEXVILO is intended to fill the gap in between: calmly, thoroughly and in such a way that you can then move forward yourself.

## What the name means

**NEXVILO** is made up of three words: **next**, the next thing that's coming our way, **virtual**, the world of apps, accounts and data, and **local**, your own device, your home, your everyday life. We explain new technology so that it reaches where you live, not the other way around.

## What you find here

Our instructions are divided into six areas: Smartphone, Computer and Windows, Secure on the Internet, Smart Home, AI in Everyday Life and Digital Life for the Whole Family. Each guide answers a specific question and ends with something you can do: set up a backup, spot a scam message, secure Wi-Fi, prepare a child's phone, or ask an AI a better question.

## How we work

We try out what we describe on Android and iPhone, on Windows computers of different ages and with devices that are found in normal households. When it comes to security issues, we follow the recommendations of the Federal Office for Information Security, consumer advice centers and the police. How we research, check and correct is described in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## What we are not

We are not a repair service, a manufacturer's hotline or legal advice. If a device is broken, an account has been blocked or money has been lost, we will tell you in the instructions who is responsible and what you should do immediately.

## How NEXVILO is financed

NEXVILO is free and is funded by Google AdSense ads. We don't sell devices, software, or contracts, and we don't take money for referrals. Questions, tips on errors and topic requests can be reached via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.
