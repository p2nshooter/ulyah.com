Anyone who explains technology bears responsibility: one wrong step can delete photos or leave an account unprotected. These principles apply to every guide on NEXVILO.

## Independent of manufacturers and retailers

We do not sell devices, software, mobile contracts or security programs, and we do not take money or test devices for recommendations. When we compare smartphones, laptops or password managers, we give criteria instead of best lists so that you can decide for yourself. Google AdSense delivers the ads on the site; they have no influence on our texts.

## Comprehensible instead of written off

Our instructions follow the manufacturers’ official help pages and documentation. We also use AI tools when researching and writing; We compare each sequence of steps with these manufacturer instructions. If the paths differ on Android and iPhone or between Windows 10 and Windows 11, we will say so. Menus change with updates: If a step looks different for you, the manufacturer's current help applies and we would be happy to hear from you.

## Comprehensible sources

When it comes to security issues, we rely on the Federal Office for Information Security, consumer advice centers, the police and the manufacturers' official help pages. If you have legal questions, we refer you to legal texts and supervisory authorities. Where experts disagree, we show the arguments from both sides.

## Write clearly

NEXVILO is aimed at people who want to use their devices, not professionals. We explain technical terms the first time they appear, we number steps, and we tell you how you can tell that something worked.

## Don't scare me

Security requires attention, not panic. We don't exaggerate threats to get clicks, and we don't recommend programs that use alerts to pressure you. We do not invent studies, quotes or numbers.

## Corrections

We revise our guides as menus, features or recommendations change. Have you discovered an error? Write to us at [kontakt@nexvilo.de](mailto:kontakt@nexvilo.de). We check every notice and correct anything that is incorrect or out of date.

## Own projects

Projects from the same publisher only appear on the marked page [From our company](/aus-unserem-haus/), never hidden in a manual.
