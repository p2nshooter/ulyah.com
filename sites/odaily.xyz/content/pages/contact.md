We love hearing from home cooks. Whether a guide worked beautifully, a step confused you or your bread came out flat, write to us:

**[hello@odaily.xyz](mailto:hello@odaily.xyz)**

## What helps us most

- **Corrections:** Tell us the title of the guide and what seems wrong. If a cooking time did not work for you, mention your oven type and the size of your pan, because both change the result.
- **Questions:** If something was unclear, ask. Often your question shows us where a guide needs a better explanation.
- **Ideas:** Which weeknight dinner, baking problem or kitchen mystery would you like us to explain next?

## What we cannot answer

We cannot give personal medical or dietary advice, for example about allergies, diabetes or weight loss. Please speak to a doctor or registered dietitian. If you suspect food poisoning, contact a medical professional or your local health service rather than waiting for our reply.

We do not publish sponsored posts, paid reviews or guest articles with advertising links, so we will not reply to requests of that kind.

## Your privacy

We use your email address only to answer you. Please do not send personal health information or payment details. More in our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).
