**ODAILY** stands for *Our Daily table*. It is a guide to the cooking most of us actually do: a Tuesday dinner after work, a loaf of bread on a Sunday, a week of packed lunches, a pot of soup that feeds four and freezes well. We are not a restaurant and we are not a food magazine. We are a kitchen like yours, with an ordinary oven, a few good pans and a family that wants to eat at seven.

## Why we started

Recipes online are everywhere, but many of them skip the part that makes cooking easier for the rest of your life: the reason a step works. Why should onions cook slowly? Why rest a steak? Why does bread need a second rise? Once you understand the why, you can rescue a sauce, swap an ingredient and cook without a recipe in front of you. That is what ODAILY tries to give you.

## What you will find here

Our guides are arranged in six sections: Kitchen Basics, Weeknight Cooking, Meal Prep & Planning, Baking, Kitchen Science, and Pantry & Storage. Each guide answers one practical question and ends with something you can cook or do tonight.

## How we work

Every method on ODAILY is cooked in a home kitchen before it is published, with ordinary equipment and supermarket ingredients. We give weights as well as cups where it helps, oven temperatures in Fahrenheit and Celsius, and we say plainly when a shortcut costs flavour or when a step matters for food safety. Read more in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## What we are not

We are not dietitians or doctors. We write about cooking, not medical nutrition. If you manage a food allergy, a medical diet or an eating disorder, please follow the advice of a qualified professional.

## How ODAILY is funded

ODAILY is free to read and is supported by advertising from Google AdSense. We do not sell cookware, accept payment for recommendations or publish sponsored recipes. Questions and suggestions are always welcome on our [contact page](/contact/).
