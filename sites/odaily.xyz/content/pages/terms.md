## Every kitchen is different

Ovens run hot or cool, pans conduct heat differently, flour absorbs more or less water, and the same chicken breast can be thick or thin. Our times and temperatures are tested starting points, not guarantees. Use your eyes, nose and a thermometer, and adjust to your kitchen.
