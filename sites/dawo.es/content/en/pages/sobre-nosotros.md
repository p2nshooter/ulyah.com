DAWO is an independent online magazine about amateur sport and active living. The name sums up what we cover: in Spanish, **Deporte, Actividad, Wellness y Ocio** — sport, activity, wellness and leisure. We write for the person who runs on Sundays, plays padel with their workmates, goes back to the gym after years away or wants their children to enjoy football without pressure.

This English edition carries the same guides as our Spanish magazine, written out in English for readers who live in Spain or simply prefer to read in English. Some examples refer to Spanish clubs, competitions and customs; the advice itself works anywhere.

## What you will find here

Long, practical guides organised in six sections: running, padel and racket sports, football, strength and mobility, rest and nutrition, and active leisure. Each article tries to answer one specific question from beginning to end: how to start, which kit to choose, how to progress without getting injured or how to fit exercise into a week of work and family.

## How we work

The texts are written and reviewed by DAWO's editorial team from reference sources: the World Health Organization's physical-activity recommendations, the sports-science literature and the official rules of each discipline. We would rather explain the reason behind each piece of advice than hand out a list of orders. When the evidence is uncertain, we say so.

We do not sell training plans, supplements or sports equipment, and no manufacturer pays to appear in our articles. When we mention a type of product, it is to explain what to look for, never to push a brand.

## What DAWO is not

DAWO is not a medical service and does not replace a physiotherapist, a sports doctor or a qualified coach. Our articles are general and informative. If you have an injury, a chronic illness or any doubt about your own case, see a professional before changing your activity.

## Advertising

The magazine is free and is funded by advertising served through Google AdSense. The ads are kept separate from the content and have no influence on what we write. You can read more in our [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/) and [cookie policy](/politica-de-cookies/).

## Write to us

If you find a mistake, want to suggest a topic or have a question, we would love to hear from you on the [contact](/contacto/) page. We correct mistakes as soon as we confirm them and note it in the article itself.
