A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. This page explains which cookies are involved when you read DAWO.

## Our own cookies

DAWO sets no cookies of its own to identify you. The visit statistics we use are calculated without cookies and in aggregate, and the magazine works just the same if you block all cookies.

We only keep a technical marker in the browser's session storage so that the welcome animation is not repeated on every page, and your reading settings (speed, voice, whether to go on to the next article) in its local storage. Neither contains personal data, and both stay on your device.

## Third-party cookies: advertising

Ads on DAWO are served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies to:

- show relevant ads based on your earlier browsing;
- limit how often you see the same ad;
- measure how well ads work and prevent fraud.

These cookies are Google's responsibility. You can read how they work on Google's page about [how it uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Consent

If you visit us from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Google shows you a notice so that you can accept or refuse advertising cookies before they are set. If you refuse them, you can go on reading the magazine as normal and, at most, you will see non-personalised ads.

## How to manage cookies in your browser

You can allow, block or delete cookies in your browser's settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies.
- **Firefox:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data.
- **Safari:** Settings › Privacy › Block all cookies.
- **Edge:** Settings › Cookies and site permissions.

Bear in mind that blocking all cookies may affect how other websites you visit work.
