This page explains how DAWO is made, so that you know what to expect from each article.

## Independence

No brand, club or manufacturer pays to appear in our articles or reviews the texts before publication. The advertising you see on the site is managed by Google AdSense and has no influence on our topics or conclusions.

## Sources

We base our explanations on reference sources: the World Health Organization's recommendations on physical activity, the scientific literature in sports science and sports medicine, and the official rules of each discipline. When a subject is under debate or the evidence is weak, we say so clearly rather than presenting an opinion as a fact.

## What we do not publish

- Promises of quick or guaranteed results.
- Recommendations of supplements, drugs or extreme diets.
- Diagnoses or treatments of injuries.
- Content copied from other publications.

## Review and corrections

Every article is reviewed before publication and reviewed again when rules or recommendations change. If we find, or are told about, a mistake, we correct it and update the review date. You can let us know at [contacto@dawo.es](mailto:contacto@dawo.es).

## Language

We write in clear, friendly language for readers of any age and level. This English edition follows the Spanish original guide by guide. We explain technical terms the first time they appear and prefer a concrete example to an abstract definition.
