We read every message that reaches the editorial team. The most direct way to write to us is by email:

**Email:** [contacto@dawo.es](mailto:contacto@dawo.es)

You are welcome to write in English or in Spanish.

## What you can write to us about

- **Corrections.** If a fact in an article is wrong or out of date, tell us the article and the sentence. We check the source and, where appropriate, correct the text and note the change.
- **Topic suggestions.** Is there a question about sport or active living that you have not found properly explained? Tell us; many of our guides start that way.
- **Questions about the magazine.** Queries about privacy, advertising or the use of our content.

## What we cannot do

We do not give medical advice, diagnoses or personalised training plans by email. If you have pain, an injury or an illness, the right person is a doctor, a physiotherapist or a qualified coach who can assess you in person.

## Response time

We usually reply within three to five working days. Messages with corrections take priority.

## Privacy of your message

We use your email address and the content of your message only to reply to you. We do not pass them to third parties or add them to mailing lists. You can read the details in our [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/).
