At DAWO we respect your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed when you visit dawo.es, what for, and how you can exercise your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights.

## Data controller

The party responsible for the website is the publisher of DAWO. You can contact us at [contacto@dawo.es](mailto:contacto@dawo.es) about anything to do with your data.

## What data we process

**Browsing without registering.** You do not need to create an account or give us your name or email address to read DAWO.

**Visit statistics.** We record in aggregate which pages are visited and on which day, without cookies of our own and without identifying the person reading. We use that information only to know which articles are useful.

**Emails you send us.** If you write to us, we process your address and the content of your message in order to reply. The legal basis is your consent when you write to us, and we keep the message only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

**Listening to an article.** The read-aloud feature uses your browser's own speech engine on your device. The text is not sent to us, and the voice you choose is remembered only in your browser.

## Google AdSense advertising

DAWO shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookie lets Google and its partners show you ads based on your browsing.

You can switch off personalised advertising in [Google's ad settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) and learn more about [how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can also opt out of third-party vendors' cookies at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

When you visit DAWO from the European Economic Area, you are shown a consent notice managed by Google before advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

## International transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. It does so under the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## Your rights

You can request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing, by writing to [contacto@dawo.es](mailto:contacto@dawo.es). If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

## Changes to this policy

If we change the way we process data, we will update this page and show the date of the latest review at the top.
