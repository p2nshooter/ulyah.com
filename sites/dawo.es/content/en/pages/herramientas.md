Our guides explain how to train better. These four calculators apply that to your own figures: your time in a race, your age and heart rate, your weight before and after training and the activities you do each week. They run in your browser, with no sign-up, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your browser only remembers your last entry.

## Running pace calculator

Enter a distance and your time, and see your pace per kilometre, your speed and estimated times for 5 km, 10 km, the half marathon and the marathon. [Open the pace calculator](/calculadora-ritmo-carrera/)

## Heart-rate zones

With your age or your measured maximum heart rate, and your resting heart rate if you know it, you get five training zones with their beats per minute and what each one is for. [Open the heart-rate zones](/zonas-frecuencia-cardiaca/)

## Sweat-rate calculator

With your weight before and after training and what you drank, work out how much you sweat per hour and whether you drank enough, just like the test explained in our hydration guide. [Open the sweat-rate calculator](/calculadora-sudoracion/)

## Energy used by activity

Choose an activity, your weight and the minutes, and compare how much energy running, swimming, cycling, padel, football, walking or strength training use. [Open the energy calculator](/gasto-energetico-por-actividad/)

## Where the numbers come from

| Calculator | Basis of the calculation | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| Running pace | time ÷ distance; Riegel's estimate for other distances | a distance and your time |
| Heart-rate zones | maximum heart rate (measured or 208 − 0.7 × age) and heart-rate reserve | age, maximum or resting rate |
| Sweat rate | weight difference + what you drank | two weights, what you drank and the duration |
| Energy used | MET × weight × hours, with values from the Compendium of Physical Activities | activity, weight and minutes |

A calculator gives an estimate, not medical advice or a personal training plan. If you have an injury, an illness or doubts about your own case, see a professional. If you find a mistake, write to us through the [contact page](/contacto/).
