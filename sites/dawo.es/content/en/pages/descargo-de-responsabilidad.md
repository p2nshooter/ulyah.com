DAWO publishes general information about sport, physical activity, rest and nutrition for informative purposes. We do our best to keep it rigorous and up to date, but no article can take every person's circumstances into account.

## It is not medical advice

Nothing published on DAWO constitutes a diagnosis, a treatment or an exercise prescription. The information does not replace an assessment by a doctor, a physiotherapist, a registered dietitian or a qualified coach. If you have symptoms, an injury or an illness, see a professional.

## Listen to your body

Stop exercising and seek medical help if you notice chest pain, dizziness, palpitations, shortness of breath out of proportion to the effort, or sharp pain in a joint. The advice on progression and loads is a guide: always adapt the effort to your level of fitness.

## Equipment and products

When we talk about running shoes, padel rackets, football boots or other sports equipment, we explain general criteria for choosing. We do not test products for money or recommend particular brands, and we are not responsible for the quality or safety of third-party products.

## Accuracy and updates

Sports rules and scientific recommendations change. We review the articles regularly, but some information may have become out of date. If you spot a mistake, write to us at [contacto@dawo.es](mailto:contacto@dawo.es) and we will look into it.

## Responsibility

How you use the information published is your responsibility. DAWO is not responsible for injuries, damage or losses resulting from applying the content without proper supervision.
