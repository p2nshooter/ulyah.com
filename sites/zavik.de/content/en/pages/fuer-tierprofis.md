## For animal lovers with a sense of art: AXTO

Cats move elegantly – so do the works of [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=zavik.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). The digital museum shows clocks with real time, stamps from fantasy realms, calligraphy from the Koran and Hadith whose text remains true to the original, and animated characters. Each piece has its own mathematical DNA and is a unique piece in the Base network.

Exclusive Editions (**AC-**) cost $1 and are paid for with AXTO Coins; There are ten per work, and you can give them away to many wallets without a gas fee. On OpenSea (**OS-**), AXTO offers 1 to 10 editions per work for a fixed price of $1 to $250, GPU works with **G**. The AXTO coin offers animal lovers an entry into the axto.io community from just $1; Individual works are created through staking as a thank you; a fixed return is not promised.

## For animal boarding houses, cat sitters, breeders with heart and practices

Anyone who works with animals professionally knows this: inquiries by phone, messenger and email, appointments on pieces of paper, vaccination certificates in folders. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=zavik.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European web development studio that puts things in order:

1. **Website with online booking** for cat boarding and sitting services so vacancies are visible 24/7.
2. **Customer portal** where owners upload documents and manage appointments.
3. **Shop** for accessories or self-sewn scratch pads if you sell on the side.

Payment is made in milestones via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT, and each stage is agreed upon in advance. For the quick way there are [Templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=zavik.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). XAA's pages are in English, Spanish and Indonesian; Messages in German are also processed.
