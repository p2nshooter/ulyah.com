We welcome messages from cat people. The best way to reach us is by email:

**Email:** [kontakt@zavik.de](mailto:kontakt@zavik.de)

We read every message and usually respond within a week. Please understand if it takes a little longer during holiday periods.

## What you can write to us about

- **Topic requests:** Are you missing a guide, for example on a specific breed, on cats with disabilities or on living with rabbits? Write to us what you would like to read more about.
- **Errors and additions:** If you find something wrong, outdated or misleading in a text, please tell us. Advice from veterinarians, veterinary assistants and employees from animal shelters are particularly valuable. We check every reference and correct the text if it is justified.
- **Experiences:** Have you successfully acclimated a second cat or stopped your cat from scratching on the sofa? Tell us what helped. We do not copy texts verbatim, but your experience can make a guide better.
- **Legal and data protection:** We also answer inquiries about your data or the content of this website via this address.

## Please no remote diagnoses

We can't judge what's wrong with your cat via email, and we don't want to waste valuable time with a well-intentioned response. If your cat appears sick, is in pain, or suddenly behaves differently, please contact a veterinary practice directly. In an emergency, even at night and on weekends, a veterinary clinic or veterinary emergency service in your region can help. How you can recognize an emergency can be found on the page [Emergency & Vet](/notfall-und-tierarzt/).

## Advertising and collaborations

We do not publish paid articles or product tests for payment. We do not answer inquiries about this.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message. More about this in the [Data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).
