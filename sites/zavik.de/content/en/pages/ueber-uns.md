ZAVIK stands for **gentle **four-legged, **smart cat care. We write for everyone who lives with a cat or wants to do so soon: for the family who gets an animal from the animal shelter for the first time, for the student with her indoor cat, for the pensioner whose old cat suddenly drinks more than before.

## Why ZAVIK exists

There are a surprising number of half-truths circulating about cats. That milk is healthy. That cats are loners who don't need anyone. That a cat that purrs is always happy. That you have to nudge an unclean cat into its mishap with your nose. On the Internet you can find ten answers to every question that contradict each other, and you often don't know who wrote them and what they are based on.

We want to be a calm, reliable contact point. A guide that explains connections instead of just listing rules. Who honestly says when a problem should be taken to the veterinary practice. And who describes cats as they are: as independent, sensitive animals with clear needs that you can learn to read.

## What you will find with us

ZAVIK is divided into six themes, which we call the “six stages” because they roughly follow the path you take with a cat:

1. **Moving in & settling in:** from adoption to reunification with other animals
2. **Nutrition:** what goes in the bowl, how much and why water is so important
3. **Health:** Prevention, vaccinations, parasites and the signs you shouldn't miss
4. **Behaviour:** Body language, sounds and the reasons behind scratching, biting and uncleanliness
5. **Apartment & outdoor area:** Cat litter box, scratching post, balcony, poisonous plants and the first trip outside
6. **Care & everyday life:** Fur, claws, play, transport box, medication and vacation

## How our texts are created

Each guide is developed specifically for ZAVIK and is not copied from other websites. We also use AI tools when researching and writing. In terms of content, we rely on specialist veterinary literature, recommendations from veterinary societies and guidelines on vaccination and parasite control. When it comes to your pet's health, no text can replace a visit to the vet's office. Where the professional world disagrees, we say so. How we work exactly is stated in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## What we don't do

We do not make remote diagnoses and do not mention medication dosages. We don't sell anything and don't get any money from feed manufacturers. ZAVIK is financed through advertisements, which are recognizable as such and have no influence on our content.

## The name and the picture

The throne of King Solomon, it is said, had six tiers of ivory, and on each tier sat lions on either side. Our lions are smaller and purr: domestic cats sit on ZAVIK's six steps, their amber eyes opening as you approach. By the way, a cat that slowly blinks at you says: I trust you.
