Anyone who reads a guide about animals trusts that the information is correct. Mistakes can harm a cat. That's why we adhere to these principles.

## Written specifically, not composed

Every text on ZAVIK is researched and written specifically for this website. We do not copy content from other websites. We also use AI tools when researching and formulating; The rules on this page apply to every sentence, regardless of how it was created. When we rely on a source, we express its content in our own words.

## Where our knowledge comes from

Our guides are based on:

- veterinary literature and textbooks on internal medicine, nutrition and behavioral medicine of cats
- Recommendations from veterinary societies and guidelines, for example on vaccinations and combating parasites
- Information sheets from animal protection organizations and veterinary associations
- the practical experience from years of living with cats, from animal shelters and from conversations with owners

We do not use reports from forums as evidence for medical statements.

## Honest with uncertainty

In veterinary medicine, there are questions to which there is no single correct answer: whether dry or wet food, whether in an apartment or outdoors, when is the best time for castration. Where experts have different opinions, we present the arguments and say what is important in each individual case. We do not sell certainty where there is none.

## Veterinarian instead of remote diagnosis

We do not mention medication dosages, home remedies for serious illnesses, or recipes for self-treatment. Every guide that touches on health clearly states when a visit to the vet is necessary. If in doubt, we always recommend practice.

## Animal welfare first

We do not recommend methods that cause fear or pain to a cat, such as punishment, water spraying, punishing neck grabs, or declaw removal. We support adoption from animal welfare, the castration of outdoor cats and an attitude that takes the needs of the cat seriously.

## Independence

ZAVIK is financed through advertisements that are automatically delivered by Google. We do not choose the ads and they have no influence on our texts. We do not take any money for articles, product tests or recommendations and do not name any brands in our guides.

## Updates and corrections

Each guide has a date. We revise texts if recommendations change or if readers point out errors to us. We correct any factual errors quickly. You can send us information via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.

## language

We write in simple, friendly language and use the first-name terms of our readers. We explain technical terms when we use them. We want people who are living with a cat for the first time to understand every text.
