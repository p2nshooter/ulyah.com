Knowing what each appliance costs is the most direct way of deciding where it is worth changing a habit. The sum is simple: the appliance's power, the time it is on and your price per kWh. The calculator adds what is often forgotten: appliances with a thermostat or motor do not draw their full power all the time, some are only used for a few months, and many keep using power on standby.

## The formula

**kWh = power (W) × hours on × share of the time at full power ÷ 1,000**

**Cost = kWh × your price per kWh with taxes**

For example, a 2,000 W washing machine that heats water for part of a ninety-minute programme, four days a week, uses about 0.9 kWh per wash and about 188 kWh a year. At €0.15/kWh, that is about €28 a year: it is the example the calculator opens with.

## Typical power and use

These are rough figures from our guide to [appliance consumption](/guias/consumo-de-los-electrodomesticos/). The real power is on your appliance's label or rating plate.

| Appliance | Typical power | Rough yearly consumption |
|---|---|---|
| Modern fridge-freezer | 100 to 150 W, cycling | 150 to 300 kWh |
| Washing machine (4 to 5 washes a week) | 2,000 W when heating | 150 to 250 kWh |
| Heat-pump tumble dryer | 800 to 1,000 W | 150 to 250 kWh |
| 80 to 100-litre electric water heater | 1,500 to 2,000 W | 1,500 to 3,000 kWh |
| Split air conditioner (summer) | 700 to 1,500 W | 200 to 700 kWh |
| 55-inch LED television | 80 to 150 W | 100 to 250 kWh |
| Router and network equipment | 8 to 15 W, 24 hours | 70 to 130 kWh |

## Why the share of time at full power matters

A 120 W fridge does not draw 120 W around the clock: its compressor switches on and off to keep the temperature. An oven at 180 °C heats at full power at first and then holds. Air conditioners and radiators adjust to the temperature of the house. That is why the calculator asks for the share of the time at full power, and fills it in when you choose an appliance from the list. If you want the exact figure for your own appliance, an inexpensive plug-in energy meter gives it within a few days.

## Standby

Televisions, consoles, chargers, music systems and set-top boxes keep using a few watts when they seem to be off. One costs little on its own; all of them together can add up to tens of euros a year. Enter the standby power and the calculator tells you how much it costs separately. Our guide to [standby consumption](/guias/consumo-en-standby/) explains how to measure and cut it.

## Which price per kWh to use

Use the price on your bill with taxes: the energy term divided by the kWh, plus the electricity tax and VAT. If you have time-of-use pricing and the appliance mostly runs off-peak, use the off-peak price. Our guide to [efficient washing machines and dishwashers](/guias/lavadora-y-lavavajillas-eficientes/) shows how much changing the time of use saves.
