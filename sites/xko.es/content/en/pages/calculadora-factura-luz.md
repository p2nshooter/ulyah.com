The electricity bill looks complicated, but it always follows the same order. With the figures from a bill or an offer, this calculator works out every line again and tells you how much you pay per day. Use it to check a bill that seems high and to find out what you would pay with an offer before you sign.

## How a domestic bill is worked out

For a home on the 2.0TD access tariff, the bill adds up these items, in this order:

1. **Power term:** contracted power in P1 and in P2 × days × the price of each period in €/kW per day.
2. **Energy term:** kWh used × the price per kWh. If your tariff has time-of-use pricing, it is worked out separately for peak, standard and off-peak hours.
3. **Electricity tax:** a percentage of the sum of power and energy. The general rate is 5.11269632 %.
4. **Meter rental:** a regulated amount per day, about €0.0266 for a domestic digital meter.
5. **VAT:** applied at the end to everything above. The general rate is 21 %.

If your offer includes services you cannot remove, such as a maintenance plan, add them before VAT.

## Where to find each figure on your bill

- **Contracted power and power prices:** in the bill's breakdown, under the power term, with one value for P1 and another for P2.
- **Days:** the billing period, at the top of the bill.
- **kWh per period:** in the consumption breakdown or on your distributor's website, which shows your consumption hour by hour.
- **Energy prices:** under the energy term. On the PVPC they change every hour, so to check a PVPC bill use the average amount shown on it.

If a line on your bill does not match the calculator, our guide to [reading the bill line by line](/guias/como-leer-la-factura-de-la-luz/) explains where the difference may lie, and the one on [how to complain about a bill](/guias/como-reclamar-una-factura-de-la-luz/) what to do if there is a mistake.

## Which part of the bill you can reduce

The calculator shows how much of the amount is power and how much is energy. Power is paid even if you use nothing, so matching it to what you need saves money every month: our guide on [how much power to contract](/guias/que-potencia-contratar/) explains how. Energy depends on how much you use and, with time-of-use pricing, on when: the [time bands](/guias/tramos-horarios-punta-llano-valle/) show which hours are cheapest.

Tax rates are set by law, and in some periods there have been temporary reductions. If your bill shows a different rate, change it in the calculator: the sum is redone instantly.
