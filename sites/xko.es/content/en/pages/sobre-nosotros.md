**XKO** stands for **eXperts in Kilowatts and Optimisation** (in Spanish, *eXpertos en Kilovatios y Optimización*). We are an independent online publication devoted to a question almost every household in Spain asks: how to use less energy without giving up comfort.

The electricity bill has become hard to understand. Network tolls, system charges, time-of-use periods, power contracted in two periods, taxes that go up and down, the regulated and the free market, surplus compensation, the social discount, energy certificates… Behind each of those terms are decisions that can save or cost hundreds of euros a year. Our job is to explain them clearly, with realistic examples and no small print.

This English edition carries the same guides as our Spanish site, written out in English for everyone who lives in Spain, owns a home here or is thinking of moving, and prefers to read about bills and contracts in English. The rules, prices and institutions are Spanish; we keep their Spanish names where you will meet them on a bill or a form, and explain them.

## What you will find on XKO

We organise our guides into six topics, like the circuits of a fuse box:

- **Electricity bill:** how to read it line by line and which items depend on you.
- **Power and tariffs:** contracted power, time-of-use periods, the regulated PVPC and the free market, the social discount.
- **Solar self-consumption:** panels on houses and on blocks of flats, compensation, batteries and paperwork.
- **Insulation and windows:** where heat escapes from a home and which improvements pay off.
- **Heating and cooling:** heat pumps, boilers, air conditioning and thermostats.
- **Appliances and lighting:** the energy label, real consumption and habits.

## How we work

Every guide starts from public, verifiable sources: legislation published in the Official State Gazette (BOE), information from the National Markets and Competition Commission (CNMC), the Institute for Energy Diversification and Saving (IDAE), Red Eléctrica and the manufacturers. When we make calculations, we explain the assumptions and round down so as not to promise savings that will not happen. We set this out in detail in [how we calculate](/metodologia/).

We review the guides when rules or reference prices change, and every guide shows its review date.

## Independence

XKO **does not sell energy, does not install panels and takes no commission** from retailers, installers or manufacturers. We do not publish sponsored content dressed up as a guide. The site is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense, clearly separated from the content, and that advertising has no influence on what we recommend.

## What we do not do

We do not give personal advice on your contract, nor do we replace a registered installer or a certified energy assessor. For any work on an electrical or gas installation, always use an authorised professional.
