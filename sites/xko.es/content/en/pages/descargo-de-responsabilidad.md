XKO publishes plain-language information about saving energy at home. We do our best to keep it accurate and up to date, but you should know its limits.

## Energy changes often

Wholesale market prices, regulated network tolls and system charges, taxes on electricity and gas, the requirements for the social discount and public grants change frequently, sometimes from one month to the next. Every guide shows its review date, but we cannot guarantee that all the figures still apply when you read it.

**Before you sign up to a tariff, change your contracted power, apply for a grant or invest in an installation, check the conditions in force** in your contract, in the CNMC's offer comparison tool, on the website of IDAE or of your autonomous community, or with a professional.

## The calculations are a guide

The savings, consumption figures and payback periods we show are based on reasonable assumptions, rounded down. Your real case may differ because of the orientation and insulation of your home, your climate zone, the number of people, your routines and the prices you pay.

## It is not professional advice

XKO's information does not replace the advice of a registered installer, a certified energy assessor, an engineer, a tax adviser or a lawyer. We do not review individual cases.

## Safety

Never work on electrical or gas installations yourself. Building work, solar installations and replacing a boiler or heating and cooling equipment must be done by registered companies, which also issue the certificates needed to legalise the installation.
