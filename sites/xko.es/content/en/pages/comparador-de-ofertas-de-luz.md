An electricity offer cannot be judged by the price per kWh in the advert. What you pay over a year depends on your consumption, how it is spread across the day, your power, the services added to the contract and how long a promotion lasts. This comparer does the full sum with your figures for up to three offers.

## What you need

- **Your yearly consumption in kWh** and how much falls in peak, standard and off-peak hours. Your distributor's website shows your hourly consumption; if you do not have it yet, start with the split in the example (30 % peak, 30 % standard and 40 % off-peak) and adjust it later.
- **Your contracted power** in P1 and P2.
- **For each offer:** the energy price (single or by time band), the power price in €/kW per day, the services that cannot be removed and, if it has one, how long the promotional price lasts and the price afterwards.

## How it calculates

For each offer, the comparer multiplies your consumption in each band by its price, adds the power for the whole year (kW × €/kW per day × 365) and the services. If there is a promotion, it applies the promotional price only for the months it lasts and the normal price for the rest of the year. Then it adds the electricity tax on power and energy, the meter rental, which is the same for every offer, and VAT.

## An example with three offers

This is the case study in our guide to [switching electricity supplier](/guias/cambiar-de-comercializadora-paso-a-paso/): a household using 3,400 kWh a year, 30 % at peak, 30 % at standard and 40 % off-peak, with 4.6 kW contracted.

| Item | Offer A (single price) | Offer B (three periods) | Offer C (single price, 6-month promotion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | €0.13/kWh | 0.18 / 0.12 / 0.08 €/kWh | €0.11/kWh for 6 months, then 0.15 |
| P1 power | €0.09/kW per day | €0.08/kW per day | €0.11/kW per day |
| Yearly energy cost | €442 | €415 | €442 |
| Yearly P1 power cost | €151 | €134 | €185 |
| **Total before taxes** | **€593** | **€549** | **€627** |

Offer C looked the cheapest in the advert, but once the promotion ends and with its dearer power it turns out to be the worst. Offer B wins because 40 % of this household's consumption is off-peak. With a different split — say, a lot of consumption in the evening — the result would change: that is why it is worth doing the sum with your own figures.

## Before you sign

- **Check how long the price lasts** and note the date it ends.
- **See whether there is a tie-in period** and what it costs to break it.
- **Untick the services** you do not want.
- **Compare with the PVPC too**, the regulated tariff: our guide [PVPC or the free market](/guias/pvpc-o-mercado-libre/) explains when each one suits.

The official CNMC comparison tool brings together the offers registered in Spain and is the reference for finding candidates. This comparer is for doing the fine sum with your own figures once you have two or three offers in hand.
