Our guides explain how to use less energy. These four calculators do the sums with your own household's figures: the ones on your bill, on your appliances' rating plates and in the offers you are looking at. They run in your browser, with no sign-up, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your browser only remembers your last entry, so you do not have to start from scratch next time.

## Electricity bill calculator

Copy your contracted power, the days, the kWh and the prices from your bill, and see each line worked out in the same order as the bill: power term, energy term, electricity tax, meter rental and VAT. Use it to check a bill and to find out what you would pay with an offer before you sign. [Open the bill calculator](/calculadora-factura-luz/)

## What each appliance costs to run

Pick an appliance from the list or type the power on its rating plate, how many hours you use it and your price per kWh, and get its cost per day of use, per month and per year, standby included. It is the quickest way to find out whether a habit or an appliance is worth changing. [Open the running-cost calculator](/calculadora-consumo-electrodomesticos/)

## How much power to contract

Tick what could be on at the same moment at the busiest time in your home, or enter the highest demand your meter recorded, and the calculator suggests a power with a margin, the nearest standard value and what you would save a year if you contract more today. [Open the power calculator](/calculadora-potencia/)

## Electricity offer comparer

Enter your yearly consumption once and how it splits between peak, standard and off-peak hours, and compare up to three offers with their energy price, power price, services and introductory promotion. You will see each offer's yearly cost before and after taxes, and which is cheapest for you. [Open the offer comparer](/comparador-de-ofertas-de-luz/)

## Where the numbers come from

The calculators use the same sums we explain in [how we calculate](/metodologia/) and in our guides: kWh = power × hours, the order of the Spanish domestic bill with the electricity tax and VAT, and the prices you enter. The values each calculator opens with are the worked examples in our guides, so you can check that the result matches.

| Calculator | What you need to hand | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity bill | A bill or an offer | Every line of the bill and the total with taxes |
| Appliance running costs | The appliance's power and your price per kWh | Cost per use, month and year, with standby |
| Power | Your appliances or your highest recorded demand | Suggested power and the yearly saving |
| Offer comparer | Your yearly consumption and each offer's prices | Each offer's yearly cost and the cheapest |

A calculator gives an estimate, not personal advice: your bill and your contract have the final say. To compare every offer registered in Spain, the official CNMC comparison tool is the reference. If you find a mistake, tell us through the [contact page](/contacto/).
