Contracted power is paid every day, whether you use electricity or not. Contracting too much is money that goes without giving you anything in return; contracting too little trips the meter's limiter when several appliances coincide. This calculator helps you find the right point in two ways: by adding up what may be on at the same moment, or by starting from the highest demand your meter recorded.

## Two ways to work it out

**Adding up appliances.** Think of the busiest moment in your home, which in many is dinner time, and tick what could be on at once. The total, in kW, is the power you need so that the limiter does not trip.

**From your recorded maximum.** Smart meters store the highest power you have used. Your distributor's customer website shows it. A margin of around 15 % above that maximum gives you a safe power, and it is usually quite a lot lower than what you have contracted.

## An example

This is the case in our guide on [how much power to contract](/guias/que-potencia-contratar/): a family has dinner at nine with the oven at 2,000 W, an induction zone at 1,500 W, the dishwasher heating water at 2,000 W, and the TV, lights and fridge at about 400 W. In total, 5.9 kW. If they set the dishwasher to run later, the peak drops to 3.9 kW and 4.6 kW is enough.

| Situation | Suggested power |
|---|---|
| Studio or small flat with gas for cooking and hot water | 2.3 to 3.45 kW |
| Flat for two or three people, electric cooking, no electric heating | 3.45 to 4.6 kW |
| Family with induction hob, oven, dishwasher and air conditioning | 4.6 to 5.75 kW |
| Home heated by an air-to-water or air-to-air heat pump | 5.75 to 8 kW |
| Home heated by electric radiators | 6.9 to 9.2 kW or more |

## How much you save

Every kilowatt less shows up in the power term of every bill. With prices of €0.09/kW per day in P1 and €0.02/kW per day in P2, one kW less is about €40 a year before taxes and a little over €50 with the electricity tax and VAT. The calculator does the sum with your prices and your current power.

## Before you change

- **Check your recorded maximum** on your distributor's website: it is the most reliable figure.
- **Spread out the big consumers:** dishwasher and washing machine on a timer, the oven not at the same time as the dryer.
- **With a smart meter**, many distributors accept values between the traditional steps, which lets you fine-tune.
- **If you plan to install a heat pump or a car charger**, bear it in mind before going down.
