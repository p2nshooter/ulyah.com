You can write to the XKO editorial team at:

**Email:** [hola@xko.es](mailto:hola@xko.es)

We usually reply within a few working days. You are welcome to write in English or in Spanish.

## What you can send us

- **Mistakes or out-of-date figures.** Energy rules change often. If you see a network toll, a tax, a deadline or a grant that is no longer right, tell us, with the source if you have it.
- **Topic suggestions.** If you would like us to explain an item on the bill, a type of installation or a particular comparison, let us know.
- **Real experiences.** Cases from households that have changed tariff, installed panels or improved their insulation, with their consumption before and after, help us a great deal.

## What we cannot do

- We do not review individual contracts or bills, and we do not give personal advice.
- We do not recommend particular retailers, installers or brands in exchange for anything.
- We do not handle complaints against companies. For that you can contact your retailer, the consumer services of your autonomous community or the CNMC.

## Privacy

We will use your email address and your message only to reply to you. We do not share them with third parties or use them to send you advertising. More in our [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/).
