ZUFIQ stands for **living happily, **orderly, **quality instead of quantity. We write for people who want to make their everyday life calmer: for the commuter whose morning begins every day in a hectic manner, for the father who can no longer switch off in the evening between toys, mail and the screen, for everyone who has too many things, too many appointments and too many notifications and longs for a little more air.

## Why ZUFIQ exists

A lot is written about order, routines and minimalism, and much of it is stressful. Perfectly tidy apartments, morning routines starting at five o'clock, lists of a hundred things that you supposedly don't need. Anyone who reads something like this often doesn't feel encouraged, but rather caught.

We believe that a calm everyday life is created differently: from small, well-prepared steps that fit your own life. From habits that work even on tired days. From organizational systems that don't have to be maintained constantly. And from the permission that not everything has to succeed at the same time.

## The name and the picture

It is said about the construction of the temple in Jerusalem that the stones were already prepared at the quarry. On the construction site itself, you could not hear any hammers, hatchets or any iron tools (1 Kings 6:7). This image accompanies us: peace arises in the preparation. If you pack your bag in the evening, you will have less noise in your head in the morning. Once you decide where the mail belongs, you don't have to think about it every day.

That's why ZUFIQ looks like a house made of limestone, with doors made of olive wood and fine lines of gold. We call our six subject areas rooms.

## What you will find with us

1. **Habits & Routines:** Morning and evening routines, building new habits, letting go of old ones
2. **Decluttering & Minimalism:** Less ballast, room by room, from the wardrobe to memorabilia
3. **Order & Paperwork:** Organization systems, fixed places and a shelf that really lasts
4. **Digital peace:** Cell phone, notifications, email, photos and files
5. **Conscious consumption:** buy in peace, less impulse purchases, subscriptions and passing things on
6. **Time, Energy & Sleep:** plan realistically, take breaks and cultivate sleep as a habit

## How our texts are created

Each guide is written specifically for ZUFIQ and is not compiled from other websites. When structuring and formulating, we also use digital writing tools, including AI-supported aids. The same rules still apply to every text: We rely on generally accepted knowledge, for example on habit formation and sleep hygiene, on information from consumer advice centers and on proven everyday knowledge. We do not invent any studies, numbers, experience reports or expert quotes. How we work exactly is stated in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## What we don't do

We do not provide any medical, psychotherapeutic, legal or financial advice in individual cases or any investment recommendations. If a topic belongs in professional hands, such as persistent sleep problems, exhaustion or debt, we say this clearly and refer you to our page [Help & Contact Points](/hilfe-und-anlaufstellen/). We do not name any brands as recommendations and do not sell anything.
