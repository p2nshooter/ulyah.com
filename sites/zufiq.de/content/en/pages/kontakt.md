We look forward to receiving messages from readers. The easiest way to reach us is by email:

**Email:** [kontakt@zufiq.de](mailto:kontakt@zufiq.de)

We read every message and respond as soon as we can. In keeping with our topic, we answer emails in batches and not every minute. So it may take a few days, and a little longer during holiday periods. Thank you for your patience.

## What you can write to us about

- **Topic requests:** Are you missing a guide, for example on cleaning out after moving, organizing a small apartment or evening routine in shift work? Write to us what you would like to read more about.
- **Errors and additions:** If you find something wrong, outdated or misleading in a text, please tell us. We check every reference and correct the text if it is justified. This particularly applies to information about deadlines, storage of documents, contracts or health issues.
- **Experiences:** Have you found a routine that has stuck with you for months, or have you finally emptied your basement? Tell us what helped. We do not copy texts verbatim or name names, but your experience can make a guide better.
- **Legal and data protection:** We also answer inquiries about your data, information according to the GDPR or the content of this website via this address.

## This way your message will be well received

- Write briefly in the subject line what it is about, for example “Error in the evening routine guide” or “Requested topic for paperwork”.
- If you're referring to a specific guide, copy the page address into the message. Then we will find the place immediately.
- Please do not send us copies of ID cards, contracts, bank statements, doctor's letters or other personal documents. We don't need them, and we don't want to save what we don't need.

## What we can't do

We are not an advice center. We cannot give you personal life, health, legal or financial advice via email, even if we understand your concerns. If something is bothering you greatly, please contact people who can accompany you personally: your family doctor's office, a counseling center or, in acute crises, the telephone counseling service. You can find an overview on the [Help & Contact Points](/hilfe-und-anlaufstellen/) page.

## Advertising and collaborations

We do not publish paid posts, paid product tests, or advertising copy for storage systems, apps, or courses. We do not answer inquiries about this. The ads on this website are automatically served by Google; we do not choose them.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to reply to your message and will delete it as soon as it is no longer needed for that purpose. More about this in the [Data protection declaration](/datenschutz/). You can find out who is behind ZUFIQ in the [Imprint](/impressum/) and on the page [About us](/ueber-uns/).
