A calm everyday life comes from good habits and the courage to leave out the unnecessary. ZUFIQ writes about how this works. However, there is ballast not only in cupboards and calendars, but also on screens: too many apps, too many accounts, too many open windows. Here we introduce you to two projects that belong to the same operator as ZUFIQ. That's why we say clearly: This is advertising on our own behalf.

## AXTO: little, but special

Tidy doesn’t mean empty. [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=zufiq.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a digital museum with few but special things: clocks in real time, stamps from imaginary realms, calligraphy from the Koran and Hadith with unchanged text and moving figures. Each work is unique from its own DNA, secured as an NFT in the Base network.

Exclusive Editions (**AC-**) cost $1 in AXTO coins, ten pieces per work, giftable with no gas fee. Public Works (**OS-**) are available on OpenSea with 1 to 10 editions for a fixed price between 1 and 250 dollars; a **G** denotes GPU paintings. To put it simply: AXTO is a participation in the axto.io community starting from 1 dollar, staked holdings are rewarded with originals - with no guarantee of an increase in value.

## A website that doesn't demand constant attention: XAA

Self-employed people, coaches, therapists and small practices know the problem of a website that constantly needs maintenance, loads slowly or breaks something with every update. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=zufiq.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European studio that develops solid and tailor-made websites and platforms - from simple business cards to appointment booking with a customer area.

What we like about it is the quiet process: the project is broken down into milestones, each step is shown and paid for individually, via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. For anyone who wants to get started quickly and easily, there are [Templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=zufiq.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem). XAA's pages are in English, Spanish and Indonesian; German inquiries will also be answered.
