ZUFIQ explains habits, order, minimalism and a calmer everyday life as thoroughly and understandably as possible. Please read this note before implementing any suggestions from our guides.

## Suggestions, not treatment

Our texts are based on generally accepted knowledge, for example on habit formation, sleep hygiene and household organization, and on proven everyday knowledge. They are not a diagnosis, not a therapy and not an individual consultation. What works well for many people may not be suitable for you, for example if you are ill, pregnant, working shifts, with small children or in a special life situation.

## When you should ask professionals

A tidy closet and a good evening routine can make a big difference, but they don't solve everything. Please get support if any of the following apply to you:

- You have been sleeping poorly for several weeks, are very exhausted during the day, or someone has noticed you stop breathing at night.
- You feel depressed, listless or overwhelmed for a long time, or you have thoughts that you no longer want to live.
- You can't get rid of things despite being under a lot of stress, and rooms in your apartment are hardly usable anymore.
- You have debts that you can no longer keep track of, or you no longer open mail out of fear.
- You realize that cell phones, games, shopping or gambling are taking over your life and you can't stop alone.

Anyone who can help in these cases can be found on our page [Help & Contact Points](/hilfe-und-anlaufstellen/). In acute emergencies, please dial 112.
