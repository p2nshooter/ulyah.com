ZUFIQ writes about habits, order and a calmer everyday life. You can try out many of these on your own. Sometimes there is more to the restlessness, exhaustion or chaos than a new routine can solve. And sometimes you just need a place that answers a practical question: What to do with your old cell phone? Who can help if a subscription cannot be canceled? This page shows you in two parts who is responsible for this in Germany. It doesn't replace advice, but shows you the way there.

## In case of emergency

- **Emergency call 112:** if your life is in acute danger, for example if someone is unconscious, has severe shortness of breath or chest pain, or if you see yourself or others in danger.
- **Medical on-call service 116 117:** if you need urgent medical attention outside of office hours, but it is not a life-threatening emergency. The appointment service of the statutory health insurance associations can also help you find doctor's appointments using this number.

## Part 1: When it's about more than order

### When the soul becomes heavy

If you have been depressed for a long time, no longer enjoy anything, hardly sleep or have thoughts of not wanting to live anymore, this is not a case for tidying tips. Please talk to someone about this.

- **Telephone pastoral care:** free of charge, anonymously and around the clock on **0800 111 0 111**, **0800 111 0 222** or **116 123**. At telefonseelsorge.de you can also get advice via chat and email.
- **Family doctor's office:** Your family doctor is a good first point of contact, even for mental health problems. They can clarify physical causes and refer you further.
- **Psychotherapeutic consultation:** People with statutory health insurance can attend a psychotherapeutic consultation without a referral. The appointment service on 116 117 can help you find an appointment.

### When sleep and strength don't return

A good evening routine and a realistic daily plan can make a big difference. However, if you have difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep for several weeks, are constantly tired during the day, snore loudly or someone has noticed that you are having pauses in your breathing, have this checked out at your family doctor's office. This also applies if you feel constantly exhausted despite getting enough sleep. There may be treatable causes behind such complaints. If necessary, you will be referred to a specialist practice or a sleep laboratory.

### When money and papers become overwhelming

Unopened letters, reminders and invoices scare many people, and that's exactly why they stay there. If debts dominate your life, **debt counseling** can help. Serious advice, often free of charge, is offered by consumer advice centers, welfare associations and municipal advice centers. Be careful with offers that promise quick debt relief in exchange for advance payment. A first step that you can take today: collect all letters in one place, even the unopened ones, and take them with you to the consultation appointment. However, you should open letters from the court and letters with formal delivery, usually in a yellow envelope, immediately because there are often deadlines in them.

### When things can no longer be let go

Almost everyone finds cleaning out difficult. But if the apartment is so full that rooms are no longer usable and the thought of throwing things away causes severe anxiety, there can be serious stress behind it. Then it's not more boxes that will help, but rather conversations: with your family doctor's office, a psychotherapeutic practice or a social psychiatric counseling center in your city. Relatives can also get advice there.

### When screen or consumption takes control

If you notice that despite good intentions you cannot stop using your cell phone, playing games, online shopping or gambling and are suffering from it, contact an **addiction counseling center**. The advice is usually free and confidential, including for relatives.

### For children and young people

The **number against grief** is available for children and young people on **116 111**, anonymously and free of charge.

## Part 2: Practical contact points for everyday life

### Contracts, subscriptions and cancellations

- **Consumer advice center:** There is a consumer advice center in every federal state. She advises on contracts, subscriptions, cancellations and unwanted invoices, sometimes free of charge and sometimes for a fee. How much advice will cost you can be found on the website of your consumer advice center.
- **Cancel online:** If you were able to conclude a contract via a website, in many cases the provider must also allow you to easily cancel via a button on their website. Save the cancellation confirmation in your contracts folder.
- **Unexplained debits:** Talk to your bank quickly. Direct debits can be returned within certain time limits and the sooner you act, the better.
- **Third-party block:** If your cell phone bill is billed for services that you never knowingly ordered, you can have your cell phone provider set up a third-party block.

### Clearing out: what to do with things?

- **Pass it on:** Circle of friends, neighborhood, gift fairs, a box “to give away” in front of the door if house rules and your city’s rules allow it, or a public bookcase for books in good condition.
- **Donations:** Social department stores, clothing stores and aid organizations are often happy to receive clean, intact things. Ask in advance what is currently needed so that your donation does not become a disposal problem there.
- **Sell:** Flea market, thrift store or classified ads. If you sell a lot and regularly, find out whether this is still considered a private sale.
- **Disposal:** Your municipal waste disposal company is responsible for bulky waste, recyclables and problem waste. The recycling center's opening hours and the rules for your location are listed on its website or app.
- **Electrical devices, batteries, rechargeable batteries, LED and energy-saving lamps** do not belong in household waste. You can drop them off at the recycling center, and many retailers will take them back. Medicines do not belong in the toilet or sink. The pharmacy or your waste disposal company will tell you how to dispose of them correctly.
- **Papers with personal data** such as bank statements or old contracts should not be thrown into the waste paper without being shredded. For small quantities, a document shredder is sufficient; for large quantities, there are service providers for document destruction.

### Pass on old cell phones and computers

Before a device leaves your home, back up your photos and files, sign out of all accounts, turn off device tracking features, remove the SIM and memory card, and reset the device to factory settings. On older computers with hard drives, simply deleting them is not always enough. A defective data carrier that you can no longer delete should be destroyed before the device is disposed of. The Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) provides consumers with understandable information about such questions.

### Repair instead of buying new

- **Repair Cafés:** In many cities, volunteers meet up and work with you to repair clothes, lamps, toys or small appliances. You can usually find appointments through your city, the library or neighborhood centers.
- **Specialist workshop:** For gas appliances and devices with a swollen or damaged battery: do not open it yourself, but ask a specialist workshop. The same goes for electronics if you're not sure what you're doing.
- **Warranty first:** If something new breaks, you have legal warranty rights against the dealer. Clarify this before you open anything yourself. The consumer advice center will explain to you which rights apply in individual cases.

### Email, SMS and phone scams

Fake news from alleged banks, parcel services or authorities relies on haste. Do not click on any links in such messages or enter any login details. If in doubt, call the office at a number you found yourself. If you have entered data or lost money: Inform your bank immediately and have your cards or access blocked. The central blocking number **116 116** applies to many, but not all, cards. Your bank's number is on their website. Also file a police report, in many federal states also online.

### Documents, deadlines and taxes

How long you should keep which documents depends on the type of document and your life situation. If you have tax questions, tax advice or, for many employees, a wage tax assistance association can help in return for a membership fee. If you have any questions about the notice, the authority that sent it is responsible. Your contact details are on the letter.

We have carefully compiled all the information on this page. Telephone numbers, offers and rules can change, so please check anything important with the relevant authority. If you notice an error, please write to us via the [Contact](/kontakt/) page.
