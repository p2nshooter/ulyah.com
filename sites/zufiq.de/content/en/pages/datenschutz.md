For us, a quiet everyday life is also part of how we handle data: we collect as little as possible. This declaration informs you in accordance with Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) about what data is processed when you visit zufiq.de, who is responsible for it and what rights you have.

## Responsible person

The provider named in [Imprint](/impressum/) is responsible for data processing on this website. You can reach us at [kontakt@zufiq.de](mailto:kontakt@zufiq.de).

## No account, no forms

ZUFIQ has no registration, no comment function, no newsletter and no contact form. You can read all the guides without telling us anything about yourself.

## Hosting and server protocols

The website is delivered via the Cloudflare, Inc. network. When you access a page, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data: your IP address, date and time, the address accessed, the browser type, the operating system and the amount of data transferred. This data is needed to deliver the site and protect it from attacks and misuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operations (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). Cloudflare can also process data in the USA. The transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on EU standard contractual clauses.

## Range measurement without cookies

So that we know which guides are being read, we count page views using the publisher's own slim count, which is also operated via Cloudflare. Your browser only transmits the name of the website and the path accessed, for example the address of a guide. A total of the views per day and page is saved. So that we can see how many people are currently reading, a timestamp is stored for each view without any further information and is automatically deleted again after a short time. No cookies are set, no IP addresses are saved and no profiles are created. The browser identifier is only evaluated at the moment of access in order to count visits by search engine robots separately and is not saved. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving our offering (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR).

## Fonts from Google Fonts

To display the fonts, your browser loads font files from servers at Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, when you access the page. Your IP address will be transmitted to Google. Google also receives the information that zufiq.de was accessed. We use the fonts for a uniform and easy-to-read appearance (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). If your browser blocks external fonts, a default font from your device will be used. More about this in the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Advertising with Google AdSense

ZUFIQ is free and is financed by advertisements from Google AdSense, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited. Google and its partners, so-called third parties, may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to serve ads, limit their frequency, prevent fraud and measure the effectiveness of ads. If you consent, advertisements may also be tailored to your interests derived from your visits to this and other websites.

Such information will only be stored and read on your device with your consent (Section 25 Paragraph 1 TDDDG, Art. 6 Paragraph 1 Letter a GDPR). We obtain consent via a consent message that appears on the first visit. There you can see the third parties involved and can agree or reject individual purposes. Non-personalized ads may continue to appear without consent. Data can also be transmitted to the USA. Google is certified according to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Further information: [How Google uses data from websites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Revoke consent

You can revoke or change your consent at any time with future effect via the link to the data protection settings in the consent message or by deleting the cookies from this website in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. Details can be found in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-richtlinie/).

## Encrypted connection

The website is delivered via an encrypted connection (HTTPS). You can recognize this by the lock symbol and the address that begins with "https://“. This means that third parties cannot read what content you are accessing during the transmission.

## Contact via email

If you write us an email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to respond to you (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b or lit. f GDPR). We do not pass on this data and delete it as soon as the request has been completed and there are no legal retention obligations to the contrary.

## Storage in the browser

Your browser will remember in the session memory whether the short celebration animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown. This information does not leave your device and is deleted when you close the browser.

## Your rights

According to the GDPR, you have the right to information about your stored data (Article 15), to rectification (Article 16), to deletion (Article 17), to restriction of processing (Article 18), to data portability (Article 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21). You can revoke your consent at any time (Art. 7 Para. 3). You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77), for example in the federal state in which you live. For inquiries, simply send an email to [kontakt@zufiq.de](mailto:kontakt@zufiq.de).

## No automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing and we do not create profiles ourselves. You are not legally or contractually obliged to provide us with personal data. You can also read the guides if you refuse consent for advertising.

## Changes

We will adapt this statement if the website or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
