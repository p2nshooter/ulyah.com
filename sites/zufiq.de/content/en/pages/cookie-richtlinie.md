This policy explains in detail which cookies and similar technologies are used when you visit zufiq.de. It supplements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device via your browser. The next time you access it, the website or an integrated service can read it again. Other technologies work similarly, such as the browser's local storage or session memory, device identifiers and small graphics that report a call (pixels). Some of these technologies are necessary for operations, others are used for advertising or measurement.

## What ZUFIQ stores itself

ZUFIQ does not set its own cookies to recognize you. Our page view count works entirely without cookies. In your browser's session memory, we only remember whether the short celebration animation for the 2026 World Cup has already been shown, so that it does not appear again every time you change pages. This information does not leave your device and disappears when you close the browser. It is technically necessary for the desired display (Section 25 Para. 2 TDDDG).

## Google AdSense and third party cookies

We use Google AdSense for the advertisements on this website. Google and Google's advertising partners listed as third parties in the consent message may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

- **Deliver ads:** technically necessary for an ad to appear at all
- **Limit frequency:** so you don't see the same ad over and over again
- **Detect fraud and abuse:** such as automated clicks
- **Measure impact:** how often an ad was viewed or clicked on
- **Personalize ads:** only with your consent, based on your interests

Which cookies are set in detail depends on your selection, your browser and the advertisements and can change. Google describes its cookies in the overview [How Google uses cookies in advertising] (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Depending on the cookie, the storage period ranges from the duration of the session to several months.

## Google Fonts

The fonts on this website are loaded from Google servers. To the best of our knowledge, no cookies are set. However, your IP address will be transmitted to Google, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/).

## The consent message

A consent message appears on the first visit. There you can agree to all purposes, reject all or select individual purposes and third parties from the options. No cookies will be stored for personalized advertising without your consent. Ads that are not based on your interests may continue to appear. Which information Google uses for this depends on the purposes you have agreed to.

## Change or revoke consent

You can change your decision at any time. To do this, use the link to the privacy settings provided in the consent message or delete the cookies from this website in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. A revocation applies to the future.

## Manage cookies in the browser

In your browser settings you can view and delete stored cookies, generally block third-party cookies or set cookies to be deleted when you close them. How to do this can be found in the help of your browser. ZUFIQ's guides remain completely readable even without cookies.
