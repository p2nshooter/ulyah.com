QKB publishes recipes and home cooking guides for educational purposes. We strive to make them accurate, safe and tested, but we cannot control the conditions under which they are made in each house.

## Results may vary

Flour, milk, kitchen temperature, oven and even tap water influence the result. The quantities and times we give are indicative. We teach you how to observe the dough, the ferment or the curd so that you can adjust them, but the final result depends on each preparation.

## Food safety

Homemade cheese, fermented and preserved cheese production requires hygiene and control. In particular:

- **Raw milk:** We do not recommend making fresh cheeses with raw milk at home due to the risk of bacteria such as *Listeria*, *Brucella* or *E. coli*. Use pasteurized milk.
- **Preserves:** Poorly prepared home preserves can favor the development of *Clostridium botulinum*. Strictly follow the acidity and heat treatment instructions, and discard any bulging or strange-smelling containers without tasting them.
- **Fermented:** A ferment with colored mold, an unpleasant odor or a questionable appearance should be discarded.

Check out our [food safety](/seguridad-alimentaria/) guide before you get started.
