A cookie is a small file that a website saves on your browser to remember information about your visit. This page explains what cookies are involved when you read QKB.

## Own cookies

QKB does not install its own cookies to identify you. The visit statistics we use are calculated without cookies and on an aggregate basis, and the site works the same even if you block all cookies.

We only save a technical flag in the browser's session storage so as not to repeat the celebration animation on each page. It is automatically deleted when you close the tab and contains no personal data.

## Third party cookies: advertising

QKB ads are served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies to:

- show relevant ads based on your previous browsing;
- limit the number of times you see the same ad;
- measure the effectiveness of ads and prevent fraud.

These cookies are the responsibility of Google. You can check how it works on the Google page on [how it uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Consent

If you visit us from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Google shows you a notice to accept or reject advertising cookies before installing them. If you reject them, you will be able to continue reading the magazine normally and, at most, you will see non-personalized ads.

## How to manage cookies in your browser

You can allow, block or delete cookies from your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies.
- **Firefox:** Settings › Privacy and security › Cookies and site data.
- **Safari:** Settings › Privacy › Block all cookies.
- **Edge:** Settings › Cookies and site permissions.

Please note that blocking all cookies may affect the functioning of other websites you visit.
