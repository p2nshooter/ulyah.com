At QKB we talk about the home bakery: the cheese that matures in the cellar, the sourdough that asks for patience, the fermented products that transform a humble vegetable. Today we leave the kitchen for a moment to present two digital projects that belong to the same editor as QKB. So that there are no misunderstandings: what follows is its own advertising.

## To brighten your eyes: AXTO

A good cheese also attracts the eyes, and [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=qkb.es&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) makes a living from that: it is a digital museum of moving art with clocks in real time, seals from kingdoms that do not exist, calligraphy from the Koran and hadiths without altering a word, and animated figures. No work is repeated, because each one arises from its own DNA.

Exclusive editions (**AC-**) are used as gifts to loyal customers: 1 dollar per edition, paid with AXTO currency, ten per work, without gas when sending them to several wallets. In OpenSea there are the public ones (**OS-**), between 1 and 10 editions with a fixed price from 1 to 250 dollars; GPU ones have **G**. From $1 you can invest in AXTO, the axto.io community currency; Staking delivers unrepeatable pieces, although no one promises benefits.

## From the workshop to the customer's table: XAA

If you sell your cheeses, breads or pickles in a market, to order or in a small store, your own website can open the door to many more customers. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=qkb.es&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European web development studio that designs and custom programs:

- **a presentation website** with the history of the workshop, seasonal products and where to find you;
- **an online store** with ordering, curbside pickup, or refrigerated shipping;
- **courses and workshops** with reservations and payment in advance, if you teach your craft.

The project is divided into milestones that are agreed upon, delivered and paid one by one, by PayPal, transfer or USDT. To go quickly there are [templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=qkb.es&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) that are customized with your photos and your colors. The XAA site is in Spanish, English and Indonesian.
