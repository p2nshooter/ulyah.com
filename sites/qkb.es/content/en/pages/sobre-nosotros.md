QKB is a digital magazine dedicated to the **domestic craftsman**: everything that can be made in a home kitchen with patience, good ingredients and a little technique. Her name summarizes the three passions that inspire her: **Cheese, Kefir and Pastries**. From there, the recipe book extends to sourdough, bread, fermented vegetables and preserves.

## Para quién escribimos

For anyone who wants to make at home what they normally buy: a fresh cheese for breakfast, a kefir that is renewed every day, a loaf of sourdough for the weekend, some Easter toast or jam from the plums from the town tree. You don't need experience or a professional kitchen. We explain each step, why it is done and what to do when something doesn't go as expected.

## How we work

QKB recipes and guides are created with the help of artificial intelligence tools and reviewed before publication. Each recipe is based on traditional techniques of Spanish cuisine and proven principles of food science: how rennet, yeasts and bacteria act, how temperature influences, why salt or sugar preserves. When we give quantities and times, they are indicative: each kitchen, each oven and each flour behaves somewhat differently, and we teach you to read the signals of the dough, milk or ferment.

## La seguridad alimentaria, primero

Making cheese, preserves or fermented foods at home is safe when some basic rules are respected: pasteurized milk, hygiene, correct temperatures and sufficient acidity in the preserves. That is why each guide includes its precautions and we maintain a specific [food safety] page (/seguridad-alimentaria/). We never recommend practices that health regulations advise against in the domestic environment.

## Independencia y publicidad

QKB is free and is financed by advertising managed by Google AdSense. No manufacturer pays to appear in our recipes; When we talk about utensils or ingredients, we explain what to look for, without promoting brands. More information in the [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/) and [cookie policy](/politica-de-cookies/).
