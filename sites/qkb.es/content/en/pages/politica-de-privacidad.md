At QKB we respect your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed when you visit qkb.es, why and how you can exercise your rights in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (RGPD) and Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and guarantee of digital rights.

## Responsible for the treatment

The person responsible for the website is the editor of QKB. You can contact us at [hola@qkb.es](mailto:hola@qkb.es) for any question related to your data.

## What data we process

**Browsing without registration.** To read QKB you do not need to create an account or provide us with your name or email.

**Visit statistics.** We record in aggregate which pages are visited and on what day, without our own cookies and without identifying the person reading. We use that information only to know which articles are useful.

**Emails you send us.** If you write to us, we process your address and the content of the message to respond to you. The legal basis is your consent when writing to us, and we retain the message only as long as necessary to respond to your query.

## Google AdSense Advertising

QKB displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. The Google advertising cookie allows Google and its partners to show you ads based on your browsing.

You can disable personalized advertising in [Google ad settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) and learn more about [how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can also disable cookies from third-party providers at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

When you visit QKB from the European Economic Area, you are shown a consent notice managed by Google before using advertising cookies, and you can change your choice at any time.

## International transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. It does so under the protection of the guarantees provided for in the GDPR, such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification, deletion, limitation of processing, portability of your data and oppose its processing by writing to [hola@qkb.es](mailto:hola@qkb.es). If you consider that we have not responded correctly to your request, you can file a claim with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

## Changes to this policy

If we change the way we process data, we will update this page and indicate the date of the last revision at the beginning of the document.
