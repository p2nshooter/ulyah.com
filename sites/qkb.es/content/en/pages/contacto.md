We love receiving messages from those who cook with our guides. You can write to us at:

**Email:** [hello@qkb.es](mailto:hola@qkb.es)

## What can you send us

- **Doubts about a recipe.** Tell us what ingredients you used, what temperature your kitchen was and what result you obtained. With this data it is much easier to find the cause.
- **Proposals for preparations.** Traditional recipes from your family or your region that you would like to see explained step by step.
- **Corrections.** If you spot an error in a quantity, time, or explanation, please let us know the item and we will revise it.

## What we can't do

We do not offer personalized medical or nutritional advice. If you have allergies, intolerances, are pregnant or have any illness, consult a health professional before consuming fermented or home-made foods. Nor can we assess by mail whether a homemade food is safe to eat: if in doubt, don't eat it.

## Response time

We usually respond within three to five business days.

## Your data

We use your email and message only to respond to you. We do not share them with third parties nor do we include them on shipping lists. Please see our [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/).
