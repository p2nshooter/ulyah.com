The right dowel depends primarily on the wall building material and only then on the load. An expansion anchor that is firmly seated in the concrete spins in the perforated brick. The finder above matches both; the tables below show the same for reference.

## Recognize the wall

| wall | How you recognize them | Drilling |
|---|---|---|
| Concrete | very hard, gray drill dust | Impact or hammer drilling |
| Solid brick, sand-lime brick | red or light-colored drill dust, uniform resistance | impact drilling |
| Perforated bricks | red drill dust, drill keeps falling into space | without a hit |
| Aerated concrete | soft, almost white drill dust | without impact, often with a smaller drill |
| Plasterboard, cavity wall | sounds hollow, plaster powder, cavity behind it | without a hit |
| Wood, chipboard | wood chips | Wood drill, no dowel required |

## Dowels according to wall and load

| wall | easy (picture, hook) | medium (shelf, mirror) | heavy (wall cabinet, TV) |
|---|---|---|---|
| concrete | Expansion or universal dowel 5–6 mm | Expansion dowel 6-8 mm | Expansion dowels 8–10 mm, long-shaft dowels |
| Solid brick, sand-lime brick | Expansion or universal dowel 5–6 mm | Expansion dowel 8 mm | Expansion or long-shaft dowels 10 mm |
| Perforated bricks | Universal dowel | long universal or frame dowel | Injection mortar with sieve sleeve |
| Aerated concrete | Aerated concrete or universal dowels | Aerated concrete dowels | Injection mortar for aerated concrete |
| Plasterboard | Plastic cavity dowel | Cavity dowel metal, toggle dowel | into the stand or a traverse |
| Wood | wood screw | Wood screw, pre-drill | long screws into load-bearing wood |

## How much does a dowel last?

Only the manufacturer or, for approved anchors, the approval of the German Institute for Building Technology says this. The drill hole depth, edge distances and the permissible loads per building material are listed there. Rules of thumb from the Internet are no substitute for this, especially not for loads overhead, over beds and sofas or in wall cupboards.

## Drill safely

Locate lines in front of each hole: Electricity and water lines usually run vertically above and below sockets and switches and horizontally in certain zones near the ceiling and floor. A line finder helps, but does not replace caution. Work on the electrical installation should be carried out by a qualified electrician. More on this in [Select and place dowels correctly](/anleitungen/duebel-richtig-waehlen-und-setzen/), [Correct drilling in concrete, brick and plasterboard](/anleitungen/richtig-bohren-in-beton-ziegel-gipskarton/) and [Locate pipes before drilling](/anleitungen/leitungen-orten-vor-dem-bohren/).
