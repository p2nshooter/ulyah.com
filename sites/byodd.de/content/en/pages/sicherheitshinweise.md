DIY is fun when you can do it without injury or damage. These rules apply to all instructions on BYODD.

## Protective equipment

- **Protective glasses** when drilling, grinding, sawing and chiseling.
- **Hearing protection** for loud machines.
- **Respirator mask** (at least FFP2) for sanding dust, drilling dust and working with solvents, better FFP3 for wood dust from hardwoods.
- **Gloves** suitable for work: cut-resistant when handling metal sheets and glass, chemical-resistant when dealing with solvents.
- **Sturdy shoes** and tight-fitting clothing when working on machines.

## Before drilling: Locate lines

Electricity, water and gas pipes do not always run where you expect them to. Check walls with a locator before drilling and stick to standard installation zones. If in doubt, turn off the circuit.

## Electricity

When working near cables, switch off the fuse and check that there is no voltage. Work on the electrical installation itself, i.e. laying cables, setting sockets or working in the fuse box, belongs in the hands of a qualified electrician.

## gas

Work on gas pipes and gas appliances is only permitted to authorized specialist companies. If you smell gas: do not operate any switches, open the windows, close the main tap, leave the house and call the gas supplier's emergency service.

## Asbestos and pollutants in old buildings

In buildings constructed before 1993, asbestos and other pollutants may be present in tile adhesives, fillers, floor coverings and panels. Do not handle suspicious materials, but have them checked.

## Ladders and height

Use stable ladders, place them on level ground and never climb to the top rungs. When working on ceilings, a scaffold or platform is safer.

## Chemicals and ventilation

Read the safety data sheets and instructions for use of paints, adhesives and cleaners. Ensure good ventilation and store products out of the reach of children.

## Rental apartments

Clarify any major interventions such as breakthroughs, removing tiles or laying solid floor coverings in writing with the landlord beforehand.
