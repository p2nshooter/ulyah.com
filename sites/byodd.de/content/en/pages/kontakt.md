We look forward to feedback. Whether you have found an error in a guide, missed a topic or have a question about your project: write to us.

## Email

**[kontakt@byodd.de](mailto:kontakt@byodd.de)**

We read every message and usually respond within a few business days.

## What particularly helps us

- **Corrections:** Please state the address of the instructions and the part that you think is incorrect or misleading. We correct errors quickly and note major changes.
- **Questions about your project:** Briefly describe the surface, the material and what you have already tried. Photos help. Please understand that we cannot give binding expert advice remotely.
- **Topic suggestions:** What work in the house or apartment would you like to do yourself? Many of our instructions arise from reader questions.

## What we don't offer

We do not provide craftsmen, sell any products or accept orders for sponsored posts. Requests for advertising space are processed via Google AdSense.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message. Please see our [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/) for details.
