These principles describe how our instructions are created and what you as a reader can follow.

## Practical and complete

Each instruction lists the tools and materials required, the approximate time required, the work steps in the correct order and the most common errors. We explain not only the how, but also the why so that you can react to different situations.

## Sources

We rely on manufacturer information and technical data sheets, relevant standards and regulations, information from consumer advice centers and professional associations as well as practical experience. We formulate legal information, for example regarding rental apartments, in general terms and refer you to advice centers if necessary.

## Safety is a priority

We clearly identify dangers: electricity, gas, asbestos in old buildings, falls, dust and solvents. We do not represent work that is reserved for specialist companies as self-construction projects. Our [Safety Instructions](/sicherheitshinweise/) apply to all instructions.

## Review and update

Each instruction has a test date. We revise content if regulations, products or best practices change, or if readers point out errors.

## Independence and advertising

We do not write paid posts and do not receive commissions for products mentioned. Advertising is displayed via Google AdSense, is recognizable as an ad and has no influence on the content.

## Corrections

We correct errors quickly. The editorial team accepts information via the [contact page](/kontakt/).
