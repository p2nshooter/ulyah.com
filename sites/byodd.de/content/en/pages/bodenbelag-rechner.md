Anyone who lays new floors buys packages, not square meters. There are also offcuts, insulation and skirting boards. The calculator above puts everything together for a rectangular room; For L-shaped rooms, calculate the partial areas individually and add them together.

## Waste according to installation type

| Type of laying | Waste | When |
|---|---|---|
| straight, remaining piece as the beginning of the next row | about 5% | rectangular rooms, few corners |
| Game association, many corners and niches | about 10% | Hallways, rooms with ledges |
| diagonally | about 15% | if the floorboards run at an angle to the wall |

An example: 5.2 × 3.8 m results in 19.76 m², straight laid with 5% waste results in 20.75 m². With 2.22 m² per package, that's 10 packages.

## Skirting boards and insulation

The skirting boards run the length of the room, minus the door openings; the calculator adds 10% for miters and remainders. You only need impact sound insulation if it is not already laminated under the covering - that is what it says on the package.

## Before laying

- Allow packages to acclimate in the room for as long as the manufacturer specifies, usually at least 48 hours.
- Surface level, dry and clean; When using screed, pay attention to the residual moisture.
- Leave the expansion joint all around according to the manufacturer's instructions.
