BYODD stands for **Bring Your Own DIY & Decoration**. The name describes what we want: People who want to do their own work in a rented apartment, a condominium or a house should not need a professional workshop. You bring your curiosity and a basic tool, we provide the instructions, the experience and the warnings.

## What you find here

BYODD is an independent German-language advice magazine for DIY, renovation and living. Our instructions are divided into six workshops:

- **Tools & Basics:** Drilling, doweling, measuring, grinding and working safely.
- **Walls & floors:** Painting, wallpapering, filling, laminate, vinyl and tiles.
- **Wood & Furniture:** Treat wood, refurbish furniture, build shelves.
- **Bathroom & Kitchen:** Refresh silicone joints, fittings, drains and tiles.
- **Decoration & Living:** Hanging pictures, lights, curtains and small rooms.
- **Household Repairs:** Dripping taps, sticking doors, wobbling furniture.

## How we work

Each instruction describes the material, tools, time required and the work steps in the order in which they occur in practice. We name typical mistakes and clearly state when a job should be entrusted to a specialist company, for example with electrical installations, gas appliances or load-bearing walls. More about this in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/) and [Safety Information](/sicherheitshinweise/).

We also use AI tools when researching and writing. Before publication, we compare each manual with manufacturer information and information from public authorities; Where there are reliable sources, we mention them under the instructions. You can find four calculators for paint, wallpaper, flooring and dowels under [Calculator](/werkzeuge/), and anything new or revised is listed with the date under [Updates](/aktualisierungen/).

## What we don't do

We do not sell tools or building materials, and we do not write instructions on behalf of manufacturers. BYODD is financed through advertising, which is displayed via Google AdSense and is clearly separated from the editorial content.
