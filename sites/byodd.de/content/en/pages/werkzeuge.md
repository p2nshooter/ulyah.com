Our instructions explain how to get the job done. These four helpers will do the math for you beforehand: How much paint, how many rolls of wallpaper, how many packages of laminate do I need, and which dowel will hold in my wall? All four calculate directly in your browser. Nothing is sent to us and you don't need an account.

## Color Calculator

From the length, width and height of the room, the number of doors and windows, the number of coats of paint and the yield stated on the bucket, he calculates the liters and suggests a combination of containers with as little leftover as possible. [To the color calculator](/farbrechner/)

## Wallpaper calculator

From the wall circumference, room height, roll size, repeat and approach, it determines the length of the strip, the strips per roll and the number of rolls - for standard rolls, double-width non-woven wallpaper and woodchip. [To the wallpaper calculator](/tapetenrechner/)

## Flooring Calculator

For laminate, click vinyl and finished parquet: area, waste depending on the type of installation, number of packages, impact sound insulation and skirting boards. [To the flooring calculator](/bodenbelag-rechner/)

## Dowel and drill finder

You choose the wall - concrete, solid brick, perforated brick, aerated concrete, plasterboard or wood - and the load, and the finder tells you the appropriate type of anchor, whether drilling is done with or without impact and how you can identify the wall. [To the dowel finder](/duebel-finder/)

## What the helpers cannot replace

The calculators give good estimates, not guarantees. What is written on the paint bucket, the wallpaper label or the package comes first. How much a dowel can carry is stated by the manufacturer or in its approval from the German Institute for Building Technology. Work on electrical installations, gas and load-bearing components should be carried out by specialist companies; What you can and cannot do yourself can be found on our [Safety Information](/sicherheitshinweise/) page. If you found an error, write to us via the [contact page](/kontakt/).
