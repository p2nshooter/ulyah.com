Furniture made from a box is practical and cheap, but for many people, assembling it is a test of patience: missing parts, incomprehensible drawings, a cupboard that is crooked after being put up, and three screws left at the end. With a few tricks, the setup can be made much more relaxed, faster and, above all, more stable.

This guide provides practical tips for assembling kit furniture, from unpacking and reading the instructions to aligning, attaching and securing tall furniture.

## Before construction

### Create space

- **Set up in the finish area** if possible. Large cupboards often do not fit through doors or under sloping ceilings.
- **Check room height:** A cabinet that is built lying down needs more height when standing up than when standing up, namely the diagonal. A 236 cm high and 58 cm deep cabinet needs around 243 cm to set up.
- **Soft surface:** Place the cardboard or a blanket on the floor. It protects the floor and the surface of the furniture.

### Check parts

1. **Unpack all parts** and sort according to the parts list in the instructions.
2. **Count small parts** and sort them into bowls or muffin tins. If something is missing, contact the manufacturer now, not after three hours of work.
3. **Check parts for transport damage.**

### Prepare tools

The included tools work, but are often inconvenient. Better:

| tool | For what |
|---|---|
| Cordless screwdriver with low torque level | Screw in screws faster and more evenly |
| Suitable bits (PZ2, Torx, Allen) | No slipping |
| Rubber mallet | Knock in wooden dowels and back walls without causing any damage |
| Spirit level | Align |
| Cutter knife | Open boxes |
| pencil | Markings |

**Be careful with the cordless screwdriver:** Always tighten eccentric connectors (the round metal fittings) and small screws in chipboard with a low torque setting or by hand. Otherwise they will break away. See [Buy and use cordless screwdriver](/anleitungen/akkuschrauber-kaufen-und-benutzen/).

## Read the instructions carefully

- **Follow through completely once** before you begin. This way you can see which parts are needed when and where there are pitfalls.
- **Pay attention to details:** Small arrows, alignment of holes, pages with and without holes. Many errors occur because a side panel is installed upside down.
- **Write part numbers:** Write small numbers on the edges with a pencil (where you won't see them later).
- **Check off steps.**

## The most important connections

### wooden dowel

- Insert into the pre-drilled holes, if necessary with a little wood glue for more stability (if the manufacturer allows it and you don't want to disassemble the furniture again).
- Tap in with the rubber mallet.

### Eccentric connector (cam connector)

1. Screw the connecting bolt into one part.
2. Place the eccentric in the round hole of the other part, arrow towards the edge.
3. Put parts together.
4. Turn the eccentric clockwise with the screwdriver until it tightens. **Do not overtighten.**

### Screws in chipboard

Tighten hand tight; Overtightened screws tear out the material. Repair torn holes as described in [Use wood screws correctly](/anleitungen/holzschrauben-vorbohren-und-senken/).

## Back walls: the secret stability

The thin back wall made of hardboard appears inconspicuous, but is crucial for stability. It prevents a cabinet from warping.

1. **Align the body at right angles** before attaching the back panel: measure the diagonals. If both are the same length, the body is rectangular.
2. **Attach the back wall all around,** using all nails or screws provided, including in the middle shelves.
3. **For split rear walls** don't forget the connecting strips.

A cabinet without a properly attached back panel will wobble and the doors will not close properly.

## Drawers and doors

- **Assemble drawer runners** exactly according to instructions; left and right are often different.
- **Adjusting doors:** Modern cup hinges have three adjustment screws for height, side and depth. Only adjust when the cabinet is in its final position and aligned. Refer to [Adjust Cabinet Door and Drawer](/anleitungen/schranktuer-und-schublade-einstellen/).

## Erect, align and secure

1. **Set up with two people:** Large cabinets are heavy and unwieldy. Do not pull on doors or drawers when standing up.
2. **Alignment:** Check with a spirit level and adjust with leveling feet or wheel chocks. A crooked cupboard warps and doors don't close.
3. **Install anti-tip protection:** Always attach tall furniture to the wall using dowels that fit the wall. The dowels supplied do not fit every wall. See [Select and place dowels correctly](/anleitungen/duebel-richtig-waehlen-und-setzen/).

**Why anti-tip protection is so important:** Dressers and shelves can tip over when children pull on open drawers or climb up. Accidents of this type are not uncommon and can be serious. The attachment takes ten minutes.

## Common mistakes

| Error | Episode |
|---|---|
| Part installed upside down | Holes are missing later, dismantling necessary |
| Eccentric over-twisted | Connection broken |
| Back wall not secured all around | Cabinet shakes, doors hang |
| Cabinet not aligned | Doors don't close |
| No anti-tip protection | Danger of accidents |
| Set up lying down, too high to stand up | Closet doesn't fit in the room |

## Dismantle again and move

- **Take photos during assembly** and keep the instructions, they are often available online.
- **Put fittings in labeled bags when dismantling**.
- **Chipboard can only tolerate repeated disassembly to a limited extent:** Repair torn holes when rebuilding with wood glue and toothpicks.

## Old building: crooked floors, skirting boards and external walls

In old apartments, floors are rarely flat and walls are rarely vertical. Therefore, plan for adjustable feet or wedges right from the start and always align the cabinet according to the spirit level, not the wall. Baseboards often prevent a cabinet from sitting flush against the wall. On many carcasses, the back wall can be notched at the bottom so that it fits over the bar, or you can compensate for the distance at the top with a spacer to which the anti-tip device is attached. Large cabinets should be a few centimeters clear of cold outside walls so that air can circulate behind them. Otherwise there is a risk of mold, as described in [Remove and prevent mold on the wall](/anleitungen/schimmel-an-der-wand-entfernen-und-vorbeugen/).

## Floors that bend

Long shelves made of chipboard bend over time under heavy books. The longer the base and the thinner the plate, the stronger. For spans of well over 80 centimeters and heavy loads, it is worth using a central support, a thicker floor or a bar under the front edge to stiffen the floor. Only load glass shelves to the extent specified by the manufacturer and ensure that all shelf supports are seated correctly.

## Protect your back

Packages with furniture parts can easily weigh 40 kilos or more. Carry heavy boxes with two people, lifting from your knees and not from your back, and it is best to open large packages right where the furniture should be. Work gloves protect against sharp edges on fittings and back panels, and a knee cushion makes hours of work on the floor more bearable. Also plan regular short breaks.

## When an assembly service makes sense

Many dealers offer installation for an additional charge. This is particularly worthwhile in fitted kitchens with water and power connections, in very large sliding door cupboards whose runners have to be precisely adjusted, and if no one is there to help. For chests of drawers, shelves and smaller cupboards, you can easily set it up yourself using these instructions.

## Practical example: A large wardrobe under the sloping roof

A couple buys a wardrobe with a height of 236 cm for a bedroom with a ceiling height of 250 cm. When planning, you notice: If the cabinet is set up horizontally, it needs a diagonal of more than 240 cm when it is set up, and there is a ceiling light on the wall that gets in the way. That's why they build the body lying down, set it up **sideways** on the free side of the room and then push it against the wall on furniture sliders.

Further steps:

1. Parts sorted and counted, a wooden dowel is missing; Replacement from the workshop box.
2. Read the instructions completely once; noticed that two side walls are mirror images.
3. Eccentric tightened by hand with the screwdriver, screws with the cordless screwdriver at level 4.
4. Body aligned perpendicularly across the diagonals before attaching the back panel.
5. Cabinet aligned with a spirit level, underlaid with wedges in two places.
6. Tilt protection with suitable dowels in the brick wall.
7. Doors only set at the very end.

Total time for two: about five hours.

## Checklist

- Room height and vertical diagonal checked.
- All parts and fittings counted.
- Read the instructions completely in advance.
- Cordless screwdriver at low torque level.
- Body aligned at right angles in front of the rear wall.
- Tilt protection installed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use wood glue?

Glue increases stability on wooden dowels. But then the furniture can no longer be disassembled cleanly.

### What do I do with leftover screws?

Check whether a step has been forgotten. Some manufacturers include a reserve.

### Why are the doors crooked after days?

The cupboard often sits on uneven ground. Realign and adjust the hinges.

### Further questions from practice

**How do I get a stuck wooden dowel out again?** Carefully pull it out using pliers and a cloth. If it breaks off, drill out the rest and insert a new dowel.

**Can I build a closet by myself?** Small furniture, yes. With tall cabinets, a second person is almost essential for setting up and hanging the doors.

**What do I do with damaged parts?** Report any transport damage to the dealer immediately, if possible with photos. Many manufacturers send replacement parts free of charge.

**My open shelf wobbles sideways. What helps?** Shelves without a back wall have hardly any bracing. A subsequently screwed-on hardboard back panel, diagonal struts on the back or small metal brackets in the corners make it significantly more stable. Additionally attach to the top of the wall.

**How ​​do I dispose of the packaging?** Cardboard boxes folded flat into waste paper, foil and Styrofoam into the recycling bin or to the recycling center, depending on local regulations.

## Conclusion

Kit furniture can be assembled in a relaxed manner if you make space beforehand, count all the parts, read the instructions completely and use good tools with a low torque level. Only tighten the eccentrics and screws hand-tight, align the body at a right angle before attaching the back wall, use the spirit level to set the cabinet straight and always secure tall furniture against tipping over.
