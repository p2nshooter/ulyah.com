Brown floral tiles from the seventies, mint green bathroom tiles from the nineties or a kitchen mirror that no longer fits the new style: knocking out old tiles is loud, dusty, expensive and usually out of the question in rented apartments. Fortunately, there are alternatives that can be used to visually renew tiles without removing them: tile paint, tile foil, tile stickers and wall panels.

These instructions compare the options, show you step by step how to paint or cover tiles, and explain what needs to be taken into account in the splash area and in rented apartments.

## Important in advance: rented apartment

The following applies to rented apartments: Painting tiles is a structural change that usually cannot be reversed when you move out. **You should not paint tiles without the landlord's consent.** Adhesive films and stickers can usually be removed, but can leave residue or damage joints when removed. ## Painting tiles

### Which paint?

- **2K tile paint** (two-component paint): Base and hardener are mixed. Very hard, abrasion-resistant, suitable for splash water areas. Limited processing time after mixing.
- **1K tile varnish:** easier to work with, but less robust.
- **Tile primer:** Necessary for many systems so that the paint adheres to the smooth glaze.

For showers and surfaces directly exposed to water, many manufacturers advise against painting or only recommend special systems. Pay attention to manufacturer's instructions.

### Preparation: the most important part

1. **Clean thoroughly:** Remove grease, limescale and soap residue with a tile cleaner or descaler. In kitchens with a grease-dissolving cleaner.
2. **Remove silicone joints,** they will be redrawn after painting. Lacquer does not stick to silicone. See [Renewing silicone joints](/anleitungen/silikonfugen-erneuern/).
3. **Repair damaged joints.** See [Renew tile joints](/anleitungen/fliesen-verfugen-und-fugen-erneuern/).
4. **Sanding:** Matt the shiny glaze with P180 to P240 or a sanding fleece. This significantly improves adhesion.
5. **Dedust and degrease** with spirit or silicone remover.
6. **Tape:** Faucets, tub edges, electrical outlets and adjacent walls.
7. **Prime,** if required by the system.

### Strike out

1. **Mix the paint** (at 2K exactly as specified).
2. **Paint the joints with a brush first** if you want them to be the same color.
3. **Apply areas thinly and evenly with a fine foam or paint roller**.
4. **Let dry,** then second coat. Some systems require light intermediate sanding.
5. **Curing:** 2K paints often need several days to reach full strength. Do not apply water during this time.
6. **Re-draw silicone joints.**

### Color the joints differently

For a modern look, the joints can be redone with a white or gray joint pen after painting.

## Tile film and tile stickers

### Large-area film

Self-adhesive films are available in plain colors, marble, concrete or wood looks. They cover the joints.

1. **Clean and dry the surface.** Deep joints can appear through the film; some fill them flush beforehand.
2. **Cut the foil** with a little extra.
3. **Remove the backing paper gradually** and press the film from the center outwards with a squeegee. Squeeze out bubbles.
4. **Cut off excess.**
5. **Warm edges slightly with a hairdryer** and press down; this improves adhesion.

### Individual tile stickers

Stickers in tile format (for example 15 × 15 cm) are glued to each individual tile; the joints remain visible. It is important to order the correct format. They are particularly suitable for kitchen back walls and wall areas without direct water contact.

### Boundaries

- **Heat:** Foil behind hobs can become detached or discolored. Observe distance and manufacturer's instructions.
- **Shower:** Films are usually not suitable for permanent water contact.

## Wall panels

Large-format panels made of plastic, aluminum composite or glass are glued or screwed directly onto the tiles.

- **Advantages:** Seamless, very easy to clean, also suitable for showers (if approved).
- **Disadvantages:** Higher costs, precise cutting required, cutouts for fittings must be set precisely.
- **Assembly:** Use assembly adhesive or adhesive tape according to manufacturer's instructions, seal connections with silicone.

| area | Recommendation |

## First knock, then design

Before you paint, stick or plan over tiles, check whether they are firmly in place. Tap the surface with the handle of a screwdriver: a bright, hard sound means a firm hold, while a dull, hollow sound shows that the tile has separated from the surface. Hollow or cracked tiles must first be fixed or replaced, otherwise the new surface will crack right there. Loose tiles can be removed, reattached with fresh tile adhesive and re-grouted.

## Protection when working with 2K paint

Two-component paints are robust because they harden chemically. That's exactly why they deserve respect: Many contain epoxy or polyurethane components, which can irritate or cause allergies when they come into contact with the skin, and solvent fumes. Wear nitrile gloves and safety glasses, ventilate vigorously during and after painting, and keep children and pets out of the room. For products containing solvents, a respiratory mask with a suitable gas filter is recommended. Read the technical data sheet and the safety instructions on the container beforehand and only mix as much as you can process in the specified pot life.

## Choose color and gloss level

Light tones make small bathrooms appear larger; A warm white or a soft gray appears friendlier than pure cold white. Strong colors create accents, for example on a single wall or in combination with a tile backsplash. The following applies to the level of gloss: Shiny surfaces are easier to wipe off, but show every unevenness and every roller streak. Semi-matt varnishes are more forgiving. Paint a single tile as a sample in front of the large area and look at it in daylight and artificial light.

## Tile on tile

If you want real new tiles, you can often put them directly on top of the old ones if they are firm and flat. To do this, the old covering is cleaned, pre-treated with an adhesive primer and re-covered with flexible tile adhesive. Remember that this will make the wall about one centimeter thicker: fitting rosettes, sockets, door frames and mirror cabinets must fit this. For most people this is a job for the tiler, but it saves chipping and a lot of dirt.

## Practical example: Guest bathroom in two days

A small guest bathroom with sink and toilet has dark brown wall tiles up to 1.50 m high. The owners want it to be bright and modern without chipping away at the tiles. There is no shower.

**Day 1:**
1. Clean tiles thoroughly with tile cleaner and then with spirit.
2. Silicone joints on the sink and floor removed.
3. Entire surface matted with abrasive fleece, vacuumed and degreased again.
4. Faucets, sink, toilet and wall above the tiles taped off.
5. Tile primer applied.

**Day 2:**
1. 2K tile paint mixed in a warm white.
2. Joints pre-painted with a brush, surfaces rolled with a foam roller.
3. After the drying time, second coat.

**After curing for a week:** New silicone joints were drawn, joints were re-tightened with a light gray joint pen. The bathroom appears twice as bright and the material costs were in the low three-digit range.

## Checklist

- Consent of the landlord in rental apartments.
- Suitable system chosen for the area of ​​application.
- Thoroughly cleaned, degreased and sanded.
- Silicone joints removed beforehand.
- Wait for curing time before contact with water.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does tile paint last?

A well-prepared 2K paint will last for many years on the wall outside the shower.

### Can I just paint the joints?

Yes, with grout paint or grout pen. This often makes old tiles look significantly fresher.

### Can tile stickers be removed without leaving any residue?

Mostly yes, with a little heat from a hairdryer. If the joints are old or porous, residue may remain.

### Further questions from practice

**Can I also paint floor tiles?** Only with special, abrasion-resistant floor coatings and very careful preparation. Even the best paint wears off on heavily trafficked surfaces. Vinyl on tiles is often the more durable solution.

**Which roller is suitable for tile paint?** A fine foam or velor roller for paint. It leaves a smooth surface without orange peel.

**How ​​do I clean painted tiles?** With a mild cleaner and a soft cloth, without abrasive cleaners. Limescale cleaner should only be diluted and according to the manufacturer's instructions.

**What to do if paint chips?** Sand, degrease and repair the area. If the paint is chipping off over a large area, the preparation was usually inadequate; then the only solution is to remove and rebuild.

**Can I have painted tiles back in the original color later?** Hardly. Tile paint can only be removed with great effort, for example with paint strippers or by sanding, and the glaze often suffers in the process. If you are unsure, start with foil or stickers.

## Conclusion

Old tiles can be modernized without any chiseling work: with 2K tile paint after thorough preparation, with large-area foil or individual stickers or with wall panels for the shower and cooking area. Cleaning, sanding and choosing a system that suits the area of ​​application are crucial. In rented apartments, speak to the landlord beforehand.
