Laminate is one of the most popular floor coverings for do-it-yourselfers: It is comparatively inexpensive, robust, available in many wood and stone looks and can be laid without glue thanks to the click system. A living room can be redesigned in a weekend. To ensure that the floor does not creak, bulge or develop joints later, preparation and a few basic rules are important.

These instructions explain the construction of a laminate floor, the necessary preparation of the subsurface, laying row by row, dealing with doors, heating pipes and transitions as well as the most common mistakes.

## Building a laminate floor

A laminate floor is laid **floating**: It lies loosely on the surface and is not screwed or glued anywhere. The structure from bottom to top:

1. **Substrate:** Screed, concrete, wooden floorboards or an old floor covering such as tiles.
2. **Vapor barrier (PE film):** on mineral surfaces such as screed and concrete, so that residual moisture does not penetrate the laminate.
3. **Impact sound insulation:** dampens walking noises and compensates for small unevenness.
4. **Laminate floorboards** with click connection.
5. **Skirts:** cover the edge joint and are attached to the wall, not the floor.

Some impact sound insulations already have the vapor barrier integrated.

## Material and tools

| material | Note |
|---|---|
| Laminate | Area plus 5 to 10% waste, more if laid diagonally |
| Impact sound insulation | Matches the laminate and underfloor heating, if available |
| PE film (0.2 mm) | For mineral substrates, overlap strips by 20 cm |
| Adhesive tape | For the foil joints |
| Skirting boards and fastenings | Clips or mounting glue |
| Spacer wedges | For the expansion joint |
| Transition profiles | On doors and changing coverings |

**Tools:** Folding rule, pencil, square, saw (jigsaw with laminate blade, chop saw or fine hand saw), rubber mallet, tapping block, pull iron, cutter knife, possibly a Japanese saw or a multi-tool for door frames.

## Step 1: Check the surface

- **Even:** Remove unevenness of more than 2 to 3 mm per meter (note manufacturer's instructions) with leveling compound. Prüfen mit einer langen Wasserwaage oder Richtlatte.
- **Dry:** New screed must be completely dry. If in doubt, have a moisture measurement taken.
- **Fest und sauber:** Lose Teile entfernen, gründlich saugen.
- **Remove carpet:** Do not lay laminate on carpet; he is too soft.
- **Old tiles:** Can usually remain as a surface if they are solid and even.

## Step 2: Acclimate the laminate

Store the unopened packages lying flat in the room where they will be laid at normal room temperature for **at least 48 hours**. The material adapts to temperature and humidity and works less after installation.

## Step 3: Plan the laying direction

- **Longitudinal to the incidence of light:** The floorboards are usually laid parallel to the main window. This means that the impacts are less noticeable.
- **In narrow rooms**, laying along the long wall appears more spacious.
- **Calculate first and last row:** Divide room width by floorboard width. If the last row is narrower than about 5 cm, cut the first row correspondingly narrower so that both edge rows are of similar width.

## Step 4: Vapor barrier and impact sound insulation

1. Lay out **PE film** on a mineral surface, overlap the strips by at least 20 cm and glue them together. Pull up about 3 cm on the walls; The excess will later be cut off behind the skirting board.
2. **Impact sound insulation** Lay out end to end, without overlap. Only lay out as much as you will soon cover with laminate to avoid damaging it.

## Step 5: The First Row

1. **Place spacer wedges** on the wall: The expansion joint to all walls, door frames, heating pipes and fixed installations is usually 8 to 10 mm, follow the manufacturer's instructions.
2. **Lay the first plank** with the groove side facing the wall in the left corner. In many systems, the tongue on the side of the wall has to be sawn off so that the plank sits straight.
3. **Weitere Dielen** der Reihe an der Stirnseite einklicken.
4. **Letzte Diele der Reihe** abmessen, zuschneiden und einsetzen. Pull to the front side with the pulling iron and rubber mallet.
5. **Check Straight:** Walls are rarely straight. Spanne eine Schnur oder prüfe die erste Reihe mit einer Richtlatte und gleiche mit den Keilen aus. The first row defines the entire floor.

## Step 6: The remaining rows

1. **Reststück als Anfang:** Das abgeschnittene Reststück der letzten Diele beginnt die nächste Reihe, wenn es mindestens 30 bis 40 cm lang ist. This creates a natural offset.
2. **Offset of joints:** The end joints of adjacent rows should be at least 30 to 40 cm apart (manufacturer's information). Avoid a regular stair pattern.
3. **Click in:** Insert the long side diagonally into the previous row and lower it. Depending on the system, first the front side, then the long side.
4. **Close joints:** Carefully tap together with a tapping block and rubber mallet. Never hit the edge directly with the hammer.

## Step 7: The Last Row

1. **Measure width:** For each plank individually, as walls often run at an angle. Distance to the wall minus the expansion joint.
2. **Längs zuschneiden** mit Stichsäge oder Tischkreissäge.
3. **Einsetzen** und mit dem Zugeisen an die vorherige Reihe ziehen.

## Door frames, heating pipes and transitions

### Door frames

Instead of cutting out the laminate around the frame, **shorten the frame at the bottom**: Place a piece of laminate with impact sound insulation on the frame as a height measure, saw off the frame at this height using a Japanese saw or multitool. The plank is then pushed under the frame. This looks clean and leaves room for stretching.

### heating pipes

1. Mark the position of the pipes on the floorboard.
2. Löcher bohren, die etwa 16 bis 20 mm größer sind als das Rohr (Dehnungsfuge).
3. Saw the plank crosswise through the center of the hole.
4. Hauptteil verlegen, abgesägtes Stück mit Holzleim an der Schnittkante wieder ansetzen.
5. Cover with pipe sleeves.

### Transitions

- **On doors between rooms** and for areas over approximately 8 to 10 m long (manufacturer's information), provide an **movement joint** with a transition profile.
- **Use transition or adjustment profiles for other coverings** such as tiles.

## Step 8: Skirting Boards

1. Remove spacer wedges.
2. Cut off any excess foil.
3. Sockelleisten **an der Wand** befestigen, mit Clips, Nägeln oder Montagekleber, nicht am Laminat. The floor must be able to move underneath.
4. Miter corners.

## Understand usage class and abrasion class

Information about the load capacity is provided on each laminate package. The **usage class** describes what the floor is intended for: Classes from 21 to 23 apply to living spaces, with 23 standing for heavily used areas such as hallways and living rooms, classes from 31 for commercial areas. The **abrasion class**, usually given as AC1 to AC6, says something about the resistance of the surface. Für Wohnzimmer, Flur und Küche ist ein Laminat der Abriebklasse AC4 eine gute Wahl, für Schlafzimmer reicht oft AC3. Höhere Klassen kosten etwas mehr, halten Stuhlrollen, Haustieren und Straßenschuhen aber deutlich länger stand.

## Care and small repairs

Laminate flooring is swept, vacuumed and mopped with a damp cloth at most. Stehendes Wasser dringt in die Fugen ein und lässt die Kanten aufquellen, das ist der häufigste Schaden. Filzgleiter unter Stühlen und Tischen und weiche Rollen unter Bürostühlen schützen die Oberfläche. Small flaws can be filled with repair wax or hard wax in a suitable color, which is heated and pressed into the area and pulled off smoothly. If a plank is badly damaged and it is close to the wall, you can pick up the rows up to there, replace the plank and click everything back in place. Für Dielen mitten im Raum gibt es spezielle Austauschtechniken; A specialist company can often help here. Therefore, always keep some spare floorboards.

## Laminate and underfloor heating

Laminate is generally suitable for hot water underfloor heating if laminate and impact sound insulation are approved for this. Important: The insulation must not impede the heat transfer too much, and the heating must have been heated up according to a set protocol before installation. Pay attention to manufacturer's instructions.

## Common mistakes

| Error | Episode |
|---|---|
| No expansion joint | Ground bulges in summer |
| Skirting boards attached to the floor | Floor cannot work, joints are cracking |
| No vapor barrier on screed | Puffy edges |
| Uneven ground not balanced | Creaking, click system breaks |
| Laminate not acclimatized | Joints or bulges |
| Hit the edge directly with the hammer | Damaged connection |

## Practical example: living room with heating pipes

A 22 square meter living room gets laminate flooring in an oak look. Two heating pipes come out of the floor and a door frame leads into the hallway.

1. Screed checked with the leveling staff, a depression filled with leveling compound.
2. PE film with a 20 cm overlap, followed by impact sound insulation.
3. First row aligned with a taut cord because the wall is slightly slanted.
4. Door frame shortened to floorboard height using a Japanese saw.
5. Holes with an oversize of 18 mm were drilled for the heating pipes, the floorboard was sawn crosswise, the fitting piece was glued in, and the rosettes were put on.
6. A transition profile in the doorway as an expansion joint.

After two days the floor was still and without joints.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I lay laminate flooring in my rented apartment?

A floating floor can be removed again. Nevertheless, it is wise to speak to the landlord beforehand, especially if existing coverings need to be removed.

### Laminate or vinyl?

Vinyl is more waterproof and quieter, laminate is often cheaper and harder. See [Vinyl flooring: click or stick](/anleitungen/vinylboden-klicken-oder-kleben/).

### How much laminate do I need?

Room area plus 5 to 10% for waste.

### Can I lay laminate in the bathroom?

Only laminate suitable for damp rooms with appropriately sealed edges, and even then vinyl or tile is usually the better choice.

## Conclusion

A successful laminate floor begins with a flat, dry surface, a vapor barrier and suitable impact sound insulation. Acclimate the floorboards, align the first row carefully, maintain an expansion gap everywhere and stagger the joints sufficiently. Shorten door frames instead of cutting them out, plan expansion joints in large rooms and only attach skirting boards to the wall. Then the floor lies calm, quiet and beautiful for a long time.
