Curtains change a room dramatically: They dampen light and sound, protect from prying eyes and can make a window appear larger or smaller. How well this works depends not only on the material, but also on where and how the rod or rail is mounted. A pole that is installed too low and too short will make any room appear squat; A poorly attached one tears out of the wall at the first strong pull.

These instructions explain how high and how wide a curtain rod should be, which attachment sticks to which wall, how to install it step by step and what solutions are available without drilling.

## Bar or rail?

| system | Optics | Advantages | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curtain rod with straps | Visible, decorative, with end pieces | Classic, many designs, eyelet or loop scarves | Living rooms, bedrooms |
| curtain rail | Discreet, almost invisible | Curtains run easily, corner solutions are also possible | Modern rooms, ceiling mounting, continuous window fronts |
| Interior reveal rod | In the window reveal | Space saving | Small windows, bathroom, kitchen |
| clamping rod | Without drilling | Flexible | Rental apartments, window reveals |

## The right height

- **Standard:** 10 to 15 cm above the lintel (the top edge of the window opening).
- **For high impact:** Rod significantly higher, just below the ceiling, and floor-length curtains. The room then appears higher.
- **For ceiling mounting:** The rail sits directly on the ceiling; the curtains reach to the floor.

**Length of the curtains:** Floor length (1 to 2 cm above the floor) looks elegant. Curtains that rest on the floor look lush, but get dirty more easily. Window sill length is suitable for radiators under the window.

## The right width

- **At least 15 to 20 cm** on each side beyond the window.
- **For more light and optical width:** 25 to 40 cm on each side. This means that the open curtains hang next to the window instead of in front of it, and the window appears larger.

## Fastening: which wall, which dowel?

There is a special feature above windows: the **window lintel.** It is often made of reinforced concrete, even if the rest of the wall is made of brick. Above this there can be a **roller shutter box** that is hollow and does not carry any load.

| underground | Fastening |
|---|---|
| Reinforced concrete lintel | Expansion dowels, hammer drill, pay attention to reinforcement |
| Brick | Universal or expansion anchors |
| Aerated concrete | Aerated concrete dowels |
| Plasterboard | Cavity dowels, if possible in the substructure |
| Roller shutter box | Do not screw in; Attach the support to the side or ceiling |

**Cables:** Cables for roller shutter drives or lighting sometimes run above windows. Locate before drilling. See [Locating Pipes Before Drilling](/anleitungen/leitungen-orten-vor-dem-bohren/).

## Installing the curtain rod: step by step

### material

- Pole, supports (two, three for poles over 2 m), end pieces, rings or gliders.
- Dowels and screws to match the wall.
- Spirit level, folding rule, pencil, drill, screwdriver.

### Procedure

1. **Set height and width:** Determine and mark the center of the window.
2. **Mark carrier positions:** Measure equidistant left and right from the center of the window. The supports are usually 5 to 15 cm away from the end of the pole so that there is room for the end pieces.
3. **Align horizontally:** With a spirit level or laser. Do not measure at the ceiling or window, which are rarely completely straight.
4. **Drilling and dowelling.**
5. **Screw on the carrier.**
6. **Middle support for long poles:** From a length of around 2 m, place a third support in the middle so that the pole does not sag. For rings, the center support must be open so that the rings slide past.
7. **Insert the rod,** put on the rings (one ring outside the supports as an end ring), put on the end pieces and secure the rod with the fixing screw.

## Mount the curtain rail on the ceiling

1. **Check ceiling construction material:** Concrete, wooden beams with cladding or plasterboard.
2. **Mark rail position:** Approximately 10 to 15 cm from the wall so that curtains hang freely, especially if radiators or window sills protrude.
3. **Attachment points** approximately every 60 to 80 cm, according to the manufacturer's instructions.
4. **Drill, dowel, screw on the rail.**
5. **Insert gliders** and mount end caps.

## No drilling: alternatives for tenants

- **Clamp supports:** Are clamped onto the window frame and support light rods or panel curtains. Only for tilt-and-turn windows with a suitable frame profile.
- **Clamping rods** in the window reveal: Hold by tension between the reveal walls.
- **Adhesive hooks** for very light curtains.
- **Pleated blinds for clamping** as a privacy screen directly on the window sash.

## Choose the right curtain

| Suspension | Optics | Note |
|---|---|---|
| eyelets | Even, large wrinkles | For poles only |
| loops | Casual, not easy to pull | For poles, more decorative |
| Curling ribbon with hooks | Classic folds | For rails and rings |
| Pleated tape (wave pleats) | Modern, even S-folds | With special rails |

**Fabric Width:** For beautiful pleats, the curtain width should be about 1.5 to 2 times the rod width.

**Blackout and warmth:** Dense fabrics insulate and darken. Thermal curtains help against heat loss from old windows in winter and against heat in summer. But it is more important that radiators are not completely covered. See [Adjusting windows against drafts](/anleitungen/fenster-einstellen-zugluft/).

## Common mistakes

| Error | Episode |
|---|---|
| Screwed into the roller shutter box | Carriers don't hold, box damaged |
| Bar too short | Curtains cover the window, less light |
| Pole directly above the window | Room appears lower |
| No center support for long poles | Rod sags |
| Not aligned horizontally | Curtains hang crooked |

## Measure and shorten curtains correctly

The measurement is only taken when the rod or rail is hanging: from the lower edge of the rings or gliders to the point where the curtain should end. Ready-made curtains are usually only available in a few standard lengths, so shortening them is almost always an option. This can be done without a sewing machine using iron hem tape, which glues the folded hem to the iron. Wash linen or cotton curtains once before shortening them if the manufacturer does not rule out shrinkage, otherwise they will suddenly be too short after the first wash. Also let the curtains hang for a day or two before fixing the hem, as heavy fabrics still stretch a little due to their own weight.

## Bay windows, corners and skylights

For bay windows and corners, there are bendable curtain rails and corner connectors for rods so that the curtains run around the corner without interruption. Only bend the rails as much as the manufacturer specifies, otherwise the gliders will jam. Skylights are a special case: classic curtains hang away from the window. Roller blinds or pleated blinds with side guide rails, which are offered to match the window model and also lie against the glass at an angle, are better.

## Cords and small children

Roller blinds, blinds and Roman blinds with pull cords or chains can be dangerous for small children because a child can get caught in them. Always keep cords and chains out of reach, for example with a cord holder high on the wall or on the window frame, and do not place beds, chairs and play furniture directly next to such windows. Newer products have safety connectors that release under tension and there are cordless blinds. Cordless solutions are the safest choice in children's rooms.

## Practical example: A large living room window with a roller shutter box

A roller shutter box sits above a 2.20 m wide window, with a concrete ceiling above it. Floor-length linen curtains are desired, which make the window appear larger.

1. **Check the surface:** Knock and a test hole shows: The roller shutter box is hollow, the ceiling is made of concrete.
2. **Decision:** Instead of a rod above the box, a curtain rail is mounted on the ceiling, 12 cm from the wall.
3. **Width:** The rail is 3.00 m long, so it protrudes 40 cm beyond the window on each side.
4. **Locate cables** in the ceiling, then a fastening point every 70 cm with 6 mm dowels.
5. **Curtains:** Two scarves with pleated tape, total width approximately twice as wide as the rail.

The open curtains now hang next to the window, the room appears higher and brighter.

## Checklist

- Height and width set.
- Surface checked, roller shutter box left out.
- Lines located.
- Center support for long poles.
- Curtain width about 1.5 to 2 times.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much weight does a curtain rod support?

This depends on the rod diameter, supports and fastening. For heavy blackout curtains, choose sturdy rods with three straps.

### Can I screw into a plasterboard lintel?

With cavity dowels for light curtains. For heavy curtains in the substructure or in the ceiling.

### How far should the rail be from the wall?

So far that the curtains hang freely in front of the window sill and radiator, usually 10 to 15 cm.

## Time required and costs

| work | time | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Curtain rod with two straps | 30 to 45 minutes | plus line location |
| Long pole with three straps | 45 to 60 minutes | Center strap open for rings |
| Curtain rail on the ceiling, 3 m | 1 to 1.5 hours | Attach every 60 to 80 cm |
| Clamp support on the window frame | 10 minutes | without drilling |

The costs depend heavily on the material and design. Simple rails and bars are cheap, designer bars made of brass or solid wood are significantly more expensive. For heavy, floor-length blackout curtains, a sturdy version with a larger diameter is worth it.

### Further questions from practice

**Eyelets or rings?** Eyelet curtains run directly on the rod and form even, large pleats. Rings with hooks allow any curtain with a ruffle tape and are easier to remove.

**What to do if the pole sags?** Add an additional center support or choose a pole with a larger diameter.

**How ​​do I clean curtains without taking them down?** Regular vacuuming with the upholstery nozzle on a low setting removes dust. Curtains can be quickly removed for laundry using gliders with hooks; Eyelet curtains are hung together with the rod. Pay attention to the care label, many fabrics can only be washed on a gentle cycle.

## Conclusion

Install curtain rods 4 to 6 inches above the lintel or higher for more room height and 6 to 16 inches wider than the window on each side. Check the surface, especially the lintel and roller shutter box, choose suitable dowels, align with a spirit level and, if the poles are long, place a central support. For tenants there are clamp supports and clamp rods without drilling.
