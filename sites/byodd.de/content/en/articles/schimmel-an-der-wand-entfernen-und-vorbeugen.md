Black dots in the corner of the room, a gray border behind the wardrobe, a musty smell in the bedroom: mold in the apartment is a common problem, especially in the cold season. It is unsightly, can be harmful to your health and comes back if you only treat the surface. If you want to get rid of mold permanently, you have to find the cause.

These instructions explain how mold develops, how you can safely remove small infestations yourself, when a specialist company is necessary and how you can prevent it with ventilation, heating and small structural measures.

## How mold occurs

Above all, mold needs **moisture**. You can find nutrients almost everywhere: in wallpaper, paste, dust or silicone. Moisture can come from two directions:

| Cause | Typical signs |
|---|---|
| **Condensation (dew water)** | Mold in corners, behind furniture on outside walls, on window reveals, more severe in winter |
| **Structural moisture** | Damp spots after rain, rising damp in basement walls, water stains under pipes |

### Understanding condensation

Warm air can hold more water vapor than cold air. A four-person household easily releases 10 liters of water into the air per day through breathing, cooking, showering and drying clothes. When this moist air hits a cold surface, such as a poorly insulated exterior wall, a corner or an area behind a cupboard, it cools and releases water. Even a permanent relative humidity of around 70 to 80% directly on the wall surface is enough for mold.

Those particularly at risk are:

- **Thermal bridges:** Corners, window reveals, roller shutter boxes, ceiling connections.
- **Areas behind furniture on outside walls** because warm air cannot reach there.
- **Bedrooms** that are not heated much and in which a lot of moisture occurs at night.
- **Bathrooms without windows** or with weak ventilation.

### A calculation example for the dew point

An example shows how quickly a wall becomes critical. If the room air is 20 °C and 60 percent relative humidity, its dew point is around 12 °C. If the surface temperature of a wall underneath drops, condensation forms. But mold doesn't need any visible water: it can grow if the air directly on the wall is constantly around 80 percent humidity. In this room climate, this is already the case with a wall temperature of around 15 °C. Corners of exterior walls and places behind cupboards easily reach these values ​​in winter. A simple infrared thermometer shows you how cold your critical areas actually are.

## Step 1: Clarify the cause

Before you remove mold, observe:

- **Where exactly** does he perform? Corners and exterior walls indicate condensation, individual spots under pipes or water damage after rain.
- **What is the humidity?** A digital hygrometer for a few euros shows it. Around 40 to 60% is ideal in winter.
- **How ​​warm are the rooms?** The risk increases if the temperature stays below around 16 °C.
- **Is there water damage?** Leaking pipes, broken gutters, cracks in the facade.

If the cause is structural, the damage must be repaired first. In rented apartments, this is the responsibility of the landlord.

## Step 2: Can I remove the mold myself?

As guidance, as provided by the Federal Environment Agency:

| Infestation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Small areas up to around 0.5 m², superficial | Can be removed by yourself using protective equipment |
| Medium areas up to around 0.5 to 1 m² or deeper in the plaster | With caution, if you are unsure, contact a specialist |
| Larger areas, recurring, after water damage | Specialist company |
| People with allergies, asthma, weakened immune systems in the household | Do not remove it yourself |

## Step 3: Remove small mold infestations

### Protective equipment

- FFP2 mask, better FFP3.
- Safety glasses.
- Disposable gloves.
- Clothes that are washed afterwards.

### Procedure

1. **ventilate the room,** close doors to other rooms.
2. **Do not brush dry:** This will stir up spores.
3. **Apply agent:**
   - **Ethanol 70 to 80%** (for example diluted denatured alcohol or isopropanol) works well on smooth and porous surfaces and leaves no residue. Fireproof and ventilate well: Ethanol is flammable.
   - **Hydrogen peroxide 3%** has a bleaching and disinfecting effect, good on light surfaces.
   - **Chlorine-containing mold removers** bleach heavily and hide the infestation, but have a strong odor and are irritating. Never mix with other cleaners.
   - **Vinegar** is unsuitable on calcareous surfaces: it neutralizes itself and can even provide food for the fungi.
4. **Let it take effect** and wipe off with disposable cloths. Dispose of wipes immediately in a sealed bag.
5. **Remove the affected wallpaper** with a damp cloth and dispose of it in bags. Mold in plaster can reach deeper; then have the plaster removed.
6. **Let dry thoroughly** and observe the area.
7. **Only then paint,** if necessary with a lime or silicate paint, which prevents mold due to its high pH value. See [Paint Wall](/anleitungen/wand-streichen-anleitung/).

**Important:** Painting over alone, even with “anti-mold paint”, does not solve the problem. If the cause is not eliminated, the mold will return.

## Step 4: Ventilate properly

### Shock ventilation instead of tilting

- **Several times a day** open the windows completely for a few minutes, preferably opposite windows for draft.
- **In winter** 5 to 10 minutes are enough, longer in the transitional period.
- **Tilted windows** exchange little air, but cool the reveal. This encourages mold right there.
- **Air immediately after showering, cooking and doing laundry**.
- **Air it out in the morning after getting up**, especially in the bedroom.

### Avoid moisture

- If possible, dry laundry outside or in a drying room.
- When cooking, use the lid and turn on the extractor hood.
- Keep the bathroom door closed when showering, then ventilate using the window or fan.

## Step 5: Heat properly

- **Keep all rooms at a sufficient temperature:** Living rooms around 20 °C, bedrooms at least 16 to 18 °C.
- **Do not heat cool rooms with warmer rooms** (leave the door open): The warm, moist air then condenses in the cool room.
- **Do not block radiators** with furniture or thick curtains.

More on this topic in [Bleeding radiators](/anleitungen/heizkoerper-entlueften/).

## Step 6: Furniture and small structural measures

- **Place furniture at a distance:** At least 5 to 10 cm from outside walls to allow air to circulate.
- **Insulate roller shutter boxes** and check window seals. See [Adjusting windows against drafts](/anleitungen/fenster-einstellen-zugluft/).
- **Check and clean the fan in the bathroom**.
- **Lime plaster and mineral paints** regulate moisture better than dense emulsion paints or vinyl wallpapers.

## Silicone joints, windows and condensation

Black dots in silicone joints in the bathroom usually grow into the material. Superficial wiping only helps for a short time; In the long term it helps to renew the joint, as described in [Renew silicone joints](/anleitungen/silikonfugen-erneuern/). On the other hand, mold on window seals and frames can be easily wiped off with alcohol. If there is water on the windows in the morning, wipe it off with a cloth or window squeegee before it runs into the frame and reveal, and then ventilate vigorously.

## Fans and ventilation concepts

Modern windows are very tight. What is good for heating costs can lead to moisture problems without regular ventilation. If many old windows in a building are replaced with new ones, the DIN 1946-6 standard therefore requires a ventilation concept that shows how the necessary air exchange is ensured. Solutions range from window rebate fans to time-controlled bathroom fans and decentralized ventilation devices with heat recovery. Check existing bathroom fans regularly: A sheet of paper should be sucked in by the running fan, otherwise it is dirty or defective.

## Mold and health

Mold spores can irritate the respiratory tract and eyes and trigger or worsen allergies or asthma symptoms in sensitive people. People with previous lung diseases and weakened immune systems are particularly at risk. Anyone who notices repeated coughing, runny nose or shortness of breath in a damp apartment should have it checked by a doctor and mention the mold.

## Tenants and landlords

Mold is a common point of contention in rental apartments. Principles:

- **Tenants must report mold immediately,** preferably in writing with photos.
- **Clarify the cause:** If there is a structural defect, such as a thermal bridge or water damage, the landlord is responsible. If it is due to inadequate ventilation and heating, the tenant may be responsible.
- **Reasonable ventilation:** What is reasonable depends on the individual case. A ventilation protocol and hygrometer readings help clarify the issue.
- **In the event of a dispute**, tenant associations and, if necessary, an expert can help.

## Practical example: Mold behind the wardrobe

While rearranging a wardrobe in the bedroom, a family discovers gray spots on about 0.3 square meters of the outside wall. The hygrometer shows 68 percent humidity in the morning.

1. **Protection:** FFP2 mask, gloves, safety glasses.
2. **Treatment:** Sprayed the area with 70 percent alcohol, allowed it to take effect, wiped with disposable cloths and disposed of in a sealed bag.
3. **Cause:** The closet was right next to the cold outside wall, the bedroom was barely heated, and in the morning there was only tilting.
4. **Measures:** Closet placed 10 cm away from the wall, bedroom temperature at 17 °C, ventilated in the morning and evening.
5. **Check:** After two weeks the humidity is 50 to 55 percent and the mold has not returned. The area is painted over with silicate paint.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is black mold more dangerous than white mold?

The color says little about the danger. Any indoor mold should be removed.

### Does a dehumidifier help?

It lowers the humidity and can help temporarily, but does not replace ventilation, heating and the elimination of structural causes.

### Can I remove mold with vinegar?

Not on calcareous walls. Ethanol or hydrogen peroxide are better.

### How quickly does mold come back?

If the cause remains, often within a few weeks.

### How long should I ventilate after showering?

Until the mirror and tiles are dry again, usually ten to fifteen minutes with the window wide open. In windowless bathrooms, the fan should run with overrun for at least half an hour. Wet towels dry better outside the bathroom.

### Do house plants or salt lamps help against moisture?

No. Houseplants even release additional water into the room air through their leaves, and salt lamps have no measurable effect on air humidity. Ventilation and heating remain the most effective means.

## Conclusion

Mold develops where moisture hits cold surfaces or components are damp. First, clarify the cause, remove small infestations with protective equipment and ethanol or hydrogen peroxide, leave larger areas to a specialist company and prevent with shock ventilation, sufficient heating, distance between furniture and outside walls and a hygrometer. The following applies to rented apartments: report and document immediately.
