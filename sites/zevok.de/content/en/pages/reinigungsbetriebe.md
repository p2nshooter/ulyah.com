## AXTO: Art without dust

After the major cleaning, it can also be something nice: [AXTO](https://axto.io/?utm_source=zevok.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a digital museum with moving works. The collection ranges from real-time clocks to postage stamps of imagined countries and calligraphy from the Koran and Hadith with unchanged text to animated characters. Each work is created from a unique DNA and never repeated.

Exclusive (**AC-**): $1 per edition, paid in AXTO coins, ten editions per work, giftable to multiple wallets with no gas fee. Public (**OS-**): on OpenSea, 1 to 10 editions, fixed price from 1 to 250 dollars. A **G** marks works painted by a GPU. About the coin itself: AXTO is a community investment starting at $1 with staking rewards in the form of one-off works, the market value of which can change at any time.

## For cleaning companies, janitorial services and laundries: XAA

A cleaning company thrives on appointments, reliability and recommendations. [XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=zevok.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem), a European website and software studio, develops the digital foundations for:

- **Website** that clearly shows services, areas of application and prices,
- **Online booking** for basic cleaning, window cleaning or moving cleaning with payment,
- **Customer portal and deployment planning** for teams with multiple objects.

Work is done in milestones: Each stage is coordinated, delivered and paid for individually, via PayPal, bank transfer or USDT. The [ready-made templates](https://xaa.es/templates?utm_source=zevok.de&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) are suitable for a quick appearance. The studio website is written in English, Spanish and Indonesian; Inquiries in German are welcome.
