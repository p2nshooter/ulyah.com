The only thing cookies have in common with cookies is the name. But they share one thing with crumbs on the worktop: you can wipe them away at any time, namely in the settings of your browser. This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies are used when you visit zevok.de, who sets them and how you decide about them. It supplements our [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device via your browser and can be read again when you access it later. The browser's local storage and session memory, device identifiers and tiny graphics that report a call, so-called pixels, work in a similar way. Legally, the technology plays no role: information on your device may only be stored or read with your consent, unless this is absolutely necessary for a service that you expressly want to use (§ 25 TDDDG).

## At a glance

| What | Who | For what | How long |

## What ZEVOK itself stores

ZEVOK does not set its own cookies to recognize you. For the 2026 FIFA World Cup, soap bubbles will rise over the page for a few seconds once per visit. To prevent this from happening again every time you change pages, your browser stores the entry “zevok-wm26” in the session memory. It only contains the value “1”, does not leave your device and disappears as soon as you close the browser. This entry is technically necessary for the presentation (Section 25 Para. 2 TDDDG) and therefore does not require consent.

Our visitor counting does not require any storage on your device. It only transmits the website abbreviation and the path to the page accessed. How it works in detail can be found in the [data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).

## Google AdSense and third party cookies

Google AdSense delivers the ads on ZEVOK as automatic ads. Google and Google's advertising partners listed as third parties in the consent message may use cookies and similar technologies for these purposes:

- **Deliver ads:** so that an ad can appear at all
- **Limit frequency:** so you don't see the same ad over and over again
- **Detect fraud and abuse:** for example automated clicks
- **Measure impact:** how often an ad was viewed or clicked on
- **Personalize ads:** only with your consent, based on your interests

Which cookies are set in detail depends on your selection, your browser and the advertisements delivered and can change. Google describes the cookies it uses and their duration in its privacy policy for advertising. Depending on the cookie, the storage period ranges from the duration of one session to several months.

## Fonts from Google Fonts

The fonts on this website are loaded from Google servers. To the best of our knowledge, no cookies are set. However, your IP address will be transmitted to Google as described in the [Privacy Policy](/datenschutz/).

## The consent message

A consent message appears on the first visit. There you can agree to all purposes, reject all or select individual purposes and third parties from the options. No cookies will be stored for personalized advertising without your consent. Ads that are not based on your interests may continue to appear. Your decision will be saved on your device so that the message does not appear again each time you view the page.

## Change or revoke consent

You can change your decision at any time. To do this, use the link to the privacy settings provided in the consent message or delete the cookies from this website in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. A revocation applies to the future. What has happened up to that point based on your consent remains lawful.

## Manage cookies in the browser

In your browser settings you can view and delete stored cookies, generally block third-party cookies or set cookies to be deleted when you close the browser. A private window forgets cookies and session storage as soon as you close it. How to do this with your browser is explained in its help.

The ZEVOK guides remain completely readable even without cookies, as do the cleaning plans and stain overviews. If you block the session memory, you will only see the soap bubbles a little more often.
