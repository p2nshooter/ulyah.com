We welcome messages from everyone who would like to have their household under control, or would like to. The best way to reach us is by email:

**Email:** [kontakt@zevok.de](mailto:kontakt@zevok.de)

We read every message and usually respond within a week. During holiday periods it may take a little longer.

## What you can write to us about

- **Topic requests:** Are you missing instructions, for example for a specific stain, a sensitive material or a care symbol that you cannot assign? Write to us what you would like to read more about.
- **Errors and additions:** If you find something wrong, outdated or misleading in a text, please tell us. Practical information, for example from textile cleaning, building cleaning or housekeeping, is particularly valuable. We check every reference and correct the text if it is justified.
- **Experiences:** Have you beaten a stubborn stain or found a cleaning schedule that really works for your family? Tell us about it. We do not copy texts verbatim, but your experience can make a guide better.
- **Legal and data protection:** We also answer inquiries about your data or the content of this website via this address.

## This is the best way for us to answer you

If you send us a question about a stain or material, a few details will help us so that we don't have to write you back first:

- **What is the piece made of?** It is best to use the information on the care label, i.e. material and care symbols.
- **What kind of stain is it,** approximately how old is it and has it been through the washing machine or dryer before?
- **What have you tried,** with what means and for how long?

A photo can help. Please make sure that no people, addresses or documents are visible on it. We do not offer telephone advice. We prefer to answer common questions in a new guide so that everyone can benefit from it.

## In the event of an accident: don't write, just call

We cannot help in an emergency via email. If a child or adult swallows cleaning product, gets it in their eyes, or inhales fumes, call your state's **Poison Control Center** immediately. If you feel short of breath, lose consciousness or have cramps, call the emergency number **112**. What you can do until then can be found on our page [Safety when cleaning](/sicherheit-beim-putzen/).

## No individual advice on valuable pieces

Whether a silk heirloom, an antique carpet or a natural stone slab can tolerate a particular treatment cannot be judged from a distance with certainty. If you have valuable or delicate items, please contact a textile cleaner or a specialist company who can inspect the item.

## Advertising and collaborations

We do not publish paid posts, paid product tests or brand recommendations. We do not answer inquiries about this.

## Data protection

If you write to us, we will only use your information to answer your message. More about this in the [Data protection declaration](/datenschutz/).
