## General instructions, not individual advice

Our guides describe best practices for cleaning, stain removal and laundry care. But you can't take into account every material, every color, every appliance and every stain that exists in a household. Instructions that reliably help with cotton can cause damage with silk, viscose or a coated fabric. That's why the following applies to everything you read from us:

- The **care label** of your textiles takes precedence over our general tips.
- The **instructions for use and safety instructions** on a cleaning product take precedence.
- The **manufacturer's information** on floors, worktops, fittings, appliances and furniture takes precedence.

If you are unsure, ask the manufacturer or a specialist shop before trying anything.

## Personal responsibility when imitating

You decide for yourself whether and how you implement instructions and act on your own responsibility. Please adhere to the basic rules that apply to all of our guides:

1. **Never mix cleaners**, especially no chlorine-containing products with acids or ammonia.
2. **Provide fresh air**, wear gloves and protect your eyes when working overhead.
3. **Test on an inconspicuous area first** and allow the result to dry before continuing.
4. **Unplug electrical devices** before cleaning and allow to cool.
5. **Keep products out of the reach** of children and pets, always in the original packaging.

We explain this in detail on the page [Safety when cleaning](/sicherheit-beim-putzen/). What our advisors can and cannot do is stated in the [Disclaimer](/haftungsausschluss/).
