When cleaning: only use as much cleaner as necessary. We do the same thing with data. In accordance with Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), this declaration informs you about which personal data is processed when you visit zevok.de, for what purpose, on what legal basis and what rights you have.

## Responsible person

Responsible for data processing on this website is p2nshooter as the publisher of ZEVOK, as stated in [Imprint](/impressum/). You can reach us by email at [kontakt@zevok.de](mailto:kontakt@zevok.de).

## What you don't have to specify to us

ZEVOK has no customer account, no registration, no comment function, no newsletter, no shop and no contact form. You can read all guides, cleaning plans and overviews without telling us your name, email address or anything else about yourself.

## Hosting and server protocols

The website is delivered via the Cloudflare, Inc. network. In order for a page to reach you, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data when you access it: your IP address, date and time of access, the address accessed, the previously visited page, if your browser provides it, information about the browser and operating system and the amount of data transferred. This data is needed to deliver pages quickly and to protect the website from attacks, overload and misuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operations (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). The length of time such logs are retained is determined by Cloudflare's policies. Cloudflare can also process data in the USA. The transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on EU standard contractual clauses.

## Visitor counting without cookies

So that we know which guides are being read and where we should make improvements, the publisher's own, slim count counts the number of page views. It is also operated via Cloudflare. When you access a page, your browser only transmits two pieces of information: the abbreviation of the website and the path to the page accessed, for example the address of a guide about grease stains. A total of the views per day and page is saved. In addition, a timestamp without any further information is stored for each call so that we can see how many people are currently reading. These timestamps are automatically deleted after a short period of time. Your browser ID is only evaluated at the moment of access in order to count visits separately from search engine robots and is not saved.

The count does not set cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not create profiles. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving our offering (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR).

## Fonts from Google Fonts

When you access the page, your browser loads the fonts Petrona and Urbanist, in which ZEVOK is set, from servers of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Your IP address is transmitted to Google and Google learns that zevok.de was accessed. We use the fonts for a uniform, easy-to-read typeface (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). If your browser blocks external fonts, the text appears in your device's standard font. All content then remains completely readable.

## Advertising with Google AdSense

ZEVOK is free and is financed by ads from Google AdSense, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited. We use automatic ads: Google itself determines where ads appear on a page and what they are. We do not select ads individually.

Google and its advertising partners, so-called third parties, may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to serve ads, limit the frequency of an ad, detect fraud and click abuse, and measure the effectiveness of ads. With your consent, ads may also be tailored to interests derived from your visits to this and other websites. Google processes, among other things, your IP address, information about the device and browser, and the page accessed.

Saving information on your device and reading out such information will only take place with your consent (Section 25 Para. 1 TDDDG, Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a GDPR). We obtain consent via a consent message that appears on the first visit. There you can see the third parties and purposes involved and can agree to all, reject all or select them individually. If you decline, ads may still appear that are not based on your interests. Data can also be transferred to the USA. Google is certified according to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For detailed information about how Google uses data from partner websites, see Google's privacy policy. You manage your personal ad settings in your Google account.

## Change or revoke consent

You can revoke your consent at any time with future effect or change your selection (Art. 7 Para. 3 GDPR). To do this, use the privacy settings provided by the consent message or delete this website's cookies in your browser. You will be asked again the next time you visit. The lawfulness of processing until revocation remains unaffected. Details can be found in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-richtlinie/).

## Storage in the browser

For the 2026 Football World Cup, ZEVOK will show a short animation with soap bubbles once per visit. So that it doesn't accompany you every time you change pages, your browser remembers in the session memory that it was already visible. This information does not leave your device, contains nothing about you and disappears as soon as you close the browser. It is technically necessary for the desired display (Section 25 Para. 2 TDDDG).

## Encrypted connection

All pages are delivered via HTTPS. You can recognize this by the address that begins with "https://“" and by the lock symbol on your browser. This means that no one can read during the transmission which guide you are currently accessing, whether it is about discolored joints or the stain on your favorite blouse.

## If you write to us

If you write us an email, we will process your email address, your name, if you mention it, and the content of your message in order to respond to you (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b or lit. f GDPR). The message is processed by the email service that operates our mailbox. We will not pass on your information and will delete it as soon as your request has been completed and there are no legal retention requirements.

Two requests from practice: If you send a photo of a stain or a care label, make sure that no people, addresses or documents can be recognized on it. And please do not send us any health information, such as details about an accident involving cleaning products. In such a case, the Poison Information Center or a doctor can help you. You can find out more on the page [Safety when cleaning](/sicherheit-beim-putzen/).

## Recipient and distribution

Personal data is only received by the services mentioned in this declaration to the extent that they are necessary for their task: Cloudflare for delivering the website and counting visitors, Google for fonts and advertisements and the email service for your messages to us. We don't sell data. We only pass on data to authorities if we are legally obliged to do so.

## Your rights

According to the GDPR you have the following rights towards us:

- **Information** about whether and what data we process about you (Art. 15)
- **Correction** of incorrect data (Art. 16)
- **Deletion** of your data (Art. 17)
- **Restriction** of processing (Article 18)
- **Data portability** (Art. 20)
- **Objection** to processing based on our legitimate interest, for reasons arising from your particular situation (Article 21)
- **Revocation** of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7 Para. 3)

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77), for example in the federal state in which you live or work. For all concerns, an informal email to [kontakt@zevok.de](mailto:kontakt@zevok.de) is sufficient.

## No automated decisions

We do not make any decisions about you that are based solely on automated processing (Article 22 GDPR) and do not create any profiles ourselves. You are not legally or contractually obliged to provide us with personal data. You can read the guides in full even if you refuse consent for advertising.

## Changes

We will adapt this statement if the website, the services used or the legal situation change. The version published here applies. The date at the top of this page shows the status.
