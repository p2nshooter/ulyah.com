Our guides explain why something works. The tools help you when you are faced with the problem: the stain on the sofa, the dosing table on the detergent package or a label full of symbols. All four calculate directly in your browser. Nothing is sent to us and you don't need an account. At most, your own browser will remember your last selection so that you don't have to start from the beginning next time.

## Stain finder

You choose the stain, i.e. red wine, blood, grease, grass, ballpoint pen, candle wax or one of 16 stains, and the material: washable cotton, synthetics, wool and silk, carpet and upholstery or an item that can only be cleaned. The stain finder tells you what to do in the first few minutes, how to treat in the right order and what to leave alone. For wool and silk, he uses bleach and enzyme-containing agents, and for carpets and upholstery, he uses soaking and washing. [To the stain finder](/fleckenfinder/)

## Water hardness and dosage calculator

The calculator converts the information provided by your water supplier into all the usual units, °dH, mmol/l, °fH and mg/l, and assigns them to the hardness range soft, medium or hard, as defined by the Detergents and Cleaning Products Act. He then converts the amount from your detergent pack to your actual load. How we calculate this is open on the page. [To the dosage calculator](/wasserhaerte-dosierrechner/)

## Care symbols decoder

Tap the characters that appear on your label: vat, triangle, square, iron, and circle. The decoder summarizes how warm you can wash, whether bleach is allowed, how the item should dry, at what level you iron and what the letters in the circle mean for cleaning. [To Care Symbols Decoder](/pflegesymbole-decoder/)

## Cleaning plan generator

You choose your rooms, how many people live with you, whether there are pets and which days you have time. The generator distributes the weekly tasks evenly over these days, adds daily tasks, monthly and seasonal tasks and estimates the time. You can print out the plan and hang it on the fridge. [To the cleaning plan generator](/putzplan-generator/)

## Where the rules come from

Each tool follows the same rules as our guides. The basis is the care labeling according to GINETEX, the hardness ranges according to § 9 of the Detergents and Cleaning Agents Act, the information from the Federal Environment Agency and the consumer advice centers on washing and cleaning and the recommendations from Forum Waschen. The sources are on every tool page and under every guide. A tool never replaces the information on your package, label or instructions for your device. If both do not match, the manufacturer applies.
