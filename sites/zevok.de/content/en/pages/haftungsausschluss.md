ZEVOK would like to help you maintain your household cleanly, safely and in a way that conserves materials. So that it is clear what our advisors can and cannot do, we have summarized the most important limitations here.

## General information

The content of ZEVOK is carefully researched, checked and regularly revised. Nevertheless, we cannot guarantee that all information is complete, correct and up-to-date at all times. Our guides are general information. They do not replace the manufacturer's instructions for use and safety instructions, the care labeling of textiles, or advice from a specialist company who looks at a piece on site.

## Every material is different

Whether a stain disappears depends on what it is made of, how old it is, what has already been tried on it and what material it is on. Dyes, finishes, coatings and blended fabrics react differently. Even if you proceed carefully, a stain can remain, a color can bleed, a fabric can shrink or a surface can become dull. That's why we recommend in every guide that you first test a product in an inconspicuous area. Anyone who uses a product that is not intended for a material can also jeopardize warranty or guarantee claims against the manufacturer. If in doubt, valuable, old or delicate pieces, such as those made of silk, leather, wool with special finishing or hand-knotted carpets, should be taken to a textile cleaner or a specialist company.

## Cleaning products and health

Cleaning products are chemicals, even if they are considered household remedies. Acids, alkalis, solvents and fragrances can irritate skin, eyes and respiratory tract. Never mix different cleaners, especially those containing chlorine, with acids or ammonia, as this can produce dangerous gases. Ventilate when cleaning, wear gloves and keep all products in their original packaging and out of the reach of children and pets.

Our information is not medical advice. If you are prone to allergies, asthma or sensitive skin, are pregnant or are unsure whether you can tolerate a certain product, talk to your doctor. After an accident involving cleaning products, contact the relevant poison control center immediately, or if you experience shortness of breath, loss of consciousness or cramps, call 112. What you can do until then can be found on the page [Safety when cleaning](/sicherheit-beim-putzen/).

## Mold and moisture

Our advice on mold prevention refers to small stains on joints, silicone and surfaces in the bathroom. Large-scale infestations, mold that keeps coming back, dampness in walls or a musty smell with no apparent cause should be in the hands of professionals because the cause usually lies in the building and not on the surface. In a rented apartment, you should also report mold to your landlord. If you associate health problems with mold, get it checked by a doctor.

## Electrical devices and installations

The oven, refrigerator, extractor hood, washing machine, dryer and kettle are switched off, disconnected from the electricity and, where necessary, cooled before cleaning. Water has no place on electrical components. The operating instructions for the device always apply to maintenance, cleaning programs and permitted means. Work on electricity, gas and water installations as well as a drain that remains blocked despite simple home remedies and a suction cup are tasks for a specialist company.

## Products, brands and prices

ZEVOK does not recommend any brands. When we mention active ingredients or product types, such as an acidic limescale cleaner, a mild detergent or a gall soap, this is not a recommendation to buy a specific product. Recipes change, and two products of the same type may have different strengths. The current label and instructions for use of the product that you are actually holding in your hand are always decisive.
