ZEVOK stands for **Z**reliable **explained, **from **ovens to **collars. We write for everyone who runs a household and wants to be less puzzled: for the student in her first apartment who doesn't know why her towels get hard, for the family whose sofa has juice stains after her child's birthday, and for everyone who has ever stood in front of a care label and wondered what the tub with two lines underneath actually means.

## Why ZEVOK exists

Hardly any other topic is as full of well-intentioned tips as the household. Salt on the red wine stain, vinegar against everything, baking soda and vinegar together for the “turbo effect”. Some of this advice works, some has little effect, and some is simply dangerous, such as mixing cleaners that should never be mixed. Often you don't know why a tip works and what its limitations are.

We want to change that. Our guides explain the connections: which type of stain needs which treatment, why wool is washed differently than cotton, what an acid does against limescale and why it has no place on marble. Anyone who understands the principle can also act correctly in situations that are not included in any instructions.

## What you will find with us

ZEVOK is divided into six themes, which we call the “six cymbals”:

1. **Systematic cleaning:** Cleaning plans, sequence, tools and techniques for floors, windows and dust
2. **Remove stains:** by stain type and material, on clothing, carpet and upholstery
3. **Washing & Care:** Sorting, temperature, dosage, care symbols, drying and ironing
4. **Clean kitchen:** Oven, refrigerator, sink, appliances and hygiene
5. **Bathroom & toilet:** Limescale, joints, urine scale, drain and mold prevention
6. **Home remedies:** Vinegar, baking soda, citric acid, soda and gall soap, used correctly and safely

There is also our page [Safety when cleaning](/sicherheit-beim-putzen/), which we refer to in many guides, and four [tools](/werkzeuge/), which calculate directly in your browser: the stain finder, the water hardness and dosage calculator, the care symbol decoder and the cleaning plan generator. What we republish and what we revise are dated on the [Updates](/aktualisierungen/) page.

## How our instructions are created

Each guide answers a specific household question and is developed individually, not copied from other websites. We also use AI tools when writing. Before publication, we compare what is created with the care labeling of textiles, with manufacturer information and with consumer information from public bodies, such as the Federal Environment Agency, the Federal Institute for Risk Assessment and consumer advice centers. We mention these sources under each guide with the date on which we last checked them. Where experts have different opinions or a popular tip does not deliver what it promises, we will write about it. More about this in our [Editorial Principles](/redaktionsgrundsaetze/).

## What we don't do

We do not recommend any brands and do not receive any money from manufacturers. We do not promise miracle cures or stains that are “guaranteed” to disappear. And we never recommend mixtures that could be dangerous. ZEVOK is financed through advertisements, which are recognizable as such and have no influence on our content.

## The name and the picture

In the first book of Kings a huge basin is described, cast entirely of bronze and called “the Brazen Sea.” It was used for washing, and its edge was shaped like a blossomed lily. This pool is the image of our site: bronze that shines in the light, clear water and a border full of flowers. It reminds us that cleanliness has always been about care and beauty.
