A care label consists of up to five characters, always in the same order: washing, bleaching, drying, ironing and professional care. Tap the character that appears on your label at the top of each row. The decoder summarizes what you need to do. The tables below show all characters with their meaning, also for printing. We drew the characters on this page ourselves. They follow the international care label (ISO 3758), which is managed by GINETEX Germany in Germany.

## Washing: the tub

| characters | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vat with 95, 60, 40 or 30 | highest washing temperature in degrees Celsius, normal program |
| a line under the bucket | milder treatment: easy-care program, drum not full, spin gently |
| two lines below the vat | very gentle treatment: fine or wool program |
| Hand in the tub | Hand wash, lukewarm, maximum 40 °C, press instead of rubbing |
| crossed out vat | do not wash, take to dry cleaners |

## Bleach: the triangle

| characters | Meaning |
|---|---|
| empty triangle | Any bleach is allowed, including chlorine |
| Triangle with two slanting lines | only oxygen bleach, no chlorine bleach |
| crossed out triangle | Do not bleach: no stain remover, no heavy-duty detergent with bleach |

## Drying: the square

| characters | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Circle in a square, two points | Tumble dry at normal temperature |
| Circle in square, one point | Tumble dry only at reduced temperature |
| Circle in a square, crossed out | not in the dryer; Air drying is permitted |
| a vertical line in a square | Line or hang dry |
| two vertical lines | Hang up dripping wet, without spinning |
| a horizontal line | dry lying flat |
| two horizontal lines | Dry lying flat while dripping wet |
| slanted line in the upper left corner | dry in the shade |

## Ironing: the iron

| characters | Meaning |
|---|---|
| three points | up to around 200 °C: cotton, linen |
| two points | up to around 150 °C: polyester, wool, silk, viscose |
| a point | up to around 110 °C, steam can damage: polyacrylic, polyamide, acetate, elastane mixtures |
| crossed out lines under the iron | iron without steam |
| crossed out iron | do not iron |

## Professional care: the circle

| characters | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Circle with P | chemical cleaning with common solvents |
| Circle with F | Dry cleaning only with gentler solvents |
| Circle with W | professional wet cleaning |
| one or two lines below the circle | gentle or very gentle treatment |
| crossed out circle | no dry cleaning |
| crossed out W | no professional wet cleaning |

The circle is aimed at the cleaning, not at you. The tub alone decides whether you can wash at home. Our guide [Understanding care symbols] (/ratgeber/pflegesymbole/) explains what you should do if the label is missing or washed out and which mistakes most often occur.
