The first minute often decides whether a stain will come out again. The stain finder above summarizes what to do immediately, how to treat it afterwards, and what makes the stain permanent for 16 common stains. He adapts the steps to the material. The tables below show the same thing for reading and printing, even without JavaScript.

## If you don't know what it is

Treat an unfamiliar stain like you would egg whites: cold, no heat, just dab. This means you won’t damage anything with any type of stain. After that, you can always switch to warmer water or another remedy. First test each product on a hidden area, such as the seam allowance or hem.

## All stains at a glance

| stain | Stain type | Immediately | Treatment | Better not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Red wine | tannin | Dab and rinse the back with cold water | Soak in cold water, oxygen bleach for white and colorfast fabrics, then wash | Rub salt, hot water |
| Coffee, tea | Tannin, with milk also protein | Dab and rinse with cold water | Soak in cold water with mild detergent or gall soap, oxygen bleach for whites | rinse hot, tumble dry |
| Fruit, berries, juice | dye | Remove any residue and rinse with cold water | Water with a little soap or detergent, leftover oxygen bleach, powder detergent | rinse with hot water, chlorine bleach |
| Oil, butter, frying fat | Fat | Dab with kitchen paper, base | Work in detergent or gall soap, rinse with warm water and wash | only cold water, iron with edge |
| Makeup, lipstick | fat and pigment | Remove, dab from outside to inside | Dishwashing liquid or gall soap, for lipstick alcohol after test | rub |
| Blood | Protein | immediately rinse the back with cold water | Soak in cold water, detergent containing enzymes, cold gall soap | hot water, dryer, iron |
| Egg, milk, porridge | Protein | Remove coarsely, rinse with cold water | Soak cold with detergent containing enzymes, then grease with dishwashing liquid | Warmth at the beginning |
| Sweat, deodorant | Protein and salts | rinse with cold water | Soak in cold water, gall soap, yellow edges with citric acid solution or oxygen bleach after test | soak in hot water, chlorine bleach |
| Grass | dye and protein | Tap out, rinse with cold water | Dishwashing liquid or gall soap, or for colorfast cotton, denatured alcohol after testing | Warmth before the end |
| Earth, mud, soot | pigment | Let dry, brush out | Pre-rinse with cold water, gall soap, wash | rub wet |
| Ballpoint pen | Dye in oil | Place cloth underneath | Dab on alcohol, change the surface, then dishwashing liquid | Hairspray, open flame |
| Candle wax | Bold, often with color | let it harden, break off | Warm the rest between kitchen paper with an iron | scrape while it is soft |
| Chewing gum | Plastic and sugar | freeze in bag | Break off any residue with dishwashing liquid or gall soap | Pull while it is soft |
| Chocolate, cocoa | Protein, fat, color | Allow to set, remove and rinse with cold water | first cold with enzymes, then dishwashing liquid, paint with oxygen bleach | first rinse with hot water |
| Tomato sauce, curry | fat and dye | take off, dab | first dishwashing liquid for the grease, then oxygen bleach for the whites | fix hot |
| Rust | metal | treat dry | Citric acid solution on white cotton or linen, rinse well | Chlorine bleach, acid on natural stone |

## The material changes the treatment

| material | What changes |
|---|---|
| Cotton, linen, blended fabrics | All steps possible as long as the care label allows it |
| Polyester, polyamide, viscose | at most as warm as permitted; Do not wring viscose when wet; Grease on polyester often needs two passes |
| Wool, silk, cashmere | no bleach, no protein-breaking enzymes, just lukewarm or cold and dab; If there are large stains, take them to the cleaners |
| Carpet, sofa, mattress | Do not get wet, use the agent sparingly with a cloth or spray bottle, dab with clear water and absorb dry |
| Vat crossed out | Just remove the rough stuff and pat it dry, then take it to the dry cleaner; tell me there what it was |

## Three rules for every remedy

1. Only use one product at a time and rinse thoroughly with water before using the next one. Mixtures are of no use and can be dangerous.
2. Little resources, but several passes. A large amount will spread the stain and leave edges.
3. Before using the dryer and iron, check whether the stain is really gone. Heat makes leftovers permanent.
