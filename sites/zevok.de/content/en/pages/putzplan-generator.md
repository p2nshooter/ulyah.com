A cleaning schedule works if it fits your household: your rooms, the people and animals who live there, and the days when you actually have time. The generator above puts together a weekly plan from this information. He distributes the weekly tasks so that each cleaning day takes approximately the same amount of time, supplements the daily tasks and the tasks for each month and each season. You can print out the finished plan and check it off.

## What's in the plan

| area | Daily | Weekly | Monthly | Semi-annually or annually |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | Sink and work surface, hob after cooking, dishes | Stove and fronts, microwave, trash can, changing dishcloths, floor | Wipe out the refrigerator, sieves in the dishwasher, grease filters, oven if necessary | Defrost inside cupboards, top of wall cupboards, freezer compartment |
| bathroom | Ventilate, remove shower wall, wipe sink | Toilet, sink, shower or bathtub, mirror, floor, towels | Hair strainer, fan grille, trash can, toothbrush cup, shower curtain | Check joints thoroughly, inside cabinets, silicone joints |
| Living and sleeping rooms | ventilate, clean up | Dust, vacuum, linen every one to two weeks | Vacuum radiators, light switches and door handles, upholstery | Windows, curtains, behind and under furniture, lamps |
| hallway | Put shoes away | Vacuum and mop the floor, shake out the doormat | Apartment door, wardrobe, mirror | Wash shoe cabinet, doormat |
| floors | Sweep away crumbs | vacuum, wet mop to suit the type of floor | Skirting boards and corners | Care according to the manufacturer's instructions, carpets thoroughly |

## How the generator distributes

Each weekly task has a rough time estimate for an average apartment. The generator takes the longest tasks first and places each one on the cleaning day that has the least time until then. This creates days of similar length instead of one big Saturday. If you have pets, have a house dust allergy or have four or more people, you can plan an additional vacuuming session. For cats, the litter box is part of their daily tasks, with animals also having bowls and a place to lie down. The times are estimates. Measure the first pass and adjust the plan.

## By season

- **Spring:** Wash and store windows and frames, curtains, balconies, winter clothes according to the label.
- **Summer:** Check insect screens, fans and the refrigerator more often.
- **Autumn:** Thoroughly dust the radiator before the heating season, wash and put away summer clothing.
- **Winter:** Wipe road salt edges in the hallway more often, oven and extractor hood after the cookie season.

## If the plan doesn't work out

If entire days are repeatedly postponed, there are too many tasks: add one more cleaning day. If a task remains, describe it more specifically or link it to a popular one. If an area is still clean after the appointment, the distance can be longer.
