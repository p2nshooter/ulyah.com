The laundry is clean, but then it stays on the rack in the bedroom for two days, smells musty, is full of wrinkles or has become a size smaller in the dryer. Drying determines how long clothes stay beautiful and whether they smell fresh. At the same time, it is one of the largest sources of moisture in the home: a machine load of laundry releases more than a liter of water into the room air when drying.

In this guide you will find out which type of drying is suitable for what, how to hang laundry so that you have to iron less, how to dry in the apartment without risking mold, what the dryer symbols mean and how to properly treat wool, down and towels.

## Leash, stand or dryer?

| Method | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|---|
| Clothesline outside | free, fresh scent, sun bleaches white laundry | Depending on the weather, sun can fade colors, pollen if you have allergies |
| Drying rack in the apartment | always available, gentle | a lot of moisture in the room air, slower |
| Drying room or basement | No moisture in the apartment | not present everywhere, often cool and slow |
| Tumble dryer | quick, soft towels, regardless of the weather | Energy consumption, shrinkage possible, not suitable for everything |

For most households, a combination makes sense: a line or rack for most of the laundry and the dryer for towels, bed linen in rainy weather and urgent items.

## Prepare when washing

- **Spin sufficiently.** The drier the laundry comes out of the machine, the faster it will dry. Cotton can tolerate high spin speeds, delicates and shirts can only tolerate lower spin speeds, otherwise they will crease heavily.
- **Remove from the machine immediately.** If wet laundry remains in the drum for hours, a musty smell will quickly develop.
- **Don't overload.** A machine that is too full will wash worse and cause the laundry to come out wrinkled.

## This is how you hang laundry correctly

1. **Shake out vigorously.** Shake each part vigorously once, this will smooth out wrinkles and loosen the fibers.
2. **Pull smooth.** Pull seams, collar and pockets into shape with your hands.
3. **Hang shirts and blouses** on a hanger and close the buttons at the top. This means they dry almost wrinkle-free.
4. **Hang t-shirts** by the hem or on a hanger, not by the shoulders with clips, otherwise marks will remain.
5. **Pants** at the waistband or legs, turned inside out so pockets dry faster.
6. **Hang towels and bed linen** over the line, not double folded.
7. **Leave space.** Air must circulate between the parts, otherwise it will dry slowly and smell.
8. **Hang dark and colorful pieces inside out** in the shade so that the sun doesn't bleach the colors.

## Drying in the apartment without mold

If you don't have a garden, balcony or drying room, dry your clothes in your apartment. This is allowed and unproblematic if you ventilate properly.

- **Shock ventilation:** Open the windows wide several times a day for a few minutes, preferably with a draft. Tilting is not enough and only cools the walls.
- **Do not dry in the bedroom** if you can avoid it. The humidity there is already high at night.
- **Heat the room slightly:** Warm air absorbs more moisture. However, the laundry does not belong directly on the radiator, which wastes energy and encourages mold behind the rack.
- **Measuring humidity:** A cheap hygrometer shows whether the humidity is getting too high. The risk of mold increases over 60 percent.
- **Distance from the wall:** Do not place the stand directly on cold outside walls.

More about moisture and mold can be found in the guide [Preventing and removing mold in the bathroom] (/ratgeber/schimmel-im-bad/).

## Understand the dryer symbols

A square on the care label shows how drying is allowed:

| symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Square with a circle and a point | Tumble dry at low temperature, gentle program |
| Square with a circle and two dots | Tumble dry at normal temperature |
| Square with crossed out circle | do not tumble dry |
| Square with arch at top | line dry |
| Square with horizontal line | dry lying flat |
| Square with vertical lines | Hang up dripping wet, do not spin |

All care symbols are explained in the guide [Understanding care symbols] (/ratgeber/pflegesymbole/).

## Use the dryer correctly

**Which programs?** Most dryers have programs such as “cupboard dry” for laundry that goes straight into the cupboard and “iron damp” for items that you still want to iron. Sensor programs measure the residual moisture and end the process in a timely manner. This saves energy and protects the fibers.

**What goes in it?**

- Cotton towels, bed linen, underwear and socks if the label allows it.
- Functional clothing only if the label specifically allows it, usually at a low temperature.
- Down jackets and down blankets on a gentle cycle, with dryer balls or some clean tennis balls to prevent the down from clumping together.

**What doesn't belong in it?**

- Wool and silk, unless they are specifically suitable for tumble drying.
- Parts with rubber prints, sequins or adhesive parts.
- Everything with a crossed out dryer symbol.

**Avoid Shrinkage:** Cotton can shrink in the dryer, especially at high temperatures and if over-dried. It's better to take out clothes slightly damp and let them air dry.

**Dryer Care:**

- **Empty the lint filter after each pass.** A full filter extends drying time and increases energy consumption.
- **Empty the condensation water container** if the device does not have a drain.
- **Clean the heat exchanger or base filter** according to the manufacturer's instructions. Heat pump dryers in particular lose performance if the filter is clogged.

**Heat pump or condensation?** Heat pump dryers require significantly less electricity than conventional condenser dryers, but often dry a little slower. When purchasing a new one, it's worth taking a look at the energy label.

## Towels soft without fabric softener

Hard, scratchy towels are a common nuisance. Fabric softener is not a good solution because it reduces absorbency. Better:

- **Use the correct dosage of detergent.** Too much detergent makes towels stiff. More in the guide [How to properly dose detergent] (/ratgeber/waschmittel-dosieren/).
- **Shake out vigorously** before hanging and after drying.
- **A quick tumble dryer:** Just ten minutes in the dryer after air drying makes towels much fluffier.
- **Dry in wind:** Towels become softer outside in the wind than inside.

## Wool and knitting

Wool becomes heavy when wet and stretches when you hang it. Therefore, lay sweaters and cardigans flat on a towel or drying rack, shape them and let them dry at room temperature, not in the sun or on the radiator. Turn them occasionally. More in the guide [How to wash wool, cashmere and silk] (/ratgeber/wolle-und-seide-waschen/).

## Bed linen, curtains and large items

**Bed linen** is best dried on two lines or spread across the entire rack so that air can reach both sides. Before hanging up the duvet covers, turn them right side out and shake them well to prevent wet balls from forming in the corners.

**Curtains and drapes** can be hung slightly damp directly back on the rod after spinning at low speed. Its own weight pulls it smoothly, saving you the trouble of ironing. Place a towel underneath if there is still any dripping.

**Sleeping bags, pillows and blankets** take a long time to dry inside. This works best outside on a warm, dry day or in the dryer on the gentle cycle with dryer balls. Before storing, check carefully whether the filling is really dry, otherwise odors and mold stains will occur.

## Drying in winter

You can also dry laundry outside in winter. When there is frost, the water freezes first, and then the ice slowly evaporates directly into the air. The laundry will dry, but it takes time and feels stiff at first. Bring it in when it's almost dry and let it finish drying indoors. It's better to dry sensitive items and clothing with elastane indoors when it's cold because frozen fibers can break when you move them.

## Typical mistakes and how to avoid them

- **Leave laundry in the machine:** It starts to smell. Hang up immediately.
- **Hanging too tightly:** The air cannot circulate, the laundry dries slowly and has a musty smell.
- **Dry on the heater:** Wastes energy and promotes mold.
- **Do not ventilate:** Moisture collects in walls and corners.
- **Forgot the lint filter:** The dryer takes longer and requires more electricity.
- **Hang up the wool:** It wears out. Always dry lying flat.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my laundry smell musty after drying?

Most of the time it took too long to dry or was left wet in the machine for too long. Hang up laundry immediately, leave enough space and ventilate well. If freshly washed laundry also smells, the washing machine may be the cause. More in the guide [Cleaning the washing machine] (/ratgeber/waschmaschine-reinigen/).

### How long does laundry take to dry?

This depends on the fabric, spin speed, temperature and humidity. Thin synthetic items often dry after a few hours, thick cotton towels and jeans sometimes only dry after a day in a cool apartment. Good ventilation and distance between the parts speed this up significantly.

### Can you dry laundry in your rented apartment?

Usually yes. Landlords are usually not allowed to completely prohibit drying in the apartment, but you must ventilate sufficiently to prevent damage from moisture. Rules for drying on balconies and common areas can be found in the house rules.

### How do I get wrinkles out of laundry without ironing?

Shake out vigorously, pull straight, hang shirts on hangers and do not spin too high. Many parts hang almost smoothly. Tips for the rest in the guide [Ironing made easy] (/ratgeber/buegeln/). You can find further instructions in the [Laundry & Care](/themen/waesche-und-pflege/) section.

## Briefly summarized

Spin sufficiently, remove the laundry immediately from the machine, shake it out and hang it up at a distance. Shirts on hangers, wool lying down, dark shirts inside out in the shade. If you dry in the apartment, ventilate vigorously several times a day and do not dry on the radiator. Pay attention to the dryer symbols, empty the lint filter after each cycle and use sensor programs. Towels become soft without fabric softener if you dose them correctly, shake them out and put them in the dryer for a short time.
