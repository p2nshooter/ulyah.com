Weekly cleaning keeps the apartment clean on a daily basis. But some places are regularly left out: the tops of the cupboards, the radiators from the inside, the corners behind the sofa, the drawers in the kitchen, the ventilation in the bathroom. A basic cleaning is therefore required once or twice a year, when moving in or out and after renovations. It takes time, but if you proceed systematically, you will be finished faster than expected and will have less work for months afterwards.

This checklist will guide you through the basic cleaning room by room. You will find out in which order you should proceed, what resources and tools you need, how much time you should plan and what is important when moving out.

## When does a basic cleaning make sense?

- **Spring cleaning:** After winter, when the heating season is over and dust and soot from candles and heating cling to walls and windows.
- **Autumn cleaning:** Before the heating season so that radiators and windows are clean.
- **When moving in:** Before the furniture is in place, all corners are freely accessible.
- **When moving out:** The apartment must be handed over in the condition required by the rental agreement.
- **After renovations:** Fine construction dust is distributed throughout the apartment.
- **For allergies:** Thorough cleaning of upholstery, mattresses and textiles can relieve symptoms.

## Preparation: sorting out, planning, providing materials

**First sort out, then clean.** A basic cleaning is the best opportunity to clear out cupboards. You don't have to dust off anything you no longer need. Set out boxes for donations, sales and trash.

**Plan your time.** You should plan a whole weekend or several evenings for a two-room apartment. Divide the work room by room. If you try to do everything in one day, you will end up exhausted and inaccurate.

**Provide materials:**

- All-purpose cleaner, dishwashing liquid, limescale cleaner, glass cleaner or spirit.
- Microfiber cloths in several colors, sponges, a dishwashing brush and an old toothbrush for corners.
- Vacuum cleaner with crevice nozzle and upholstery nozzle.
- Flat mop with multiple covers and a bucket.
- A sturdy step ladder.
- Radiator brush, gloves and garbage bags.

The few resources you really need can be found in the [cleaning supplies basic equipment] guide (/ratgeber/putzmittel-grundausstattung/). And remember: never mix cleaners. You can find the rules on the [Safety when cleaning](/sicherheit-beim-putzen/) page.

## The order

The basic rules also apply to basic cleaning: from top to bottom, from dry to wet, from the back to the door. Completely finish each room before moving on to the next, leaving the entire apartment's floors until last. The guide [Cleaning Efficiently] (/ratgeber/putzen-reihenfolge/) offers a detailed explanation.

## Checklist for all rooms

- [ ] **Remove cobwebs from ceilings and wall corners** using a broom with a soft cloth or the vacuum cleaner nozzle.
- [ ] Turn off **lamps**, allow to cool and dust off. Remove and rinse glass shades.
- [ ] Dust **cupboard tops** and wipe with a damp cloth.
- [ ] **Dust off picture frames, shelves and decorations** and give the books a quick pat.
- [ ] **Clean windows** including frames, seals and window sills. Instructions in the guide [Cleaning windows without streaks] (/ratgeber/fenster-putzen/).
- [ ] **Wash curtains and curtains** according to the care label.
- [ ] **Wipe radiator** from the outside and clean between the slats with a radiator brush or vacuum cleaner.
- [ ] **Wipe doors, door frames and handles**.
- [ ] **Wipe light switches and socket covers** only with a damp cloth, never wet. The cloth must not drip.
- [ ] **Move away furniture** and vacuum behind and under it.
- [ ] **Wipe baseboards**.
- [ ] Vacuum **upholstered furniture**, wash pillowcases according to label. Treat stains according to the guide [Removing stains from carpets, sofas and mattresses] (/ratgeber/flecken-teppich-polster/).
- [ ] **Carpets** vacuum thoroughly, have cleaned if necessary or treat with a carpet cleaning device.
- [ ] Vacuum and mop **floors** at the end. Instructions in the guide [Wipe floors correctly] (/ratgeber/boden-wischen/).

## bedroom

- [ ] Change and wash bed linen, have pillows and duvets washed or cleaned according to the care label.
- [ ] Vacuum the mattress, especially seams and edges, and turn or turn it if possible.
- [ ] Vacuum under the bed, empty and wipe out bed drawers.
- [ ] Clear out the wardrobe, sort it out and wipe the compartments with a damp cloth. Allow to dry before putting the clothes back.
- [ ] Clean bedside tables and dressers inside and out.

- [ ] **Clean oven** and trays. Instructions in the guide [Cleaning the oven] (/ratgeber/backofen-reinigen/).
- [ ] **Clean the outside of the extractor hood** and rinse the grease filter. Instructions in the guide [Cleaning the extractor hood] (/ratgeber/dunstabzugshaube-reinigen/).
- [ ] **Clean the hob** thoroughly. Instructions in the guide [Cleaning ceramic hobs, induction hobs and gas stoves] (/ratgeber/ceranfeld-reinigen/).
- [ ] **Dishwasher** clean with strainer and seals. Instructions in the guide [Cleaning the dishwasher] (/ratgeber/spuelmaschine-reinigen/).
- [ ] **Descale the kettle and coffee machine**. Instructions in the guide [Descale kettle and coffee machine] (/ratgeber/entkalken-wasserkocher-kaffeemaschine/).

- [ ] Wash out the **trash can** and let it dry.
- [ ] **Wipe the fronts, handles and skirting boards** of the kitchen furniture.

## Bathroom and toilet

- [ ] **Remove limescale** from fittings, shower head and shower wall. Instructions in the guide [Removing limescale in the shower] (/ratgeber/kalk-in-der-dusche/).
- [ ] **Clean tile joints**. Instructions in the guide [Cleaning tile joints] (/ratgeber/fliesenfugen-reinigen/).

## Hallway, storage room and balcony

- [ ] Clear out the shoe cabinet, sort out the shoes, wipe out the closet.
- [ ] Dust off your wardrobe, sort out your jackets.
- [ ] Knock out or wash the dirt trapping mat.
- [ ] Clean up the storage room, wipe down the shelves, vacuum the floor.
- [ ] Sweep the balcony, wet clean the floor, wipe down the railings and balcony furniture, clean the flower boxes.

## After a renovation

Construction dust is very fine and settles everywhere, including in cupboards and on lamps. A few tips:

- **Clean several times:** Fine dust often reappears after the first wipe. Plan on two or three passes.
- **Use a vacuum cleaner with a fine dust filter** and change the bag more often. Normal vacuum cleaners can be damaged by construction dust.
- **Wipe with a damp cloth instead of sweeping** so that the dust is not stirred up.
- **You can remove cement film on tiles** using a special cement film remover according to the manufacturer's instructions. It must not be used on natural stone and calcareous materials.

## When moving out: what swept clean means

Many rental agreements require that the apartment be left swept clean when you move out. This usually means: Coarse dirt has been removed, the floors have been swept or vacuumed, cobwebs are gone and there is no more rubbish lying around. A basic cleaning with window cleaning is often not expressly required, but many landlords expect a clean apartment and it makes handover easier.

Important:

- **Read your rental agreement.** It states in which condition the apartment must be handed over.
- **Clarify questions in advance** with the landlord or property management.
- **Document the condition** upon handover with photos and a handover protocol.

This information does not replace legal advice. In the event of a dispute, a tenants' association can help.

## Finished together faster

Deep cleaning is not a job for one person alone. If several people live in the household, divide the work into rooms or tasks. What has proven to be successful is for each person to take over a room completely and at the end everyone vacuums and mops together. Depending on their age, children can sort toys, dust books, fluff pillows, clean shoes or beat out the dirt trapping mat. It is important that children do not use cleaning products without supervision and do not climb ladders.

Plan breaks, provide drinks and a simple snack, and reward yourself with something nice at the end. A basic cleaning that ends with a meal together in a clean apartment will be remembered better than a long, lonely day of cleaning.

The times depend heavily on the condition of the apartment, the number of cupboards and how many people are helping.

## Typical mistakes and how to avoid them

- **Start without a plan:** You lose time and overview. Work through the checklist room by room.
- **First the floors:** The dust from above falls onto the clean surfaces.
- **All in one day:** Fatigue makes you inaccurate. Distribute the work.
- **Wipe sockets and switches wet:** This is dangerous. Just damp and careful.
- **Put away damp cupboards:** Let wiped compartments dry completely, otherwise it will smell musty.
- **Mixing cleaners:** This can produce toxic gases.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should you do a basic cleaning?

Once or twice a year is enough for most households, for example in spring and autumn. If you clean thoroughly every week, you can choose longer intervals.

### Is a cleaning company worth it?

When you move out, after renovations or if you don't have the time, a cleaning company can make sense. Compare offers, clarify the scope of services in writing and let us show you whether windows, ovens and cupboards are included.

### Do I also have to wash walls during basic cleaning?

As a rule, it is enough to dust the walls and carefully remove individual stains. Washable wall paints require a damp cloth with a little detergent. With normal emulsion paint and wallpaper, test in an inconspicuous area, otherwise light-colored wipe marks will appear.

### What do I do first, windows or floors?

First the windows, then the floors. When cleaning windows, water drips onto the window sill and floor, and the floor always comes last. You can find further instructions in the [Systematic Cleaning](/themen/putzen-mit-system/) section.

## Briefly summarized

Sort out before the basic cleaning, plan enough time and have all the resources ready. Work from top to bottom, room by room, leaving the floors until last. This includes ceilings, lamps, cupboard tops, radiators, doors, windows and the surfaces behind the furniture in all rooms. Appliances, cupboards, joints, limescale and drains are added in the kitchen and bathroom. Only wipe sockets with a damp cloth, let cupboards dry before putting them away and never mix cleaners. When you move out, what counts is what is in the rental agreement.
