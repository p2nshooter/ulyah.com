At DAI we process the minimum data possible. This policy explains what they are and what you can do with them.

## Responsible

p2nshooter, DAI editor. Contact: [contacto@dai.lat](mailto:contacto@dai.lat).

## Data we process

- **Your messages.** If you write to us, we use your email and what you tell us only to respond to you. We do not use it to send you advertising nor do we give it to anyone.
- **Reading counter.** We record in aggregate which pages are read, without our own cookies, without profiles and without keeping your full IP address.
- **Technical data.** Cloudflare, the service that delivers our pages, processes technical data such as IP to make the site work and to protect it from abuse.

## Advertising

DAI is funded by Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or identifiers to display ads, measure their effectiveness, and, if you allow, personalize them to your activity on other sites. You can manage personalization in [Google ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and read [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before advertising cookies are used.

## Your rights

Depending on the legislation of your country - such as the Mexican federal law on the protection of data held by individuals, Law 1581 of 2012 of Colombia, Law 25,326 of Argentina, Law 29733 of Peru or the European GDPR - you can request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition. Write to us and we will respond to your request. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country.

## Security

All pages are served via encrypted connection. We keep messages only as long as necessary to respond to them.

More details in the [cookie policy](/politica-de-cookies/).
