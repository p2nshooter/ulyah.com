Cookies are small files that websites save on your browser. Here we explain which ones are involved when you read DAI.

## What DAI keeps

DAI does not use its own analysis or tracking cookies. We count visits without cookies. We only save, in the browser's session storage, a flag not to repeat the welcome animation on each page; It disappears when you close the tab.

## Advertising cookies

Ads are served by Google AdSense, which with its partners may set cookies to:

1. display advertisements and limit their repetition;
2. check what has been seen and detect fraud;
3. customize ads based on your interests, if you give your permission.

If you visit DAI from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a prompt will appear to accept or reject these cookies before they are installed. You can read [how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Control cookies

From the privacy settings of your browser you can view, block or delete cookies, both on your computer and on your cell phone. If you block them, the magazine continues to work the same; It only changes the advertising you will see.

## Changes to this policy

If the use of cookies on the site changes, we will update this page and its date.
