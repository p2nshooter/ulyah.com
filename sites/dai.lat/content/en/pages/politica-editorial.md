This page explains how the DAI editorial team works.

## What we look for in each guide

- **Answer a specific problem** of a real house: heat, humidity, lack of light, little space, noise, an uncomfortable kitchen.
- **Explain the reason** for each recommendation, so that you can adapt it to your case.
- **Take the region into account.** Latin America covers tropical, high altitude, arid and temperate climates. When a solution depends on the weather, we say so.

## How we verify

We contrast the technical data with reference sources: manufacturers' sheets, technical standards, manuals from public organizations and publications specialized in bioclimatic architecture and construction. When the sources differ or the data depends on each product, we indicate it.

## What we don't do

- We do not invent projects, architects, interviews or product tests.
- We do not publish prices or quotes that we cannot support.
- We do not charge brands to appear nor do we use affiliate links.
- We do not present advertising as editorial content.

## Corrections

If an article contains an error, we correct it and update the review date. You can notify us at [contacto@dai.lat](mailto:contacto@dai.lat).
