A house in Monterrey is not resolved the same as one in Bogotá, Lima, Montevideo or Guayaquil. The sun, the rain, the humidity, the materials available, the way of cooking and even the way of receiving visitors change. However, a large part of the decoration ideas that circulate come from very different climates and ways of life.

**DAI** —Design, Architecture and Interiors— is a magazine that tries to explain the Latin American house from the inside: how to orient a home so that it does not get hot, how to ventilate an apartment, what floor can withstand the humidity of the coast, how to plan a kitchen where real cooking is done or how to turn a patio into the best room in the house.

## What we publish

- **Architecture and houses:** distribution, orientation, cross ventilation, structure and extensions.
- **Interiors and decoration:** color, furniture, textiles, lighting and order.
- **Kitchens and bathrooms:** planning, materials, storage and maintenance.
- **Materials and finishes:** brick, concrete, wood, adobe, ceramic, stone and paints.
- **Patios and exteriors:** shade, plants, exterior floors and terraces.
- **Comfort and sustainability:** heat, humidity, noise, water and energy with passive solutions.

## Who does DAI

DAI is an editorial project of **p2nshooter**. The editorial team writes, reviews and signs the articles collectively. We do not invent architects, designers or projects that do not exist: we explain principles, criteria and processes with reference sources, and we warn when a job requires a licensed professional.

## Independence

We do not sell furniture or materials and we do not charge brands to appear in our guides. The magazine is financed by Google AdSense advertising, always separate and recognizable. If you want to propose a topic or correct a fact, write to us from the [contact] page (/contacto/).
