DAI publishes general information on design, domestic architecture, materials and interiors. We make sure it is correct, but no guide knows your house, your land or the regulations of your municipality.

## Works and security

- **Structure.** Opening a wall, adding a floor, building a slab or hanging heavy loads can compromise the stability of a home. These jobs require a professional to calculate and sign off on the solution.
- **Facilities.** Electricity, gas and water present risks of fire, poisoning and flooding. They must be installed by authorized technicians in accordance with the regulations of your country.
- **Permits.** Many renovations require municipal permits or notices. Find out before you start.

## Products and prices

We do not publish material prices or construction estimates: they vary by country, city, season and supplier. Comparisons between materials are general and must be confirmed with the manufacturer's technical sheets.
