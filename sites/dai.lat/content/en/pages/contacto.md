You can write to the DAI editorial office at:

**[contact@dai.lat](mailto:contacto@dai.lat)**

## When to write to us

- **To correct a fact.** If an article contains a technical error or a recommendation that does not fit the reality of your country, tell us which one and why. We review each notice.
- **To propose a topic.** Tell us what problem you have at home: humidity in the walls, a small kitchen, heat on the top floor, a patio without shade. Many of our guides are born from questions like those.
- **For questions about the magazine**, advertising or the use of our texts.

## What we cannot offer

We do not design projects, we do not calculate structures or budget works remotely, and we do not recommend specific contractors. For works that affect the structure, electricity, gas or water, go to a licensed professional in your city and consult the permits required by your municipality.

## Answer

We normally respond within five business days. Your data is only used to respond to you, according to our [privacy policy](/politica-de-privacidad/).
