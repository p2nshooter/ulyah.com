Textiles are the fastest way to change a house. A well-chosen curtain filters light and provides privacy, a rug defines the room and softens the echo, and some cushions and a blanket turn an impersonal sofa into a cozy place. They are also the elements that suffer the most from the sun, humidity, dust and daily use, and the ones that accumulate the most size and material errors.

In Latin America, furthermore, the climate weighs heavily in this election. A thick velvet curtain makes sense in a cold house in Bogotá or La Paz, but it can become a nest of dust and mold in Guayaquil or Veracruz. In this guide you will see what type of curtain is best in each case, how to hang it, what size rug to choose for each environment, which fibers perform best depending on the climate and how to care for them so that they last.

## What is a curtain for?

Before choosing fabric and model, decide what you need in each window:

- **Privacy** day, night or both.
- **Light control:** filter it, soften it or completely darken it.
- **Thermal control:** reduce heat loss in cold climates or glare in hot climates.
- **Acoustics:** Thick fabrics absorb some of the noise and echo.
- **Decoration:** color, texture and visual height.

Many times the best solution is to combine two layers: a light fabric for the day and a denser one for the night.

## Types of curtains and blinds

| Type | What does it do well | Where it works |
|---|---|---|
| Curtain or voile | filters light and provides privacy during the day | living rooms, dining rooms, windows to the street |
| Medium fabric curtain | night privacy and warmth | living rooms and bedrooms |
| Opaque or blackout curtain | almost completely dark | bedrooms, television rooms |
| Thermal curtain or with lining | reduces heat loss and protects the fabric from the sun | cold climates, very sunny windows |
| Fabric roller blind | practical, takes up little space, easy to clean | kitchens, studios, small apartments |
| Screen or solar fabric | reduces glare and allows you to see out | home offices, west windows |
| Slat blind | regulates light and privacy precisely | bathrooms, kitchens, bedrooms |

## How to hang a curtain so it looks good

- **Hang it high,** close to the ceiling or at least well above the window frame. This makes the ceiling appear higher.
- **Make it wider than the window,** so that when you open it the fabric rests on the wall and does not cover the glass.
- **Calculate the gathering:** to make it look full, the total width of the fabric is usually between one and a half and two and a half times the width of the rail, depending on the type of construction.
- **Choose the length:** touching the floor or one or two centimeters away. In humid climates or with pets, prevent it from dragging: it accumulates dust and humidity.

In small apartments, tall, light curtains make the space feel larger, as we explain in the guide to [gain space in a small apartment](/articulos/departamento-pequeno-ganar-espacio/).

## Curtains and climate

**In cold or high altitude climates,** a thick lined curtain that covers the window well and reaches the floor reduces heat loss at night. The ideal is to open it on sunny mornings to let the sun in and close it at dusk. More ideas in the guide on the [house for high altitude cold climate](/articulos/casa-para-clima-frio-de-altura/).

**In warm climates,** interior curtains do little to help against the heat, because the sun has already passed through the glass. They serve for privacy and glare, but the real thermal protection is outside the window, as we explain in the guide to [cooling the house without air conditioning](/articulos/enfriar-la-casa-sin-aire-acondicionado/). Choose light, breathable fabrics that do not accumulate moisture.

**Sun discolors:** Fabrics exposed to direct sun lose color over time. A light lining protects the main fabric, and solid-color fabrics tend to hide wear better.

## Safety: blind cords

Cords and chains on curtains and blinds can cause strangulation in small children. If there are children at home, choose cordless systems, collect cords with fixed tensioners out of their reach, and do not place beds or cribs next to windows with cords.

## Rugs: the right size

The most common mistake with rugs is choosing them too small. A small rug in the middle of the room looks like a lost island. Some references:

| Environment | How it should be |
|---|---|
| Room | at least the front legs of the sofa and armchairs on the carpet |
| Dining room | that protrudes about 60 cm from the table on each side, so that the chairs remain on it when removed |
| Bedroom | that protrudes between 45 and 60 cm from the sides and foot of the bed |
| Hallway | leaving a visible floor margin on both sides |

## Fibers and materials

| Fiber | Advantages | Care |
|---|---|---|
| Wool | warm, resistant, hides stains | vacuum often, be careful with moths in humid climates |
| Cotton | economical, many can be washed | gets dirty and crushed faster |
| Jute, sisal or fique | natural, fresh, textured | water stains them, they can become moldy with humidity |
| Synthetic fibers | moisture resistant and easy to clean | can accumulate static electricity, variable quality |
| Polypropylene outdoor rugs | they withstand humidity and sun | ideal for terraces and high-use areas |

In very humid climates, synthetic fibers or washable rugs are often more practical than thick plant fibers, which retain moisture. In cold climates, wool is still one of the best options.

## Carpet Safety

- **Use a non-slip base** under rugs, especially on smooth floors and in homes with older people or children.
- **Avoid rolled edges** or small rugs in walking areas, which cause tripping hazards.
- **Do not place rugs on wet floors:** They trap moisture and can damage both the rug and the floor.

## Handmade textiles

The region has an enormous textile tradition: alpaca fabrics from the Andes, Bolivian aguayos, Mexican serapes and rebozos, hammocks from Yucatán and the Colombian Caribbean, Mapuche looms from southern Chile. A handmade textile can be the center of a room. If you buy them, look for artisans or cooperatives that pay them fairly, and follow the care instructions for each material.

## Upholstery and cushions

For a sofa for daily use, choose abrasion-resistant fabrics and, if there are children or pets, with washable or removable covers. In humid climates, quick-drying fills and breathable fabrics reduce the risk of odors and mold. Changing cushion covers depending on the season is an economical way to refresh the environment.

## Care to make them last

1. **Vacuum** carpets and curtains regularly, with the appropriate accessory.
2. **Clean stains immediately,** absorbing with a cloth without rubbing.
3. **Rotate the rugs** from time to time so that the sun and footsteps wear them evenly.
4. **Wash according to label** and dry completely before replacing.
5. **Store seasonal textiles** clean, dry and in ventilated places, as we explain in the guide on [closet and storage without humidity] (/articulos/closet-y-guardado-sin-humedad/).

## Frequent errors

- **Rugs too small** that float in the center of the room.
- **Curtains hung just over the frame** and do not cover the width of the window.
- **Thick, dark fabrics** in hot and humid climates.
- **Blind cords** within reach of children.
- **Plant fibers** in humid areas or areas with a lot of spillage.
- **Rugs without non-slip backing** on smooth floors.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should curtains reach the floor?

In most environments, yes: they look more elegant and make the ceiling appear higher. In kitchens, next to radiators or in homes with high humidity, a shorter curtain or blind may be more practical.

### What rug is best if I have pets?

One made of synthetic fiber or short-haired wool, in medium tones that hide hair and stains, and if possible washable.

### How often should you wash the curtains?

It depends on the dust, humidity and proximity to the kitchen. For reference, vacuum them every few weeks and wash them once or twice a year, or more often if they are near the stove or on a busy street. Always check the label: some fabrics shrink in hot water and others require dry cleaning.

### Do curtains help with noise?

Thick fabrics absorb some of the echo inside the room and some of the noise coming through the window. For real insulation, windows with good closure are needed. We see it in the guide to [acoustic insulation at home](/articulos/aislamiento-acustico-en-casa/).

## In summary

Choose each curtain according to its function: privacy, light, temperature or acoustics, and combine layers when necessary. Hang it high, wider than the window and at the right length, adapt the fabric to the climate and eliminate dangerous cords. Choose rugs of the correct size for each environment, with fibers appropriate to the humidity of your home and a non-slip base. Value the artisanal textiles of the region and take care of all textiles with regular vacuuming, immediate stain cleaning and good drying.
